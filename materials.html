Volo's Guide to All Things Magical

Name Rarity/Category Magical Effect Incorrect Believed Effect Notes
Agni Mani Ornamental Found as meteoritic glass in deserts; vaporizes in any blast (fireball, bead of force) but negates 1d4 points of blast damage per stone worn or carried. —
Alabaster Hardstone No known magical properties; impervious to moisture short-term, making it a good potion container. —
Alestone (Clinozoisite) Semiprecious Deflects solid projectiles; bearer must make a Dexterity check to catch or snatch it; increases effective AC by 1 vs. projectiles from the side the stone is on. — More is not better — only one stone's protection applies regardless of quantity.
Alexandrite Fancy Favored focal stone for luck, favor, and protection items (luckstones, lodestones). — Appears greenish in daylight, reddish under magical light.
Algae (Algae Quartz) Ornamental All beings or items in contact with it make saving throws vs. polymorph or shape-change at +2 bonus — even if the change is willing. —
Amaratha (Shieldstone) Jewel (soft) Absorbs electricity within 10 ft radius; a 1-inch-diameter sphere absorbs up to 6 points of electrical damage and is then consumed at that volume rate. Excess discharge beyond the stone's capacity still affects targets normally. — Too soft for exposed ring or staff settings; suited to protected locations on personal jewelry or ornamental armor.
Amber Fancy Spell component and spell ink ingredient in lightning and electrical enchantments (shocking grasp through chain lightning). Falsely believed to ward off disease and plague.
Amethyst Fancy (nine secrets) Component in communication spells (magic mouth) and Wisdom-augmenting magics; can be transformed into an ioun stone. Worn whole at mornbright (half-hour centered on 8 a.m.) by a being who wears as many pure amethysts as Wisdom points: grants the magical defense adjustment and spell immunities of Wisdom 23 (without bonus spells). Falsely said to ward off drunkenness and neutralize poison. Represents safety in seer visions; romance when seen in women's dreams.
Andar (Andalusite) Semiprecious Powdered, boiled with an amethyst/gold/silver dragon scale, then combined with an oily base: alternative ingredient for a potion of treasure finding. —
Angélar's Skin (Aasimon's Skin) Fancy Powdered, mixed with seawater, and followed by polymorph self, water breathing, and a free action variant: induces controlled were-shark transformation (permanent, manageable). — Named for the sorcerer Angélar who underwent the transformation. Pink coral, shallow tropical reefs.
Aquamarine Precious Orgonils ritual (exact process unknown, held as a secret in Telflamm): sacrificing an aquamarine causes another gemstone to become two identical copies. —
Archon (Fluorspar/Blue John) Hardstone Glows with a faint greenish radiance when magically invisible objects or creatures come within 20 feet. — Also see Fluorspar (the gem-grade variety).
Augelite Ornamental Magically inert; in fact lessens spell effects in its vicinity: –1 damage per die for spells within 10 ft; +2 bonus to all saves vs. spells and magical effects within the same area. — Cannot be carved into delicate shapes without splitting.
Aventurine (Love Stone) Semiprecious Powdered, penetrates magical disguises; touch shatters most illusion and transformation magics. —
Azurite Ornamental Prevents all heat damage to any being in direct flesh-to-gem contact; reduces most fire/flame damage by half. — Related to malachite but restricted to fire protection only.
Banded Agate Ornamental Powdered in sleeping drafts: used as a spell ink ingredient and potion base. Powder not actually effective as a sleep aid on its own. Also used as a calming "soothe stone" by merchants during negotiations.
Beljuril (Fireflashil) Jewel Periodically absorbs ambient heat, light, and vibration within 30 ft and discharges as sparkling cold flash (harmless). Chips mounted at the end of lightning wands add 1d6 damage beyond normal. Powdered, prized in spell ink and item-enchanting baths for all electricity-related magic. — Usually fist-sized; extremely hard to cut. Flash rate varies randomly regardless of size.
Black Opal Gem Powdered, mixed with powdered orl and introduced to open flame in the proper way: explosion dealing 6d8 (within 10 ft), 4d8 (11–20 ft), 2d8 (21–30 ft); save vs. petrification for half. Items within 10 ft must save vs. disintegration; 11–20 ft vs. crushing blow. —
Black Sapphire Jewel Once cut and polished, prevents temporal stasis, time stop, all chronomancy spells, and time sphere spells from functioning within 30 ft. Such magics cease if the gem is brought within 30 ft; some resume after it leaves, others are ended permanently depending on their nature. — Mostly from the Great Rift; prized by dwarves.
Bloodstone Semiprecious Laid on an open wound: instantly closes it, banishes disease and blood poisoning, stops bleeding (dissolves in doing so). Works once per being per lunar cycle. Alternative component for invisibility, divination, and storm magics. — Combined with a sprig of heliotrope: full alternative for the invisibility spell.
Bluestone (Sodalite) Ornamental Powdered and added to water lit by any magical radiance: acts as either neutralize poison or heals 1d2 damage. Added to any magical healing potion: adds a neutralize poison function plus 1d4 additional healing. —
Blue Quartz Ornamental Used in scrying spells; best gem for gems of seeing; sliced and treated for magical eyes (eyes of the eagle, etc.). —
Boakhar (Wulfenite) Semiprecious Erupts in a 7-foot jet of flame lasting 1 round when a magic missile is cast or passes within 10 ft — consuming the gem and dealing 2d4+2 fire damage. — Found in old Sembian/Calishite furniture inlays. Ignites flammable substances touched, never the item the gem came from.
Brandeen (Stibiotantalite) Fancy Cure for deafness: powdered and added to sap of any living hardwood, a message spell cast on the mixture (vowel sounds only), kept out of sunlight, and drunk within a day. —
Carnelian (Sard) Semiprecious Component in luck- and protection-against-evil spells and items. All saves and ability checks of wearers aided by +1; once properly enspelled and then sacrificed, allows a failed save, system shock, or resurrection roll to be rerolled at +2/+20% bonus (gem vanishes in a flash). —
Chalcedony Semiprecious Used in magical items warding undead or with necromantic powers; preferred when human bone is to be avoided. Powdered, enchanted simply: tasteless antidote to alcohol (added to a drink, prevents drunkenness). — Chalcedony is a broad category including carnelians, chrysoprase, and agates; "chalcedony" used specifically refers to the white/gray/black uncolored variety.
Chrysoberyl Fancy (nine secrets) Used in protection-from-magic-jar, possession, and incorporation-necromancy enchantments; forged with chips/stones into a weapon with the right enchantments: weapon can strike ethereal and gaseous foes at all times. Dust + juice of specific flower: cures mummy rot and all molds/fungal growths instantly on contact. Certain Netherese message stones. Can be transformed into an ioun stone. —
Chrysocolla Ornamental Neutralizes alcohol on contact; ingredient in animate dead spell inks and castings (often thrown as powder into a fire). —
Chrysoprase (Stormrock in Cormyr) Semiprecious (nine secrets) Used in items and inks for invisibility magic and for seeing invisible beings and objects. Can be transformed into an ioun stone. —
Citrine (False Topaz) Semiprecious Prevents magic jar attacks from affecting any being wearing or carrying one; conversely, whole citrines are a favored gemstone for use as the jar itself. —
Clelophane Semiprecious Echo effect: 70% chance to record a silent 3D mental image of the bearer and their surroundings when a spell is cast by or on them. 70% chance to overwrite prior echoes; otherwise adds a new image. Echoes can be called up repeatedly at will; appear as a slightly luminous 10-ft globe beside the stone for 3 rounds. — Images persist indefinitely, always at perfect clarity even if the original scene was confused.
Coral Fancy Powdered: highly effective ingredient in potions of healing and extra-healing — reroll all 1s and 2s on hit dice restored. — Pink and crimson varieties used as gemstones.
Corstal (Petalite) Ornamental When touched by magical radiance of any kind (fireball to faerie fire), mirrors that light's hue and intensity within itself, becoming a light source for 2d12 rounds before fading. —
Crown of Silver (Psilomelane Chalcedony) Ornamental Powdered and applied to ferrous metals: prevents rusting. Ingredient in spell ink and component of the everbright spell. Can substitute for iron filings in most castings (such as protection from evil). — Black metallic bands within the stone.
Datchas (Datolite) Semiprecious If powdered datchas is ingested by a wizard (with a nonalcoholic drink), it doubles the duration of a spider climb spell affecting them. —
Diamond Jewel Near-universal spell ink ingredient (especially vision, divination, and locating-object spells). Best gems for gems of seeing. Essential in a helm of brilliance. Worn at throat or head: wards off dream visions and enchantment/charm magics; +2 bonus to all saves vs. enchantment/charm. Diamond dust used in neutralize poison processes. Best used at highsun (noon). — Powdered diamond used at noon: increases magical working efficacy by 2 or 25%.
Dioptase (Diopside) Semiprecious Dissolves in an already-enchanted liquid in 2 turns; each stone dissolved into such a liquid restores 1 hit point when drunk. —
Disthene (Kyanite) Ornamental Powdered ingredient in spell inks for controlled fiery effects (shaped flames, not explosive). —
Emerald Jewel Used in fertility, health, growth, and lie-detection spells and items. Worn on flesh directly contacting the flesh of a living deceiver: shatters to reveal the falsehood (does not trigger on the wearer's own lies). When mounted on healing or growth items: +2 bonus to all those item's saving throws; adds 2d4 extra charges; item spontaneously recharges (1 charge on a 5–6 on 1d6 each night). Use at waterclock (2 p.m.) for 20% better odds. — Kings wear emerald rings at parleys to detect treachery without a spell.
Epidote Ornamental Ingredient in potions of undead control and in protection-from-undead scroll inks. —
Euclase Precious If a spell is cast on one or on a bearer: automatically triggers a flame strike effect (6d8/3d8 on save), consuming the gem. —
Eye Agate Ornamental Powdered in sleeping drafts. Powder does not actually improve sleep drafts' effectiveness.
Fire Agate Ornamental Whole stones dissolved in fire lizard or pyrolisk blood: most favored base for potions of fire resistance. —
Fire Opal Gem Used in items that cause, release, or control fire; essential in helms of brilliance. A whole mounted fire opal adds 1 point per die to damage done by any fire-producing magical item it adorns (not cumulative with multiple fire opals). —
Flamedance (Rhodizite) Precious Stone and items set with it automatically succeed at all saves vs. normal and magical fire. Beings wearing one or more flamedance gems: +3 bonus to all fire-related saving throws. — Very rare; hard; ideal for carving.
Fluorspar (Fluorite) Ornamental (multiple grades) All forms, including Archon/Blue John: glows with a faint greenish radiance when magically invisible (not merely disguised or ethereal) objects or creatures come within 20 feet. — Pink/red varieties ("cabra stones") are the rare, more valuable type.
Frost Agate (Frost Stone) Ornamental Polyhedron-cut versions often become luckstones. Powder in potions: ensures peaceful miscibility — removes any chance of explosion or of a failed potion being poisonous. — Skilled cutting required to produce faceted version without splitting.
Garnet Precious Properly enchanted weapons set with garnets deal double or triple normal damage. Enchantments should commence at high morn (half-hour centered on 11 a.m.). Generally considered useless in magical work. Thought by some fading faiths to be divine avatar's hardened blood.
Gold Sheen (Gold Sheen Obsidian) Semiprecious Added to any spell creating radiance: caster precisely controls the spell's color. Added to invisibility magics: increases duration by 1d3 rounds. — Also see Sheen (the broader obsidian variety).
Goldline (Cacoxenite) Ornamental Consumed in open flame with the right spells: a relatively easy way to give a bladed metal weapon a bonus enchantment. —
Greenstone (Chlorastrolite) Ornamental (nine secrets) Finest stones enchanted into greenstone amulets: wearer is immune to many mind-influencing spells (as ongoing mind blank). Powdered: component in spells resisting mental attacks and protective barriers. Can be transformed into an ioun stone. Nystul's magic aura is often used on cheap stones to fake valuable amulets. Powdered greenstone must be used with care; reports of it completely negating some spells.
Hambergyle (Hambergite) Semiprecious Held in a flame while a light spell is pronounced over it: creates a continual light effect at the cost of the gem, which vaporizes. —
Heliodor (Golden Beryl) Precious Component in all priest sun-sphere spells in place of missing components (provided they need not be specially constructed). Essential ingredient in forging a sun blade. —
Hematite Ornamental (nine secrets) Strong magical affinity with blood and life forces; especially responsive to healing and necromancy enchantments. Used in periapts of healing and periapts of foul rotting. Can be transformed into an ioun stone. —
Horn Coral (Night Coral) Precious Wearer who touches it with one of their own tears gains the ability to water walk (as the 3rd-level priest spell) for up to 6 turns; gem dissolves when the effect ends. Some special pieces become only inert for a turn and can then be reactivated. —
Hornbill Ivory Hardstone Material component in spells and items concerned with flight. — From hornbill beaks, not true ivory.
Hyaline (Milky Quartz) Ornamental Glows with a blue radiance when active magic takes effect, is launched, or passes within 20 feet. —
Hydrophane Semiprecious Used in water-oriented potions and items (water breathing, control water elementals). When used as an additional component in color spray: targets save at –1. — Opaque white when dry; transparent and iridescent when soaked in water.
Hypersthene (Bronzite) Semiprecious Wyvern warding: bearer is not seen or struck by a wyvern watch spell; they can pass through its warded area freely while the spell remains active. —
Iol (Iolite/Cordierite/Violet Stone) Semiprecious (nine secrets) Best gemstone type to transform into ioun stones (with the proper secret spells). — Commonly displays a color-change effect when viewed from different angles.
Iris Agate Hardstone Spell reflection: spells hurled at a screen or statue of iris agate rebound directly back at their source. — Sometimes placed as false guard statues or silhouettes in vault doorways.
Irtios (Danburite) Semiprecious Prevents mildew, rot, and molds from affecting any organic substance in contact with it; protection includes yellow mold, mummy rot, and fungal diseases. Extends to a living or undead creature in continuous direct-flesh contact. —
Ivory (Dentine) Hardstone Dragon teeth and fangs of certain magical creatures (such as displacer beasts) have magical uses; common beast ivory generally does not. — Unicorn horn (alicorn) is not ivory; commands thousands of gp from alchemists.
Jacinth (Hyacinth/Flamegem) Jewel Ingredient in potions and items protecting against fire (rings of fire resistance). Has an inner flickering flame visible from afar (not bright enough to illuminate). — Found only in the Realms; other crystal spheres have an inferior garnet substitute.
Jade (Jadeite/Nephrite) Fancy/Hardstone Powdered: preferred base for spell inks for all illusion/phantasm spells; substitute for nonorganic components in all such castings. Essential ingredient in enchantment baths for illusion-capable items. Use in such processes initially at candleglass time (half-hour centered on 9 p.m.). Said to enhance musical ability — when certain simple enchantments are laid upon it, jade actually confers perfect pitch and sharp hearing to all who wear it.
Jargoon Fancy Magic missile cast into a held jargoon: gem explodes (1d4+2 damage to caster), but the number of missiles in the spell is doubled. Missiles behave normally in all other respects. Falsely credited with preventing a lycanthrope from changing out of human form.
Jasmal Gem Hard enough to hold a cutting edge; can be worked into small nonmetallic weapons. In rows along a blade: holds multiple or complex enchantments that visit upon victims when the jasmals strike. Powdered: ingredient in enchantment baths for magical armor and in ink formulae for Veladar's Vambrace. —
Jasper Ornamental Crushed: universal substitute ingredient in potions, antidotes, and items protecting against or neutralizing poison. Drinks stored in carved jasper vessels for at least a day are purged of all poisons, taints, and corrosive powers. — Preferred stone for both periapts of foul rotting and periapts of proof against poison.
Jet Fancy Used in magic jars (favored gemstone). Certain treatments or spells cast too close to jet may free a long-imprisoned mage or creature from within — or summon a wizshade. Stone of mourning and sorrow; fell reputation partly due to magic jar use.
Kings Tears (Frozen Tears/Lich Weepings) Jewel Presence within 90 feet reduces casting time of legend lore to 2 turns and brings a name, word of activation, or key term regarding the subject into the caster's mind. A secret high-level ritual allows a spellcaster to permanently gain 1 point of Wisdom by sacrificing one. Can be cut into up to four gems of insight with proper enchantments. — Extremely rare; unique to the Realms; never yet cut, fractured, or chipped by any means. Each contains a vivid, unchanging scene from a long-dead ruler's life.
Kornerupine Fancy Green variety only: if a magic mouth spell is cast on one and the last word left unsaid, it records all sounds within 20 ft for 4 rounds when the final word is later spoken. Records indefinitely; cannot be added to, silenced, or altered without shattering the stone. Brown variety has no known properties.
Laeral's Tears (Beryllonite) Fancy (nine secrets) Rarest of the nine secrets; can be transformed into an ioun stone. When prepared by a complex secret process known to Rashemani witches: absorbs all harm done to the wearer until the stone shatters — typically 24 points of damage before crumbling. —
Lapis Lazuli Ornamental Best gemstone for a periapt of health if ruby is unavailable. Powdered: principal base ingredient in potions of heroism and super-heroism. — The deeper the blue, the more valuable.
Lumachella (Fire Marble) Hardstone Powdered: ingredient in spell inks and components for both water breathing and airy water spells. —
Luriyl (Apatite) Semiprecious Glows and vibrates slightly when touched by magical tracer or scrutiny (any scrying or clairaudience spell) — warns of unseen eavesdroppers. —
Luspeel (Magnetite) Hardstone Considered vital by many for properly tempering swords that will bear multiple enchantments. — Ferromagnetic; used in temple furnishings for dramatic effect.
Lynx Eye (Green-flash Labradorite) Ornamental Dissolved in griffon blood or tears of a catoblepas: forms either a base for all healing or necromantically helpful potions, or an alternative ingredient in Keoghtom's ointment. — Green-flash variety of labradorite.
Malachite Ornamental Component in priest elemental-sphere spells and wizard spells involving ice, cold, fire, and heat. Powdered: ingredient in enchantment baths for items of warmth, fire resistance, and cold resistance. Falsely rumored to prevent falls. Often set on rings of feather falling to exploit the false rumor for sales.
Malacon Semiprecious Unique magical trap/delay: a spell can be cast into a malacon for later release in one of three ways — when dropped or struck; when the caster wills it forth (gem vaporizes); or at a preset time (gem dwindles). Specialized spells are needed to call forth a malacon's magic if one is not the original caster. —
Marble (Calcite) Hardstone Magically inert (so far as is known). —
Meerschaum (Sepiolite) Hardstone Can be converted into bone replacements by the stretchbone wizard spell, becoming indistinguishable from real bone for purposes of animate dead or similar magic. — Rumors of clairaudience and wizard eye casting through prepared distant meerschaum, and of necromantic uses, remain unconfirmed.
Mellochrysos Semiprecious If held in open flame within a day of a light spell being cast on it: a single flame rises from the stone and burns for up to 12 hours, dwindling the stone away slowly. The flame is not extinguished by wind or water (even full immersion). Can be thrown to start long-duration fires in hard-to-reach places. Counter: phenalope. —
Microcline (Amazonstone) Ornamental Powdered and dissolved in sap of any deciduous tree: most versatile and abundant alternative to octopus/squid ink as a base for spell inks. —
Moonbar Ornamental Powdered: used in ink formulae, potions, and spell components for magic controlling, healing, or creating undead; can replace important missing ingredients related to necromancy. — Naturally large rectangular crystals with smooth surfaces.
Moonstone Semiprecious True uses: powdered, component in abjuration and barrier spells; in evocation spells, used as a magical storage medium and for sudden energy redirection. Moonstones can substitute for many nonorganic abjuration/evocation components. Falsely said to force lycanthropes into animal form when seen. Considered sacred to Selûne. Glows faintly for about an hour in darkness after light is removed.
Moss Agate Ornamental Ground fine: ingredient in medicines inducing enforced deep sleep; in all potions, causes normally incompatible substances to mix stably — always a safe additive, removing any chance of explosive or poisonous potion failure. —
Mykaro (Smithsonite) Semiprecious Cures blindness when powdered, mixed with any nonalcoholic drink, a wizard eye spell's viewing component is passed through the mixture, and the afflicted being drinks within 1 turn. Effect in 1d4+1 rounds (longer if eyeballs must regenerate). —
Mynteer (Phenakite) Semiprecious Spell ink ingredient for levitation, telekinesis, and force-construct spells (unseen servant, Tenser's floating disc, etc.). —
Nelvine (Albite) Ornamental Spell and ink component in magics that disguise or change appearance without altering true nature. —
Nune (Staurolite/Cross Stone/Fairy Stone) Ornamental Powdered ingredient in dispel magic spell ink; used in the bath during formative enchantment of a wand of negation. — Cross-shaped crystals up to 1 inch across.
Obsidian Ornamental (nine secrets) Material component in spells emulating the priestly blade barrier spell; used in obsidian steed figurines. Best known of the nine secrets; can be transformed into an ioun stone. — Too fragile for most other magical uses.
Octel (Scheelite) Fancy When touched to a ring of free action, becomes "awakened" — glows bright enough to illuminate 2 ft in darkness and permanently prevents all paralyzation and hold magics from affecting anyone touching or bearing it. — The awakened property is permanent once gained.
Onyx Semiprecious (nine secrets) Contact with onyx aids in safe, relatively painless childbirths. Can be transformed into an ioun stone. Any unenchanted amount of onyx on a being: –1 penalty to all saves and ability checks. Generally considered unlucky.
Oolite (Ool Stones) Ornamental Powdered: ingredient in castings and spell inks of purification and neutralization magics. — Too small to cut; usually polished and mounted in patterns.
Opal Precious Near-universal component in items that store spells for later release without loss of efficacy. Can replace missing components when other components are lacking. Never use in items that invoke both fire and lightning — immediately causes a chain lightning (12d6)-equivalent explosion that spawns Melf's minute meteors at each new target. Best used at twilight (6 p.m.); using it then increases enchanting odds by 2 or 25%. —
Ophealine (Axinite) Fancy When worn: prevents all magical hold spells and paralyzations. Warning: so deadening to magic that it cannot itself be used in any castings or spell preparations — the magic fails. — Called "knuckle stones" on Waterdeep streets; often sharpened and worn as a punching weapon.
Orbaline (Benitoite) Precious Renders objects (not living things or undead) in contact with it resistant to fire: +4 bonus to item saves vs. normal fire, +5 vs. magical fire. —
Orblen (Honeystone) Gem If a healing spell is cast into it: radiates warmth and golden light in a 60-ft radius for 12 turns; beings in the radiance gain potion of vitality effects; remaining 6+ consecutive turns also cures 2d6 damage. If an invocation/evocation or combat-sphere spell is cast into it: explodes as a triple-strength meteor swarm (twelve 2-ft-diameter spheres, 10d4 damage each, 20 ft in all directions). —
Orl Gem See Black Opal — same explosive combination: powdered orl + powdered black opal + open flame = the explosion described under Black Opal. — Red-hued orls are most valued.
Orprase (Pollucite) Semiprecious Powdered and mixed with wine consecrated to Tymora by a full priest of the goddess (at least 3 oz liquid + 1 stone): imbiber gains +6 bonus to their next save or ability check, however far in the future. — In high demand among Tymora's faithful.
Pearl Precious Material component of spells transforming acids into harmless water and of neutralize poison. Basis for pearl of power, pearl of the sirines, and pearl of wisdom. Powdered: valued in enchanting all magical mirrors. — Rare rainbow and black pearls are most valuable.
Peridot Precious Used in abjuration spells and protective items: one peridot per spell level (consumed) provides the equivalent of a proper component for casting or warding. —
Phenalope (Rhodonite) Semiprecious Prohibits all magical flames (including fireballs) from igniting or remaining alight within 60 ft — manifests as a momentary flash and puff of smoke only. Also extinguishes nonmagical fires (6-in-8 chance per round; 4-in-8 for oil-based fires); prevents reignition. — Found in palace floor mosaics for protection.
Pipestone (Catlinite) Hardstone Can substitute for wood in any wizard (not priest) spell. —
Psaedros (Lepidolite) Hardstone A hand-sized or larger mass can replace both the fire and holy water as material components of a wind walk spell. — Colors fade with prolonged sunlight.
Rainbow Obsidian Semiprecious Pass stone for prismatic magics: can pierce shells of prismatic spells without triggering them or being harmed, and can bring inorganic materials attached to it through as well. Living creatures and undead wearing it still face normal prismatic effects. —
Raindrop (Flawless Cassiterite) Precious See also Woodtine. Temporarily darkens when touched to any gemstone, metal, or stone that bore a deliberate enchantment but no longer does — more sensitive than detection spells, which only reveal the strongest residual enchantments. —
Ravenar (Black Tourmaline/Schorl) Gem Shares the tourmaline property: any spell of any level or class cast into a ravenar is transformed into three 6d6 straight-line lightning bolts radiating in directions chosen by the caster; consumes the gem. — Also see Tourmaline (all varieties share this property).
Red Tears (Tempus Weeping) Semiprecious Universal substitute for all material components of healing spells (if not specially constructed); ingredient in inks for mending-object spells. — Teardrop-shaped, cherry-red, blood-crimson, or fiery orange crystals.
Rhodochrosite (Rosenstone) Ornamental Powdered in a special tea (full eyeball-sized stone): 20% chance of acting as neutralize poison or cure disease; entire batch must be consumed by one being. —
Rock Crystal Ornamental Common component in spells requiring gemstone material of a stated value without specifying the type. When treated with two minor secret spells plus Veladar's Vambrace: becomes fusible like glass, then solidifies harder than normal — usable for mace heads, climbing spikes, or spear/ram heads. —
Rogue Stone Jewel Increases all chances of magic going wild, going wrong, or magic-related saving throw failures by 1 per stone within 20 ft (cumulative if multiple stones are together). Used in the gemjump spell. Primitive tribes believe them to be sentient dragon or hero essences. Always found as singletons; no two rogue stones in one place at once; origin rock type unknown.
Rosaline (Unionite/Thulite/Pink Zoisite) Ornamental Trichroic crystals with the right color combination (rare): each crystal consumed negates one layer of a prismatic spell (outermost first). Identifying which crystals work is a deadly process that consumes the stone during testing. — Most rosaline crystals do not have the right nature; identifying the correct ones is itself dangerous.
Ruby Precious All items concerned with improving personal fortune (augmenting ability checks, saves, and survival rolls) can be made with +25% chance of enchanting succeeding if one ruby per function is powdered into the process (+10% if only one ruby for a multi-function item). Ruby dust widely used in sun-sphere and elemental priest spells and abjuration, alteration, and evocation wizard spells. Best at summer sunset (~5 p.m., +2 or 25% to efficacy). Essential in a helm of brilliance; preferred for healing items. Generally considered lucky. Can correctly ward lightning and earthquakes; in very rare cases, contain unknown living creatures released when the gem is carefully shattered.
Rusteen (Microlite) Precious Spell component for wall of force and forcecage magics. —
Saganite (Needlestone/Love Stone/Hairstone) Ornamental Added to components of any spell with direct explosion or burst-of-flame effects: augments damage by 1 point per die. —
Samarskite Semiprecious Reduces all damage done by undead attacks by 1 point per attack (or per die of damage for multi-die attacks) for any being wearing or carrying it. — Used as mourning gemstone.
Sanidine Ornamental Any piece the size of the caster's thumb or larger: sole spell component for all spells involving water or purification (if components need not be specially constructed). — Favored by Bedine and other desert nomadic peoples.
Sapphire Precious Widely used in items related to magical prowess, the mind, and the element of air. Wearer partially protected against fear, anger, despair, and insanity effects; +2 bonus to all saves vs. emotion/mind-clouding magic (charms, feeblemind, insanity) or a normal save when none would ordinarily be allowed. A whole sapphire (5,000+ gp) in an enchantment bath plus powdered sapphire: 55% chance of raising a weapon's magical bonus by 1. As spell component: spells last for the longest possible time and deal maximum damage in their first round. Best at midmorn (10 a.m.). —
Sarbossa (Thomsonite) Ornamental Added to components of spells involving shape transformation of a victim: adds 1d2 rounds to spell duration. — Found in nodules up to 1 inch in volcanic cavities.
Sardonyx Semiprecious (nine secrets) Used in spells and items affecting Wisdom. Same magical uses as kings tears. Can be transformed into an ioun stone. Sardonyx gemstones sometimes (not reliably) guard against magic missile: 2-in-6 chance of absorbing 1 point of damage per missile (not per spell); gem is never harmed. —
Satin Spar (Feather Gypsum) Ornamental Partially negates magic missiles: any missile aimed at the bearer vaporizes a satin spar stone worn/carried/touched, but deals only half damage. — Too fragile to wear normally.
Scapra (Fine Scapolite) Fancy When added to components of a guards and wards spell (consumed in casting): permits addition of either one extra magical effect beyond the normal five, or one phantasmal force effect. One extra effect per gemstone, maximum five additions. —
Serpentine (Williamsite) Semiprecious/Hardstone Each stone (gemstone or the right hardstone variety) functions once against cold and once against fire as the priestly resist fire or resist cold spell, then crumbles. Multiple serpentines on one bearer: only one activates per trigger event. Malachite is falsely rumored to prevent falls; serpentine is actually the fire/cold protection stone.
