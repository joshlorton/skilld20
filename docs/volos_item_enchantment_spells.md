# Item Enchantment Spells

*Volo's Guide to All Things Magical (TSR 9535), pp. 20–24 and 27–32.*

Spells are listed by class, then by level, then alphabetically within each level. Where a spell appears in both lists with identical or near-identical mechanics, key differences between the wizard and priest versions are noted in the effect summary.

---

## Wizard Item Enchantment Spells

### 4th Level

---

#### Dweomerflow
**Level:** Wiz 4 (Alteration)
**Casting:** 4 / Components: V, S
**Range/Area/Target:** 20 yards or touch / — / One person or one item
**Duration:** 1 round
**Saving Throw:** Special

Two casting modes. **Mode 1 (spell redirect):** Cast on a spellcaster (self or another); the recipient's next spell flows into a prepared receptacle — a focal stone, crystal ball, magical item needing recharging, or similar vessel — instead of taking its normal effect. No saving throws apply. If no valid receptacle is in range when the spell takes effect, a ghostly radiance appears and fades within the round; the spell is wasted. **Mode 2 (charge transfer):** Transfers charges or stored spells from one item to another. The caster must touch both items to bridge the energy. Both items save vs. spell: if the sending item fails, the spell is ruined but nothing is lost; if the receiving item fails, the transferred charge or spell is lost — sometimes spectacularly.

---

#### Merald's Meld
**Level:** Wiz 4 (Alteration)
**Casting:** 1 round / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / — / Two nonliving objects
**Duration:** Permanent
**Saving Throw:** None

Permanently binds two objects together without physical fasteners. The caster touches both items and positions them in the desired join; the magic fuses them unbreakably under normal circumstances. Living or undead material cannot be melded. The two items are not altered — a given item can be involved in any number of melds. The join can be broken by four *dispel magic* spells targeted specifically at it, unless a *crown meld* was later applied, in which case the item is entirely impervious to dispel magic. If the item fails an item saving throw, a meld is more likely to fail (separating the pieces cleanly) than the materials themselves are to be damaged — unless a crown meld was applied, in which case joins never separate this way. *Material components:* a flake or drop of resin, an iron filing, a strand of spider silk, and a paste of ground legumes.

---

#### Obar's Lesser Purification
**Level:** Wiz 4 (Abjuration)
**Casting:** 4 / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** 3 yards / One item or volume of liquid or raw materials no greater than the caster's body volume / —
**Duration:** Permanent
**Saving Throw:** None

Destroys curses, magical tracers, and remnants of previous magics on a single nonliving item or body of liquid. Causes the target to glow to reveal active or dormant dweomers, but does not trigger, identify, or alter them. Also works on dry or powdered materials gathered into a continuous volume. Does not affect recently living materials (dead less than a month, or undead). Secondary function: purges liquids — potions, tinctures, unguents — of natural or magical impurities simultaneously with the main dweomer-purging. *Material components:* a pinch of limestone dust, a pinch of salt, and three drops of a *potion of sweet water.*

---

### 5th Level

---

#### Abeyance
**Level:** Wiz 5 (Alteration)
**Casting:** 5 / Components: V, S
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / — / 1 focal stone or 1 magical item
**Duration:** Special
**Saving Throw:** None

Two modes. **Mode 1 (freeze a focal stone):** Freezes a spell already cast into a focal stone, holding it in suspension indefinitely. The abeyance is broken by a *dweomerflow*, *wondrous web*, or *holy vesting* spell (which conveys the stored magic onward without releasing it), by shattering the focal stone (which releases the spell instantly), or by certain other extraction spells. *Dispel magic* is specifically not one of these. **Mode 2 (delay an item activation):** The caster touches a magical item (not an artifact) with bare flesh at casting; the next time that item is activated, its effect is delayed 1 round. The abeyance then dissipates.

---

#### Eternal Flame
**Level:** Wiz 5 (Alteration, Necromancy)
**Casting:** 5 / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / One item or body not exceeding twice the caster's own body volume / —
**Duration:** Special
**Saving Throw:** None

Two targets. **On an item:** Imbues the item with a receptive neutral enchantment so that later spells cast at it (or transferred into it via *dweomerflow*) enter it without a saving throw, transfer failure, or damage — up to one spell or effect per level of the *eternal flame* caster. Does not make the item usable; only allows it to hold magics. The window for imbuing powers closes when an *awakening* spell is cast. If the item is shattered before awakening, stored magics release explosively. **On a mammalian body (living, dead, or undead):** The next spell to contact the prepared body takes full effect at maximum potency with no saving throw, system shock, or resurrection survival roll required. Exceptions: enchantment/charm and illusion/phantasm spells are not affected by eternal flame. *Material component:* a spark struck by a dweomer-bearing item against a rock, bladed weapon, or dweomer-bearing metal item; neither item is harmed.

---

#### Focal Stone
**Level:** Wiz 5 (Alteration)
**Casting:** 1 round / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / — / 1 clear or translucent crystalline gemstone no larger than the caster's balled fist
**Duration:** Permanent
**Saving Throw:** Special (item save vs. magical fire at casting; failure destroys the gem)

Restructures a clear or translucent crystalline gemstone to hold a spell dweomer. On success the stone glows with a soft internal radiance and emits a faint dweomer. Exceptional-quality stones may receive a +1 to +3 bonus to the saving throw. Special property: if immersed in a poisonous liquid the focal stone turns vivid purple; it can then be commanded by touch and will to neutralize the poison, after which it dissolves and is destroyed. Common misconceptions aside, a focal stone cannot trap passing spells or draw in enchanted creatures. It resists natural and magical heat, flame, lava, extreme cold, and mineral contamination, but not deliberate crushing. Shattering it releases any stored spell instantly in a manner the DM determines. *Dispel magic* has no effect on a focal stone or its contents. *Material component:* a pinch of opal dust of any type.

---

#### Veladar's Vambrace
**Level:** Wiz 5 (Alteration)
**Casting:** 5 / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / One item no larger in volume than the caster's body volume / —
**Duration:** Permanent
**Saving Throw:** None

Strengthens inorganic substances so they do not rot or age. The item may contain organic substances (glues, etc.) only if they are less than one-tenth of total volume; otherwise the spell fails. Existing fractures and blemishes are purged; the item appears like new. Fragile substances are hardened, and hard substances made more resilient; the item gains +5 on all saving throws vs. acid, crushing blow, disintegration, fall, normal fire, and cold. *Material components:* a whole diamond of not less than 2,000 gp value; a sliver or chunk of ironwood of the same size or larger than the diamond; a piece of chitin of about the same size from a beholder, ankheg, or bulette; and a drop of giant slug spittle.

---

### 6th Level

---

#### Crown Meld
**Level:** Wiz 6 (Alteration)
**Casting:** 1 round / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / — / One item
**Duration:** Permanent
**Saving Throw:** None

Required when more than four different substances are used in a magical item's construction, or when four or more *Merald's meld* spells are applied. Weaves multiple preexisting melds into a resilient whole, treating all joined pieces as one unflawed continuous object. When the item must make a saving throw, it uses the most favorable saving throw of any of its component substances for each situation (e.g., a wood-and-glass scepter saves as thin wood vs. a fall but as glass vs. fire). A crown-melded item is entirely impervious to dispel magic targeting its joins and never separates at a meld due to a failed item saving throw. *Material components:* a lump of resin as large as the caster's thumb, an iron nail, a strand of spider silk, a handful of paste of ground legumes, and a flawless diamond of any size and at least 2,000 gp value.

---

#### Wondrous Web
**Level:** Wiz 6 (Alteration)
**Casting:** 1 round / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / Special / —
**Duration:** 1 round/level
**Saving Throw:** Special

Transfers magical powers from source items into a destination item at one power or control property per round. Sources may be potions, spell scrolls, parchments inscribed with magical command words, focal stones, or existing magical items. The transfer creates a visible humming network of glowing white lines of force between all items involved. Powers are held in the destination item in stable magical stasis until an *awakening* spell is cast. Only existing magical items get a saving throw: base 7 for items with fewer than three functions; base 9 plus one per function beyond four for items with four or more functions. A successful save means no effect; failure means a random function is drawn out. Charge-based functions lose 1d4 charges per round rather than the function itself; non-charge functions are permanently stripped. If ended prematurely by the caster's will or by *dispel magic*, magic in transit is lost; already-transferred powers and untouched sources are unaffected. Missiles striking a *wondrous web* are teleported 1d6×10 feet in a random direction but are otherwise unharmed. *Material components:* six strands of spider silk and a piece of fishing net or lace.

---

### 7th Level

---

#### Awakening
**Level:** Wiz 7 (Enchantment, Invocation)
**Casting:** 2 rounds / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / — / One item
**Duration:** Permanent
**Saving Throw:** Special

Activates a multi-enchantment item. Requires *eternal flame* and usually *wondrous web* (or *holy vesting*) and *dweomerflow* castings first. Can also be used to add new powers to an existing *enchant an item* item once all original powers have been used at least once. The item makes a saving throw using the caster's save vs. spell, modified: –1 per being other than the caster who contributed magic; +1 per *Merald's meld* and *crown meld* existing on the item; +1 if both *eternal flame* and *Veladar's vambrace*, *Nulathoe's ninemen*, or *holy might* were cast on the item. A natural 1 always fails. Failure: roll 1d6 — 1 or 2, explosion as a fireball (1d6 damage per caster level) and item destroyed; 4–6, item collapses to magically inert dust carried away by a sudden wind. Success: item activates instantly; a flash of light may occur but is never a harmful discharge. *Material components:* a wizard spell scroll bearing at least as many spells as the item has functions; a gem of any kind worth at least 2,500 gp per item function; and a living plant that has been magically altered.

---

#### Azundel's Purification
**Level:** Wiz 7 (Abjuration)
**Casting:** 1 round / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** 3 yards / One item or volume of liquid or raw materials no greater than the caster's body volume / —
**Duration:** Permanent
**Saving Throw:** None

The more potent version of *Obar's lesser purification*. Destroys curses, magical tracers, and remnants of previous magics, and additionally expunges existing wizard spells and wizard-imposed spell-like abilities without triggering them. Priest spells and priest-imposed abilities on the item are not removed but glow to indicate their presence. Does not trigger, alter, or identify any dweomers. Also purges liquids of natural or magical impurities simultaneously. Does not affect recently living or undead materials. Cast twice on the same item (regardless of time between castings), all component parts of the item are refined to the finest possible state: internal and visible imperfections removed, craftsmanship raised to the highest level, making the item ready for the most powerful and complex enchantments. *Material components:* a pinch of dust from a powdered focal stone, a flawless diamond of any size or value, and at least three drops of a *potion of sweet water.*

---

#### Blood Link
**Level:** Wiz 7 (Necromancy)
**Casting:** 1 round / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / — / 1 sentient creature
**Duration:** 1 turn
**Saving Throw:** Neg.

Can only be used in conjunction with a *permanency* or *permanency prayer* spell; must be cast within 1 turn of either to have any effect. Drains the 1-point Constitution loss involved in casting permanency from a touched sentient creature instead of from the caster. Unwitting or unwilling targets save to negate the loss. If the target can move and is hostile or suspicious, a successful attack roll is required to touch them. Good- and neutral-aligned casters risk alignment shift unless divine command or other mitigating circumstances apply. *Material components:* a short chain carved from zalantar wood, three walnut shell halves, and a drop of blood.

---

#### Dweomer Divination
**Level:** Wiz 8 (Divination)
**Casting:** 3 rounds / Components: V, S
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / — / One item
**Duration:** 1 round/level
**Saving Throw:** None

Reveals information about an enchanted item in a sequential unfolding process, one category of information per round, until the spell expires or all information has been gained. A repeat casting on a previously examined item skips already-known information. Sequence: (1) all dweomers present; (2) alignment/class restrictions and special benefits; (3) primary function fully revealed (how it works); (4) words of activation for the primary power; (5) any flaws or gaps in the primary power. This cycle repeats for each additional power in descending order of strength, then reveals control words and restrictions, recharging methods, and finally images of how the item was made. On normal (non-enchanted) items, performs as a sophisticated *detect magic* only. Ends immediately if the caster loses contact with the item for a full round or if the item has no dweomer. Provides no protection against curses or traps identified in the process, and may not reveal unintended side effects. Note: even non-enchanted items can bear trace dweomers if touched by spells — lasting 1 day per spell level for normal spells, and months or years per spell level for permanent or trigger-based magics.

## Priest Item Enchantment Spells

### 4th Level

---

#### Dweomerflow
**Level:** Pr 4 (Alteration) — Sphere: All
**Casting:** 7 / Components: V, S
**Range/Area/Target:** 20 yards or touch / — / One person or one item
**Duration:** 1 round
**Saving Throw:** Special

Functionally identical to the wizard version, with one significant addition unique to the priest version: a **holy water font or consecrated altar** can accept up to a dozen flowed spells as a repository. Any being of the faith who touches the altar and wills the magics forth may release them one per round thereafter, with silent mental control over targeting. Beings unfamiliar with the stored magics need 1 round of contact per stored spell to identify and control them; one random magic is revealed at a time to beings of the same faith (or via *dweomer divination*). Spells of 5th level or higher must be accompanied by an *abeyance* cast on the altar or vessel within 1 turn of being flowed in, or they erupt at a random time and random target. Altars commonly store offensive spells for lesser priests defending holy ground; damaging, defiling, or using cold iron on an altar almost always activates stored magics on the spot.

### 6th Level

---

#### Higher Consecration
**Level:** Pr 6 (Alteration, Evocation) — Sphere: All
**Casting:** 2 rounds / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / — / One item
**Duration:** Permanent
**Saving Throw:** Special (item save vs. spell)

Dedicates an item to the service of the caster's deity, cleansing it of all other influences and other deities' marks. The item makes a saving throw using the caster's save vs. spell, modified cumulatively: +1 if crafted by or for the faith; +1 if cast on a consecrated altar or sacred ground; +1 if at the deity's express or dream command; +1 if all material components were personally made or gathered by the caster; –1 if unholy water is omitted; –1 if holy water is omitted; –2 if plain water is used; –2 if the item is known to be flawed or incomplete. Success is typically shown by a brief levitation and blue-white glow, though the deity may choose another color, wreathe the item in flame, or make it vanish if the priest is deemed unworthy. *Material components:* the priest's holy symbol; a spark struck from flint by cold iron (all three consumed in casting); a pinch of dung-heap earth on which plants have grown; a drop of holy water; a drop of unholy water; and a sealed vial of air from an enclosed space in which a dragon used its breath weapon within 1 turn of sealing.

---

#### Holy Might
**Level:** Pr 6 (Alteration) — Sphere: All, Creation
**Casting:** 9 / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / One item no larger in volume than twice the caster's body volume / —
**Duration:** Permanent
**Saving Throw:** None

Priest counterpart to *Veladar's Vambrace*. Strengthens inorganic substances against rot and age. Same rule on organic content (must be less than one-tenth by volume). Purges fractures and blemishes; item appears like new. Grants +6 (vs. +5 for *Veladar's Vambrace*) on saving throws vs. acid, crushing blow, disintegration, fall, normal fire, and cold. Allows item volume up to twice the caster's body (vs. equal to the caster's body for the wizard version). *Material components:* a whole, unflawed ruby, emerald, or sapphire of not less than 3,000 gp value; a sliver or chunk of duskwood of the same size or larger; a piece of chitin of similar size from a beholder, ankheg, or bulette; a piece of obsidian of any size; and black dragon acid.

---

#### Holy Vesting
**Level:** Pr 6 (Alteration, Evocation) — Sphere: All, Creation
**Casting:** 3 rounds / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / Special / —
**Duration:** 1 turn + 1 round/3 levels
**Saving Throw:** Special

Priest counterpart to *Wondrous Web*, with the same purpose and general mechanics: transfers magical powers from source items into a destination item at one power per round, creating a visible shimmering in the air between connected items. Key differences from *Wondrous Web*: the saving throws for drained items are higher (base 9 for fewer than three functions; base 11 plus one per function beyond four); and when cast on an altar dedicated to the caster's deity, the spell is immune to *dispel magic*. Missiles striking the caster during casting have the normal chance of ruining it, but after casting is complete only the caster's death or unconsciousness can end it prematurely. *Material components:* one drop of holy water consecrated to the caster's deity for the recipient item, plus one additional drop per source item; and a stick or block of burned incense.

#### Dweomer Divination
**Level:** Pr 7 (Divination) — Sphere: Divination
**Casting:** 3 rounds / Components: V, S

**Saving Throw:** None

#### Permanency Prayer
**Level:** Pr 7 (Alteration) — Sphere: All, Creation
**Casting:** 3 rounds / Components: V, S
**Range/Area/Target:** Touch / — / One item's enchantments
**Duration:** Permanent
**Saving Throw:** Special

Renders the enchantments on an item permanent. Cannot make the effects of cast spells permanent on a creature — only on an item's powers. Base 12-in-20 chance of success, modified cumulatively: +1 if cast on a consecrated altar or sacred spot; +1 if the caster performed an exceptional service for the deity within the season (and cast no other *permanency prayer* this season); +3 if the item is being made per the deity's direct or dream-vision command; –1 if the caster intends personal gain or aggrandizement; –2 if the item is flawed, damaged, or incomplete. Whether it succeeds or fails, the caster loses 1 point of Constitution (negated if a *blood link* has been cast within the previous turn to transfer the loss to another). The caster knows the outcome by a feeling of exaltation (success) or chill foreboding (failure) when touching the item after casting. No material components.

#### Ritual of Transference
**Level:** Pr 7 (Alteration) — Sphere: All, Creation
**Casting:** 1 turn / Components: V, S, M
**Range/Area/Target:** 20 yards / — / One item or being
**Duration:** 1 turn (after the casting turn)
**Saving Throw:** Special

A cooperative ritual allowing participants to cast spells into a prepared item or being, transfer charges between items, or infuse an item or being with powers from another magical item, altar, or artifact. The caster must have physically touched the destination or at least one donor source at some prior time; no contact during casting is required. A continuous droning chant by at least one participant must be maintained throughout the casting turn; the original caster may stop chanting if others continue. Additional priests join by casting their own *ritual of transference* or a *combine* spell and touching the initial caster with bare flesh. After the casting turn, the ritual remains active for 1 turn during which participants cast spells or transfer charges into the recipient. Each incoming magic triggers a separate saving throw: for an item, base 12-in-20 per magic; for a being, normal save vs. spell. Cumulative modifiers: +2 per additional participating priest; +1 if the recipient is on or in an altar or sacred spot; –1 per magic contributed by a non-initial-caster; –1 per participant not serving the same deity; –2 if any hostile magic affects the recipient or any participant; –5 per participant slain or deliberately wounded during casting. Only spell-like powers can be transferred into beings; purely mechanical item abilities cannot. Beings can later release stored magics by act of will or absorb them as healing energy (one release per round). *Material components* (specific not stated in the text; implied to include prayer materials and holy implements).

## Metal Treatments (pp. 58–59)

These are not spells but durable material processes that modify metal permanently. Blueshine has since been reproduced as a wizard spell; Everbright has not.

---

### Blueshine
**Source:** Dwarven metallurgical process; a wizard spell (Wiz 4, Alteration) duplicating its effects was developed by Toth of Calimport and published here for the first time (the wizard was killed by Red Wizards attempting to suppress it, but the spell had already been stolen by a rival and survived).

**Spell stats:** Level 4 / Casting time 4 / Components V, S, M / Range touch / Area the metal parts of one item not exceeding the caster's body volume / Duration permanent / Saving throw none.

**Effect:** Purges all existing oxidation from metals and prevents future rusting. Purges fractures and blemishes from the item, leaving it whole and appearing like new. All treated metals glow with a deep blue sheen when they catch available light. Grants +1 bonus to all item saving throws vs. acid and all corrosive effects — venom, various creature bloods, ochre jelly secretions, black dragon acid.

**Organic content limit:** If organic substances (glues, etc.) make up more than one-tenth of the item's total volume, the spell fails.

**Material component:** A small piece of cobalt blue glass or a chip of a blue-hued gemstone.

---

### Everbright
**Source:** Dwarven metallurgical process. No spell equivalent has been found despite long-running research by at least three mages (Athlagh of the Many Locks, Halandrar Crowncloak, and Spelargh of Phelzol) and one archpriest (Beltorvan Duircragh, Bright Blade of Tempus in the Bloodbright Temple of Dolselar).

**Effect:** Metals treated with everbright gain a permanent bright chromium-like shine and become immune to tarnishing, all discoloration, acidic corrosion, and rusting — including rusting caused by rust monster antennae. This protection does not diminish with time.

**Limits:** The everbright protection is broken if the treated item is shattered into more than three pieces, or if it comes into contact with lava, dragon fire, spellfire, or forge heat exceeding the temperature used in the latter stages of the everbright process itself.
