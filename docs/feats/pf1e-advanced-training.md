# SKILLd20 Feats -- PF1e Advanced Weapon/Armor Training

Draft SKILLd20 XP costs for the two real Paizo talent-menu systems tied to the Fighter's base Weapon
Training / Armor Training class features, priced as standalone Feats via the formula in
`conversion-methodology.md`. Shield-related feats (Shield Focus, Shield Master, Missile Shield, etc.)
are already covered in `pf1e-feat-costs.md`'s Combat table -- not duplicated here.

Scope: **Advanced Weapon Training** from the *Weapon Master's Handbook* (18 of 23 AoN-listed options
-- the other 5 come from later, out-of-scope sourcebooks). **Advanced Armor Training** from the
*Armor Master's Handbook* (12 of 12 -- the entire list lives in this one book). Baseline "must have
the Weapon Training / Armor Training class feature" access isn't counted as a dimension, same as
weapon/armor proficiency elsewhere.

**30 options total** (18 + 12). 27 of 30 price at the formula's floor of 2 -- unlike Rogue Talents'
Advanced tier (gated hard on rogue level 10) or the main PF1e feat catalog's BAB/level-heavy Combat
feats, both Weapon and Armor Training's advanced options are almost entirely prerequisite-free once
the baseline class-feature gate is set aside.

## How to apply

Priced as standalone Feats, same treatment as `pf1e-rogue-talents.md` -- no assumption these feed a
future Focus-list system. Weapon Mastery and Armored Master are the only two "variable" entries
(self-selecting from another feat/chain), each priced at the formula's generic fallback rather than
an invented ceiling value -- if either ever gets priced per-specific-choice, expect a wide range
depending on which underlying feat is selected.
