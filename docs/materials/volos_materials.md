## Gemstone Rarity Categories

From least to most valuable: **hardstones** (natural substances found in large quantities, suited to carving), **ornamental stones**, **semiprecious stones**, **fancy stones**, **precious stones**, **gems**, and **jewels**. Many stones appear in both massive (hardstone-grade) and cut (gem-grade) forms; a stone listed as semiprecious may also appear in a cheaper hardstone variety.
