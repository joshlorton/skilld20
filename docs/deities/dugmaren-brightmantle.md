# Dugmaren Brightmantle (The Gleam in the Eye, the Wandering Tinker, the Errant Explorer)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Dugmaren Brightmantle
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Gleam in the Eye, the Wandering Tinker, the Errant Explorer
- **Portfolio:** Scholarship, invention, discovery
- **Symbol:** An open book; the physical holy symbol is a silver locket crafted to resemble an open
  book, often used to carry small scraps of lore inside

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics) and specialty priests of Dugmaren receive the
  standard dwarven clergy grants (literacy (Dethek runes), modified turn undead) -- see
  `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** Viewed with mild distrust by most dwarves, who admire the learning and
  inventiveness of Dugmaren's followers but shy from the risk of getting caught in a spectacular
  failed experiment or from the tiring, quixotic energy of his faithful; other races tend to be more
  tolerant. Temples sprawl both above and below ground, crammed with the detritus of experiments and
  artifacts gathered on distant travels, centered on a great library of rune stones and foreign tomes;
  altars are a simple granite block bearing a single ever-burning candle. The clergy is dominated by
  specialty priests (85%), with a few clerics (12%) and fighter/clerics (3%); by subrace it runs shield
  dwarf (53%), gold dwarf (46%), and a scattering of gray dwarves, jungle dwarves, and gnomes (1%). The
  priesthood remains unevenly split by gender (96% male, 4% female).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. The Curious (novice)
  2. Seeker of Truth and Mystery (full priest)
  3. Questing Wanderer
  4. Avid Fiddler
  5. Philosophical Tinker
  6. Seeking Scholar
  7. Searching Sage
  8. Errant Philosopher
  9. High Savant (a High Old One, 7th level or higher)

### Dogma

- The secrets of the world are waiting to be revealed. Travel widely, broaden your mind at every
  opportunity, and pursue the life of a scholar.
- Cultivate the spirit of inquiry among the young, and be a teacher to all.
- Seek to recover the lost and arcane knowledge of ages past, and apply it in the world of today.
- Try new methods of doing things just for the joy of experimenting.
- Learn a little of everything, for you never know what might be of use down the road.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Spend the day in scholarly pursuit -- learning, teaching, and advancing nearly any field of
  knowledge even marginally of interest to dwarves.
- Serve as instructors to the young, or record and archive current dwarven practices for future
  generations.
- Travel widely, seeking new experiences, new ideas, and the recovery of lost dwarven lore.
- Whisper a prayer of thanks to Dugmaren upon any significant discovery or the recovery of forgotten
  lore.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Greengrass and Higharvestide | Spring and autumn (annual) | The only holy days regularly observed. Each begins with several hours of private early-morning meditation spent staring into the heart of a single lit candle, followed by a day-long scholarly convocation where the results of investigation since the last gathering are presented, defended, and discussed. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* The Order of the Lost Tome -- a loosely structured fellowship of errant dwarven scholars,
  usually working alone or alongside unaffiliated adventurers, dedicated to recovering lost dwarven
  lore from the fallen strongholds that hold it, combining a passion for knowledge with the martial
  skill to clear out whatever now occupies those ruins.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Xothor (singular xothar)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapons broad sword & short sword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light or Medium (up to and including chain mail); no shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Divination, Guardian, Numbers, Protection, Thought, Travelers,
  Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Chaos, Creation, Healing
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (engineering)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on literacy (Dethek runes); +1 bonus on
  linguistics (pick any two ancient or modern languages); +1 bonus on lore (ancient history); pick two:
  art, craft (brewing), craft (carpentry), craft (cobbling), craft (fishing), craft (leatherworking),
  craft (pottery), rope use, seamanship, craft (tailoring), craft (stonemasonry), craft (weaving),
  craft (blacksmithing)

**Judgment call:** the book's avatar wields a "broad sword +4" named Sharptack, but modern published
3.5e sources give Dugmaren's favored weapon as the short sword -- both are listed per project
convention. No explicit favored weapon is stated for the xothor specialty priest itself in the source
text; this file infers it from the avatar's weapon and the real-world cross-reference.
