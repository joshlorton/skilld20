# Ghaunadaur (That Which Lurks, the Elder Eye, the Ancient One)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Ghaunadaur
- **Titles/Epithets:** That Which Lurks, the Elder Eye, the Ancient One
- **Portfolio:** Oozes, slimes, jellies, outcasts, ropers, rebels, all things subterranean
- **Symbol:** A purple circle outlined with an inner ring of violet and an outer ring of black, with a
  single black-rimmed, violet-on-mauve eye at its center -- or (an older symbol) an inverted amber
  triangle on a purple background, amber lines inside forming an upside-down "Y" whose arms bisect the
  triangle's sides

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics), crusaders, and specialty priests of
  Ghaunadaur receive religion (drow), religion (elven), and reading/writing (drowic) as bonus
  proficiencies -- see `drow-priests-general.md`. Any living creature -- even oozes and jellies -- may
  join the clergy, since Ghaunadaur values devotion over ability; ghaunadan and many slithermorphs are
  all counted as members. Among the "solid" (nonamorphous) clergy, 95% are male.
- **Organization:** Titles vary widely among the solid clergy; examples include Loathsome Ooze, Spawn
  of the Pit, Eater of Wastes, Noxious Slime, Creeping Doom, and Amorphous Annihilator. Specialty
  priests (amorphites) make up over 60% of the clergy; clerics, fighter/clerics, and crusaders make
  up 12%, 8%, and 5% respectively, with the remainder a wide range of nonpriest creatures. Temples,
  usually underground, are lit by purple, mauve, and lavender radiances and drifting luminous mists,
  with floors of polished porphyry, obsidian, black marble, or similar dark stone. The altar chamber
  has ceiling-support pillars of polished obsidian, malachite, or serpentine graven with Ghaunadaur's
  runes, sometimes permanently enchanted (including hidden teleport runes known only to the temple's
  priests). A pillar-flanked aisle rises across three tiers to a dull, porous, rusty black rectangular
  altar stone on the second tier, surrounded by gongs, chimes, drums, candelabra, and braziers.

### Dogma

- All creatures have their place, and all are fit to wield power.
- Those who hunt weed out the weak and strengthen the stock of all.
- Those who rebel or walk apart find new ways, try new things, and do the most to advance their races.
- Creatures of power best house the energy of life, which Ghaunadaur reveres and represents.
- Make sacrifices to the Eye, and persuade others to sacrifice themselves to Ghaunadaur or in his service.
- Further the knowledge and fear of Ghaunadaur, and in the end give yourself to him in unresisting self-sacrifice.
- Convert all beings you can to Ghaunadaur's worship.
- Slay the clergy of other faiths, plundering their temples and holdings to better your own lot and
  further Ghaunadaur's worship.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Do whatever pleases Ghaunadaur best, and serve the Eye absolutely.
- Ensure, by force or threat, that a ready supply of sacrifices reaches Ghaunadaur's altars; the god
  supplies spells and tentacle rods to this end.
- Bring the Eye creatures that offer themselves without resistance -- regardless of whether they were
  charmed or otherwise coerced into doing so -- as such offerings are especially prized and favored.
- Become familiar with the use and manufacture of acids, poisons (including gases and incenses), and
  flaming oils; temples and priestly abodes are typically well supplied with such weapons.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Sacrifice | Daily | Ghaunadaur expects a prayer of adulation accompanied by a sacrifice at least once per day. If a live sacrifice cannot be procured, offerings of bones and food burned in oil, along with braziers of perfumed incense, suffice. |
| The Hand Offering | Whenever no other offering can be procured | The priest prays while holding one hand -- coated in any magical oil or potion (oils/potions of fire resistance are instantly converted to lamp oil) -- in an open flame. If the prayer is accepted (55% chance), the hand is healed of the damage it sustains immediately after sustaining it. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal, largely historical) Fanatics of the Overflowing Pit -- an elite order of dark elven
  crusaders of Ghaunadaur in ancient Ilythiir who waged endless war on the clergies of rival faiths;
  believed to survive in some form in the city of Llurth Dreier.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Amorphite
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapon tentacle rod (functions as a horseman's flail),
  per drow priests of Ghaunadaur -- later published sources instead give an amorphous tentacle
  treated as a warhammer; list both
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Any (Light, Medium, or Heavy all permitted)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Chaos (nonlawful amorphites only), Combat, Elemental, Guardian,
  Healing, Necromantic, Sun (reversed only)
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Creation, Divination, Numbers, Protection, Summoning, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in survival (Underdark)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on Blind-fighting

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Immune to diseases, even magically induced ones |
| 3 | Immune to all poisons |
| 3 | Once per day, for 1 round per level, resists acids/corrosives/caustic substances: mild corrosives cause no harm to the priest (though gear can still be damaged), stronger corrosives (such as black dragon breath or Melf's acid arrow) deal only half damage, and a +3 bonus applies to any required saving throw (half damage on a failed save, quarter on a success) |
| 5 | Immune to all acids and corrosive fluids and substances |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Mists of Ghaunadaur (3rd-level priest, 30 mana) *or* Evard's Black Tentacles (4th-level wizard, 40 mana), choose one per use, once per day |
| 7 | Once per day, gains a barrier that amorphous monsters (slimes, jellies, oozes, puddings, cubes, slithering trackers) will not touch, automatically foiling their natural attacks; ends immediately if the priest attacks or forces the barrier against such a creature |
| 10 | Immune to breath weapons |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Amorphous Form *or* Elder Eye (5th-level priest spells, 50 mana), choose one per use, once per day |
| 13 | Immune to all spells and effects from the elemental school or sphere |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Wall of Tentacles (7th-level priest) *or* Acid Storm (7th-level wizard), choose one per use, once per day (70 mana) |
