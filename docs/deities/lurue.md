# Lurue (The Unicorn, the Unicorn Queen, Queen of Talking Beasts and Intelligent Monsters)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Lurue
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Unicorn, the Unicorn Queen, Queen of Talking Beasts and Intelligent Monsters
- **Portfolio:** Talking beasts and intelligent nonhumanoid creatures
- **Symbol:** A unicorn's head against a silver crescent moon, or a white unicorn rampant before a silver
  crescent moon

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders)
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, specialty priests, crusaders, and mystics of Lurue receive religion
  (Faerunian), land-based riding (horses), and land-based riding (unicorns) as bonus skills. All of
  Lurue's clergy must be female, and may be human, half-elf, or elf.
- **Organization:** Lurue has no dedicated temples, and is instead worshiped in sylvan glades and
  moonlit glens wherever unicorns tread -- the banks of the Unicorn Run in the southern High Forest are
  representative of her holy sites. Her priestesses have no formal hierarchy of titles: junior clergy are
  called Sisters of the Moonlit Sky, and senior clergy Sisters of the Silver Moon; any priestess who has
  ever ridden a unicorn is forever after known as a Ladycorn, regardless of rank. The clergy comprises
  clerics (~40%), crusaders (~10%), mystics (~5%), and specialty priests known as silvermaids (~45%),
  with little differentiation between the types in duties or in the respect they accord each other.
  Lurue also counts many beasts among her clergy -- most unicorns, and quite a few pegasi, could be
  considered her lay priests.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Sister of the Moonlit Sky (junior clergy)
  2. Sister of the Silver Moon (senior clergy)

  Ladycorn is a separate honorific, held for life by any priestess who has ridden a unicorn, independent
  of the ranks above.

### Dogma

- The Unicorn is a symbol of hope, joy, salvation, and protection for the needy, forlorn, and forsaken.
- Life is to be relished and lived with laughter; quests are to be taken on a dare, and gifts made on a
  whim.
- Impossible dreams are to be pursued for the sheer wonder of the possibility of their completion.
- Everyone, no matter how unique, is to be praised for their strengths and comforted in their
  weaknesses.
- Evil melts quickest in the face of a rapier wit and unshackled joy.
- Search for the unicorn, and in the pursuit find happiness.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Provide aid and comfort to the needy, lend support to dreamers so they can achieve their aspirations,
  and rescue all who need aid from whatever assails them.
- Rarely stick to a single duty for long, though they see any particular task through to its end; most
  are periodically struck with wanderlust.
- Many are adventurers who travel the Realms seeking wrongs to right, balancing merriment, new
  experience, and self-improvement.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Midsummer's Eve | Midsummer | A night-long festival of revelry, wild rides through the countryside and sky, theatrical skits, humorous oratories, mock duels, grand communal songs, and romantic declarations. Many unicorns take a mate for life on this night. |
| Feast of the Moon | Onset of winter | A quiet ceremony remembering those who have passed away and now "dance with the unicorns." Great works of art and epic song are unveiled, especially in the city of Silverymoon, which also celebrates its founding on this day. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Order of the Unicorn (formerly the Knights of the Unicorn) -- began as a band of
  high-born adventurers from Baldur's Gate, blessed by an avatar of Lurue after a whimsical,
  riddle-laden quest into the elven ruin of El'lahana Raikeil in the Moonwood. Reorganized after the
  Time of Troubles into a loose collection of affiliated adventuring companies who wander the Realms
  "chasing the Unicorn," righting wrongs as they travel.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Silvermaid
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapon shortspear ("unicorn horn")
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light and Medium (up to and including chain mail), plus shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Charm, Divination, Healing, Guardian, Sun, Travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, Plant, Protection, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lance (any type), 1 rank in long sword, 1 rank in lore (nature); 1 rank
  in performance (singing) or art (pick one)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on herbalism; +1 bonus on land-based
  riding (horse); +1 bonus on land-based riding (unicorn); +1 bonus on airborne riding (pegasi)
- **Other Clergy Grants:** Any unicorn who worships Lurue may cast one beneficial priest spell of any
  sphere or level, once per day (unicorns are considered lay priests of Lurue).
