# Deep Sashelas (Lord of the Undersea, the Dolphin Prince, the Knowledgeable One, Sailor's Friend, the Creator)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Deep Sashelas
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of the Undersea, the Dolphin Prince, the Knowledgeable One, Sailor's
  Friend, the Creator
- **Portfolio:** Oceans, sea elves, creation, knowledge, underwater and sea elven beauty, water magic
- **Symbol:** A dolphin

### Symbol -- Midjourney prompt

```
heraldic emblem, a leaping dolphin silhouette in profile, sea-green and aquamarine palette, flowing wave motif beneath, bubbles trailing from the dolphin's arc, clean flat heraldic linework, holy symbol, high detail --ar 1:1
```

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, crusaders, druids, and specialty priests of Deep Sashelas receive
  religion (elf, aquatic) and literacy (Espruar) as bonus proficiencies. Priests of Deep Sashelas must
  be aquatic elves, aquatic half-elves, or malenti.
- **Organization:** Managed by an organized clerical hierarchy collectively called Delphions, locally
  autonomous per temple but sharing intelligence on sahuagin and other threats. Temples serve as the
  spiritual, physical, and social centers of aquatic elven communities -- coral-grown structures in the
  Sea of Fallen Stars, spiraling stone-and-shell constructs in the Great Sea, laced with small
  continual-light-lit passages. Aquarians (50%) and clerics (35%) form the bulk of the clergy and are
  strongly tied to a single community; crusaders (10%) are the militant arm and migrate between
  communities as tensions with sahuagin rise; sea druids (5%) live largely solitary lives tending the
  unsettled Undersea, organized into two circles (the Circle of the Great Sea and the Circle of Fallen
  Stars).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. The Impure (novice)
  2. The Bathed (acolyte)

  Beyond acolyte, titles vary by sea: temples of the Great Sea use Sea Otter, Seal, Walrus, Sea Lion,
  Delphinus, Narwhal, and Balaenas (ascending); temples of the Inner Sea (Sea of Fallen Stars) use
  Clam, Oyster, Nautilus, Argonaut, Trophon, Cowrie, Abalone, Conch, and Pearl (ascending). High
  priests of either tradition are collectively called Delphites, each with a unique individual title.

### Dogma

- Swim the great currents and the shallow seas.
- Exult in the everchanging beauty and life of the bounteous Undersea.
- Revel in the joy of creation, and increase its myriad aspects.
- Seek not to hold that which is everchanging, but instead love the change itself.
- Seek out fellow swimmers who honor the Lord of the Undersea, and ally with them against those who
  see only the darkness of the deeps.
- Follow the way of the dolphin.
- Promote the use of the seas by all reasonable folk for all time to come; fight those who would hoard
  its riches or pollute its depths.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Interact regularly with local dolphins; senior priests are almost always accompanied by a dolphin
  companion.
- Establish and maintain contacts with land-dwelling elves where feasible.
- Prevent sahuagin incursions through extensive contact networks, earning the sahuagin's undying
  hatred; conduct ritual shark hunts and attacks on sahuagin communities.
- Create beautiful works of art in the Creator's honor -- sculptures of living coral, jeweled and
  pearled coral art, or trained fish performing spectacular displays.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The High Flow | Especially high tides | A joyous celebration emphasizing beauty, creativity, and artistry; offerings of precious natural objects and works of art are made while acolytes swim in complex patterns with dolphins, singing songs of praise. |
| The Deep Ebb | Especially low tides | A grim, martial mirror to the High Flow, emphasizing remembrance of the lost and vigilance against the Undersea's ever-present enemies. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Knights of the Killer Whale -- crusaders dedicated to destroying evil sea races
  (ixitxachiti, koalinth, krakens, merrow, scrags, sahuagin), based in the Citadel of the Seven Seas.
- *(Formal)* Lances of the Sea Unicorn -- clerics, crusaders, and aquarians garrisoning nine undersea
  citadels across the floor of the Sea of Fallen Stars, containing the sahuagin of the Alamber Sea.
- *(Informal)* Several loosely affiliated pods of sea elven rangers are sponsored by Deep Sashelas but
  are not considered part of his clergy hierarchy.

## Specialty Priests

Deep Sashelas has two specialty-priest tracks: sea druids, solitary tenders of the wild Undersea, and
aquarians, the community-bound majority of the clergy.

### Sea Druids

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Net, trident, spear, dagger, knife; favored weapon trident
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (sea elven shell mail) or Medium (sea elven scale mail)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Elemental (Air, Earth, Water), Healing, Plant, Sun, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Divination
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as druids)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in herbalism
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on linguistics (pick one from: dolphin,
  dragon turtle, koalinth, locathah, merman, morkoth, sahuagin, sea elvish, sea sprite, triton,
  wereshark, whale); +1 bonus on sea lore; +1 bonus on Swim; +1 bonus on survival (underwater)

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | May be an aquatic elf, aquatic half-elf, or malenti; allowed to multiclass where normally permitted by race; +2 bonus on saves vs. electrical attacks |
| 1 | Gains an extra linguistics slot for aquatic languages every 3 levels (dolphin, dragon turtle, koalinth, locathah, merman, morkoth, sahuagin, sea elvish, sea sprite, triton, wereshark, whale) |
| 3 | Passes through aquatic vegetation (seaweed, kelp beds) without leaving a trail and at full movement rate |
| 3 | Perfectly identifies aquatic plants, animals, and untainted fresh or salt water |
| 7 | Immune to charm spells cast by aquatic creatures such as kelpies, nixies, and sirines |
| 7 | May shapechange up to 3 times/day into a normal (not giant) reptile, fish, or mammal native to the ocean depths, once per category per day |

### Aquarians

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Net, trident, spear, dagger, knife, underwater crossbow; favored weapon
  trident
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (sea elven shell mail) or Medium (sea elven scale mail)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Chaos, Creation, Divination, Elemental Water, Guardian, Healing,
  Necromantic, Protection, Wards, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, Elemental (Air, Earth), Summoning, Sun
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in art, 1 rank in Swim
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on linguistics (dolphin); +1 bonus on
  sea lore

## Sashelan Spells (deity-specific)

Priests of Deep Sashelas may also cast the 6th-level priest spell conjure water elemental, detailed in
Faiths & Avatars under Istishia.

| Name | Est. Mana | Foundation | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surface Sojourn | 10 | Elemental Water | Lets a water-dwelling recipient exist comfortably on land for hours, wrapped in a thin mantle of moisture. |
| Shark Charm | 20 | Animal, Charm | Sets sharks (or shark-related creatures) swaying in a hypnotic pattern, ceasing all other activity for a duration that shortens the more agitated or bloodthirsty they already are. |
| Summon Cetacean | 30 | Summoning, Elemental Water | Calls nearby dolphins, porpoises, or whales (up to 32 Hit Dice total) to aid the caster, staying until the task is done or they are dismissed. |
| Cetacean Form | 70 | Combat | Lets the caster assume the form of any cetacean (commonly a dolphin or narwhal) except giant whales or leviathans, gaining its abilities but not its Intelligence-based or magical ones, and may change forms repeatedly for the duration. |
