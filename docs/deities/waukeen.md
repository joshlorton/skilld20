# Waukeen (Merchants' Friend)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Waukeen
- **Titles/Epithets:** Merchants' Friend
- **Portfolio:** Trade, money, wealth
- **Symbol:** A gold coin displaying the face of the goddess facing to the sinister (heraldic left)

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** Loosely hierarchical; roughly 40% clerics and 60% specialty priests. All temples
  of Waukeen in Faerûn answer to a single head of the church, the Holycoin. Novices are called
  Telchar. Clergy members overall are known as Waukeenar, nicknamed "coinspinners" for their lavish
  spending rather than hoarding.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Telchar (novice)
  2. Coin
  3. Abreeant
  4. Counter
  5. Trabbar
  6. Investor
  7. Halanthi
  8. Lender
  9. Syndo
  10. Manycoins
  11. Grand Trabbar
  12. Spender
  13. Grand Syndar
  14. Overgold (general term for all high clergy)
  15. Holycoin (supreme head of the church)

### Dogma

- Mercantile trade is the best road to enrichment for all; increasing general prosperity brings
  everyone closer to the Golden Age that lies ahead, if people conduct themselves rightly.
- Destroy no trade goods, and raise no restrictions to trade.
- Propagate no malicious rumors that may harm trade; challenge and refute such rumors when they are
  heard.
- Give money freely to beggars and businesses alike, to demonstrate the goddess's bounty and to keep
  coin circulating rather than hoarded.
- Worship Waukeen and know wealth; to guard your funds is to venerate her, and to share them well
  brings the Golden Age nearer.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Travel the world aiding merchants, or staff temples in large cities that serve as moneylending and
  currency-exchange houses, safe-storage warehouses, and (covertly) fences for stolen goods, all in
  exchange for fees.
- House wealthy, generously-tithing merchants in priest-guarded temple accommodations during their
  stays in the city.
- Direct church funds into any enterprise with a reasonable hope of success if run by devout
  worshipers, and consider outside investment offers from entrepreneurs willing to make substantial
  offerings to the goddess.
- Quietly manipulate trade through rumors, buy-ups, and hired brigands when it serves the church, but
  never openly -- personal enrichment must come through arms-length investment, not unlawful acts.
- Tithe: every priest pays 25% of personal income (investment profits, adventuring treasure, rewards,
  fees) to the church.

### Affiliated Orders

- No formal knightly or military orders.
- (Informal) The church readily sponsors independent adventuring companies that show a reasonable
  prospect of profit in exchange for a 20% tithe on returns, and hires mercenary and adventuring
  companies to guard trade caravans and shipments of church goods overseas.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Goldeye / Goldeyes
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon nunchaku
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** Astral, Charm, Creation, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic, Protection,
  Travelers, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** All, Elemental, Summoning, Sun, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in etiquette
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon), appraising, and craft (gem cutting) checks; pick one
  additional language; +1 bonus on diplomacy checks when negotiating deals
