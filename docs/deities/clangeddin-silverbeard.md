# Clangeddin Silverbeard (The Father of Battle, Lord of the Twin Axes, the Giantkiller, the Goblinbane, the Wyrmslayer, the Rock of Battle)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Clangeddin Silverbeard
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Father of Battle, Lord of the Twin Axes, the Giantkiller, the Goblinbane, the
  Wyrmslayer, the Rock of Battle
- **Portfolio:** Battle, war, valor, bravery, honor in battle
- **Symbol:** Two crossed battle axes; the physical holy symbol is a pair of miniature steel battle
  axes welded together in a cross, worn on a chain around the neck

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics), crusaders, and specialty priests (including
  fighter/specialty priests) of Clangeddin receive the standard dwarven clergy grants (literacy (Dethek
  runes), modified turn undead) -- see `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** An elite warrior caste within many dwarven clans, widely revered for dedication and
  martial skill even by dwarves who wish they were less belligerent, and often mistaken by other races
  for mere bloodthirsty berserkers. Sacred shrines are cairns raised on old battlefields, whether on
  the surface or in the Underdark; caverns won in a great victory are sometimes dedicated outright as
  temples, especially when founding a new clanhold. Temples feature statues of past dwarven heroes,
  captured armor and weapons, and blood-red granite altars on which weapons are offered up. The clergy
  runs evenly between gold dwarves (50%) and shield dwarves (50%), with rare jungle or gray dwarves; it
  is numerically dominated by its most martial members -- crusaders (25%), fighters (23%), specialty
  priests (20%), fighter/specialty priests (11%), fighter/clerics (11%), and clerics (10%) -- and is
  90% male.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Unblooded (novice)
  2. Axebrother/Axesister (full priest)
  3. Axecutter
  4. Squire
  5. Knight of the Third Rank
  6. Knight of the Second Rank
  7. Knight of the First Rank
  8. Knight Commander
  9. War Prince/Princess (a High Old One, 7th level or higher)

### Dogma

- War is the finest hour of dwarvenkind. Seize the opportunity to defend the Stout Folk and ensure
  their victory wherever conflict erupts.
- Revel in the challenge of a good fight, and never waver in the face of adversity, no matter how
  ominous.
- When not fighting, prepare for the next conflict physically, tactically, and by acquiring resources.
- Attack hill giants whenever possible, and other evil giants when necessary.
- Death on the field of battle is never welcomed, and lives should never be thrown away foolishly --
  but if necessary for victory, the highest service a follower can perform is self-sacrifice to protect
  as many other dwarves as possible.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Form an elite warrior caste, training hard physically every day and always preparing for the next
  war -- physically, tactically, and by acquiring resources.
- Further the weapon training, tactical training, and battle skills of every living dwarf; pass on
  battle knowledge to any dwarf who will listen.
- Stay alert for new tactics, traps, and weapons -- including the devices of nondwarven inventors such
  as the worshipers of Gond.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Battle Anniversaries | Varies by temple; the calendar is filled with them | Every past battle, won or lost, is honored on its anniversary; conflicts whose heroes have faded from memory are marked only every decade, century, or millennium. On these days, the faithful chant, pray, and break weapons anointed with their own blood -- the god sometimes manifests to consume the offered weapons in a glowing radiance. |
| Weapon Sacrifice Rite | The evening before an anticipated battle, or at the burial of a great dwarven warrior | A slow procession of the faithful, in their most battered armor, marches onto the battlefield or to the gravesite behind drumbeating, armored priests, chanting a rising dirge that ends in a single clear note. The priests then close their eyes, focus on an image of the god, and walk blindly toward wherever they sense his presence is strongest, converging to make the weapon sacrifice and speak the names of the valiant fallen -- concluding with either interment and a solemn march, or a war chant and a "wild run" into the next day's battle. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Countless dwarven fighting companies raised in every clanhold across the ages, including
  the legendary Knights of the Ninth Axe, the Valorous Harts of High Shanatar, the Order of the
  Crescent Moon (jointly dedicated to Clangeddin and Selûne), the Fellowship of the Bleeding Axe, the
  Sailors of the Mountainous Waves, the Shining Blades of Iltkazar, the Glory of Gauntlegrym, and the
  Company of the Last Kuldjargh.
- *(Informal)* The Axe Dwarves -- cults of dwarves who worship sentient magical axes; Clangeddin has
  embraced some of these, granting spells to a minority whose practices still adhere to his principles,
  though most such cults have strayed far from his faith and answer to other powers instead. The most
  fearsome relic of the movement is the Living Axe, a neutral evil, sentient, adamantite battle axe
  believed to have once belonged to an avatar of the Father of Battle.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Alaghor (singular alaghar)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapon battle axe
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Guardian, Law, Protection, Sun, War
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Creation, Divination, Elemental Earth, Healing, Necromantic, Travelers,
  Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in battle axe; pick one: craft (armorer), craft (weaponsmithing)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on literacy (Dethek runes); +1 bonus on
  blind-fighting
- **Other Clergy Grants:** Clerics of Clangeddin (unlike most dwarven clerics) may use any weapon, not
  just bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons.
