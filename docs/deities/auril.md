# Auril (Frostmaiden, Icedawn, the Cold Goddess, Lady Frostkiss)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Auril
- **Titles/Epithets:** Frostmaiden, Icedawn, the Cold Goddess, Lady Frostkiss
- **Portfolio:** Cold, winter
- **Symbol:** A white snowflake on a gray diamond (a heraldic lozenge), with a white border

### Symbol -- Midjourney prompt

```

```

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** loosely and informally organized; clergy wander independently. Most priests use
  only the informal honorific "Hand of Auril" or "Icebreath." Formal ranks exist only at larger
  temples.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order, formal temples only):
  1. Postulant
  2. Votary
  3. Icewind
  4. Storm Sister / Storm Brother (the title held by most clergy between 3rd and 8th level)
  5. Frosttouch
  6. Lady/Lord Cold
  7. Lady/Lord Deep Winter
  8. Lady/Lord Cold Circle
  9. High Hand of Ice (head of a major temple)

### Dogma

- Cover all lands in ice and cold.
- Quench fire wherever it is found.
- Cut down windbreaks and breach walls/roofs so her breath may enter.
- Work darkness to hide the sun so her chill may slay.
- Make all Faerûn fear her.
- Revere her and sing her praises into every winter wind.
- Bring down her cold power on all so that she is known and feared.
- Do not raise a hand against another priest of Auril.
- Take the life of an arctic creature only in great need; slay any other creature at will.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Work to make all folk fear the goddess and her clergy (partly to reduce how often clergy themselves
  are attacked).
- Pursue personal wealth and influence by performing tasks others can't manage in extreme winter
  weather, and by magically protecting (for pay or obedience) those who would otherwise suffer the
  worst of the cold.
- Tithe/offering: scatter accumulated wealth into falling snow during a storm, or throw it into
  cracks in river ice or glacial crevasses during winter.
- During the cold months, each priest is expected to compel or persuade at least one person to pray
  to Auril properly (a prayer lasting as long as it takes a piece of ice larger than the supplicant's
  hand to melt against bared flesh, performed outdoors, preferably at night).
- During winter, each priest is also expected to personally slay at least one creature by cold --
  often to feed worshipers/potential worshipers, or to dispatch a personal foe.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Midwinter Night | Midwinter (annual) | The most holy night of the year -- an all-night ice-dancing festival where clergy are meant to both enjoy themselves and lure new worshipers into the faith. |
| The Embracing | Any time (ordination rite, not calendar-fixed) | The rite of admission to Auril's clergy. The postulant runs through a blizzard all night wearing only boots, a thin shift, and sacred body paint. They either perish of exposure or are "accepted" by being rescued from death by the goddess's favor. If performed in summer, the postulant must first travel to an arctic or high-mountain region to find a blizzard. |
| The Coming Storm | Onset of winter | Informal but enthusiastically celebrated ritual: clergy work en masse with cold magic to call down a fierce storm marking winter's start. |
| The Last Storm | Onset of spring | Mirror ritual to the Coming Storm, marking winter's end. |

*(These four are added to the standalone [holy-days-calendar.md](holy-days-calendar.md).)*

### Affiliated Orders

- No formal military or knightly orders -- she'd rather send a blizzard than troops.
- *(Informal)* Cult of Frost -- seeks the artifact known as the Ring of Winter.
- *(Informal)* Frost Witches -- a group of wizards who keep at least one tome of frost/cold magic in
  Auril's name, reputed to know the location of the *Codicil of White* (a priestly ritual book).
- *(Informal)* Sisters of Istishia -- nominally serve Auril but their worship seems to actually
  benefit Istishia instead.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Icepriestess / Icepriest
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapons battleaxe & ice axe
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light & medium armor, shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Combat, Divination, Elemental (no fire-related spells), Healing,
  Necromantic, Protection, Time, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Guardian, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (weather), 1 rank in ice axe
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on survival (pick one terrain from
  arctic, alpine, or other cold) -- direction sense is a survival skill action

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: **mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day.** Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first, e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2. Where no spell level is
given but a class level is (e.g. "at 12th level..."), spell level = class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Immune to natural cold and exposure, granted upon ordination (the Embracing, see Holy Days above); +3 bonus on saves vs. cold/ice-based attacks (save results improved one step) |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Frost Fingers (10 mana) |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Frost Whip (20 mana) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Ice Blade (40 mana) |
| 8 | Foundation bonus: Ice Storm *or* Wall of Ice, choose one per use (8 mana -- 4th-level spell, 2/tenday = level×2) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Cone of Cold (50 mana) |
| 12 | Foundation bonus: Summon Ice Para-elemental (mana = 6 + 6 per additional rank beyond 12; spell level 6 from class level 12÷2) |
