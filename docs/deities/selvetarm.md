# Selvetarm (Champion of Lolth, Thane of Lolth, the Spider That Waits, the Spider Demon, Prince of the Aranea, Lord of the Venomire)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Selvetarm
- **Titles/Epithets:** Champion of Lolth, Thane of Lolth, the Spider That Waits, the Spider Demon,
  Prince of the Aranea, Lord of the Venomire
- **Portfolio:** Drow warriors
- **Symbol:** A crossed sword and mace overlaid with the image of a spider

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including cleric/fighters, a multiclassed combination allowed to drow
  priests of Selvetarm), crusaders, and specialty priests of Selvetarm receive religion (drow),
  religion (elven), and reading/writing (drowic) as bonus proficiencies -- see
  `drow-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** The clergy is collectively known as the Selvetargtlin, drow for "warriors of
  Selvetarm." Temples are typically large subterranean chambers dominated by a huge black stone
  spider idol (the Chapel of the Sericeous Sargh in Undermountain is a representative example).
  Titles used at the drow city of Eryndlyn include Edge of the Axe, Crush of the Mace, Steel of the
  Blade, Tusk of the Boar, Hunger of the Swarm, Claw of the Cave Bear, Talon of the Wyrm, and
  Bloodlust of the Berserker, with unique individual titles for high-ranking priests. Specialty
  priests are called spiderswords. The clergy includes male (70%) and female (8%) dark elves as well
  as male (15%) and female (7%) aranea, and is drawn from specialty priests (35%), crusaders (25%),
  cleric/fighters (20%), fighters including nonpriest multiclassed fighters (15%), and clerics (5%).
  Little emphasis is placed on tactics or strategy, so few of the Selvetargtlin achieve high military
  rank despite their renowned skill in personal combat.

### Dogma

- War is the ultimate expression of individual power, and only through battle and death can one
  realize the respect of one's comrades.
- Hone fighting skills constantly, and teach those skills to those who will follow into the fray.
- Never give or receive quarter, and die amid the bloodlust of battle against overwhelming odds.
- Cultivate as many different weapon tricks and combat maneuvers as a spider has arms, and never fear
  that hidden venom -- like a secret vengeance waiting to strike -- will serve you ill.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Guard fortifications, hone fighting skills, participate in patrols, and guard slave caravans.
- Frequently get into fights over status and petty slights.
- Many spend much of their time training other warriors in the art of war.

| The Return of Zanassu | 6th of Kythorn (annual) | Observed instead by the aranea of the Spider Swamp, who venerate Selvetarm as Zanassu with fasting and ritual combat, commemorating the day he returned after a thousand-year exile. ### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal, recently formed) Selvetargtlin -- after Selvetarm's avatar rampaged through Eryndlyn during
  the Time of Troubles, a new military order of this name (also the clergy's collective name at large)
  arose among the city's drow, shifting the balance of power toward Lolth's clergy and driving the
  worshipers of Ghaunadaur and Vhaeraun into an uneasy alliance.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Spidersword
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; no missile weapons. The book gives no single favored weapon but
  favors two-weapon combinations (sword & dagger, sword & mace, sword & axe); later published sources
  give heavy mace (Venomace) as Selvetarm's favored weapon
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Any (Light, Medium, or Heavy all permitted); no shields except
  spiked bucklers
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Chaos, Charm, Combat, Healing, Sun (reversed only), War
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Elemental, Guardian, Protection, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics and warriors)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in Blind-fighting
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on survival (tracking)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
