# Tyr (the Maimed God, the Just God)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Tyr
- **Titles/Epithets:** the Maimed God, the Just God
- **Portfolio:** Justice
- **Symbol:** A balanced set of scales set upon the head of an upright war hammer

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters (all clergy became specialty priests after the Feast of the Moon in 1369
  DR, when Tyr granted every priest the powers formerly reserved for Holy Justices alone)
- **Organization:** A highly organized, formal priesthood maintaining internal rules and a network of
  fortified temples. Temples offer the faithful lodging, fresh mounts, healing, spell aid, weapons,
  gear, and holy advice, as well as confession and penance for lapses in service.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):

### Dogma

- Reveal the truth, punish the guilty, right the wrong, and be always true and just in your actions.
- Abide by the laws, and let no others break them; mete out punishment wherever lawbreaking occurs.
- Keep complete records of your own rulings, deeds, and decisions, so your errors can be corrected,
  your grasp of the law can grow, and lawbreakers can be identified by others.
- Let no known injustice done by a Tyrran priest go unbalanced.
- Remain ever vigilant, watching for forces and beings that intend or will cause future injustice, and
  act to prevent such challenges to law and order before they come to pass.
- Never enforce a law that contradicts other laws or can be shown to be unjust -- not merely unfair,
  but out of compliance with the principles of the wider body of law it belongs to.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve as judge, jury, and executioner in wilderness areas where there is no law but that of the
  sword, applying "an eye for an eye" tempered by "the mercy of ignorance" -- a truly ignorant
  first-time offender may be forgiven once, with a warning recorded in the priest's Book of Lawgiving
  so a second offense will not be excused.
- In civilized lands, serve as legal experts and lawyers, dispensing advice and speaking for accused
  persons at trial; the fees charged go to the Church of Tyr.
- Lecture others on their failures to follow laws, rules, and regulations, and fearlessly carry
  complaints about unjust formalities to the authorities responsible for them.
- Undertake formal missions of just vengeance on behalf of those who cannot exact it themselves,
  making open promises to the wronged and forcing open confrontation with the guilty rather than
  working through intrigue.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Seeing Justice | 1st day of each month | High ritual with chanted prayers and hymns; a gigantic war hammer, glowing blinding white, is conjured above the congregation. |
| The Maiming | 13th day of each month | High ritual; a gigantic right hand bursts into view above the congregation surrounded by a nimbus of burning blood, then tumbles away into darkness. |
| The Blinding | 22nd day of each month | High ritual; two eyes burst into fountains of flaming tears until spent. Celebrants are blindfolded early in the rite with diaphanous damask, recalling Tyr's own blindness. |
| The Awakening | Daily, at dawn | A gentle, uplifting renewal of faith. |
| The Hammer at Highsun | Daily, at noon | A stirring, exultant expression of the church's vigilance and martial might. |
| High Justice | Daily, at evenfeast (roughly six o'clock) | A stern, proud celebration of Tyr's commandments and the church's purpose. |
| Remembrance of the Just Fallen | Daily, in the evening (roughly nine o'clock) | A haunting, softly chanted reverence for those who have died for justice, inside and outside the faith. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Just Knights of the House of Tyr's Hand in Milvarune -- heavy cavalry famed for repeatedly
  breaking Thayan invasions.
- (Formal) Knights of Holy Judgment -- a church-sponsored paladin order emphasizing the "lawful" half
  of lawful good.
- (Formal) Knights of the Merciful Sword -- a church-sponsored paladin order emphasizing the "good"
  half of lawful good.
- (Formal) Hammers of Grimjaws -- an elite order drawn from paladins of 7th level or higher from
  either of the two orders above. Admission requires nomination and a second by a senior priest of
  Tyr, followed by a night-long vigil in a temple sanctuary; a vision of Tyr's war hammer confirms
  acceptance, a vision of his sword demands an atoning quest (which cannot be abandoned) before entry
  is granted.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Holy Justice / Holy Justices
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons, bastard sword; favored weapon longsword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Combat, Creation, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Law,
  Necromantic, Protection, Summoning, Sun, Wards, War
- **Minor Spheres:** Elemental
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in etiquette
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon), lore (local history), and lore (ancient history --
  of their native land); +1 bonus on sense motive checks
