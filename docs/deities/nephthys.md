# Nephthys (Guardian of Wealth and Commerce, Protector of the Dead, the Devoted Lady, the Lady of Sands, the Avenging Mother)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Nephthys
- **Titles/Epithets:** Guardian of Wealth and Commerce, Protector of the Dead, the Devoted Lady, the
  Lady of Sands, the Avenging Mother
- **Portfolio:** Wealth, commerce, guardian of the dead, protector of children
- **Symbol:** A golden offering bowl surmounted by an ankh (also used alone: a lunar disk behind an
  ankh)

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics and specialty priests of Nephthys receive religion (Mulhorandi), literacy
  (Mulhorandi), and linguistics (Common) as bonus skills. As Mulhorandi, they all also know Mulhorandi
  as their native tongue. All of Nephthys's clergy must be married or widowed female humans of
  Mulhorandi extraction, and must marry before or upon entering the clergy.
- **Organization:** Over 40% of the clergy are specialty priests (goodwives), who typically hold the
  faith's leadership positions; the remainder are clerics. All priestesses are addressed publicly as
  "Lady Priestess," or "Lady High Priestess" at 9th level or higher; within the clergy, titles such as
  Faithlady, Moneychanger, Avenging Sister, Deathmaiden, Eternal Guardian, and Golden Precept are used,
  varying by temple. Nephthys is popular with the wealthy, who pray to her to protect their fortunes,
  and is venerated by the nobility and middle class in most cities; most housewives keep a household
  shrine to her and store afterlife goods in a chest dedicated to her. Temples are opulent, heavily
  fortified stone edifices with a single guarded entrance, sitting atop trap-laden catacombs that store
  the church's wealth; they do not pay interest, but function as informal banks for the Mulhorandi elite
  for a modest fee, and offer money-changing services for 1% of the coinage's value.

### Dogma

- Be faithful to your husband, and encourage faithfulness in others.
- Protect Nephthys's worshipers, and diligently root out and destroy evil, especially the works of Set.
- Avenge the deaths of Mulhorandi killed by the Red Wizards of Thay.
- Accumulate great wealth, but never hold money above devotion to the goddess.
- Protect the tombs and remains of the dead, and smite those who rob or desecrate them.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Manage the economy of Mulhorand (and, increasingly, Unther), serving as tax collectors,
  money-changers, and investment advisers to the noble class, and administering the labor of slaves.
- Contend constantly with the clergies of Horus-Re, Anhur, and Isis over the financing of projects and
  the distribution of profits.
- Bless children brought to them (for a donation), placing each under the church's protection -- and
  vengeance -- until adulthood.
- Manage burial rites for every noble or middle-class death: safeguard grave goods, serve as executors
  of wills, perform mummification rituals (alongside the clergy of Osiris), and conduct the Passage
  Onward -- warding and sealing a crypt against tomb robbers with spells such as major curse, enduring
  ward, and sanctify crypt. Common graves receive a more modest blessing and at least a minor ward.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Feast of the Silver Coin | The Feast of the Moon | A somber, elaborate day-long rite of thanks to Nephthys in which the clergy tithe half their wealth to the church, then begin a fresh year of accumulation; attempting to shelter wealth from this tithe through clever accounting is unthinkable to the devout. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) The Order of the Golden Coin -- an elite sisterhood of priestesses serving as bodyguards,
  caravan escorts, and temple sentinels for steep fees; few adversaries manage a successful theft under
  their watch.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Goodwife
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapon, net, lasso; favored weapon staff
  (the Was-scepter) -- real-world Egyptian mythology consistently attributes the was-scepter, a
  ceremonial staff of dominion, to Nephthys, which also fits her own book-given weapon list (unlike
  "whip," the D&D 3.5e-sourced answer this supersedes, which matched neither her weapon list nor any
  real-world attribute found)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Combat, Creation, Guardian, Healing, Protection, Necromantic,
  Numbers, Sun, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in staff, 1 rank in flail, 1 rank in appraisal
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on craft (cooking); +1 bonus on gaming

All goodwives must be married or widowed female humans of Mulhorandi extraction, and must tithe 75% of
any nonmagical treasure they gain while adventuring to the church; in exchange, the church guarantees
their (and their husband's) proper burial and eternal care of their grave goods, and swiftly avenges the
death of a goodwife, her husband, her children, or her wards, or the disturbance of their tombs.
