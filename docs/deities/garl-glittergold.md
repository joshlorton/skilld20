# Garl Glittergold (The Joker, the Watchful Protector, the Priceless Gem, the Sparkling Wit)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Garl Glittergold
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Joker, the Watchful Protector, the Priceless Gem, the Sparkling Wit
- **Portfolio:** Protection, humor, trickery, gem cutting, finesmithing and lapidary, the gnome race
- **Symbol:** A gold nugget

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics, cleric/illusionists, and cleric/thieves) and
  specialty priests receive a bonus rank in religion (pantheon).
- **Organization:** Garl is the acknowledged leader of the gnome pantheon and is venerated by nearly
  all gnomes who embrace communal life, even those who also worship another deity. Temples are worked
  caves just below the surface at the heart of gnome communities: a circular, domed sanctuary split
  into four quadrants by soaring arches carved with gnomes of every walk of life, walls plated in
  gold leaf or studded with gold nuggets, and concentric stone pews facing a raised central dais.
  Temples double as venues for entertainers, joke-tellers, and illusionists, open to the whole
  community. High female priests are collectively called Star Rubies and high male priests Star
  Sapphires, with neither group senior to the other. Clergy is 73% rock gnome, 25% forest gnome, 2%
  deep gnome, and roughly evenly split by gender (48% female / 52% male). Composition is specialty
  priests (40%), cleric/illusionists (19%), cleric/thieves (17%), fighter/clerics (15%), and clerics
  (9%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. The Uncut (novice)
  2. Amethyst
  3. Topaz
  4. Opal
  5. Jacinth
  6. Diamond
  7. Emerald
  8. Ruby
  9. Sapphire (highest common rank; individual high priests hold unique titles -- collectively Star
     Rubies for women, Star Sapphires for men, neither senior to the other)

### Dogma

- While life may sometimes be hard, it is important to keep a sense of humor and always welcome
  opportunities for laughter and delight.
- Communities are forged through the cooperation and communal spirit of individuals who work and
  play together.
- A great prank can help to lighten hard times and make good ones shine.
- Those in authority should never take themselves too seriously, or they lose touch with those they
  direct and care for.
- Teach and preserve the tales and traditions of the Forgotten Folk, so they are never forgotten
  among their own kind.
- Do not fear change or the unorthodox, for therein lies the future.
- In all things, do what works.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve communities as craftsmen, educators, entertainers, mediators, and protectors -- even
  wandering adventurers serve this function, as their exploits enter the oral tradition.
- Combine earthy practicality with humor when teaching the young, to heighten interest and learning.
- Work as smiths (particularly goldsmiths), miners, and gem cutters.
- Maintain careful vigilance against hostile races, especially kobolds, and watch over the welfare of
  the Forgotten Folk.
- Maintain a good archive of jokes, jests, and tales, and be capable, enthralling raconteurs.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Communion of Laughter | 13th day of every month | A day-long observance combining prayer and quiet contemplation, dancing atop the central dais, communal meals, storytelling accompanied by magical visual displays, and joke-telling contests that run late into the night; worshipers offer gold (or another precious metal) to the god. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Companions of Arumdina -- a military order of fighters, clerics, fighter/clerics, and
  specialty priests with strong ties to the church of Gaerdal Ironhand, unusually stern by the
  standards of Garl's faith. Members (called Justifiers) are skilled in defensive tactics in hills
  and forests, often double as marshals, and specialize in combating ankhegs, bulettes, and other
  burrowing monsters.
- *(Informal)* Glittering Jesters -- a loosely affiliated band who relate or imitate Garl's most
  outrageous exploits, traveling from community to community as performers and pranksters; an elite
  few become adventurers, hoping their deeds will one day be attributed to Garl himself in gnome
  oral tradition.
