# Fenmarel Mestarine (the Lone Wolf)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Fenmarel Mestarine
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Lone Wolf
- **Portfolio:** Feral elves, outcasts, scapegoats, isolation and isolationists
- **Symbol:** A pair of elven eyes in the darkness

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All specialty priests of Fenmarel receive religion (elf) and literacy (Espruar) as
  bonus proficiencies. Fenmarel's clergy is composed entirely (100%) of specialty priests -- he has no
  clerics, druids, or other priest classes among his faithful.
- **Organization:** Regarded with suspicion and hostility in most elven societies; his followers build
  no temples. Two loose camps make up his clergy: elven outcasts, who are loners by nature and rarely
  even seek out fellow worshipers, and isolated, primitive elven tribes who venerate him collectively.
  Both types construct secret personal shrines, typically hidden hollows containing bones, teeth, or
  claws (representing the world's savagery) and sticks and leaves (representing the environment that
  offers both protection and sustenance); exiles often add a personal token symbolizing their
  separation from elven society.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. The Lost (novice)
  2. The Unbowed (full priest)

  Beyond full priest, Fenmarel's priests invent their own individual titles or forgo titles entirely;
  there is no further fixed ladder. Specialty priests are called lone wolves.

### Dogma

- The world is a harsh and unforgiving place, with uncompromising demands on those who would forge
  their own path.
- Rely not on others for protection, for betrayal comes easily -- rely on your own skills and those
  taught by the Lone Wolf: camouflage, deception, and secrecy.
- Follow the way of the Lone Wolf, for his is the path of self-sufficiency.
- Fear not hard work, for the fruits of your labor prove your worth to yourself.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Instruct fellow feral elves in spying, solitary survival, guerrilla tactics, and the use of poison
  to take down enemies with subtlety.
- Otherwise have few formal responsibilities beyond ensuring their own personal survival.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Personal observance | Individual, varies | The church of Fenmarel celebrates no widely recognized holy days. Each individual or band venerates the Lone Wolf in worship of their own devising -- many outcasts mark the day of their personal banishment with private contemplation, while feral tribes mark anniversaries of events in their oral history, often tied to astronomical events. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No formal knightly orders exist. Among the Or-Tel'Quessir (the feral elven tribe of the Misty Vale),
  every able-bodied adult fights for the Lone Wolf, so in a sense the entire tribe serves as the
  faith's militant arm.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Lone Wolf
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Blowgun, club, dagger, dart, hand axe, knife, quarterstaff, short bow,
  sling, spear, staff-sling; favored weapon dagger
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (hide, leather), shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Chaos, Combat, Creation, Healing, Plant, Protection, Summoning,
  Travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Elemental, Sun, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in herbalism, 1 rank in survival (hunting)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on lore (nature); +1 bonus on survival
  (pick one type)
