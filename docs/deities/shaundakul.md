# Shaundakul (the Rider of the Winds, the Helping Hand)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Shaundakul
- **Titles/Epithets:** the Rider of the Winds, the Helping Hand
- **Portfolio:** Travel, exploration, long-range traders, miners, caravans, windghosts
- **Symbol:** An upright silver left hand, palm out, fingers together, its wrist trailing away into
  rippling winds, on a black or deep purple background of circling winds

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **Organization:** Shaundakul has few temples, as his clergy are generally struck with wanderlust and
  rarely stay in one place; instead they maintain numerous ancient shrines throughout the Moonsea
  North, typically a stone dais atop a high place crowned with a stone seat, accompanied by wind-pierced
  stone pillars. Roughly 10% of clergy are crusaders (windfists), 20% are rangers (zephyrs/mistrals),
  and the rest are specialty priests (windwalkers). There is no clear formal hierarchy, though priests
  who served before the Godswar hold great respect.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Seeker of the Wind
  2. Scout
  3. Trailblazer
  4. Explorer
  5. Guide of the Hidden Ways
  6. Rider of the West Wind
  7. Rider of the South Wind
  8. Rider of the East Wind
  9. Rider of the North Wind
  10. Lord High Windhand

### Dogma

- Ride the wind and let it take you wherever it blows.
- Aid those in need and trust in the Helping Hand.
- Seek out the riches of the earth and the sea and journey to distant horizons.
- Be the first to see the rising sun, the mountain peaks, the lush valleys; let your footsteps fall
  where none have tread.
- Actively work to reestablish the god's sphere of influence among traders, particularly trailblazers
  who explore new lands and open new trade routes.
- Act as scouts, guards, and leaders for parties of explorers, caravans, and mining expeditions.
- Unearth ancient shrines of Shaundakul and resanctify them.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Provide for themselves by living off the land, hiring out as scouts and caravan guards to trading
  costers, or running caravans through the Moonsea North and beyond.
- Serve as guides for adventuring companies or as explorers; a very few are Harpers.
- Visit the scattered shrines of Shaundakul as often as possible and construct new ones when resources
  allow.
- Utter a simple prayer whenever the wind changes significantly.
- Mark any newly discovered territory (an undiscovered valley, lake, or island) with a small throne of
  rocks bearing Shaundakul's symbol.
- If of sufficient level, raise a shrine to Shaundakul using stone shape magic.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Windride | 15th of Tarsakh (annual) | Shaundakul's only annual holy day. At dawn, priests seek out a strong breeze and cast wraithform or wind walk (priests without access to either are granted wraithform for this one day regardless of level, and duration is extended from dawn to dusk); they then spend the day soaring wherever chance takes them, always landing safely somewhere new. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Fellowship of the Next Mountain -- an order of rangers and windwalkers who typically work
  alone, blazing trails through the uncharted wilderness of the Sword Coast North and Moonsea North.
- (Formal) Knights of the Shadow Sword -- an elite order of crusaders, windwalkers, and rangers
  founded by the half-elf Jax Nightsong, based in Shaundakul's Throne, dedicated to cleansing Myth
  Drannor of the evil that haunts its streets and ruins.
- (Formal) Riders of the West Wind -- an order of windwalkers and a few rangers who hire out as a
  mercenary company guarding caravans through uncharted wilderness to distant lands.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Windwalker
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All; favored weapon two-handed sword/greatsword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light & medium armor, light & medium shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Combat, Elemental Air, Healing, Protection, Travelers, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Creation, Divination, Elemental Earth, Elemental Water, Guardian,
  Necromantic, Plant, Sun
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in herbalism or survival (pick one), 1 rank in two-handed
  sword/greatsword -- tracking is a survival skill action
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on survival & stealth (pick one terrain), endurance, and religion
  (pantheon) checks -- direction sense and navigation are both survival skill actions
