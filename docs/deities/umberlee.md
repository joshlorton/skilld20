# Umberlee (The Bitch Queen, Sea Queen, Queen of the Deeps)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Umberlee
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Bitch Queen, Sea Queen, Queen of the Deeps
- **Portfolio:** Oceans, currents, waves, sea winds
- **Symbol:** A forked blue-green wave, curling in breakers to both left and right, on a background of
  black streaked with white


## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** Loosely and informally organized, much given to internal dueling with hooked
  sicklelike knives to settle rank and primacy. Priests roam coastal cities living off the offerings
  of fearful sailors. Novices are called the Untaken; once confirmed, a priest may take offerings,
  lead prayers, and bestow blessings in Umberlee's name. Specialty priests (Waveservants) make up the
  bulk of the clergy.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** No strict ascending ladder. Novices are the Untaken. Once
  confirmed, full priests may adopt any of the following titles regardless of true rank or power:
  Flood Tide, Dark Breaker, Puissant Undertow, Wave of Fury, Savage Seawind, and Wavemistress/Wavelord.
  Specialty priests (Waveservants) use the same titles with "Dread" prefixed.

### Dogma

- All should know Umberlee and fear her, for the wind and the wave can reach everywhere.
- Fair offerings bring fair winds to travelers over the waters; the sea is as cold as Umberlee's heart
  to those who do not pay their respects.
- The dead serve Umberlee most faithfully.
- Spread word of the might of holy Umberlee, and let no service be performed in her name without
  price.
- Make folk fear the wind and the waves, unless a priest of Umberlee is present to protect them.
- Slay any who ascribe sea and shore storms to Talos.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Roam coastal cities and shrines, preaching of the doom Umberlee has wrought in the past and the
  storms yet to come, building enough favor to be teleported to safety by the goddess if ever
  endangered at sea.
- Sell the safety of their own presence aboard ships (an Umberlant priest may charge as much as 500 gp
  for a voyage down the Sword Coast, or triple that for a large, well-armed cargo vessel) and charge
  extra, by the spell, for magic used to defend or protect a ship.
- Cast stormcloak and speak with the drowned dead for pay -- the two most popular services beyond
  common healing magic, the latter especially prized by pirates seeking sunken treasure.
- When no worshipers are present, remove the offerings left on shrine altars and sluice the stone
  blocks with buckets of seawater and seaweed, signifying that the Sea Queen has come to claim what is
  rightfully hers.
- Tithe/sacrifice: sailors pipe valuable cargo overboard (ideally something still alive, if peril is
  great) while playing tunes dedicated to Umberlee, to secure fair winds or survive a storm.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Daily worship | Daily | Offerings, prayer, and self-anointing of the brow, hands, and feet with sea water. |
| The Drowning | Whenever an Untaken is elevated to full priesthood (not calendar-fixed) | Private, clergy-only rite. The supplicant lies before an altar ringed with candles as a senior priest casts a spell that floods the room with a huge wave and then drains it away. Survivors are confirmed as priests and warned that drowning awaits any who betray the Sea Queen. |
| First Tide | When the ice breaks up in a harbor (seasonal) | Public flute-and-drum parade; clergy bind a live animal to a rock and hurl it into the sea. If it survives and reaches shore, it is healed and becomes a sacred animal holding the rank of an Umberlant. |
| Stormcall | As needed, not calendar-fixed | Public mass prayer calling a storm down on a target harbor or ship, or turning one away. Worshipers kneel around pools of floating lit candles on driftwood, throwing in sacrifices while priests levitate the candles to keep them lit -- a doused candle signals Umberlee's anger. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Informal) Sea Queen's Breakers -- the church's adventuring order, pursuing treasure-hunting and
  recovery operations beneath the waves, or on land when the promise of gold outweighs the danger of
  working far from water.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Waveservant / Waveservants, "true servants of the wave"
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons, harpoon, Umberlant dueling knife; favored weapon
  trident
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light armor; shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Chaos, Combat, Creation, Elemental Water, Healing, Necromantic, Summoning,
  Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Charm, Elemental (all aspects other than water), Guardian, Protection,
  Sun
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** None
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon) and swim checks; pick two aquatic languages
  from Dragon Turtle, Sea Elvish, Koalinth, Kuo-toan, Locathah, Nereid, Merman, Morkoth, Sahuagin, or
  Triton; +1 bonus on survival checks while at sea

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cannot turn or command normal undead; may command undead that were killed at sea or are aquatic by nature |
| 1 | Breathes water automatically; +1 bonus on swim checks |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Watery Double (30 mana) |
| 5 | Water Walk at will (no mana cost) -- cannot be conveyed to others except by casting an actual water walk spell |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Summon Striking Wave (40 mana) |
| 10 | Moves through water as if under the effect of a ring of free action (no additional benefit if the waveservant later acquires one) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Control Weather (70 mana) -- usable only at sea or the shore of an ocean/large lake; can only worsen conditions, never improve them |
| 12 | Foundation bonus: Call and Control Sharks, 1d4 sharks arriving in 1d6 rounds (60 mana) |
