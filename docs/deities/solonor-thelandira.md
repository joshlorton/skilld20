# Solonor Thelandira (Keen-Eye, the Great Archer, the Forest Hunter)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Solonor Thelandira
- **Titles/Epithets:** Keen-Eye, the Great Archer, the Forest Hunter
- **Portfolio:** Archery, hunting, wilderness survival
- **Symbol:** A silver arrow with green fletching; the priesthood's physical holy symbol takes one of three forms -- an oversized arrowhead (at least three inches long) embossed on both faces with Solonor's symbol, a silver medallion embossed with a stag's head, or three feathers bound to a leather disk on a cord

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including multiclassed half-elven clerics and elven cleric/rangers) and rangers receive religion (pantheon) and literacy (Espruar) as bonus skills. Single-classed clerics of Solonor must additionally select proficiency in either the long bow or short bow, at double the normal cost.
- **Organization:** Well regarded throughout elven society, though most worshipers are drawn from Fair Folk living outside the great cities in small forest communities -- especially green elves and moon elven commoners engaged in the daily work of living in harmony with nature. Gold elves and even haughty Teu-Tel'Quessir nobility romanticize the Great Archer's teachings while looking down on the base commoners who actually make up his clergy. Temples sit hidden at the heart of deep forests, reached only via carefully guarded woodland paths, cultivated from concentric rings of forest giants grown to form natural hollows linked by vine-rope bridges, ringed by near-impregnable earthen and root-woven defenses, and surrounded by paths deliberately overgrown to slow invaders while allowing archers clear shooting lanes. The clergy is moon elves (33%), green elves (28%), gold elves (22%), half-moon elves (8%), half-green elves (3%), half-gold elves (2%), lythari (3%), and other elves/half-elves (1%); made up of rangers (36%), cleric/rangers (33%), and clerics (31%); almost evenly split between male (52%) and female (48%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Fledgling (novice)
  2. Hawkeye (full priest)
  3. Fletcher
  4. Bowyer
  5. Archer
  6. Gray Wolf
  7. Snow Tiger
  8. Grizzly Bear
  9. Blood Hawk
  10. Fire Falcon
  11. Gold Eagle

  High-ranking priests hold unique individual titles. Specialty priests are known as rangers.

### Dogma

- Walk in harmony with nature, and oppose those who would disturb her delicate balance.
- Preserve the wild places from excessive encroachment, while working with settlers to preserve the beauty that first drew them to the land.
- Hunt only for sustenance, culling the old and the weak so that all species may prosper.
- Like an arrow in flight, it is difficult to arrest the consequences of an action -- choose your targets carefully, for an ill-considered action can have a long-reaching impact.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve as scouts and archers in elven armies, and as bowyers, fletchers, and archery instructors in elven settlements.
- Act as hunters and providers for far-flung rural communities, and preside over adulthood initiation ceremonies among Fair Folk who largely eschew civilization.
- Work to maintain the balance of nature.
- Act as deadly enemies of those who worship Malar, Talos, or Moander, often joining forces with Rillifane's faithful to exterminate their followers.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Full Moon Gathering | Monthly, under the full moon | The faithful give thanks for the skills Solonor has taught and the bounty provided; hunters sacrifice trophies that cannot otherwise be used, and unbroken arrows engraved with Solonor's symbol are fired skyward (never toward anywhere they could hurt someone) to let the light of his teachings shine through. |
| Shieldmeet (Cinnaelos'Cor, the Day of Corellon's Peace) | Shieldmeet (quadrennial) | The faithful gather for great archery contests; winners receive the Keen-Eye mark, a blessing granting a +1 attack bonus with bows until the next Shieldmeet. |

### Affiliated Orders

- Solonor's church is affiliated with numerous small military orders, few numbering more than several dozen warriors -- among them the Stag Hunters, the Fellowship of the Fleeting Hart, the Wolves of Dawn, the Shadowsheafs, the Knights of the Green Bow, the Keen-eyed Hunters, and the Archer Knights -- bands of elven rangers, fighters, and priests dedicated to guarding the woodlands, the Fair Folk, and their allies.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Ranger
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapon longbow
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor), though ranger special abilities suffer penalties if wearing armor heavier than studded leather (i.e. above Light)
- **Major Spheres:** Not applicable -- Solonor's ranger specialty priests follow the standard Ranger class from the Player's Handbook rather than a priest sphere list
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Combat, Plant, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (nature); 1 rank in survival (trapping/set snares); 1 rank in survival (woodland)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on craft (bowyer/fletcher); +1 bonus on survival (hunting); +1 bonus on survival (tracking)
