# Erevan Illesere (the Trickster, the Chameleon, the Green Changeling, the Evershifting Shapechanger, the Fey Jester, the Jack of the Seelie Court)

*Note: the source book's own section header spells this deity's name "Erevan Illesere" (double L), matching
the table of contents; nearly every other cross-reference to him elsewhere in the same book instead spells
it "Erevan Ilesere" (single L). This file follows the section header spelling per project convention.*

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Erevan Illesere
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Trickster, the Chameleon, the Green Changeling, the Evershifting
  Shapechanger, the Fey Jester, the Jack of the Seelie Court
- **Portfolio:** Mischief, change, rogues
- **Symbol:** A nova star with asymmetrical rays

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Rogues
- **All Clergy:** All clerics and specialty priests of Erevan receive religion (elf) and literacy
  (Espruar) as bonus proficiencies. Priests of Erevan include multiclassed half-elven clerics, elven
  cleric/thieves, elven fighter/cleric/thieves, and elven specialty priest/thieves.
- **Organization:** Regarded by most elves as little more than a loose fellowship of adventuresome
  rogues and pranksters -- too unpredictable to be widely venerated. Erevan may never be worshiped in
  the same location twice, so few temples of any note exist; his houses of worship are little more
  than permanent shrines used for meetings, and priests must go elsewhere to actually pray.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. The Gullible (novice)
  2. Quicksilver (full priest)

  Beyond full priest, Erevan's priests invent their own individual titles and frequently change them;
  there is no further fixed ladder.

### Dogma

- Change and excitement are the spice of life.
- Live on the edge, unbound by the conventions of society, in a spirit of constant self-reinvention.
- Puncture the self-righteousness, sanctimony, and pretension of orderly society with mischievous
  pranks that both amuse and enlighten.
- Inspire laughter, happiness, and giddy silliness so the routine of day-to-day existence never grinds
  all the joy from life.
- Celebrate the spontaneous, and practice random acts of helpfulness.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Play tricks on others for the sheer joy of it; oppose settled interests of all sorts and delight in
  upsetting both the rule of law and the powerful.
- Minister to the faithful primarily through example and by instructing them in the skills of
  mischievous rogues, rather than through formal duties.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight Gambol | Monthly, under the full moon | Held in a secret sylvan glade whose location is passed by word of mouth; anyone who discovers it through their own ingenuity is welcome. Includes the sacrifice of beautiful (often borrowed) objects, dancing, wine-drinking, tale-telling, and endless prank-playing, often joined by mischief-loving Seelie Court subjects such as sprites and pixies. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No formal knightly orders.
- *(Informal)* Knaves of the Missing Page -- a fellowship of elven spellfilchers (mage/thieves) based
  in the Vale of Evereska but active throughout Faerûn, specializing in recovering elven magical
  artifacts, spell scrolls, and spell tomes taken by other races.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Mischiefmaker
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapon short sword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (leather, padded, studded leather) or Medium (silenced elven
  chain mail)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Chaos, Charm, Creation, Guardian, Healing, Protection, Travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Plant, Summoning, Thought
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** None beyond baseline
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on disguise

equivalent first.
