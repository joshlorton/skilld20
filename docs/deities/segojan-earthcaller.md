# Segojan Earthcaller (Earthfriend, the Rock Gnome, Lord of the Burrow, Digger of Dens, the Badger, the Wolverine)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Segojan Earthcaller
- **Titles/Epithets:** Earthfriend, the Rock Gnome, Lord of the Burrow, Digger of Dens, the Badger,
  the Wolverine
- **Portfolio:** Earth, nature, the dead, rock gnomes
- **Symbol:** A glowing gemstone

### Symbol -- Midjourney prompt


## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics, cleric/illusionists, and cleric/thieves) and
  specialty priests receive a bonus rank in religion (pantheon).
- **Organization:** Temples are underground complexes of linked caverns that resemble both a museum
  and a zoo -- every surface adorned with generations of gnome artisan work (metal sculptures,
  jewels, elaborate illusions) while hundreds of small animals, from shrews to giant badgers, run
  loose among the displays. Segojan's priests preside over most gnome funerary rites. Clergy is 80%
  rock gnome, 18% deep gnome, 2% forest gnome, and majority female (70%). Composition is specialty
  priests (50%), clerics (30%), fighter/clerics (10%), cleric/illusionists (9%), and cleric/thieves
  (1%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. The Unearthed (novice)
  2. Shrew
  3. Mole
  4. Vole
  5. Ermine
  6. Groundhog
  7. Wolverine
  8. Badger
  9. Aurumvorax (highest common rank; individual high priests hold unique titles)

### Dogma

- The earth is the heart and soul of the Forgotten Folk; on its surface and amidst its tunnels and
  caves they dwell in life, and beneath its silent shroud they rest in death.
- Preserve and protect the natural world that lies beneath the roots of those who dwell on the
  surface.
- Dig burrows, tunnel, and explore, for Segojan welcomes all gnomes into his domain.
- Beware the evil that ensnares those blinded by avarice and the destructive impulses of the Crawler
  Below.
- The Lord of Burrows shall protect those who dwell in his demesne and live in harmony with his
  teachings.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Work with the clergy of Baervan Wildwanderer to preserve and protect the underground ecology.
- Work with the clergy of Flandal Steelskin to supervise mining operations and protect gnome miners.
- Work with the clergy of Callarduran Smoothhands to forge ties between surface and deep gnomes.
- Serve as emissaries between rock gnomes and their deep-gnome kin, seeking to establish and maintain
  trade between the subraces.
- Preside over most gnome funerary rituals, interring the dead within Segojan's domain.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Quarterly Rite of the Buried Treasure (unnamed in the source) | The first day of each season | The faithful gather in plainly adorned earthen dens and offer gemstones, worked and unworked, which are then buried by badgers; Segojan is said to command burrowing animals to relocate the jewels afterward for gnomes to discover anew. |
| The Earthen Embrace | Performed at death, whenever a gnome (particularly a rock gnome) dies | The dominant gnome funerary rite: the body is coated in specially prepared mud that hardens over three days into a stone-hard "earthen mummy," which illusionists work into the deceased's likeness; the body is then blessed by the assembled clergy and borne to its final resting place. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Watchers Below -- a loosely structured order of gnome fighters, fighter/clerics,
  clerics, and specialty priests found in most rock gnome communities and among the svirfneblin,
  organized into semiautonomous, flat-hierarchy chapters; guards gnome homelands against Urdlen's
  cult and against Underdark races raiding gnome-held territory.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Earthfriend
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Any (Heavy)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Creation, Elemental (Earth), Guardian, Healing, Plant, Protection,
  Summoning
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Elemental (Air, Water), Sun, Travelers, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in blind-fighting
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on lore (nature); +1 bonus on craft
  (mining); +1 bonus on craft (stonemasonry)
