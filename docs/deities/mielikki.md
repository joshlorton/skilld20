# Mielikki (Our Lady of the Forest, the Supreme Ranger, Daughter to Silvanus)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Mielikki
- **Titles/Epithets:** Our Lady of the Forest, the Supreme Ranger, Daughter to Silvanus
- **Portfolio:** Forests, forest creatures, rangers, dryads, autumn
- **Symbol:** A white unicorn on a green field, or a white unicorn's head facing sinister; an older
  form of the symbol shows a tiny star of dazzling white balanced on an open, lush green oak or ash
  leaf

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **Organization:** Loosely organized -- worship happens in peaceful forest glades more often than
  formal temples, and shrines are far more common than temples, concentrated in the North, the Western
  Heartlands, and parts of the Dalelands. In 1369 DR the church was reorganized into three branches:
  the Heartwoods (the spirits of the trees themselves -- dryads, hamadryads, and treants), the
  Forestarms (clerics and druids, the protectors of the forest), and the Needles (rangers, the
  defensive/warrior arm, which includes the druid/ranger order the Shadoweirs).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order, Forestarms track):
  1. Quester (novice)
  2. Spring Stag (clergy of less than two winters' service)
  3. Stalker in the Green (experienced clergy without major achievements or senior appointments yet)
  4. Forest Flame (senior temple staff, envoys, recognized tutors)
  5. High Ranger (temple leaders and champions of the faith)
  6. Hawk of the Lady (a personal title bestowed by Mielikki herself on her most cherished followers)

  Temple staff also use simple functional titles: Cook, Master of Novices, Doorwarden, Housemaster,
  Prior, Abbot, and Worship Master.

### Dogma

- Intelligent beings can live in harmony with the wild without requiring the destruction of one in the
  name of the other.
- Embrace the wild, and do not fear it -- the wild ways are the good ways.
- Keep the balance, and learn the hidden ways of all life.
- Do not allow trees to be needlessly felled or the forest to be burned.
- Live in the forest and be a part of it, not in endless battle against it.
- Protect forest life; defend every tree; plant anew where death fells a tree.
- Strive to keep the balance that indiscriminate fire-users and woodcutters break.
- Live in harmony with the woods, and teach others to do so.
- Punish and frustrate those who hunt for sport (not food) and who practice cruelty on wild creatures.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- The Forestarms wander among small communities at forest edges, teaching care and respect for trees
  and the life beneath their boughs, and teaching careful forest husbandry to slow civilization's
  encroachment.
- The Needles support the Forestarms and the clergies of Eldath and Silvanus in defending, renewing,
  and extending forests; they work against fire-magic users (the Red Wizards, followers of Kossuth)
  and encourage city- and farm-dwelling folk to revere natural life.
- Both branches support the Harpers when doing so does not conflict with their primary duties.
- Rangers of all faiths are assisted whenever possible; seeds of trees and woodland plants are
  gathered, nurtured, and planted so that 40 new trees rise for every one taken by flame or axe.
- Forestarms and Needles regularly visit and heal foresters, guiding them to take as few trees as
  possible and to cull weak or unnatural predators, not creatures in the prime of health.
- In recent years, deer have been bred in large herds for food and pelts north of Melvaunt, in eastern
  Amn, and in the lands of the Dessarin, letting wild deer populations recover.
- Tithe/offering: no coin tithe is described. Every clergy member must perform the Song of the Trees at
  least once a month, serving any dryads, hamadryads, or treants it calls forth. Every fire a member of
  the clergy lights must have the Dread Prayer whispered over it; in return the fire burns hot in a
  chosen direction with almost no smoke or spread risk.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Four Feasts | Solstice and equinox nights (First Feast, Second Feast, and so on) | Holy rituals and revels celebrating the sensual side of existence; the faithful sing the Lady's praises in forest depths wherever possible. |
| Greengrass | Spring | A greater ritual combining Four-Feasts-style revelry with planting rites and the Wild Ride. |
| Midsummer Night | Summer | As Greengrass -- revelry, planting rites, and the Wild Ride; if Shieldmeet follows Midsummer, the Ride continues an extra day and night. |
| The Wild Ride | During Greengrass and Midsummer Night | Mielikki gathers unicorns into herds to gallop through the woods; the faithful may ride them bareback through the night, and the unicorns' teleport ability is empowered to triple range for the duration. |
| Song of the Trees | Monthly | Every clergy member must perform this song and serve any dryads, hamadryads, or treants it calls forth. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* The Shadoweirs -- a secretive religious knighthood of half-elf druid/ranger woodland
  knights originating in the northern High Forest. Unlike the Forestarms or Needles, they are activist
  and proselytizing, advancing the regrowth of ancient forests and often adventuring with missionary
  zeal against civilization's encroachment.
- *(Informal)* Order of the Unicorn's Horn -- a small society of itinerant healers who bring solace to
  injured people, animals, and plants.
- *(Informal)* Close ties to Those Who Harp (the Harpers), an organization working against the rise of
  great powers that endanger natural life.

## Specialty Priests

Mielikki's specialty priests fall into three types -- Rangers (Needles), Druids (part of the
Forestarms), and Druid/Rangers (the Shadoweirs). Beyond the analyze balance ability granted at rank 1
(see each track's Rank Progression below), none has a bulleted list of unique deity-specific granted
powers; their class abilities otherwise follow the standard Ranger and Druid rules in the Player's
Handbook.

### Rangers (Needles)

- **Requirements:** Strength 13, Dexterity 13, Constitution 14, Wisdom 14
- **Prime Req:** Strength, Dexterity, Wisdom
- **Alignment:** LG, NG, CG
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** all; favored weapon scimitar
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Any (Light, Medium, or Heavy all permitted), though ranger special
  abilities suffer penalties if the armor worn is heavier than studded leather (i.e. above Light)
- **Major Spheres:** Not applicable -- Needles follow the standard Ranger class from the Player's
  Handbook rather than a priest sphere list
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Plant, Time, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in survival (woodland)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on stealth (woodland); pick three: survival
  (hunting), survival (trapping/set snares), lore (nature), linguistics (elvish)

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | When in a forested area, may cast a variant of the 1st-level priest spell analyze balance at will (used on a person, creature, or object it functions as the spell; used on the forest itself, it lets the priest detect whether the local ecology is seriously disrupted) |

No unique granted-power list beyond this; standard Player's Handbook Ranger class abilities otherwise apply.

### Druids (Forestarms)

- **Requirements:** Wisdom 12, Charisma 15
- **Prime Req:** Wisdom, Charisma
- **Alignment:** N
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, sickle, dart, spear, dagger, knife, scimitar, sling, staff; favored
  weapon scimitar
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (padded, leather, or hide), plus a nonmetallic (wooden, bone,
  or shell) shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Combat, Elemental, Healing, Plant, Time, Wards, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (nature), 1 rank in herbalism
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on diplomacy (fey & forest creatures);
  +1 bonus on survival (woodland) -- tracking is a survival skill action; +1 bonus on linguistics (pick
  two from: brownie, dryad, elvish, korred, pegasus, pixie, satyr, sprite, sylph, treant, unicorn)

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

No unique granted-power list beyond this; standard Player's Handbook Druid class abilities otherwise apply.

### Druid/Rangers (Shadoweirs)

- **Requirements:** Strength 13, Dexterity 13, Constitution 14, Wisdom 14, Charisma 15
- **Prime Req:** Wisdom, Charisma / Strength, Dexterity, Wisdom
- **Alignment:** NG
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, sickle, dart, spear, dagger, scimitar, sling, staff, long sword, long

- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Any (Light, Medium, or Heavy all permitted); ranger-ability
  penalties apply above Light armor, except elven chain mail (Medium), which Mielikki's grace exempts

- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Combat, Elemental, Healing, Plant, Time, Wards, Weather
