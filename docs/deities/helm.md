# Helm (He of the Unsleeping Eyes, the Watcher, the Vigilant, the Vigilant One, the Great Guard, the God of Guardians)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Helm
- **Titles/Epithets:** He of the Unsleeping Eyes, the Watcher, the Vigilant, the Vigilant One, the Great Guard, the God of Guardians
- **Portfolio:** Guardians, protectors, protection
- **Symbol:** An open, staring eye with a blue pupil and outline, painted on the back of a right-hand war gauntlet or the palm of a left-hand war gauntlet

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms, monastic orders
- **Organization:** Strongly hierarchical and near-military in structure. Since the Time of
  Troubles the church has been rebuilt under the leadership of veteran pre-Troubles priests known
  as the Tested and True, people of inflexible belief and loyalty. Specialty priests (Watchers) are
  derisively nicknamed "Godseyes." All clergy know their place by rank; Helm himself sets goals for
  his high priests and determines which temples cooperate on which efforts.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Novice
  2. Adept
  3. Trusty
  4. Alert
  5. Watchknight
  6. Guardian
  7. Overblade
  8. High Watcher
  9. Senior Steeleye (title applied to all senior clergy)

### Dogma

- "He also serves who stands and waits and watches carefully."
- "Careful planning always defeats rushed actions in the end."
- Be vigilant, fair, and diligent in the conduct of one's orders.
- Protect the weak, the unpopular, the injured, and the young -- never sacrifice them for others.
- Anticipate attacks and be ready; know your foes; care carefully for your weapons.
- "Never betray your trust."
- Always obey orders, provided those orders follow the dictates of Helm.
- Commanding undead, rather than turning or destroying them, is abhorrent and forbidden to the clergy.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Work to win back Helm's standing through demonstrated excellence of vigilance and purity of loyalty as guardians and protectors.
- Train bodyguards, and spread the word that only Helm-tested worshipers of the God of Guardians are truly worthy and reliable to hire.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ceremony of Honor to Helm | Every Shieldmeet | The faith's only calendar-fixed ritual. |
| Consecration of a Postulant | As needed | Dedication of a being seeking confirmation as clergy of Helm. |
| Consecration of a Glymtul | As needed | Dedication of a special item ("glymtul," an ancient word for "favored thing") to Helm's service. |
| The Purification | As needed | A renewal of faith undergone by those returning to the faith or atoning for a lapse in vigilance, loyalty, or worship. |
| The Holy Vigil | Upon a priest's ascension to higher rank | A night-long ceremony in which the priest keeps a levitated, ritually enspelled weapon hovering by will and vigilance alone. |
| Daily devotion | Upon waking and before sleep | Standard daily prayer to the Vigilant One. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Companions of the One True Vision -- clerics, fighters, and crusaders; unswervingly
  loyal shock troops, though their reputation has suffered since their role in the Maztica invasion.
- (Informal) Watchers Over the Fallen -- a small fellowship of battlefield healers.
- (Formal) Everwatch Knights -- dedicated bodyguards Helmite temples hire out to others to generate revenue.
- (Formal) Vigilant Eyes of the God -- an order of paladins.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Watcher (informally "Godseyes")
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon bastard sword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Heavy (up to and including full plate); shield allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Combat, Divination, Guardian, Protection, Sun, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Elemental, Healing, War
- **Magical Items:** No limitations; additionally may use crystal balls as wizards do
- **Skills Required:** None
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on blind-fighting; +1
  bonus on etiquette; +1 bonus on perception (vigilance)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Receives a full plate armor suit, open-faced great helm, and medium shield free; must wear this armor (or none) except in sanctioned emergencies -- repeated violations draw a -50% XP penalty and a freeze on new spells from Helm until the watcher atones |
| 1 | +2 bonus to surprise rolls (reduced to +1 if accompanied by non-watchers); not cumulative with other surprise bonuses, but does stack with penalties |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: short-term Glyph of Warding, lasting 1 day/level (30 mana); may choose any effect from spells the watcher could currently cast, regardless of sphere |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Sentry of Helm (10 mana) *or* Wyvern Watch (20 mana), choose one per casting |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Mace of Odo *or* Exaltation, choose one per casting (30 mana) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Seeking Sword (40 mana) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Summon Spectator (5 mana -- 5th-level spell, 1/tenday = level × 0.1) |
