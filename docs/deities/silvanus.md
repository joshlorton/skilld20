# Silvanus (Oak Father, the Forest Father, the Old Oak, Treefather, Old Father Tree)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Silvanus
- **Titles/Epithets:** Oak Father, the Forest Father, the Old Oak, Treefather, Old Father Tree
- **Portfolio:** Wild nature, druids
- **Symbol:** A green, living oak leaf, an oak tree in summer, or a wooden staff sprouting tiny green
  leaflets and buds down its length

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** Known as the "greenleaf priesthood." Has a strong base among clerics in urban
  areas and druids in the wilder territories, with druids favored as the leaders and backbone of the
  faith, especially those who dwell in and steward the forest. A few shamans serve among Faerûn's
  nomadic and barbarian societies. Clergy are spread throughout Faerûn, favoring small communities over
  large cities, though sizable communities of Silvanites exist in cities such as Waterdeep. Urban
  clergy often become gardeners, preserving or creating walled pockets of wild forest within cities.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** No ascending rank/title list is given in the source; the church
  has no fixed hierarchy of titles beyond its class distinctions (cleric/druid/shaman), with druids
  generally the most favored and respected.

### Dogma

- Silvanus sees and balances all -- wild water and drought, fire and ice, life and death alike.
- His priests strive to see the whole macrocosm, not any one person's or nation's idea of what is
  best.
- The faithful are strongly on the side of wild nature and the natural state of things over any
  civilizing force.
- Keep the sacred Balance clearly in view, help others see it too, and work against all who would
  disturb it -- best done by watching, anticipating, and quiet manipulation.
- Resort to violence and open confrontation only when pressure of time, situation, or hostile action
  makes it necessary.
- When one must act one way one day, take the opposing side another day -- always keep the Balance.
- Fight against the felling of forests, banish disease wherever found, and defend and plant trees
  whenever possible.
- Seek out, serve, and befriend the dryads and learn their names.
- Kill only when needful, for all things in the forest are in balance.
- Destroy fire and those who employ it carelessly or with destructive intent; beware orcs and others
  who bring axes into the forest.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Work to redirect development and control population pressures through covert sponsorship of
  brigands, breeding and placement of predators, and other means -- kept as secret as possible.
- Balance this covert work with visibly public-facing efforts: wildlife breeding, nursing sick
  animals, and replanting trees and wild shrubs.
- Study, through instruction and lifelong observation, the intricate life-cycles of all living
  creatures in Faerûn.
- Take the long view, so the manifold implications of every action can be foreseen well before they
  occur.
- Preach the peace and purity of the wilds, and dispense herbs and sweetsap drinks (maple syrup, mint
  teas, sweetroot brews) to attract followers.
- Tithe/sacrifice: something made from wood must be ceremonially broken and buried -- never burned,
  and never a blood sacrifice.

| Midsummer Night | Midsummer (annual) | A holy day of the greenleaf priesthood. |

| Call of Oak, Ash, and Thorn | As needed (simplest prayer) | A priest gathers leaves of the three named trees, floats them on water, and entreats Silvanus to hear a prayer. |
| Vigil | As needed (for deeper concerns) | The worshiper anoints their body with a powder of crushed acorns and mistletoe mixed with rainwater or spring water and lies in contact with a growing, moss-covered tree for most of a night. |
| Song of the Trees | As needed | A droning, haunting chant that heals burned, diseased, and scarred trees, and in rare moments of favor even re-erects fallen or felled trees; draws woodland creatures as silent witnesses. |
| Dryad Dance | As needed | A wild ritual of piping, dancing, and carousing that calls dryads and hamadryads out of the woods and empowers them to travel far from their trees for a lunar cycle. |
| Thorncall | As needed (only after a servant of Silvanus is slain or sheds much blood nearby) | The most commonly performed Silvanite ritual -- raises permanent, labyrinthine walls of tearing thorns to keep out those who would burn or despoil the forest. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No formal knightly orders are sponsored directly by the church.
- (Formal, via Mielikki) Firm ties to several orders of rangers who serve Mielikki, who in turn serves
  Silvanus.
- (Informal) The Emerald Enclave -- a large, aggressive society of druids active in the Vilhon Reach;
  viewed as somewhat radical by many Silvanites elsewhere.
- Holy groves and forest pool shrines are often guarded by the clergy of Eldath alongside Silvanus's
  own druids and clerics.
- Ties to the Harpers.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Druid (all specialty priests of Silvanus are simply druids)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, sickle, dart, spear, dagger, scimitar, sling, staff; favored weapons
  maul & staff
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (padded, leather, or hide); wooden, bone, shell, or other
  nonmetallic shield allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Elemental, Healing, Plant, Time, Wards, Weather, Sun
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in two of: craft (brewing), craft (cobbling), craft (cooking),
  craft (fishing), craft (weaving); 1 rank in lore (nature)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on survival (pick one terrain); +1
  bonus on lore (weather); +1 bonus on linguistics (pick one from: brownie, dryad, elvish, korred,
  nymph, pegasus, pixie, satyr, sprite, sylph, treant, unicorn)
