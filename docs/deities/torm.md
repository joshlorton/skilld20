# Torm (The True, the True God, the Brat, the Foolish, the Loyal Fury)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Torm
- **Titles/Epithets:** The True, the True God, the Brat, the Foolish, the Loyal Fury
- **Portfolio:** Duty, loyalty, obedience, paladins
- **Symbol:** A right-hand metal gauntlet held upright, palm open and toward the viewer -- or a silver
  or gray metal shield bearing the open-handed gauntlet device with three black arrows embedded in it

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **Organization:** A three-tier hierarchy. The top tier is the Tormtar (the priesthood proper), who
  ascend through their own ranks; holy champions (specialty priests) make up 40% of the priesthood and
  often lead it, with clerics at 30%, crusaders at 20%, and paladins at 10%. The second tier is the
  knightly orders dedicated to Torm (the "Swords of Torm"), largely crusaders and paladins allied with
  but not commanded by the clergy. The third tier is Torm's lay faithful, including many warriors and
  government officials. Following the Time of Troubles, when the church's leadership persecuted other
  goodly faiths under a corrupt high priest, Torm imposed the Penance of Duty -- a set of ongoing
  atonement obligations described under Day-to-Day Activities below.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Unproven (novices)
  2. Anduran (confirmed priests of lower rank)
  3. Faithblade
  4. Warden
  5. Vigilant
  6. Watchful Venturer
  7. Loyan
  8. Enforcer
  9. Guardian
  10. Knight
  11. Vanguardier
  12. Champion (the most holy priests of the faith)

  Separate duty-titles tied to specific posts (in ascending order) also exist: Patrol Captain, Revered
  Messenger, Doorwarden, Seneschal, Templemaster, High Priest, and Priest Inquisitor (teachers and
  internal disciplinarians of the faith).

### Dogma

- Salvation may be found through service; every failure diminishes the Lord of Duty, and every success
  adds to his luster.
- Maintain law and order, and obey your masters to the utmost of your power with alert judgment and
  anticipation.
- Obedience is not blind -- a servant working for an evil master is responsible to a higher authority
  in his loyalty.
- Stand ever alert against corruption, and strike quickly and hard against any rot found in the hearts
  of mortals.
- As the sword arm of justice, bring painful, quick deaths to betrayers.
- Question unjust laws by suggesting improvements or alternatives, never by mere additions.
- Duty is fourfold: to faith, to family, to masters, and to all good fellow beings of Faerûn.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Provide training for, give sanctuary to, and lend support (money, gear, mounts, armor, and weaponry)
  to guardians, orders of paladins and loyal knights, and loyal courtiers across Faerûn.
- Send forth agents to ferret out corruption in courts and organizations, especially those that set
  themselves up as righteous or morally superior, and watch for impending trouble such as orc hordes
  or warlike realms.
- Recruit promising young folk into the service of Torm or into positions as loyal warriors or
  bodyguards.
- Rarely, act militarily against forces of evil, disloyal citizens readying coup attempts, or thieving
  and outlaw organizations.
- A few adventuring Tormtar are granted greater personal latitude in their deeds, in exchange for
  tithing 60% or more of their income (plus payment for magical aid) and reporting back all they learn
  of distant regions, beasts, and concerns to their more stationary brethren.
- Observe three special rituals: Torm's Table (at least once every two months, hosting a stranger to a
  grand meal while fasting, then confessing sins to a fellow Tormtar within three days), Investiture
  (the ceremony admitting a novice into the priesthood), and the Holy Vigil (repeated at every rank
  advancement, in which the supplicant stands alone in the sanctuary beneath a levitating sword that
  slowly sinks toward their head if fear, sleep, or doubt takes hold).
- Pray to Torm at least four times daily (noon, dusk, midnight, and dawn) in a prescribed, chant-like
  litany that, since the Time of Troubles, includes recitation of the Penance of Duty.
- Tithe yearly to the local temple as one is able.
- Fulfill the Penance of Duty, a set of debts imposed on the church after its persecution of other
  faiths under the corrupt high priest Tenwealth:
  - **Debt of Dereliction:** Expend all possible effort to eliminate any surviving cults of Bane, and
    to oppose the efforts of Cyricists, Xvimists, and the Zhentarim.
  - **Debt of Destruction:** Relieve the damage done to the weave of magic during the Time of
    Troubles -- report and repair all dead magic areas, undo lasting effects of the magical chaos,
    and report and eradicate wild magic areas.
  - **Debt of Persecution:** Aid other goodly religions in reestablishing themselves, to repay the
    Tormish church's past persecution of them.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Divine Death | 13th of Eleasias | Solemn remembrance of Torm's death fighting Bane near Tantras during the Time of Troubles. After prayer and a feast, Tormtar visit the graves or battle sites of their own fallen, light candles, and pray through the night recounting the deeds of the dead. Torm may send comforting dream visions -- but seeing Torm's own death in such a vision is taken as an omen of the dreamer's death in his service within the coming year. |
| True Resurrection | 15th of Marpenoth | Celebrates Torm's return to power at the end of the Godswar; a joyous feast and revel in which normal laws are set aside for one day and night. Torm sends inspiring dream visions afterward and occasionally grants the Loyal Boon -- a single free casting of a spell otherwise beyond the recipient's current ability. |
| Shieldmeet | Shieldmeet (quadrennial) | A ritual of loyalty and renewed oaths and pledges. Tormtar prefer to marry, perform marriages, and finalize apprenticeship or training agreements on this day. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Order of the Golden Lion -- founded after Torm's rebirth, led by First Champion of Torm
  Sir Dylan Lionshand; paladins of this order are specially dedicated to actively serving the Penance
  of Duty, wandering the Realms in Torm's service, guarding the temples of a wide variety of peaceful
  faiths, or questing to recover lost relics of other good gods. A member who fails to serve the
  Penance loses paladinhood, reclaimable only through an atonement spell and a difficult quest set
  according to the Penance's goals; members cannot advance in level without serving to alleviate one
  of its debts. Torm occasionally rewards devoted service with the minor quest spell lion's roar
  (equivalent to a great shout). Ceremonial garb is a full suit of well-polished battle armor with an
  ornate helm, the armor polished to a bright golden color.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Holy Champion
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Same as clerics; favored weapon greatsword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Combat, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Law, Protection, Summoning,
  Sun
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Elemental, Necromantic, Travelers, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in etiquette
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon), heraldry, and handle animal (horse) checks; +1 bonus
  on sense motive checks
