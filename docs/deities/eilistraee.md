# Eilistraee (the Dark Maiden, Lady of the Dance, Lady Silverhair)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Eilistraee
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Dark Maiden, Lady of the Dance, Lady Silverhair
- **Portfolio:** Song, beauty, dance, swordwork, hunting, moonlight
- **Symbol:** A silver long sword outlined against a silver moon, with silvery filaments (Eilistraee's
  own hair) surrounding it all, set within a nimbus

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including multiclassed clerics), crusaders, mystics, and specialty
  priests (including multiclassed specialty priests) of Eilistraee receive religion (drow), religion
  (elven), and reading/writing (drowic) as bonus proficiencies -- see `drow-priests-general.md`. Must
  be female, though of any intelligent race (not only drow).
- **Organization:** Loosely organized and collectively known as the Dark Ladies; acolytes and
  aspirants who have not yet attained full priesthood are called Maids. The vast majority (90%) of
  the clergy are specialty priests (sword dancers). Clerics make up a small and shrinking fraction
  (6%). Crusaders (2%), found mostly in the South, are all members of the Darksong Knights. A
  handful (under 1%) are mystics who discover Eilistraee on their own, outside any established
  church. Temples are almost always sited on the surface -- cavern mouths and dim forests from which
  priests can venture out to dance under the moon -- and rarely founded below ground. A full temple
  complex typically includes a dancing glade with an unobstructed view of the moon, a dark place away
  from daylight, a thick tree canopy, a lively freshwater stream, a forge and smithy for crafting
  swords, an access tunnel to the Underdark, and a vein of iron or other metal for sword-making; the
  simplest shrine needs only a moonlit glade and a song.
- Titles vary widely by temple and are sometimes chosen personally in a private rite called the Flame
  Song; common examples include Moon Dancer, Moon Singer, Dark Huntress, Argent Maid, Living Sword,
  Unsheathed Blade, Sword Smith, Bright Edge of Darkness, and Ghost of the Moonstruck Night.

### Dogma

- Aid the weak, the strong, the grateful, and the churlish alike; be always kind, save in battle with evil.
- Spread happiness everywhere; lift hearts with kind words, jests, songs, and merriment.
- Learn how to hunt and cook game, and learn new songs, dances, sword-work, spices, and recipes --
  then pass this learning on to others.
- Learn to play, make, and repair musical instruments; practice music and swordwork constantly.
- Defend and aid all folk, and promote harmony between the races.
- Treat strangers as friends; shelter the homeless, under your own roof if need be.
- Repay rudeness with kindness. Repay violence with swift violence, that the fewest may be hurt and
  danger fastest removed from the land.
- Aid drow in distress; if they fight other drow, stop the combat with as little bloodshed as
  possible (so long as they are not working evil on others), and bring them Eilistraee's message: a
  rightful place awaits them in the Realms Above, beneath the sun, where trees and flowers grow.
- Feed any hungry, nonthreatening traveler; carry food while traveling for this purpose, and gather
  or hunt for it where it cannot be bought or spared.
- In harsh winters, patrol the land to find and take in the lost, the hurt, and those caught in the
  cold.
- Eat with the accompaniment of song whenever possible; keep feasts merry except on properly sad
  occasions.
- When faithful or allies fall in battle, provide burial, a funeral song, and comfort to the bereaved
  whenever possible.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Encourage drow to return to the surface world, and work to build harmony between drow and the
  other races so drow may be seen as rightful, nonevil inhabitants of Faerûn.
- Nurture beauty, music, instrument-making, and song wherever found; assist hunters and hunting, and
  help others in small acts of kindness whenever the chance arises.
- Must be skilled in at least one of the Dark Maiden's favored instruments (horn, flute, or harp), be
  an adequate singer, and be a fit, graceful dancer; gather songs and musical knowledge constantly and
  train with the sword whenever possible.
- May keep and accumulate wealth given as offerings, so long as it is spent on food, instruments, and
  tools (such as good swords) in service of the goddess. Priests may adventure so long as they feed,
  aid, and defend the needy they meet along the way.
- Try to convert at least one stranger per month to Eilistraee's worship; leading a convert in prayer
  is itself an offering, and the goddess often (68% chance) manifests a sign to the convert.
- When evil creatures must be fought and slain, burn their bodies as an offering to the goddess --
  unless the creatures are edible and nonsentient and hungry folk are nearby, in which case they are
  eaten instead.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Circle of Song | Daily, whenever possible | The customary worship of the Dark Maiden: a hunt followed by a feast and dancing, then a Circle of Song in which worshipers dance and sing by turns around a circle, ideally outdoors in a moonlit wood. |
| Evensong | Nightly, personal | A private, often wordless release of the day's gathered emotion. |
| The Sword Dance | Whenever a sword is finished or first taken up | The priest plants the blade point-down in open ground by night and dances the Sword Dance around it, drawing blood from each of her limbs against the edge. If successful (45% chance per night attempted), the blade glows with silvery radiance and, for three months, cannot break or rust and can strike as though magical. |
| The High Hunt | At least once each season | A night hunt of a dangerous beast or monster led by priests of Eilistraee, who go naked and carry only a single sword; ends in a chanted prayer and circle dance if the quarry is slain. |
| The Run | At least once a year | Non-drow priests darken their skin, and all priests dye their hair silver, then wander the surface world as strangers -- especially seeking out elven communities -- bringing game, kindness, and aid while learning new songs, music, and sword-ways. |
| The Last Dance | Once, in old age | A priest's final ritual: hearing the goddess singing to her by night, she dances unclad beneath the moon until she fades away entirely into a shared, melancholy song with Eilistraee. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Darksong Knights -- an elite order of Eilistraeen crusaders and warrior-priests active in
  the South, beneath ancient Ilythiir, each dedicated to furthering the Dark Maiden's ethos and, in
  particular, to destroying the Abyss-spawned yochlol, the handmaidens of Lolth.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Sword Dancer
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any (swords preferred); favored weapon bastard sword (the singing sword,
  a silver bastard sword +3 that sings when unsheathed) -- matches the bastard sword ("Moonsword")
  favored weapon given for Eilistraee in later published sources
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Any weight, but only magical armor or armor of drow make may be worn
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Creation, Elemental, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic, Protection,
  Sun, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Charm, Divination, Summoning, Travelers, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (ancient history) or lore (local history), 1 rank in art (any
  one), 1 rank in survival (hunting)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on performance (dancing); +1 bonus on
  musical instrument (horn, flute, or harp); +1 bonus on singing

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | May release a wielded sword of dancing to fight on its own during any round when its enchantment bonus is not +1 |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Eilistraee's Moonfire (20 mana per casting; usable once per day per level of experience, effectively unlimited within a day) |
| 5 | May release the sword of dancing on any round; it then fights on its own for a number of rounds equal to the priest's level before returning |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Magic Missile (1st-level wizard spell, 4 missiles) *or* ignite an edged slashing weapon with a fiery glow for a +3 attack bonus next round, choose one per use, up to twice per day (20 mana) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Enchanted Weapon (4th-level wizard spell, by touch, +2 attack/damage for 7 rounds), three times per day (120 mana) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Spell Turning (7th-level wizard spell), once per day (70 mana) |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Commune *or* True Seeing (5th-level priest spells), choose one per use, once per day (50 mana) |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Stone Tell (6th-level priest spell, 60 mana) *or* Plane Shift (5th-level priest spell, 50 mana), choose one per use, once per day |

Priests of the Dark Maiden may also cast Stalk (2nd-level, *Faiths & Avatars*, under Mielikki).
