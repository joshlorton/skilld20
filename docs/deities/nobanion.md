# Nobanion (Lord Firemane, King of the Beasts, King of Lions and Strong Beasts, the Lion King,
Lord of the Gulthmere Forest, Guardian of the Vilhon Reach)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Nobanion
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord Firemane, King of the Beasts, King of Lions and Strong Beasts, the Lion
  King, Lord of the Gulthmere Forest, Guardian of the Vilhon Reach
- **Portfolio:** Lions, good-aligned beasts, lammasu, wemics, werelions, felines, noble beasts, royalty
- **Symbol:** A male lion's head, or a golden lion rampant on a green shield

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders)
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, specialty priests, crusaders, and shamans of Nobanion receive religion
  (pantheon) as a bonus skill. Wemics in Nobanion's service may become clerics, crusaders, or specialty
  priests in addition to shamans; Nobanion has no witch doctors.
- **Organization:** Worship is scattered throughout the Vilhon Reach, Dragon Coast, and Shining Plains
  regions, but Lord Firemane is venerated primarily within the Gulthmere Forest, in the city of
  Nathlekh, and among the wemics of the Shining Plains. Aside from his temple in Nathlekh (the Pride of
  Nobanion, a vast pillared acropolis structure), Nobanion is worshiped at simple, often hard-to-find
  shrines rather than elaborate houses of worship -- many are carried by nomadic wemic tribes and set
  up atop granite outcroppings or beneath solitary trees on the savanna. Clergy, collectively known as
  the Pride of Nobanion, are not given to elaborate titles: specialty priests (firemanes) are commonly
  called Lights of the Lion's Mane, clerics and crusaders are Roaring Avengers, and shamans are Speakers
  of the Paw and Roar. Lammasu and greater lammasu are considered part of the clergy and are addressed
  as Elders and Reverend Elders, respectively.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** No single numbered hierarchy is given -- clergy use the
  functional/class titles above rather than an ascending rank ladder.

### Dogma

- Hunt only when hungry, and never gorge without need; waste nothing, so that all shall have plenty.
- The cycle of life links all living things into one being, and that being is life itself.
- The law of the jungle is that only the strong survive, but they survive best as leaders who protect
  the weak, not as tyrants who bully them.
- All creatures have strengths suited to their assigned roles and should be encouraged to find their
  niche; cooperation between beings of differing strengths produces the strength of teamwork and
  community, the strongest force of all.
- Through compassion, tolerance, and living within the land, all creatures may find harmony with nature
  and one another.
- Stay true to yourself and your pride, and conduct yourself with dignity and honor, so as to earn the
  respect of your peers.
- (Most of Nobanion's teachings reach the wider Realms filtered through the wemics, whose harsher
  reading runs closer to: "only the strong survive," "live and let live unless provoked," and "protect
  the pride, but grant the injured or ill a swift, painless end to suffering.")

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Fill leadership roles in communities that revere the Lion God -- serving as benevolent monarchs,
  judges, militia, or constables, or as guardians against evil.
- Form the vanguard of armies loyal to Nobanion, typically staffed by clerics and crusaders.
- Teach hunting and martial skills to the young, passing on moral instruction and tradition by word and
  deed.
- Among the wemic tribes of the Shining Plains, shamans typically rank second only to the chieftain or
  king -- choosing what to hunt, blessing the kill, and confirming the passage of the young into
  adulthood.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Festival of the Pride | First week of Ches | A time of frolicking, dancing, courting, and celebrating life's bounty and potential; a child/cub conceived this night is believed destined to one day rule the church/pride. |
| Newborn Celebration | Third week of Kythorn | Celebrates Nobanion's birth (or rebirth -- church teaching is vague) and the importance of the hunt; marked by a great hunt and feast. Newborns undergo the Rite of the First Blooding, their hand or paw placed in the blood of the prey and blessed by a priest. |

*(The two holy days always fall exactly three and a half months apart, mirroring a lion's gestation
period.)*

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Legion of Lions -- a military order of wemic firemanes, clerics, and crusaders founded
  after the Time of Troubles by Karrlon, a former scout turned crusader. Small but elite, the fellowship
  has quickly become the champion of good-aligned beasts throughout the Realms and the bane of
  Malarites everywhere; individual members also often join adventuring companies of other races to see
  the world and spread the faith.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Firemane
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons, spear, natural weaponry; favored
  weapon a lion's head (heavy pick)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); in practice usually hide armor
  or no armor at all
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Combat, Guardian, Healing, Law, Protection, Summoning, War, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Elemental, Necromantic (no reversed), Plant, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in survival (fire-building), 1 rank in survival (hunting), 1 rank in
  athletics (running)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on lore (nature); +1 bonus on survival
  (trapping/set snares); +1 bonus on survival (pick one terrain from forest or plains)

Only humans, wild (wood) elves, any sort of feline lycanthrope (werecats, weretigers, werelions,
werepanthers, etc.), and wemics can become firemanes.
