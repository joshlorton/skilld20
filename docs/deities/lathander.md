# Lathander (the Morninglord, Commander of Creativity)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Lathander
- **Titles/Epithets:** the Morninglord, Commander of Creativity
- **Portfolio:** Spring, dawn, birth, renewal, creativity, youth, vitality, self-perfection, athletics
- **Symbol:** A disk of rosy pink hue

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** spellcasters, men-at-arms.
- **Organization:** No overarching hierarchy or central authority exists between temples; each
  Lathanderite priest is considered master of their own temple, shrine, or parish regardless of rank
  or experience. About 35% of organized clergy are specialty priests, the rest clerics or crusaders.
  Novices are called the Awakened, and become Dawnbringers upon ordination as full priests.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Awakened (novice)
  2. Dawngreeter
  3. Dawnlord
  4. High Dawnlord
  5. Dawnmaster
  6. Morninglord (rank title, distinct from the specialty priest class of the same name)
  7. High Morninglord
  8. Mornmaster
  9. High Mornmaster
  10. Sunrise Lord

### Dogma

- Strive always to aid, to foster new hope, new ideas, and new prosperity for all humankind and its
  allies.
- Perfect thyself, and guard ever against pride.
- It is a sacred duty to foster new growth, nurture growing things, and work for rebirth and renewal.
- Be fertile in mind and body.
- Consider always the consequences of thine actions so that the least effort may bring the greatest
  and best reward.
- Wherever you go, sow seeds of plants, tend the growing things you find, and plant seeds of hope, new
  ideas, and plans for a rosy future in the minds of all.
- Whenever possible, see each dawn.
- Acting in service of Lathander through helping, encouraging, and aiding matters far more than strict
  adherence to ritual or the dictates of superior clergy.
- Favorite sayings: "From death, life"; "There is always another morning"; "In the dawn, beauty
  reigns, and the way is clearer."

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Build anew, encourage the rebirth of barren lands and productive growth in cultivated ones, and
  drive out evil.
- Battle monsters to nurture civilization; plant seeds and seedlings; encourage and aid adventurers,
  travelers, traders, and pilgrims.
- Recover lost magical items, literature, and works of art, then study, restore, duplicate, or expand
  upon them.
- Sponsor athletic competitions (wrestling, distance throwing, archery, running, jumping, riding, and
  more) and literary/fine-arts competitions, awarding money, special items, or commissioned works to
  victors.
- Aid adventurers and communities so long as such aid is returned in good faith; encourage (without
  insisting on) conversion of the good-aligned.
- Tithe/offering: regular offerings of ideas, inventions, coins, discovered artifacts, or food; far
  more valuable magical items or gold are demanded of nonbelievers in exchange for special services
  such as rescue missions or raising the dead.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dawn Prayers | Daily, at dawn | The most important ceremony of the faith; actions taken and contracts (including marriages) agreed to at dawn are considered especially blessed. |
| Twilight Devotions | Daily, at dusk | Second in importance only to the dawn prayers. |
| Highsun Prayer and Song | Daily, optional | Some temples add this additional midday ceremony to the two daily devotions. |
| The Going Down | At death, as needed | A solemn candlelit funeral ceremony followed by a wake lasting until dawn prayers; not held for those who are to be raised. |
| The Song of Dawn | Midsummer morning, and the mornings of the vernal and autumnal equinoxes | Priests perform a blend of vocal harmonies and counterharmonies of great complexity in praise of Lathander. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Order of the Aster -- a knightly order of paladins, crusaders, and fighters who protect
  temples and shrines, lead levied military groups as needed, and roam the land doing good and
  promoting Lathander's worship. Individual temples otherwise rarely maintain standing forces of their
  own beyond the Aster knights they retain.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Morninglord
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Same as clerics; favored weapon mace (light or heavy)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Heavy armor, shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Creation, Elemental, Healing, Necromantic (restorative forms
  of reversible spells only), Plant, Thought, Sun, Wards, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, Divination, Guardian, Time
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** None
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); pick two: appraisal, art (any one), craft (any
  one), perform (any one)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Turns undead at +4 effective levels against undead affected by direct sunlight |
| 1 | +2 base bonus to appraising, artistic ability, carpentry, cooking, juggling, leatherworking, musical instrument, or pottery checks |
| 1 | +10% bonus to resurrection survival checks when casting raise dead or resurrection (cannot exceed 99%) |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Create Light (30 mana -- 1st-level spell, 3/day) |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Faerie Fire (10 mana) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Sunrise (30 mana) |
