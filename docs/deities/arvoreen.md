# Arvoreen (The Defender, the Vigilant Guardian, the Wary Sword)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Arvoreen
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Defender, the Vigilant Guardian, the Wary Sword
- **Portfolio:** Martial defense, war, vigilance, halfling warriors, duty
- **Symbol:** Two crossed short swords

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** Small, fortified temples built partially above and below ground in
  halfling-populated regions, serving as armories, militia training grounds, and last-resort
  sanctuaries. Interiors are mazes of narrow low halls sized for halflings and gnomes, cellars of
  stores, and tunnels for guerrilla sorties. Clergy is 55% hairfeet, 30% stouts, 15% tallfellows;
  males (54%) slightly outnumber females (46%); a large fraction of the clergy multiclasses with
  fighter (specialty priests 34%, clerics 26%, fighter/specialty priests 22%, fighter/clerics 18%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Shieldbearer (novice)
  2. Warder
  3. Guardian
  4. Defender
  5. Protector
  6. Magistrate
  7. Sheriff
  8. Marshal (full priest, "Arvoreen's Marshal")
  9. High Marshal

  High-ranking priests also carry unique individual titles. Specialty priests are called trueswords.

### Dogma

- Keep the community's burrows secure, and always be prepared for threats and attacks.
- Prepare an active defense, drill continuously, and leave nothing to chance.
- Put down danger before it even rears its head.
- Seek out allies, no matter how unorthodox -- those who give aid against a mutual foe are friends
  to be rewarded and trusted.
- Stealing from other halflings and allies is never acceptable, but thieving is not necessarily
  dishonorable if employed against enemies to better the odds in combat later.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Construct and review defensive barriers, signaling systems, beacons, and traps for halfling
  communities.
- Regularly patrol communities, investigating the slightest hint of a threat.
- Organize local militias, procure weapons for volunteers, and train able-bodied halflings in combat
  or in how to best seek safety.
- Adventure to gain magical weapons and defensive items of all kinds.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-battle blessing | Before every battle, when time permits | A brief sermon and private prayer followed by the communal Battle Hymn of the Keepers; at most one silvered weapon per worshiper is sacrificed on the altar. If Arvoreen is pleased, he takes only the silver plating, leaving the weapon behind enchanted with a +1 attack/damage bonus for 24 hours. |
| Ceremony of Remembrance | Feast of the Moon (annual) | Followers gather in temples and on old battlefields to remember the names of the fallen; close friends and relatives sometimes report a brief, wordless encounter with a fallen loved one's spirit. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Arvoreen's Marchers -- a knightly order recognized by the crown of Tethyr, formed from
  the band that held back the anarchy of Tethyr's Interregnum in the Purple Hills; patrols the Purple
  Marches (particularly County Vintor) and includes warriors, priests of Arvoreen, and rogues acting
  as spies and couriers. Chapter house: Keeperstone, north of Barrowsmorn.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Truesword
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All weapons; favored weapon short sword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Law, Protection, Sun, War, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Necromantic, Summoning, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics and fighters)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in short sword, 1 rank in craft (engineering), 1 rank in literacy
  (common)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on alertness; +1 bonus on
  blind-fighting; +1 bonus on survival (trapping/set snares)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | May hide in shadows and move silently as a ranger of the same level (modified by race, Dexterity, and armor) |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Blessed Watchfulness (cross-reference -- detailed under Gorm Gulthyn in the Gnome Pantheon chapter, not yet converted) *or* Detect Evil, choose one per use (10 mana), once per day |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Strength (2nd-level wizard spell) *or* Weapon Shift, choose one per use (20 mana), once per day |
| 5 | Lay on hands (as the paladin ability), healing 1 hit point per level, once per day |
| 5 | May invoke courage with a word, granting self and up to six others within 60 feet immunity to fear and a +1 bonus to attack rolls and saving throws for 1 turn; usable once per day per level |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Glyph of Warding *or* Strength of One, choose one per use (30 mana), once per day |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Easy March *or* Unceasing Vigilance of the Holy Sentinel, choose one per use (50 mana), once per day |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Champion's Strength (50 mana) *or* Haste (3rd-level wizard spell, self only, no aging penalty, 30 mana), choose one per use, once per day |
