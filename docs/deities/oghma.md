# Oghma (the Binder, the Wise God)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Oghma
- **Titles/Epithets:** the Binder, the Wise God
- **Portfolio:** Knowledge, invention, inspiration, bards
- **Symbol:** A simple, blank scroll

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, monastic orders, bardic performers
- **Organization:** The entire church hierarchy was historically devoted to the Grand Patriarch of
  Oghma (based in Procampur), who vanished without a trace during the Time of Troubles. The church
  now runs without an ultimate head and has split into factions: the Orthodox Church of Oghma (which
  holds the missing Patriarch is still rightfully in office), the Church of Oghma in Sembia (which
  holds a new Patriarch has been appointed and all knowledge should be tested before release), and the
  Pursuers of Pure Knowledge in Mintar (aligned with Sembia on hierarchy, not theology). Acolytes are
  called Seekers (and Senior Seekers with accomplishment); once confirmed by two priests, an acolyte
  becomes a full loremaster.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order, main hierarchy):
  1. Loremaster
  2. Loremaster Amanuensis
  3. Loremaster Venturer
  4. Loremaster Bold
  5. Lore-Scribe of the God
  6. Wise Anticipator
  7. Inspirator
  8. Inspirator High
  9. Atlar
  10. Higher Atlar
  11. Loremaster High
  12. Loremaster Most High
  13. Eye of Oghma
  14. Divine Hand of Oghma

  *(The Church of Oghma in Sembia and the Pursuers of Pure Knowledge in Mintar instead use, ascending:
  Advocate, Accomplished Advocate, Loremaster of the Twelfth through Loremaster of the Second,
  Loremaster First, Loremaster High, Learned One, Patriarch.)*

### Dogma

- Spread knowledge whenever it is prudent to do so.
- Keep no secrets for their own sake.
- Curb and deny falsehood, rumor, and deceitful accounts and histories whenever you encounter them.
- Write or copy some lore of value and give it away freely at least once a year.
- Hide some writings away while distributing others widely, so written knowledge grows larger over
  your lifetime.
- Sponsor, assist, and teach minstrels, bards, scribes, and recordkeepers whenever you encounter them
  and perceive a need.
- Spread truth and knowledge throughout the Realms so that all folk may know more.
- Never deliver a message falsely or incompletely, but always just as you receive it.
- Teach any folk who ask how to read and write, charging no fee for the teaching.
- Never slay a singer, nor stand by while others do so.
- Listen to new bards when you meet them and sponsor bards when you can.
- Stifle no new ideas, no matter how false or crazed they seem -- let them be heard and considered
  freely.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Cloistered priests spend their lives in analysis, reading gathered tomes, and copying texts and
  spells as requested.
- Wayfaring priests travel Faerûn seeking the writings that fill abbey libraries, often carrying spell
  scrolls and coin to trade for learned works.
- Most abbeys of Oghma support themselves by selling maps, scribework, and spell scrolls; a member of
  the clergy may sell a map known to err in some respects but never a knowingly false one.
- Every Oghmanyte is expected to publish at least one book and deliver it to at least three temples of
  the Wise God.
- Priests of Curna (Oghma's aspect in Durpar, Estagund, and Var the Golden) are paid to give advice
  and draw up contracts, and receive tribute before merchants make important business deals.
- Two solemn daily rituals are observed: the Binding (morning) and the Covenant (evening) -- see Holy
  Days below.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Midsummer | Midsummer (annual) | The most sacred day of the Oghmanyte calendar; an occasion when agreements are made or renewed and many contracts and bonds are drawn up. |
| Shieldmeet | Shieldmeet (quadrennial) | Shares Midsummer's significance for making and renewing agreements. |
| The Binding | Daily, morning | Symbols of Oghma are written in dirt, ashes upon a stone altar, or in the mind (if the clergy member is shackled or otherwise unable to write), accompanied by a silent prayer of loyalty and praise. |
| The Covenant | Daily, evening | A passage from a work of wisdom is read aloud or recited from memory, a song or poem is offered, and some item of knowledge learned that day is spoken aloud to the god and any fellow clergy present. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No formal military or knightly orders are sponsored by the church.
- (Informal) Children of the Passive Voice -- learned monks who protect many libraries and abbeys.
- (Informal) Order of the Gilt Laurel -- an honorary society of historical fiction authors.
- (Informal) Fellows of the Forest -- a naturalist society.
- (Informal) Companions of the Silver Strings -- an order of heroic bards who acted valiantly at risk
  of their own lives in the church's service.
- (Informal) Those Who Harp (the Harpers) -- close ties, though not a sponsored order.

## Specialty Priests

Oghma has two specialty priest tracks: Lorekeepers (the general priesthood) and, regionally in Durpar,
Estagund, and Var the Golden -- where Oghma is worshiped as Curna, goddess of wisdom, as part of the
Adama (the Durparian concept of a world spirit) -- Wise Ones, with a divination- and
business-advice-focused sphere and skill list.

### Lorekeepers

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon longsword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Heavy (up to and including banded mail); no shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Combat, Charm, Divination, Elemental, Guardian, Healing, Protection,
  Summoning, Thought, Travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Creation, Necromantic, Sun, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (ancient history), 1 rank in lore (local history)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on linguistics (pick one modern
  language); +1 bonus on literacy (Common); +1 bonus on literacy (Thorass)

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first. Where no spell level is given but a class/character level is, use spell level =
that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2 bonus to attack rolls when wrestling; suffers no armor penalty for wrestling |
| 1 | Treated as four additional experience levels when attempting to turn creatures native to the Outer Planes |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Identify, usable only in a temple/shrine sanctified to Oghma or within a protection from evil radius (10 mana) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Friends (10 mana) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Mass Charm, 2/tenday (16 mana) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Legend Lore, usable only in a temple/shrine sanctified to Oghma, 2/tenday (12 mana) |

### Wise Ones (Curna)

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons; no favored weapon specified in the
  source, and none found in real-world published material
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (nonmetallic armor only); nonmetallic shields permitted
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Creation, Divination, Healing, Protection, Summoning, Thought,
  Travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Guardian, Necromantic, Sun, Weather, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations; additionally may use items that enhance knowledge, such as crystal
  balls or wands of magic detection
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (local history, pick one place outside Durpar, Estagund, and Var
  the Golden), 1 rank in linguistics (pick one)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on lore (local history -- Durpar,
  Estagund, or Var the Golden, pick two); +1 bonus on linguistics (pick one); +1 bonus on literacy
  (Common); +1 bonus on literacy (Thorass)

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first. Where no spell level is given but a class/character level is, use spell level =
that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3% chance per level to recognize the general purpose and function of any magical item examined (as a bard's item-lore ability, not exact knowledge) |
| 1 | Casts Divination sphere spells as if three levels higher in experience |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Comprehend Languages (10 mana), once per day |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Friends (10 mana), once per day |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Zone of Truth *or* Hold Person (20 mana each), choose one per use, once per day |
| 10 | Can generate a blinding flash from their holy symbol once per day, targeting one creature; a failed save blinds the target until magically dispelled or the wise one lifts it by silent act of will |
| 12 | Permanent, constant Free Action (as the 4th-level priest spell; no mana cost) |
