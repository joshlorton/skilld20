# Thard Harr (Lord of the Jungle Deeps, Disentangler)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Thard Harr
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of the Jungle Deeps, Disentangler
- **Portfolio:** The wild dwarven race, jungle survival, hunting
- **Symbol:** Two crossed metal gauntlets of silvery-blue, luminous metal, ending in claws and covered
  with lapped scales

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics and specialty priests of Thard Harr receive the standard dwarven clergy
  grants (literacy (Dethek runes), modified turn undead) -- see `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** Thard's faith is nearly monotheistic among the wild (jungle) dwarves of Chult and
  the Mhair Jungles, who find it almost inconceivable to worship any other power; outside those
  cultures the Disentangler is little more than legend, even to gold dwarves. Priests are called
  shamans (though not members of that class) and reject any formal hierarchy of titles -- each priest
  instead receives a personal title in a dream on the night of initiation, typically naming a great
  jungle beast over which the priest is then believed to hold a small measure of supernatural control.
  Temples avoid artificial structures entirely, gathering instead at isolated sites of great natural
  beauty (cliffs, waterfalls, gorges, hot springs, caverns, volcanic mud flats) deep in the jungle,
  usually watched over by up to a dozen priests who can call on the surrounding jungle's beasts and
  nearby wild dwarf tribes to defend the site. The clergy is almost entirely jungle dwarf (99%), with a
  small handful of gold, gray, and shield dwarves; historically male-only, it is now 60% male / 40%
  female. Thard's clergy is nearly evenly divided between specialty priests (53%) and clerics (47%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Shaman (full priest; personal title granted in a dream at initiation, usually naming a great
     jungle beast)
  2. Lords/Ladies of the Jungle (High Old Ones, collectively; individuals also hold unique titles)

  Specialty priests are known as vuddor (singular vuddar), a dwarvish word loosely translated as
  "those of the jungle."

### Dogma

- The jungle is the fullest expression of the earth, the wind, the sun, and the rains; live in harmony
  with nature under the wise and benevolent protection of the Lord of the Jungle Deeps.
- Outsiders seek to pillage and destroy, and their unnatural ways bring misery.
- Like the great tigers of the jungle, be strong and wary of beasts, whether they walk on two legs or
  four.
- Seek to understand that which you do not, but be wary of bringing unknown gifts into your lair.
- Be one with nature -- live neither against it nor apart from it.
- Honor the ways of your people, but assume not that Thard's way is the only way -- just the best way
  for his children.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Protect dur Authalar (the People) with powers granted by the god, leading them on prosperous hunts
  and careful explorations; priests are the leaders, generals, and speakers of wild dwarven society.
- Lead attacks against persistent intruders (unless dwarven) into wild dwarven territory as fearlessly
  as Thard himself; if a foe proves too strong, a priest tries to call Thard to the scene, who may
  respond with a manifestation or, rarely, an avatar.
- Try to capture at least one intruder alive for questioning before any sacrificial use, and spare
  those likely to bring future benefit to the dwarves.
- Conduct trade with outsiders for metal, glass, and tools (in exchange for pelts, meat, or live
  beasts), but only on ground of the dwarves' own choosing, with traps and ambushes set to guard
  against treachery.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jungle Gathering | Nights of the full moon, and often the new moon | Several hunting bands converge under one or more priests of Thard; drums and chants echo through the jungle, striking terror into intelligent beings and beasts alike. Blood sacrifices of beasts and/or captured intruders are offered to the god (especially at the full moon), and the assembled wild dwarves then eat the still-warm sacrifice regardless of species -- though they are not habitual cannibals. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* The Pack -- an ad hoc war-band formed only when Thard's clergy collectively judges war
  necessary, drawing the best warriors of the scattered hunting bands (bloods of 2nd-4th level, war
  leaders of 5th-7th level, and priests of demonstrable fighting skill) into a nearly unstoppable force
  that disperses again once its objective is achieved.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Vuddor (singular vuddar)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All weapons; favored weapon clawed gauntlet (spiked gauntlet)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** None -- vuddor rely on ritual grease (AC 8) or mud-caked grease
  (AC 7) in place of true armor, and use no shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Combat, Elemental, Guardian, Healing, Plant, Protection, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Chaos, Charm, Creation, Divination, Necromantic, Sun, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in herbalism
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on lore (nature); +1 bonus on survival
  (jungle)
- **Other Clergy Grants:** Clerics of Thard Harr (unlike most dwarven clerics) may use any weapon, not
  just blunt (Type B) ones.
