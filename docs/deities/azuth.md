# Azuth (The High One, Patron of Wizards, the Lord of Spells, the Hand of Sorcery, the Lord of Spellcraft)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Azuth
- **Titles/Epithets:** The High One, Patron of Wizards, the Lord of Spells, the Hand of Sorcery, the
  Lord of Spellcraft
- **Portfolio:** Wizards, mages, spellcasters in general
- **Symbol:** A human left hand, pointing upward, outlined in a nimbus of blue fire

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, monastic orders
- **Organization:** The clergy is roughly 45% wizards, 30% clerics, 20% specialty priests (magistrati),
  and 5% monks. Relations between the groups are generally good, with some resentment over a recent
  trend of promoting magistrati into positions of power. The ruling clergy member of a temple, shrine,
  or monastic community holds the title "the First" and is addressed as "Revered One"; the leading
  specialist in a given school of magic within a parish is titled accordingly (First Transmuter, First
  Diviner, etc.), a title the local First may bestow or revoke. Clergy of high rank and long service
  are granted the title "Master." The church otherwise eschews further titles.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Ordinary clergy member (wizard, cleric, specialty priest, or monk)
  2. Master (granted for high rank and long years of service)
  3. First [School] (e.g. First Transmuter, First Diviner -- the leading specialist of a school within a parish)
  4. The First (the ruling clergy member of a temple, shrine, or monastic community; addressed "Revered One")

### Dogma

- Teach the wielding of magic, and dispense scrolls, items, and spellbooks throughout Faerûn so that
  the use and knowledge of magic spreads.
- Encourage everyone to try their hand at wielding magic.
- Drive home the lesson that with magical power comes grave responsibility, and live that lesson
  yourself.
- Try to obtain a copy of every new spell, spell variant, or magical idea encountered, regardless of
  its worth or importance, and make a copy of that copy for a temple library.
- Train others in what you know of magic rather than hoarding your knowledge.
- Encourage creativity in magic in all ways and at all times.
- Reason is the best way to approach magic; it may be examined and reduced to its component parts
  through study and meditation.
- Use calm and caution, since even magic cannot always undo a mistake.
- Use the Art wisely, and be always mindful of when it is best not to use magic at all.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve as trusted, neutral messengers between mages.
- Organize annual Mage Fairs, where clergy settle feuds, curb overly destructive or deceitful magic,
  and sponsor spellweaving contests, awarding scrolls of winning spells and small useful magical items
  as prizes.
- Work to ensure no spell or magical item is unique to a single mage in Faerûn -- so that one wizard's
  death cannot erase a spell or item's knowledge from the world -- through magical spying, temporary
  theft, copying every wizardly writing found (including command words and cryptic phrases, not just
  full incantations), and encouraging the barter of spells.
- Tithe/offering: organize tome drives in which wizards are paid handsomely to contribute a spell to
  the ongoing Azuthan spell cyclopedia, a magically mass-duplicated, bound collection distributed by
  the priesthood for a minor fee covering production costs.

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Order of the Forgotten Page -- an honor, not a martial order: clergy who have done great
  service recovering magical knowledge thought lost are voted in by the church's Firsts and allowed to
  wear special silver trim on their ceremonial vestment collars.
- *(Informal)* "Shield of the High One" -- a personal honor (not an organized order) granted to
  members who help eliminate a magical imbalance or monstrosity; recipients receive a minor protective
  magical item and a secret phrase granting them aid (healing, shelter, small loans) from any temple or
  shrine of Azuth.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Magistratus (plural: Magistrati)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon quarterstaff
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** None -- magistrati wear no armor and use no shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Combat, Creation, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Law,
  Necromantic, Protection, Summoning, Sun, Thought, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Elemental, Travelers, War
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in spellcraft
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on lore (arcana)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Casting time on any priest spell of one round or less is reduced by 3 (minimum 1); longer spells are unaffected |
| 2 | May pray for wizard spells in place of priest spells, cast as a mage of half their level (rounded down), restricted to a single chosen school (except read magic, always available); wizard spell occupies a priest spell slot; cannot access 8th- or 9th-level wizard spells |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Identify (10 mana per use; a given item may only be identified once by a particular magistratus, no rest or Constitution cost required) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Magic Missile (10 mana) |
| 9 | Foundation bonus: Fireball or Lightning Bolt, choice of one per use (30 mana) |
| 12 | Foundation bonus: Wall spell of choice -- Wall of Fire, Wall of Ice, Wall of Force, Wall of Iron, or Wall of Stone (40-50 mana depending on the wall chosen) |

## Azuthan Spells (deity-specific)

These spells are usable identically by both priests and wizards of Azuth's clergy (only casting times
differ between the two). They are hidden from any wizard outside the faith -- pages bearing them
appear blank to non-clergy eyes.

| Name | Est. Mana | Foundation | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Azuth's Fedensor | 40 | Thought | Lets the next 1st- through 5th-level spell cast by the caster or a touched being be unleashed without material components and without being forgotten from memory. |
| Azuth's Exalted Triad | 50 | Thought | Allows one memorized 1st- through 5th-level spell to be cast up to three times total without additional memorization; only the first casting requires normal components. |
| Azuth's Spell Shield | 70 | Protection | Renders the caster or a touched being immune to all magical effects, including ongoing ones, for one round. |
| Destroy Magedoom | 70 | Summoning, Protection | A specialized attack usable only against the magedoom monster, forcing a saving throw or destroying it outright, and dealing heavy damage even on a success. |
| Azuth's Immobility | 50 | Charm | Paralyzes all creatures within a 20-foot radius as though held, with the effect on all of them ending the instant the caster touches or attacks any one held target. |
| Azuth's Alteration Mantle | 60 | Protection | Shields the recipient with an aura that absorbs two chosen spells and lets the wearer convert the stored energy into healing or a redirected spell of the caster's choosing. |
| Azuth's Firing Frenzy | 60 | Combat | Fires three bursts of five magic-missile-like bolts, each burst unerringly striking a different one of up to three targets. |
