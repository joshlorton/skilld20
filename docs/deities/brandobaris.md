# Brandobaris (Master of Stealth, Misadventure, the Trickster, the Irrepressible Scamp, the Friendly Rapscallion)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Brandobaris
- **Titles/Epithets:** Master of Stealth, Misadventure, the Trickster, the Irrepressible Scamp, the
  Friendly Rapscallion
- **Portfolio:** Stealth, thievery, adventuring, halfling rogues
- **Symbol:** A halfling's footprint

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Rogues
- **Organization:** No formal temples or permanent shrines at all -- Brandobaris is instead honored
  through adventurous activity and the retelling of tales of his exploits and those of his
  followers; a "shrine" is created temporarily whenever such a story is told. Clergy is 60%
  hairfeet, 30% stouts, 10% tallfellows; males (65%) outnumber females (35%); the clergy is mostly
  specialty priests (30%), thieves (25%), specialty priest/thieves (22%), fighter/thieves (18%), and
  clerics (5%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Wayward Rascal (novice)
  2. Scamp
  3. Rascal
  4. Swindler
  5. Blackguard
  6. Trickster
  7. Rapscallion
  8. Knave
  9. Master Rogue (full priest, "Hand of Misadventure")

  High-ranking priests also carry unique individual titles. Specialty priests are called
  misadventurers.

### Dogma

- Adventure and risk are the spice of life, and stealth and subtlety are the tools of the trade.
- Seek excitement and danger wherever your feet take you, for risk-taking leads to life's greatest
  rewards.
- Lust for the thrill, not for the treasure, for greed obscures the true prize of the experience.
- At the end of the day, the wildest tale is the greatest reward.
- Learn to tell a good yarn, and sometimes your tongue will get you out of trouble.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Actively adventure, seeking lives of excitement and danger and employing the skills taught by the
  Master of Stealth.
- Pursue daring thefts, smooth cons, and other larcenous behavior as thrill-seekers rather than
  bandits -- driven by acquisition, not hoarding, and often spending freely at halfling-owned
  establishments.
- Those unable to adventure (age, infirmity) run safehouses and spread tales among the sedentary
  majority of halflings.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Trickster's Tithe | Nights of the new moon | Followers hide one or more stolen items from the past month's take in the best hiding place they can find. If Brandobaris is pleased -- judged by the risk taken to acquire the offering, not its value -- it vanishes from its cache by morning and the worshiper is blessed with a +1 bonus on a single saving throw usable within the following month. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Midknights of Misadventure -- a loose fellowship of mostly halfling clerics and
  fighter/thieves who perform jailbreaks and rescue operations when an imprisoned follower of
  Brandobaris faces death or torture and cannot escape unaided.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Misadventurer
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, dagger, dart, hand crossbow, knife, lasso, sap, short sword, sling,
  staff; favored weapons dagger & short sword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (leather, padded leather, or studded leather) or Medium
  (elven chain mail); no shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Chaos, Creation, Divination, Healing, Protection, Sun, Travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Combat, Guardian, Time
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics and thieves)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in acrobatics (tumbling), 1 rank in deception (ventriloquism)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on disguise; +1 bonus on gaming
