# Cyrrollalee (The Hand of Fellowship, the Faithful, the Hearthkeeper)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Cyrrollalee
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Hand of Fellowship, the Faithful, the Hearthkeeper
- **Portfolio:** Friendship, trust, the home, the hearth, honesty, hospitality, crafts (especially
  weaving and needlework)
- **Symbol:** An open door

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** Priests' homes double as temple and community home-away-from-home; there is
  little to distinguish a Cyrrollaleen house of worship from an ordinary halfling home, except that
  its door is always open while a priest is in residence, welcoming any halfling unable to return to
  their own bed for the night. Clergy is 65% hairfeet, 25% stouts, 10% tallfellows; nearly evenly
  split between specialty priests (35%), mystics (34%), and clerics (31%); females (85%) far
  outnumber males (15%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Befriended (novice)
  2. Cheery Homemaker
  3. Hearth Warden
  4. Hand of Friendship
  5. Hale Host (or Hostess)
  6. Homespun Companion
  7. Neighborly Householder
  8. Open Door
  9. Burrow Patriarch/Matriarch (full priest, "Homefellow")

  High-ranking priests also carry unique individual titles. Specialty priests are called
  homesteaders.

### Dogma

- Be generous in friendship, and welcome all friends into your home.
- Earn the trust of your neighbors and repay them with kindness.
- Guard fiercely the burrows in which you and your friends dwell, and keep a benignly watchful eye
  on the home of your neighbor.
- Never betray the trust of your host, break an oath, or violate the sanctity of another's home.
- Busy hands make a happy home, and things crafted with love will serve you and others well.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve as the quiet caretakers and nurturers of halfling society -- an overlooked but sorely-missed
  role.
- Defend the home from outside threats and cultivate the familial bonds of those who dwell within.
- Oversee the drawing up of contracts and agreements of all kinds, and educate young halflings.
- The priesthood is homely and prosaic, not an adventuring one.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hearthday | First day of each month | Devout halflings gather in the home of a fellow parishioner, rotating to a different dwelling each month. Cyrrollalee asks for nothing beyond simple prayers requesting her blessing; friendship among worshipers is the highest praise offered to her. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No affiliated knightly orders -- the church relies on militant priests and warriors of Yondalla and
  Arvoreen instead.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Homesteader
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning weapons; favored weapons club & quarterstaff
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Charm, Creation, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Law, Protection,
  Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, Necromantic, Plant, Sun, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (cooking), 1 rank in craft (weaving)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on etiquette; +1 bonus on craft
  (fire-building); +1 bonus on lore (local history); +1 bonus on craft (tailoring)
