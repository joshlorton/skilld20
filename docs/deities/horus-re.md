# Horus-Re (Lord of the Sun, Master of Vengeance, Ruler of Mulhorand, Pharaoh of the Gods)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Horus-Re
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of the Sun, Master of Vengeance, Ruler of Mulhorand, Pharaoh of the Gods
- **Portfolio:** The sun, vengeance, rulership, kings, life
- **Symbol:** A hawk's head wearing a pharaoh's crown, surrounded by a solar circle

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics and specialty priests of Horus-Re receive religion (Mulhorandi), literacy
  (Mulhorandi), and linguistics (Common) as bonus skills. As Mulhorandi, they all also know Mulhorandi
  as their native tongue. All of Horus-Re's clergy must be humans of Mulhorandi extraction, and the
  priesthood is largely hereditary, drawn from the House of Helcaliant (human descendants of the divine
  incarnations of Horus, Re, and Horus-Re).
- **Organization:** A political and religious structure at once, since Mulhorand is a theocracy.
  Horustep III, the pharaoh, is the religious head of the church, though its actual leader is his vizier
  (traditionally the highest-ranking priest of Horus-Re). The priesthood suffers extensive infighting and
  power struggles, and its clergy are the most arrogant nobles of the land, demanding public deference
  from commoners. Specialty priests (sunhawks) make up only about 20% of the clergy and rarely hold
  high rank -- most are too busy adventuring to develop the political skill the hierarchy rewards, and
  the few of chaotic bent (tolerated in memory of Horus's own chaotic past) often clash with the church's
  unchanging order. Temples double as centers of government, built of gleaming white limestone or
  marble and open to the sky.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Avenger
  2. Hawkflyer
  3. Huntlion
  4. Heliant
  5. Illuminator
  6. Lightbringer
  7. Sonrey ("sunray")

### Dogma

- Maat -- justice, honor, order, and righteousness -- is the natural state of the universe; conform to
  it and discourage change, for Mulhorand is eternal.
- Provide leadership, use church property honestly, and guard the person, property, and hallowed places
  of Horus-Re and the pharaoh (the living Horus-Re on earth) with your life.
- Cause the most good through the least harm; make no hasty changes or decisions, for their consequences
  are always suspect.
- Protect the weak, avenge all wrongs, and abide no traitors -- smite them with righteous vengeance.
- Set and his agents are without honor and against the order of things; oppose them without mercy.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve as precepts (provincial governors and city rulers) and administrators, controlling vast tracts
  of land and thousands of slaves; the vizier, traditionally the highest-ranking priest of Horus-Re, is
  Mulhorand's most powerful government office after the pharaoh.
- Engage heavily in court intrigue and political infighting, particularly against the rival clergy of
  Anhur, who priests of Horus-Re blame for many of Mulhorand's past military losses.
- Since the Time of Troubles, work to reorient the highly politicized priesthood toward greater
  religious fervor, spreading the faith and increasing Horus-Re's worshipers across the Realms.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Greeting of the Lord | Every sunrise | A set of welcoming prayers greeting the return of Horus-Re. |
| Zenith Aspirant | Every noon | The most important daily ceremony: a sermon delivered from temple balconies to the gathered public, with rival factions of priests competing to draw the largest crowds. |
| Girding of the Guard | Every evening | A private ceremony in which the clergy pledge to guard Mulhorand through the night, involving the ceremonial girding of weapons. |
| Midsummer's Day | Annual | The most important holiday: nearly all of Mulhorand's government shuts down as the priesthood gathers to sing Horus-Re's praises. |
| Solar Eclipse | Irregular | Seen as a reaffirmation of Horus-Re's eternal rule (the visible corona is his crown). A priest who slays a priest of Set or thwarts a major plot of his during an eclipse receives a limited wish or wish, once in a lifetime. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) The Claws of the Sun and the Ankh -- an order of paladins founded by Horus-Re after the Time
  of Troubles (previously all Mulhorandi paladins served Osiris). Young and expanding quickly, its
  fanatical members care nothing for the day-to-day politics of Mulhorand, spending most of their energy
  battling the Fangs of Set and occasionally sparring with the clergy of Anhur.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Sunhawk
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Mace, staff, staff sling, khopesh, long sword; favored weapon khopesh
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Combat, Elemental (Fire and Air), Law, Sun, Summoning, War
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Divination, Elemental (Earth and Water), Guardian, Animal, Healing,
  Necromantic, Protection, Time, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in etiquette, 1 rank in linguistics (Untheric)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on handle animal (chariot driving)

Only humans of Mulhorandi extraction can be sunhawks.

- **Other Clergy Grants:** Paladins of the Claws of the Sun and the Ankh (an order, not a distinct
  clergy class) may cast test of maat at will, with concentration, while within the Mulhorandi
  pantheon's geographic sphere of influence, in place of their normal detect-evil ability.
