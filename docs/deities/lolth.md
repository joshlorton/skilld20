# Lolth (the Spider Queen, Queen of Spiders, Demon Queen of Spiders, Demon Queen of the Abyss, Queen of the Demonweb Pits, Weaver of Chaos, the Hunted, the Mother of Lusts, Dark Mother of All Drow, Lady of Spiders)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Lolth
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Spider Queen, Queen of Spiders, Demon Queen of Spiders, Demon Queen of the
  Abyss, Queen of the Demonweb Pits, Weaver of Chaos, the Hunted, the Mother of Lusts, Dark Mother of
  All Drow, Lady of Spiders
- **Portfolio:** Spiders, evil, darkness, chaos, assassins, the drow race
- **Symbol:** A black spider with a female drow head at the bottom of the figure -- or, in her
  Zinzerena aspect, a black cloak and short sword

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** Must be drow or chitines (little is known of chitine clergy). All clerics (including
  fighter/clerics), crusaders, and specialty priests of Lolth receive religion (drow), religion
  (elven), reading/writing (drowic), and ancient languages (high drow) as bonus proficiencies -- see
  `drow-priests-general.md`. All clergy -- clerics, crusaders, and specialty priests alike -- also gain
  access to the spells faerie fire and continual faerie fire regardless of class. Of the dark elf
  clergy, 96% are female; the rare male priests (4%) are very seldom permitted by Lolth to rise above
  7th level.
- **Organization:** Noble Houses maintain private temples, and every drow city has at least one large
  open gathering-area for major rituals and calls to war, plus a grand temple used to train priests.
  Inner chambers are reserved for worship (shrouded in darkness but for ritual and spell radiances);
  antechambers host war-councils and business with drow males and outsiders. Temples keep guardian
  spiders (sometimes armored or magical) and black-stone statuettes of the Spider Queen; marble and
  obsidian furnishings, ornate spider-carved braziers, and at least one black stone altar are
  constants. Titles vary from city to city; in Guallidurth, the Temple City of Lolth, the clergy are
  collectively Yom'y-athrins. High-ranking priests are collectively yathtallars; a Matron Mother
  ruling a noble house is Ilharess, and other senior priests are titled Streea'Valsharess (Black
  Widow). Specialty priests are called arachnes. The clergy is 94% dark elves and 6% chitines, and is
  drawn from specialty priests (40%), clerics (30%), fighter/clerics (20%), and crusaders (10%),
  though these fractions vary by city.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order, as used in Guallidurth; titles vary by city):
  1. Noamutha
  2. Khalessa
  3. Kyorla
  4. Alura
  5. Quartha
  6. Talintha
  7. Elamshina
  8. Xundusa

### Dogma

- Fear is as strong as steel, while love and respect are soft, useless feelings that none can lean on.
- All drow who do not worship Lolth must be converted or destroyed.
- All weak and rebellious drow must be weeded out; all who impugn the faith must perish.
- Males or slaves of other races who act independently of Lolth's dictates (and other priests who do
  the same) must be sacrificed to her.
- Faithful whose loyalty is weak must be eliminated.
- Children are to be raised as loyal worshipers, and each family should produce at least one priest
  who serves the Spider Queen better than his or her parents did.
- Arachnids of all sorts are to be revered; anyone who mistreats or kills a spider must die.
- Questioning Lolth's motives or wisdom is a sin; aiding nondrow against drow is a great sin, as is
  ignoring the Spider Queen's commands in favor of love.
- Drow who lose Lolth's favor are given a single chance to redeem themselves -- usually a dangerous or
  difficult mission, though the Spider Queen may instead set no task at all and simply observe. Those
  who willfully fail are destroyed; those who fail through mischance or poor execution are usually
  transformed into driders.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Priests are the rulers, police, judges, juries, and executioners of drow society, wielding power in
  a manner that mirrors Lolth's own cruel and capricious nature.
- Manipulate -- often by brutal force -- their fellow drow into acting as Lolth wishes.
- Pursue and protect the Favor of Lolth above all else, since a priest's standing in death is believed
  to depend on it.
- Treachery and cruelty in Lolth's service are often rewarded, but priests who let personal grudges or
  revenge bring defeat or shame upon their House, clan, city, or band are not looked upon kindly.

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Elf Sacrifice | Nights of the full moon, monthly | Ceremonies sacrificing captured surface elves as a deliberate affront to Sehanine Moonbow, Lolth's hated rival. |
| Rituals of Offering | As needed, whenever Lolth's aid is required | Blood sacrifice (of drow faithful or captured foes) with a spider-shaped ritual knife, or the burning of gems, oils, and incense in braziers; major rituals use eight braziers, one for each of Lolth's legs. ### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Militant Myrlochar (Order of Soul Spiders) -- an elite military order of male crusaders,
  found only in the few cities where Lolth is revered and males are permitted into her priesthood;
  serve the ruling Matron Mothers as agents of destruction, hunting a designated quarry until recalled
  (rare) or destroyed (common).
- (Formal) Handmaidens of the Spider Queen (Daughters of the Yochlol) -- an order of female crusaders
  with no permanent ties to any one city, serving as instruments of Lolth's will whenever an entire
  city must be brought back into line; has assaulted and destroyed whole drow cities at least three
  times in recorded history, and otherwise works in small companies harassing Vhaeraun-protected
  merchant trains and raiding cities of the Masked Lord or Ghaunadaur.
- (Informal, debased) She-Spider Cult -- a Thayan sect that once tried to link the worship of Shar
  with devotion to Lolth; opposed by both goddesses, it degenerated into a criminal gang without
  divine support, though it still stages fake rituals to thrill worshipers and slay foes under the
  guise of sacrifice.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Arachne
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; the book gives no single explicit favored weapon, though it strongly
  associates clerics with adamantite maces and crusaders/arachnes with adamantite short swords and
  long daggers -- later published sources instead give dagger (a spider-shaped ritual dagger) as
  Lolth's favored weapon; list both
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Any (Light, Medium, or Heavy all permitted)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Animal, Chaos, Combat, Elemental, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic,
  Protection, Summoning, Sun (reversed only)
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Creation, Divination, Time, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in etiquette, 1 rank in craft (weaving)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on Handle Animal (spiders); +1 bonus on
  spellcraft

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| 1 | Can communicate with spiders of all kinds, who never harm the priest except at Lolth's own desire |
