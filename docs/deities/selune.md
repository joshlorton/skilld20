# Selûne (Our Lady of Silver, the Moonmaiden, the Night White Lady)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Selûne
- **Titles/Epithets:** Our Lady of Silver, the Moonmaiden, the Night White Lady
- **Portfolio:** Moon, stars, navigation, navigators, wanderers, seekers, good and neutral
  lycanthropes
- **Symbol:** Two darkly beautiful human female eyes surrounded by a circle of seven silver stars

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **Organization:** A diverse hodgepodge of clerics, specialty priests, crusaders, mystics, informally
  blessed individuals, and good-aligned lycanthropes (natural and infected), all cooperating in
  relative -- if rollicking -- peace. Novices are known as the Called. Titles are followed by
  "Priestess/Priest," and higher ranks are followed by "Priestess/Priest of the [temple-specific
  form]." Elite specialty priests are known as silverstars.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Touched
  2. Enstarred
  3. Moonbathed
  4. Silverbrow
  5. Lunar
  6. Initiate
  7. High Initiate

### Dogma

- Let all on whom my light falls be welcome if they desire to be so.
- As the silver moon waxes and wanes, so too does all life.
- Trust in my radiance, and know that all who love and live under my light shall know my blessing.
- Turn to the moon, and I will be your true guide.
- All are to be made welcome in the faith and seen as equal.
- Fellow Selûnites should be aided freely, as if they were one's dearest friends.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Wander Faerûn reaching out to the faithful and to potential worshipers of the moon goddess.
- Tell fortunes for small coin -- with real accuracy, since Selûne herself guides the reading.
- Face lycanthropes fearlessly, winning respect among farmers and other common folk.
- Are generous with healing by the Lady's command, often charging little beyond a meal and a warm
  place to sleep.
- Are encouraged to be self-reliant and humble, yet to make as much success in the world as possible
  while remaining helpful and friendly to the lonely and to decent folk.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Night Stalks | Every full moon and new moon | Open-air dances and prayers under the moonlight; offerings of milk and wine are poured upon a central altar. |
| Conjuring of the Second Moon | Shieldmeet only | Summons the Shards (planetar servitors) to do the bidding of the mortal clergy, often to battle minions of Shar; the Shards always take one mortal priestess with them before departing. |
| Mystery of the Night | Once a year, per priest (mandatory) | Secret spells are cast and the priest lies before Selûne's altar, flying up to spiral around the moon in a trance and speaking with the goddess directly; inflicts 1d12 points of damage from the strain, healed normally. |
| Selûne's Hallowing | Annual, Waterdeep | The High Priestess of the House of the Moon carries the holy replica of the Wand of Four Moons at the head of a parade from the temple to the harbor at moonrise. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Informal) Swords of the Lady ("the Lunatics") -- a fanatic order led by Selûnite crusaders,
  specialty priests, and mystics who act rapidly against threats from Shar and her priesthood.
- (Informal) Oracles of the Moon -- a group of female diviners who worship the Night White Lady.
- Ties to the Harpers.
- No dedicated formal knightly/military order is described beyond the fanatic, informally organized
  Swords of the Lady.

## Specialty Priests

Selûne has two specialty priest tracks: Silverstars (the general priesthood) and, regionally in
Durpar, Estagund, and Var the Golden -- where Selûne is worshiped as Lucha, She Who Guides, as part
of the Adama (the Durparian concept of a world spirit) -- Guides, with a travel- and
direction-focused ability list.

### Silverstars

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon heavy mace
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Heavy (any armor type); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Astral, Combat, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic, Numbers,
  Summoning, Sun, Travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Elemental, Plant, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** None
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on survival (navigation is a survival
  skill action); +1 bonus on lore (astrology)

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first. Where no spell level is given but a class/character level is, use spell level =
that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Gains 30-foot infravision |
| 1 | The moon's hand (a mace) gains +1 to hit and damage in a silverstar's hands and counts as a +1 magical weapon |
| 1 | Not immune to lycanthropy, but if infected by a chaotic good lycanthrope, gains full control over transformations and immunity to the special vulnerability of silver weapons |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Starshine, gaining one additional daily use for every 5 levels beyond 1st (30 mana) |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Moonbeam (50 mana) or Lower/Raise Water (40 mana), choose one per use |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Moonblade (30 mana) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Moon Path (50 mana) or True Seeing (50 mana), choose one per use |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Shooting Stars, up to three per tenday (24 mana) |

### Guides (Lucha)

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons, nets, lassos, and other capture
  weapons that allow capture without harming the victim; favored weapon quarterstaff (a guide's first
  learned weapon must always be the quarterstaff)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Divination, Healing, Necromantic, Protection, Plant, Sun, Travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Elemental, Guardian, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in direction sense
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on survival (navigation is a survival
  skill action)

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first. Where no spell level is given but a class/character level is, use spell level =
that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +3 bonus on survival checks involving direction sense |
| 1 | Immune to maze and to the direction-confusing effects of guards and wards and similar spells or spell-like effects |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Protection from Evil, gaining one additional daily use for every 3 levels beyond 1st (10 mana) |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Locate Object (10 mana) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Locate Creature (40 mana) *or* Moonblade (30 mana), choose one per use, once per day |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Moon Path (50 mana) *or* True Seeing (50 mana), choose one per use, once per day |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Find the Path (60 mana), once per day; a guide's first priority with this ability is to find something that has been lost |
