# Flandal Steelskin (Master of Metal, Lord of Smiths, the Armorer, the Weaponsmith, the Great Steelsmith, the Pyromancer)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Flandal Steelskin
- **Titles/Epithets:** Master of Metal, Lord of Smiths, the Armorer, the Weaponsmith, the Great
  Steelsmith, the Pyromancer
- **Portfolio:** Mining, physical fitness, smithing, metalworking, weaponsmithing, armoring
- **Symbol:** A flaming hammer

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics, cleric/illusionists, and cleric/thieves) and
  specialty priests receive a bonus rank in religion (pantheon).
- **Organization:** Temples are subterranean forges that double as houses of worship and working
  smithies, usually built atop the mines that supply their ore. Clergy is 65% rock gnome, 25% deep
  gnome, 10% forest gnome, and 88% male. Composition is specialty priests (55%), fighter/clerics
  (35%), clerics (7%), cleric/illusionists (2%), and cleric/thieves (1%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. The Unworked (novice)
  2. Tinsmith
  3. Bronzesmith
  4. Brasssmith
  5. Coppersmith
  6. Silversmith
  7. Electrumsmith
  8. Goldsmith
  9. Platinumsmith
  10. Steelsmith (highest common rank; individual high priests hold unique titles)

### Dogma

- The treasures of life were buried within the earth's embrace at Segojan's hand, and only hard
  labor, dedication, and great craftsmanship, as taught by the Master of Metal, can reveal their
  hidden beauties and wonders.
- Dig mines, extract ores, and forge suits of armor, weapons, and other items of metal.
- Strive to refine known techniques of metalworking, invent new processes for tempering and
  refining it, and discover new alloys and test their potentials.
- Stay physically fit, to pursue the rigors of the forge and mine with your whole heart.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Continuously hone mining and smithing skills, seeking intuitive understanding of earth and stone.
- Teach other gnomes to detect veins of ore, unsafe environments, and hostile creatures.
- Oversee the safety of gnome miners and inspect the output of gnome smithies.
- Undergo regular strength and stamina training to stay fit for mining, smithing, or battle.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The High Forge | Midsummer's Day (annual) | The faithful offer forged metal weapons to the god and sing rhythmic hammer hymns culminating in a period of utter silence at midday; the afternoon and evening are given over to exchanging techniques, exhibiting fine wares, and trading with merchants who gather for the occasion. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Fellowship of Steel -- fighters, clerics, fighter/clerics, and specialty priests who
  defend the mines of the Forgotten Folk, guarding miners and clearing tunnels of danger before
  gnomes begin work there.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Pyrosmith
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any metal-bladed or metal-headed weapon
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Heavy (any metal armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Creation, Divination, Elemental (Earth, Fire), Guardian, Protection,
  Sun, Time
- **Minor Spheres:** Elemental (Air, Water), Healing, War
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in war hammer, 1 rank in craft (armorer) or craft (weaponsmithing)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on craft (blacksmithing); +1 bonus on
  endurance; +1 bonus on craft (mining)

## Flandalian Spells (deity-specific)

Priests of the Master of Metal may also cast detect metals and minerals (1st level, detailed under
Geb in *Powers & Pantheons*), added to the standard priest spell list rather than granted as a free
casting. Members of Flandal's clergy have also developed fire-based variants of many divination
spells (a subspecialty called pyromancy), typically substituting a flickering flame for the usual
divinatory medium.

| Name | Est. Mana | Foundation | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steelskin | 10 | Protection | Transforms the recipient's hide into an alloy of flesh and steel, granting a growing Armor Class bonus with caster level at the cost of reducing their Dexterity to two-thirds normal. |
| Metal Shape | 30 | Elemental Earth | Lets the caster reshape an existing piece of nonmagical metal into any rough form -- a crude weapon, trapdoor, or idol -- and halves the time and cost to finish it if worked further by a smith afterward. |
| Shades of Rhondang | 40 | Elemental Fire | Wreathes the caster's own hammer in flame, temporarily granting it the properties of a flame tongue weapon; ends if the caster releases their grip, is knocked unconscious, or is slain. |
