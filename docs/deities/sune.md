# Sune (Firehair, Lady Firehair, the Lady of Love, the Princess of Passion)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Sune
- **Titles/Epithets:** Firehair, Lady Firehair, the Lady of Love, the Princess of Passion
- **Portfolio:** Beauty, love, passion
- **Symbol:** The face of a beautiful, ivory-skinned human maiden with long, red tresses

### Symbol -- Midjourney prompt

```
heraldic emblem, the face of a strikingly beautiful ivory-skinned maiden in profile, long flowing red tresses that curl into flame-like tips at the ends, painted heraldry style, warm crimson and gold palette, soft romantic lighting, symmetrical composition, holy symbol, high detail --ar 1:1
```

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** Loose and informal; leadership changes regularly with the whims of the clergy,
  and the most charismatic priests and priestesses tend to rise to the top. A priest dropping
  everything to chase something beautiful causes little scandal.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** No formal ranks are named in the source; standing within the
  church follows personal charisma and renown rather than seniority.

### Dogma

- Love none more than yourself save Sune, and lose yourself in love of the Lady Firehair.
- Perform a loving act every day, and seek to awaken love in someone new each day.
- Respond to love at least once in a day.
- Encourage beauty wherever you find it; acquire beautiful items, and encourage, sponsor, and
  protect the artists who produce them.
- Keep your own body as comely as possible and as attractively displayed as situations warrant; let
  hairstyle and clothing best suit your appearance.
- Do not hide away -- always present yourself in a pleasing variety of garbs and activities so as to
  move others with love and desire.
- Love those who respond to your beauty and to all beauty, and let warm friendship and admiration
  flower where love cannot or dare not.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Buy beautiful items of art, sculpture, and handiwork whenever found, sponsoring good artists and
  overpaying so as to drive up prices, demand, and ultimately the supply of beauty in the world.
- Use disguise when performing unsavory tasks, both to protect the body from harm and to conceal
  identity.
- Hire or support adventurers and others to destroy those who vandalize beautiful creations.
- Personally create beauty, preferably as a fine artist (glasswork, painting, tapestry) or, failing
  that, as a dancer; train others for free once skilled, passing any training fees to the church.
- Build friendships and romantic feelings wherever they go, seeking out the lonely in particular.
- Assist anyone, however homely or of another faith, with gifts of clothing, hair styling, cosmetics,
  and lessons in deportment, dancing, and manners.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Greengrass | Annual | Celebrated with a great deal of outdoor frolicking. |
| Midsummer Night | Annual | Night-long flirtatious chases through forests and parks. |
| Grand Revel | At least once a month | A dusk-to-dawn party open to outsiders; dancing and minstrelsy dominate as the temple seeks converts by showing off beautiful art or magical treasures. |
| Feast of Love | At least once a tenday | An intimate affair open only to the faithful: couches, liqueurs, savory bitelets, sweet pastries, solo dancers, and readings of romantic verse and prose. |
| Candle Vigil | As needed | A night-long personal ritual of prayer for guidance, entry into the faith, or atonement, performed in beautiful ritual garments while standing in a pool or bath and looking into a candle- or moonlit mirror. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* A shifting array of honorary titular orders that change with every new high priest or
  priestess, conveying honors for excellence in various artistic pursuits.
- *(Formal)* Sisters and Brothers of the Ruby Rose -- a small knightly order of fighters, paladins,
  and bards who guard temples and holy sites and sometimes quest in Sune's name. Candidates are
  admitted after standing vigil all night in a church of Sune and receiving a vision or sign of her
  favor. Members write essays and songs of courtly love and often adopt a beautiful individual to
  adore from afar.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Heartwarder
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon whip
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Creation, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Protection, Sun,
  Time, Thought
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, Elemental, Necromantic, Summoning
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in performance (art, singing, or dance -- pick one focus)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon) and etiquette checks; +1 bonus on diplomacy
  checks made to charm, seduce, or inspire admiration

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.
