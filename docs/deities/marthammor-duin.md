# Marthammor Duin (Finder-of-Trails, the Watcher over Wanderers, the Watchful Eye, the Hammer, the Finder, the Wanderer)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Marthammor Duin
- **Titles/Epithets:** Finder-of-Trails, the Watcher over Wanderers, the Watchful Eye, the Hammer, the
  Finder, the Wanderer
- **Portfolio:** Guide and protector to dwarven adventurers, explorers, expatriates, travelers, and
  wanderers, lightning
- **Symbol:** An upright mace over a single fur-trimmed leather boot, toe to the right -- or, in an
  older form, a mace held in gauntlets

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders)
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, crusaders, and specialty priests of Marthammor receive the standard
  dwarven clergy grants (literacy (Dethek runes), modified turn undead) -- see
  `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** Marthammor's clergy has shifted dramatically toward specialty priests (85%), with
  clerics, fighter/clerics, and crusaders splitting the remaining 15% evenly. The priesthood draws
  almost entirely on shield dwarves (96%) and is 81% male, though the number of women is rising quickly
  -- Marthammor broke with the old same-gender-as-deity tradition even before the Time of Troubles.
  Worship happens on the bare heights of stony tors on moonless nights, or (for holy days and important
  rituals) in unaltered natural caverns; priests pray before a simple stone-cairn or wooden-tripod
  altar bearing a stone hammer, head uppermost, and the god places visions in their minds guiding who
  guards temples, who explores which regions, and so on.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Lost (novice)
  2. Sun Seeker
  3. Far Wanderer
  4. Trail Finder
  5. Vigilant Guardian
  6. Stalwart Protector
  7. Valiant Hammer (full priests collectively: Watchful Eyes)

  High Old Ones hold unique individual titles with no collective name. Specialty priests are known as
  trailblazers.

### Dogma

- If the Children of Moradin are to survive as a race, they must adapt, grow, and learn to dwell in
  harmony with other good races, particularly humans.
- The Stout Folk must be encouraged to emerge from the illusory safety of their hidden delves and find
  true security in fellowship with humankind and demihumankind.
- Help fellow wanderers and sojourners in the world, giving all that is needful.
- Guide those who are lost and guard those who are defenseless.
- Seek out new ways and new paths, and discover the wide world in your wanderings.
- Herald the way of newfound hope.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Mark trails in the wilderness northlands of the Realms, from Uttersea to the Great Ice Sea, and
  establish way-caches of food and supplies (boots, clothing, weapons, water, bandages, splints,
  firemaking supplies, and the like) along them.
- Patrol these ways, healing and guiding dwarves they meet, providing a warm fire, a hot meal, and
  friendly companionship to exhausted, lonely, lost, or hurt dwarves of any faith or race.
- Work with healers and priests of all races to help dwarves and their allies and companions; though
  they do not formally accompany adventurers, they in a sense adventure themselves, facing the same
  perils.
- Travel in small bands (often 3d4 priests), especially along the northern Sword Coast, revealing their
  clerical status only when dealing with dwarves or known dwarven allies.

*Every festival day of the Calendar of Harptos, and the ninth day following it, is holy to Marthammor;
on most of these days (and at least once a year for every worshiper) the faithful burn used ironwork
and dwarf-made footwear in homage to the Watcher.*

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Knights of the North Star -- a widely dispersed order of Marthammoran priests who
  individually join adventuring companies of humans and demihumans in the North, aiming to ease the
  integration of Wanderer dwarves into other societies and to steer such companies toward goals shared
  by dwarves in general. Each knight must report, orally or in writing, to the nearest Marthammoran
  enclave at least once a year.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Trailblazer
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Bludgeoning weapons only; favored weapon mace
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Combat, Creation, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Protection, Sun,
  Travelers, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Charm, Elemental Earth, Necromantic, Plant, Summoning
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in: endurance or survival (mountains)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on survival (mountains) -- direction
  sense and tracking are survival skill actions; +1 bonus on lore (local history: neighboring human
  communities)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)
