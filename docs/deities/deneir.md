# Deneir (Lord of All Glyphs and Images, the Scribes of Oghma)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Deneir
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of All Glyphs and Images, the Scribes of Oghma
- **Portfolio:** Glyphs, images, literature, literacy, scribes, pictorial and literary art, cartography
- **Symbol:** A single candle set above a staring purple human eye with a triangular pupil, or simply a single lit candle

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, monastic orders
- **All Clergy:** Required to take reading/writing as a nonweapon proficiency, regardless of class.
- **Organization:** Clergy are collectively known as Deneirrath. Only about 15% are specialty
  priests (Glyphscribes), but Glyphscribes hold every high position in the church hierarchy; clerics
  and monks are welcome but rarely lead temples. Monks may head an abbey or monastery but always
  rank beneath that community's temple high priest. The title "Priest" is used regardless of gender.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order, denoted by cloak color):
  1. Applicant
  2. Underpriest
  3. Aspirant Priest
  4. Full Priest
  5. Priest Illuminator
  6. Priest Calligrapher
  7. Priest Editor
  8. Priest Secretary
  9. Priest Librarian
  10. Aspirant Scrivener
  11. Full Scrivener
  12. High Scrivener (leader of a temple)
  13. Writer Inquisitor, Librarian, and High Librarian -- ranks above High Scrivener, granted only personally by Deneir himself

### Dogma

- Information that is not recorded and saved is information that is lost.
- Anyone who defaces or destroys a book should be punished in proportion to the value of the
  information lost, unless they make full replacement of it.
- Deneirrath must write everything down and fill every idle hour copying what is written.
- Bring copies of every writing gathered to every temple of Deneir visited (or make copies there) so
  knowledge may spread and nothing be lost.
- Information should be free to all, so that lying tongues cannot distort things out of proportion.
- Write down all that people say, believe, and observe, leaving others to judge what is true, of
  value, and proper.
- Learn to read and write, and teach at least ten non-worshipers to do so as well.
- Uphold an oath of charity: never refuse another's request to write letters or transcribe
  information; take no payment from the needy, but a fair rate from those who can afford it.
- Keep secret any confidence exposed to in the course of charitable scribing duties.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Gather and write down -- or copy out if already written -- knowledge and creative works (fiction,
  poems, songs, witticisms, diaries) so that nothing is lost.
- Some never leave a temple's copying cloisters; others travel collecting half-remembered ballads,
  run lending libraries, or delve into ruins and tombs for lost writings.
- Provide charitable scribing: free for the poor, a single silver piece plus paper/ink cost for
  commoners, and market rates for the wealthy (as much as 4 gp a page for illuminated writing).
- The Price of the Silent Scribe: a steep fee charged for writing performed in strictest confidence,
  split one-third to the priest and two-thirds to the nearest temple.
- Maintain secret libraries of forbidden and magical writings, guarded by spells and monsters
  ranging from watchspiders to watchghosts; senior priests secretly copy spells and make scrolls for
  sale to adventurers, nobles, and wealthy merchants.
- Teach literacy to the public for nominal fees, but charge stiff fees to train scribes, limners,
  illuminators, bookbinders, and parchmentmakers.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shieldmeet | Shieldmeet (quadrennial) | Copies of old contracts are displayed publicly, and any mortal may request to see a copy of any nonmagical writing in a temple of Deneir they can travel to (specific requests only, and nothing violating a vow of confidentiality). |
| The Gilding | Shieldmeet | A rite in which priests circle a levitated manuscript and cast spells that impress golden letters onto the page, setting forth the Words of Deneir for public display. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Literate Brotherhood -- all temple scribes certified skilled and trustworthy by the
  church; members wear a pin or badge of a white quill with a gold nib.
- (Informal) Preservers of the Ordered Way -- a cloistered monastic order performing copying and
  illuminating work.
- (Informal) Disciples of the Free Word ("Pens of the Poor") -- pursues the church's mission of
  scribing for the indigent with great vigor.
- (Informal) Zealots of the Written Word ("Zealots" or Carmendines) -- an adventuring order that
  accompanies priests on holy quests and pursues money-making ventures for the church.
- Deneir has no militant knightly orders. Alongside Mystra and other powers, he has influence over
  the Harpers; his church in Berdusk (Twilight Hall) is a front for an extensive Harper organization.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Glyphscribe
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All one-handed simple weapons; favored weapon dagger
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Heavy (up to and including banded mail); no shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Combat, Creation, Divination, Elemental, Guardian, Healing,
  Protection, Summoning, Thought
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Plant, Sun, Time, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in linguistics (Common)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on linguistics (any language); +1
  bonus on craft (calligraphy)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first. Where no spell level is given but a class/character level is, use spell level =
that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +4 bonus on saves vs. damage or malicious effects from reading, touching, or being near books, scrolls, runes, or magical writing (including curses and mishandled magical tomes); if no save is normally allowed, a normal unmodified save is granted instead |
| 1 | Can speak any language spoken by natives of Toril and Realmspace, up to as many tongues held permanently in mind as points of Intelligence; unlearned tongues are usable only while conversing face-to-face with a speaker of that tongue |
| 1 | Can attempt to identify a ward or runic symbol via two Intelligence checks: the first reveals the type of being who left it, the second its general meaning |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Glyph of Revealing (20 mana) *or* Erase (10 mana), choose one per casting |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Glyph of Warding (30 mana), limited to wards of 50 square feet or less, taking five rounds to cast regardless of area |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Amanuensis *or* Dispel Magic, choose one per casting (30 mana) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Speak with Dead (30 mana), Speak with Plants (40 mana), *or* Speak with Monsters (60 mana), choose one per casting, up to 4 uses/day total |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Symbol (70 mana) |
