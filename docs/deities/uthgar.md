# Uthgar (Father of the Uthgardt, Battle Father, Honor Father, Master of the Beast Virtues, Son of Beorunna, Son of Tempus)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Uthgar
- **Titles/Epithets:** Father of the Uthgardt, Battle Father, Honor Father, Master of the Beast Virtues,
  Son of Beorunna, Son of Tempus
- **Portfolio:** The Uthgardt barbarian tribes, physical strength
- **Symbol:** None -- the symbol of a worshiper's beast totem cult (Black Lion, Black Raven, Blue Bear,
  Elk, Gray Wolf, Great Worm, Griffon, Red Tiger/Snow Cat, Sky Pony, Thunderbeast, or Tree Ghost) always
  serves to represent him instead.

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All shamans of Uthgar receive religion (Faerunian) as a bonus skill.
- **Organization:** Uthgar has no formal church. Each Uthgardt tribe follows one of the primeval beast
  totems Uthgar mastered and drew power from, and worship blends veneration of Uthgar, his sons, and the
  tribe's long-dead chieftains and shamans with veneration of the totem itself. Uthgardt shamans hold
  titles that vary widely from tribe to tribe, and are insular loners who rarely cooperate even with
  shamans of other Uthgardt tribes, let alone outsiders. Uthgar has neither temples nor shrines in the
  ordinary sense -- ceremonies are held wherever necessary, though each tribe's ancestor mound (a huge
  turf-covered earthwork, often shaped like the tribe's totem beast, ringed by burial cairns) is Uthgar's
  most holy site and the place his people return to each fall to winter near their ancestors.

### Dogma

- Strength is everything; civilization is weakness.
- Men should fight, hunt, and raid from the weak to provide for their wives and families.
- Family is sacred, and its bonds are not cast aside lightly.
- Magic that does not come from the gods is effete, self-indulgent, and ultimately leads to weakness --
  shun reliance on secular magic as evil and a false path leading only to death and ruin.
- Revere Uthgar, Beorunna, the Uthgardt ancestors, and the beast spirit that guides your tribe.
- Study your tribe's beast so that you know its virtues and weaknesses; claim its virtues as your own,
  and weed its weaknesses from your spirit.
- Make others of the tribe fear and respect your power and knowledge, so they heed the wisdom your
  ancestors speak in days of trouble.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Tend to the tribe's needs: teach tribal history and customs through oral tradition, heal the sick and
  injured, and counsel the chieftain and elders.
- Initiate youths into adulthood once they complete their tribal quest, usually a mission against the
  tribe's ritual enemy.
- Consult ancestral spirits and the tribe's totem great spirit for guidance whenever the tribe faces a
  new situation or a quandary.
- Officiate disputes settled by tests of strength or ritual combat (to first blood, to surrender, or to
  the death, depending on the matter's severity), since personal strength is believed to demonstrate
  purity of purpose.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Birthing | Spring equinox | A day and night of fasting, paired with a vision quest communing with Uthgar and the tribal spirits for guidance in the months ahead. |
| The Fullness | Summer solstice | As the Birthing -- a day and night of fasting and a guiding vision quest. |
| The Darking | Winter solstice | As the Birthing -- a day and night of fasting and a guiding vision quest. |
| Runemeet | Autumnal equinox, during Eleint (coinciding roughly with Higharvestide) | The holiest time of year: all of a tribe's clans converge at its ancestral mound to worship, set policy, marry, celebrate births, formalize adoptions, and mourn the dead. Shamans establish the mound's spirit guardians for the coming year and stabilize its gate to the Astral Plane. Youths and warriors undertake the Runehunt (a ritual victory over the tribe's ritual enemy, usually orcs); those who succeed are inducted into adulthood in a ceremony called the Telhut. |

### Affiliated Orders

- None formal -- the Uthgardt tribes have no warrior or knightly orders, and are not regimented enough in
  outlook to maintain honorary ones. Rank within a tribe instead follows a rough pecking order of
  strength, battle prowess, and raid plunder.
- *(Informal)* Individual tribes maintain loose, tribe-specific ties to outside groups -- the Harpers, the
  church of Malar, lower-planar fiends, or particular figures such as Alustriel of Silverymoon -- with no
  faith-wide pattern to them.

## Specialty Priests

All of Uthgar's specialty priests are beast-cult shamans; their alignment, extra sphere access, and
unique granted Beast Power all depend on which of the eleven surviving totems their tribe follows (see
Totem Variants below). Two further totems -- Golden Eagle and Red Pony -- belonged to tribes long extinct
and have no living shamans or stats given in the source.

- **Name/Title:** Beast-Cult Shaman
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Hand axe, knife, spear, battle axe, atlatl and javelin, long bow; favored
  weapon battleaxe
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light and Medium (up to chain mail); shields permitted
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Summoning, Protection, Travelers, Wards (plus totem-specific spheres,
  see below)
- **Minor Spheres:** Healing, Plant (plus totem-specific spheres, see below)
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in survival (hunting), 1 rank in lore (weather), 1 rank in survival
  (trapping/set snares), 1 rank in survival (fire-building)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on endurance; +1 bonus on survival
  (arctic); +1 bonus on survival (tracking)

Uthgardt shamans must be Uthgardt humans (nonhumans only by direct divine intervention). Uthgardt
tradition rarely permits women to become shamans of Uthgar or the beast totems (women exclusively serve
as shamans of Chauntea among the Uthgardt); female Uthgardt shamans exist only at the DM's discretion.
