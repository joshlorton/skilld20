# Kelemvor (Lord of the Dead, Judge of the Damned, Master of the Crystal Spire)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Kelemvor
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of the Dead, Judge of the Damned, Master of the Crystal Spire
- **Portfolio:** Death, the dead
- **Symbol:** An upright, bone-colored skeletal arm and fist holding the golden scales of justice
  balanced evenly, against a steel-gray field

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** A young church (newly formed after the Time of Troubles), still assembling its
  identity. Ordinary clerics comfort the dying, administer last rites, and handle funerals and burials;
  doomguides anticipate where death will occur to direct the other clergy, enforce discipline (chiefly
  against clerical sentimentality that would prolong life), and handle fundraising. Many former
  Myrkulyte (and some former Cyricist) temples have converted wholesale to Kelemvor's worship.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** None established yet -- the source explicitly states the church
  has not existed long enough to develop even an informal consensus on rank titles.

### Dogma

- Death is but part of life: fear it not, evade it not, view it not as evil.
- To fear death delivers you into the hands of those who can bring death down upon you.
- Die with dignity, neither raging nor seeking to embrace undeath.
- Do honor to the dead, for their strivings in life brought Faerûn to where it is now.
- It is Kelemvor's will that no sentient being die a natural death without one of his priests at their
  side.
- Do not seek to rush death, nor to artificially prolong life beyond its natural limits -- including
  through undead creations such as liches.
- Undeath is an affront to Kelemvor; destroy or grant true rest to undead creatures whenever they are
  encountered.
- Death is not a final ending but the next step in an ongoing journey. Kelemvor is the Great Guide who
  awaits all -- he is not to be feared, for he believes in justice and wields mercy.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Comfort the dying, administer last rites, and assist in funerals, burials, and the orderly settling of
  a deceased's affairs.
- Place warning marks of plague and other disease.
- Ensure the will or expressed wishes of the deceased are followed.
- When someone dies alone without a will, known heirs, or business partners, the church claims their
  goods to fund its ongoing ministry to the dying -- never for clergy's personal benefit.
- Fight plagues, hordes, and monstrous predators that would kill many before their time; try to interest
  adventuring bands in the problem first, but deal with it directly if none can be found.
- Bring death as swiftly and painlessly as possible to those in great pain, ravaged by disease, or
  mutilated where death would be a mercy -- the sole office of Kelemvor's priests.
- Seek out and destroy or give true death to undead creatures, hiring or accepting help from other
  faiths as needed to stamp out the "great sin of undeath."
- Answer holy missions from the god or senior clergy, defending church members and holdings and bringing
  death to others when necessary.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Passing | Personal, at the moment of death | A simple chant of comfort, performed by a priest or priests, calling on Kelemvor to be alert for the coming essence of the deceased. |
| The Lament for the Fallen | As needed, after mass-casualty events | A larger version of the Passing, sung over a battlefield, ruined village or fortress, or other site where many have recently died. |
| The Remembrance | Daily, each morning | A dignified rite of songs and prayer led over graves, usually attended by relatives of the dead. |
| The Daeum (Thanks to the Guide) | Daily, after nightfall | A celebration of the Great Guide's strength and purpose, attended only by the faithful; the goods of the dead are distributed and holy missions dispensed at its close. |
| Shieldmeet | Shieldmeet (quadrennial) | One of the church's two great calendar-fixed holy days. Priests recount the Deeds of the Dead, and any priest casting speak with dead may converse freely with the departed for as long as desired rather than being limited to yes/no questions. |
| Feast of the Moon | Annual | The other great calendar-fixed holy day, observed with the same rites as Shieldmeet. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Most Solemn Order of the Silent Shroud -- gravediggers, embalmers, and other cemetery
  workers and crafters who serve the church without being clergy themselves; members recognize one
  another by secret signs and report any sign of undead activity or grave desecration to the church.
- *(Formal, proposed but not yet founded)* Knights of Eternal Order -- a holy order of crusaders and
  paladins to target fearsome undead creatures, seriously under consideration by the church; the name
  itself is only tentative and still being debated by church scholars.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Doomguide
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning and piercing weapons, sickles, and scythes (no other
  slashing weapons); favored weapon bastard sword ("Fatal Touch"), always usable despite the
  no-slashing restriction
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Medium armor; no shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Combat, Healing, Law, Necromantic, Summoning, Time, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Divination, Elemental, Guardian, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** None
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on languages (pick two); +1 bonus on
  lore (the dead/undead)
