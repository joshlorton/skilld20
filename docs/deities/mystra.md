# Mystra (the Lady of Mysteries, the Mother of All Magic, Our Lady of Spells)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Mystra
- **Titles/Epithets:** the Lady of Mysteries, the Mother of All Magic, Our Lady of Spells
- **Portfolio:** Magic, spells, the weave
- **Symbol:** A circle of stars with a red mist rising toward (or flowing from) the center (older
  symbol, still in common use: a single blue-white star)

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters (predominantly arcane-tradition, reflecting her portfolio as goddess of
  magic itself), bardic performers
- **Organization:** A wide, varied hierarchy separated into orders focused on one form of magical
  energy or another; clerics, specialty priests, wizards, and bards mix freely without regard to
  experience level or origin, and talent for the job outweighs social rank. Relations between orders
  and subgroups are very good. Priests of Mystra are called Servants of Mystery; standing as a place
  sacred to the goddess lets Mystran priests cast spells at maximum possible effect.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order; titles vary further by individual temple):
  1. Servant of Mystery
  2. Lady/Lord of Mystery (higher-level clergy, both landed and legendary adventuring priests)

### Dogma

- Love magic for itself, not as a ready weapon to reshape the Realms to your will.
- Learn when not to use your magic -- that is the path to true wisdom.
- Play with magic and learn how best to wield it, but never at a price paid by others.
- Strive to use magic less and less as your powers develop; the threat and promise of the Art often
  outstrips its actual performance.
- Remember that magic is an Art, the Gift of the Lady, and that its wielders are privileged in the
  extreme -- conduct yourself humbly, not proudly.
- Use magic deftly and efficiently; eschew carelessness and recklessness in unleashing the Art.
- When magic imperils you, hide it or hurl it away into other planes rather than destroy it -- any
  destruction of the Art is a sin.
- Seek always to learn new magic and to create new magic; experimenting to craft something yourself
  is better than merely buying scrolls or hiring tutors.
- Exult more in creation than in hurling spells, and ensure your creations are shared with others so
  they outlive you.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Work hard to preserve all magical lore in secret libraries, private safeholds, well-guarded research
  laboratories, and small hidden stashes.
- Search out beings skilled in spell use, keeping watch on the identities, powers, and behavior of
  individuals likely to become important magic-wielders.
- Devise entirely new magic upon gaining sufficient experience -- all clergy are expected to do so, so
  that magical study remains a growing, vibrant thing rather than a mere tool.
- Sacrifice/tithe: burning items that have been temporarily enchanted with a spell is the easiest and
  most common way to appease the goddess.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gods' Day | 15th of Marpenoth (annual) | Celebrated in Waterdeep as the anniversary of Midnight's elevation to divinity as the new Mystra; a huge festival centered on the House of Wonder ending in magical fireworks; gradually spreading to shrines and temples throughout Faerûn. |
| Starflight | Personal (initiation or wedding ceremony) | Cooperative magic worked by several priests that empowers one of the faithful to fly for as long as stars are visible in the sky. |
| Magefire | Personal (renewal ceremony, performed as needed) | Cooperative healing magic described by Mystrans as "the most blissful feeling one can know"; often ends in a Starflight ceremony. |
| Hymn to the Lady | Funerals and magemoots | A solemn plainsong dirge that calls up visions of dead mages and Mystran clergy; may end in a modified Magefire that raises the honored dead aloft on a floating pyre. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Informal) Those Who Harp (the Harpers) -- the church of Mystra has close ties to this organization,
  which works for good against the rise of great powers.
- (Informal) Children of the Starry Quill -- non-clergy bards who act as information gatherers and
  rumormongers, or research magical knowledge in designated libraries; some are also Harpers.
- (Formal) Knights of the Mystic Fire -- an order of paladins granted their spells directly by Mystra;
  they escort clergy on quests for lost magic and lead the guard forces at Mystra's larger temples.
- (Formal) Order of the Shooting Star -- an order of rangers granted their spells directly by Mystra;
  they serve as long-range scouts and spies, and deal with magical threats to the natural order.
