# Ibrandul (Lord of the Dry Depths, the Skulking God, Lurker in Darkness)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Ibrandul
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of the Dry Depths, the Skulking God, Lurker in Darkness
- **Portfolio:** Caverns, dungeons, the Underdark, skulks
- **Symbol:** Four interlocking silver circles on a dark purple background

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** Since Shar secretly usurped Ibrandul's portfolio and now impersonates him, she
  makes all new initiates specialty priests (Darkwalkers); roughly 20% of the clergy are older-line
  clerics who predate the takeover. Priests tend to roam the Underdark alone (leading to a high
  mortality rate among low-level clergy), though the wiser ones stick with groups until better
  established. Personal titles are common and become official once recognized by a temple's
  Impenetrable Lurker.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Child of Ibrandul (novice, not yet initiated; addressed as "child")
  2. Lurker (initiated priest)
  3. Mysterious Lurker (senior priest, above 5th level)
  4. Impenetrable Lurker (leader of a temple)

### Dogma

- The Underdark is every bit as vital as the surface world, and darkness is its greatest redeeming
  quality.
- In a world without light there is no tedious, inescapable march of day and night, and no end to the
  variety of shapes and textures to experience tactilely.
- Nothing is good or evil in the dark unless you consider it so; such value judgments are frivolous.
- "There is perfect freedom in perfect darkness: independence, individuality, liberty from the
  judgment of others."
- Ibrandul protects and guides his followers in the dark ways, drives away those who would harm his
  children, and reveals great treasures to those who venture into the depths.
- Remain steadfast to him, and he will stand by you.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Proselytize among adventurers and the poor folk of cities.
- Build temple fortresses deep in the Underdark to serve as sanctuaries for those who venture below.
- Offer protection to those who must hide underground (from justice, enemies, plague, weather, or
  attack) in exchange for regular rental payments for a bed, food, a niche in the temple, and
  Ibrandul's favor.
- Wander the Underdark as adventurers or as aides to adventuring parties, persuading everyone met to
  remain below the surface and acknowledge Ibrandul as their defender while underground.
- Tithe: each time a priest gains a level, they throw a tenth of their total wealth into a deep pit,
  where it is meant to remain unrecoverable forever.
- All priests and casual followers throw a copper piece into any pit they cross as thanks for safely
  crossing it, and into any nearby hole or crevice whenever they believe they have witnessed a sign
  from Ibrandul.
- When turning undead, clerics of Ibrandul do not use a holy symbol -- instead they grasp a handful of
  earth or gravel and let it trickle through their fists.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Elah'ranak (Protective Armor of Darkness) | Upon reaching 5th level (individual rite, not calendar-fixed) | Secret ritual involving ingestion of ibrandlin blood, conducted by an Impenetrable Lurker; grants base AC 5 but a permanent loss of 1d3 Charisma. |
| The Foreshadowing | Cloud-covered nights with a new moon, beginning at midnight | Celebrates Ibrandul's promise to eventually envelop all the land above and below in darkness. |
| Deliverance Unto Darkness | Midwinter's Eve | Thanks the Lurker in Darkness for delivery from the dark elves; involves sacrifice of a monstrous spider or other drow/Lolth-associated being and the casting of dark path spells; long chains of the faithful then wander the Underdark without light, trusting Ibrandul to guide them. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Shadows of the Night -- a sinister band of skulks based in Calimshan's Underdark, bolder
  than most of their cowardly kin, believed to be led by some powerful evil creature (perhaps a
  renegade drow or illithilich).
- *(Informal)* Knights Fallen -- a mixed band of priests, rogues, and warriors who stalk the northern
  Underdark, seeking out those lost in the darkness and delivering them to safety for a significant
  fee; believed based in the Deep Temple of Dark Hope, with bands ranging as far as Anauroch and Amn.
- *(Informal)* Horgardin -- an itinerant band of wanderers of various races led by a half-drow gray
  druid of Ibrandul, following a twisting Underdark path said to have been carved by an ancient
  horgar under Ibrandul's control.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Darkwalker
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All weapons; favored weapon greatclub
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Medium armor; no shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, elemental, guardian, protection, sun (only the darkness-creating versions of
  reversible spells), travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Chaos, combat, creation, divination, healing, necromantic
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in handle animal (ibrandlin), 1 rank in craft (mining)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on blind-fighting; +1 bonus on survival
  (Underdark terrain) -- direction sense is a survival skill action; +1 bonus on stealth (underground,
  no-light conditions)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is (e.g. "at 12th level, priests gain the ability to..."), use
spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Casts all darkness-related spells (and darkness-related spell-like abilities) with twice the normal area of effect and duration |
| 1 | Granted 30-foot infravision (or a 10-foot extension if infravision is already possessed) |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Darkness *or* Dark Path, choose one per use (10 mana) |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Continual Darkness (30 mana) |
| 5 | Undergoes the elah'ranak ceremony: base AC 5, permanent loss of 1d3 Charisma |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Blacklight *or* Meld into Stone, choose one per use (30 mana) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Stone Shape (50 mana) *or* Skulk (40 mana), choose one per use |
| 10 | Eyes fully adapted to darkness: sees in absolute darkness as if in daylight, but suffers a -1 penalty to saves/attacks in twilight and a -4 penalty to AC, saves, and attacks in daylight or equivalent light |
