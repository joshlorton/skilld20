# Abbathor (Great Master of Greed, Trove Lord, the Avaricious, Wyrm of Avarice)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Abbathor
- **Titles/Epithets:** Great Master of Greed, Trove Lord, the Avaricious, Wyrm of Avarice
- **Portfolio:** Greed
- **Symbol:** A gold coin at least two inches across, stamped on both faces with Abbathor's sigil of a
  jeweled dagger

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Rogues
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including cleric/thieves) and specialty priests of Abbathor receive the
  standard dwarven clergy grants (literacy (Dethek runes), modified turn undead) -- see
  `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** Publicly reviled ("gone to Abbathor" is dwarven slang for lost treasure) yet
  quietly acknowledged as a legitimate member of the Morndinsamman, since even dwarves who decry his
  creed recognize the streak of avarice in their own race. Temples are always hidden underground --
  gold-leafed caverns doubling as treasure vaults, most sacred when built inside a plundered dragon's
  hoard-cave. The priesthood is nearly evenly split between specialty priests (35%), cleric/thieves
  (33%), and plain thieves (32%), with clerics a small remainder (10%); by subrace it runs gold dwarf
  (50%), shield dwarf (40%), gray dwarf (9%), and jungle dwarf (1%). Male priests still dominate (97%).
  Abbathor's Commandment binds all clergy: no priest of Abbathor may steal from, or scheme against,
  another dwarf -- a rule enacted only after angry dwarves once slaughtered thief after thief wearing
  Abbathor's scarlet.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Goldseeker (novice)
  2. Hand of Greed (full priest)
  3. Coveter of Copper
  4. Seeker of Silver
  5. Luster of Electrum
  6. Hoarder of Gold
  7. Plunderer of Platinum
  8. Miser of Mithral
  9. Master of Greed (a High Old One, 7th level or higher)

### Dogma

- Seek to acquire all that shines or sparkles, and revel in its possession.
- The wealth of the earth belongs to those dwarves strong and crafty enough to acquire it, by any
  means necessary.
- Greed is good -- it motivates the acquisition and holding of all that is truly precious.
- Do not seize wealth from the children of the Morndinsamman, nor conspire against the favored of
  Abbathor; such strife in the name of avarice weakens the clan.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Enrich yourself, using position and influence to steal or otherwise acquire personal wealth, cached
  in remote, heavily trapped hideaways -- amassing enough loot to retire in luxury is the great
  driving game of the priesthood.
- Never steal from, or move against, another dwarf, per Abbathor's Commandment.
- Work (quietly) toward common dwarven profit alongside the clergies of Vergadain and Dumathoin,
  chiefly through smuggling -- the underground ways dwarves know so well make ideal contraband routes
  that undercut borders and duties.
- Trade with anyone for the right price, including duergar, drow, illithids, the Zhentarim, orcs, and
  giants -- dwarves have died to weapons an Abbathoran priest sold their enemies.
- Broker introductions between outsiders and the priesthood for a fee, arranging meetings that keep
  the priest safe from ambush, kidnapping, or arrest.
- Secretly work to undermine the faith of Dumathoin (in revenge for the post Abbathor felt was his)
  and of Berronar (whose efforts to prevent theft directly oppose the church's aims) -- always
  cautiously, and never in a way that could endanger the clan.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Days of Hidden Sun | Solar eclipses, or any day darkness (volcanic ash, etc.) blots out daylight | Always considered holy. |
| The Sacrifice (Buying Grace) | Annually | An enemy of the dwarves (orcs, trolls, and giants are favored) is sacrificed on the altar; the faithful lay gems in the sacrifice's blood as an offering, and the value offered is said to set the god's favor for the coming year. The body and gems are then burnt to ash -- though especially valuable or magical gems sometimes vanish before the burning is complete. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* The Wyrm Cult -- one of dwarven society's oldest recurring cults, worshiping dragons
  and other beasts dwarves treat with respect, and secretly granted spells by Abbathor through its
  priests, the noroghor. Found in isolated dwarven communities (most commonly in the North), the cult
  employs dwarven sympathizers as spies and rewards them with recreation or revenge taken in beast
  form; consumed by anger at those who have oppressed dwarves, its priests have taken to attacking
  nondwarven adventurers who wander into their territory.

## Specialty Priests

Abbathor grants two distinct tracks of specialty priesthood: the aetharnor, the mainstream priests of
greed, and the noroghor, secret priests of the Wyrm Cult who channel Abbathor's blessing toward
beast-worship and revenge. The two do not mix, and most dwarves are unaware the Wyrm Cult answers to
Abbathor at all.

### Aetharnor

- **Name/Title:** Aetharnor (singular aetharnar)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, dagger, dart, hand crossbow, knife, lasso, short bow, sling, broad
  sword, long sword, and short sword, plus staff; favored weapon dagger
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Combat, Divination, Guardian, Summoning, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Healing, Necromantic, Protection, Summoning, Sun
- **Magical Items:** No limitations; additionally may use devices normally restricted to thieves
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (mining)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on literacy (Dethek runes); +1 bonus on
  appraisal; +1 bonus on craft (gem cutting)
- **Other Clergy Grants:** Clerics of Abbathor (unlike most dwarven clerics) may use any weapon, not
  just bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons.

### Noroghor

- **Name/Title:** Noroghor (singular noroghar), priest of the Wyrm Cult
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapon
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Combat, Guardian, Protection, Summoning, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Elemental, Healing, Plant, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in handle animal
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on literacy (Dethek runes); +1 bonus on
  lore (nature); +1 bonus on linguistics (pick one monstrous tongue, such as Draconic or Aklo)
- **Other Clergy Grants:** Clerics of Abbathor (unlike most dwarven clerics) may use any weapon, not
  just bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons.
