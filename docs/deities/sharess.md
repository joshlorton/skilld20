# Sharess (the Feasthall Madam, the Feasthall Mistress, Feline of Felicity, Succubus of Sensation, the Tawny Temptress, the Dancing Lady, Foe of Set, Mother of Cats)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Sharess
- **Titles/Epithets:** the Feasthall Madam, the Feasthall Mistress, Feline of Felicity, Succubus of
  Sensation, the Tawny Temptress, the Dancing Lady, Foe of Set, Mother of Cats
- **Portfolio:** Hedonism, excess, lust, sensual fulfillment, festhalls, cats, pleasure seekers
- **Symbol:** Feminine lips (as Sharess), or, in her older identity as Bast, a cat's head wearing
  delicate golden hoop earrings

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, mystics, and specialty priests of Sharess receive religion (Faerûnian)
  and religion (Mulhorandi) as bonus skills.
- **Organization:** Collectively known as the Sharessin, the clergy is overwhelmingly composed of
  charismatic, physically beautiful human women, though both male humans and a small but significant
  number of elves and half-elves (most of whom venerate Sharess as Zandilar the Dancer) are also found
  in its ranks. Specialty priests are called sensates. The clergy is split evenly between clerics and
  specialty priests, with the balance slowly shifting toward specialty priests. Alignment restrictions
  are weak and a gentle slide toward evil is tolerated; priests who remain persistently evil are
  secretly courted by agents of Shar to shift their true devotion to the Dark Goddess while outwardly
  remaining Sharess's clergy. Worship is concentrated in large urban centers such as Waterdeep and
  Calimport and other cities along the Sword Coast, drawing professional escorts, the decadent rich,
  and pleasure seekers of every stripe; in Mulhorand, as Bast, she is also revered by those who fight
  Set, rewarding their efforts with occasional nights of wild pleasure. The faith is young, and its
  loosely structured worship services resemble extended feasts and revels more than formal liturgy. Few
  true temples exist -- built to resemble elaborate festhalls, with fluting pillars, domed halls carved
  to mimic forest glades, secluded nooks, mineral-spring bathing pools, banquet halls, and scented
  massage parlors, guarded by staunch fighters and even exotic sentient monsters -- but small shrines to
  Sharess can be found in almost every festhall in the Realms.

### Dogma

- Life is meant to be lived to the fullest, in decadent sensual fulfillment of yourself and others.
- What is pleasurable is good, and what is good is pleasurable -- pleasure is to be sought at every
  opportunity.
- Spread the goddess's bounty so that all may join the Endless Revel of Life, and bring joy to those in
  pain.
- Explore the new as eagerly as you savor the old; infinite experience awaits those willing to seek it.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Run pleasure houses in large cities, or serve decadent rulers directly, offering fantastic feasts,
  heavenly baths and massages, and every other pleasure imaginable.
- Staff wealthy festhalls (usually one or two mid-level Sharessin per house) or wander the countryside
  seeking new pleasing sensations to add to the faith's repertoire.
- In Mulhorand, reward those who labor against Set's agents with nights of wild pleasure to spur them to
  further efforts.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Endless Revel of Life | Continuous -- almost any occasion (sunrise, sunset, a full moon, the turning of a season) | The collective name for the countless festivals and wild revels the clergy hold; new ones are invented as old ones are forgotten, and the general populace is always invited. |
| Midsummer's Eve | Midsummer | The single greatest occasion of rejoicing among Sharess's faithful, marked by the most extreme pursuit of boundless pleasure. |

### Affiliated Orders

- Sharess sponsors no military or knightly orders.
- (Informal) Escort guilds -- most professional escorts in major cities belong to formal or informal
  guilds led by Sharess's clergy.
- (Informal) The Eyes of Evening -- a secretive sisterhood of female werecats who also pay tribute to
  Selûne; their true aims are unknown, though they are rumored to hunt cultists of Shar and Loviatar on
  nights of the full moon.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Sensate
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, dagger, knife, khopesh, net, sling, scimitar, whip; favored weapon a
  great cat's paw (claw bracer) -- consistent with Sharess's own claw-based combat style, her avatar
  rakes with "deceptively delicate fingernails... as sharp and deadly as the claws of a hunting cat"
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor), always decorated as
  provocatively as possible
- **Major Spheres:** All, Chaos, Charm, Combat, Healing, Guardian, Protection
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Creation, Elemental (water), Necromantic, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in performance (dancing)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on performance (singing); +1 bonus on art
  (pick one); +1 bonus on performance (instrument, pick one)

Although humans make up the majority of the clergy, elves, half-elves, and the rare halfling may also
become sensates.
