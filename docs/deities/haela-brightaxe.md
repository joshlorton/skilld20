# Haela Brightaxe (Lady of the Fray, Luckmaiden, the Hard)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Haela Brightaxe
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lady of the Fray, Luckmaiden, the Hard
- **Portfolio:** Luck in battle, patron of dwarves who love to fight and who battle monsters, love/joy
  of battle, dwarven monster-slaying, dwarven fighter adventurers
- **Symbol:** An unsheathed sword encircled by a flaming bolt (a two-ended spiral of flame)

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders)
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, crusaders, and specialty priests of Haela receive the standard dwarven
  clergy grants (literacy (Dethek runes), modified turn undead) -- see `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** Haela's clergy is evenly split between specialty priests (34%), clerics (33%), and
  crusaders (33%), with about half of the specialty priests and clerics also multiclassed as fighters.
  Temples are caves or underground rooms -- storehouses, smithies, and armories -- never garrisoned by
  fewer than a dozen priests (usually 16-20) and always rigged with a lethal trap to deny the arsenal
  to any enemy who takes the temple by force. The clergy draws mostly on shield dwarves (70%), with a
  growing gold dwarf following (28%) and a small gray dwarf minority (2%); before the Time of Troubles
  the priesthood was entirely female, and it remains about 85% female today.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Unblooded (novice)
  2. First Blood
  3. Deadly Dirk
  4. Stout Spear
  5. Sharp Axe
  6. Shining Sword
  7. Flamebolt
  8. Brightaxe (full priests collectively: Blades of the Brightaxe)
  9. Hallowed Crimson (High Old Ones, who also hold individual titles)

  Specialty priests are known as luckmaidens.

### Dogma

- Through battle there is validation, liberation, and exultation.
- Trust Haela to see you through the fray -- the monsters of the world shall fall to your blades
  regardless of their apparent strength or numbers.
- Rejoice in the power of your swing in battle, the sound of your weapon smiting a worthy foe, and the
  challenge of the fray.
- Show mercy to a noble foe who abides by a code of honor; hold no mercy for the treacherous, the
  liars, and the honorless.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Wander throughout the Realms aiding dwarves in battle, trusting to Haela's guiding hand to place them
  where they are needed.
- Aid beleaguered dwarves (and known allies and companions of dwarves) against monsters of all sorts by
  healing, casting spells, and fighting alongside them; the goal is dwarven victory and the maximum
  possible number of dwarven survivors.
- Go always heavily armed and are often skilled at weapon and armor repair; freely give away spare
  weapons to dwarves in need while always keeping at least one (sometimes well hidden) for themselves.
- Practice throwing weapons so they land upright and ready beside those who need them, gaining a +3
  bonus to the check when delivering a weapon this way.
- Teach juniors tactics, secrets, and monster lore -- habits, lairs, and weaknesses -- so that a priest
  can advise adventurers who ask the right questions.
- Expect payment for aid in the form of a spare weapon (or, failing that, a shield, gauntlets, or other
  armor/gear) to pass on to some other needy band; it is considered bad form to give a priest back a
  weapon she or he just gave you.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Time of the Spawning | Greengrass (annual) | Grim preparation for the next wave of orcs and monsters expected to pour from long-fallen dwarven holds: battle hymns, rhythmic drumming, and the ritual shattering of weapons and armor seized from past foes. |
| Axe Held High | Midday, outdoors, in full sun | Glorifies Haela's valor; the faithful hold that an unsheathed sword appears momentarily in the sun at high noon. Every Blade of the Brightaxe in good standing who takes part gains the benefit of Haela's enveloping aura (see her manifestations) for the next 24 hours. |
| Commemoration of the Fallen | Feast of the Moon | Remembers dwarves and nondwarves who fell defending the Stout Folk against monstrous foes, by recounting their battles and consecrating new arms and armor in their memory. |

*On all three holy days, the faithful offer several drops of their own blood (one per level) and the
blood of dwarven enemies they have slain since the last holy day (one drop per foe, one foe per level).*

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Haela's Host -- the militant order based at Endar Aglandtor (the Sword Grotto, in the Tor
  of Swords), which watches over the Hill of Lost Souls and other old battlefields and fights the
  monsters of the Serpent Hills, the Marsh of Chelimber, and the Forest of Wyrms.
- *(Formal)* The Dauls of the Luckmaiden, the Shining Host of the Underdeeps, the Dancing Damsels of the
  Brightaxe, and the hippogriff-mounted Skyriders of Aglandar -- other religious/military orders, most
  short-lived, generally focused on reducing evil monster populations in their home region.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Luckmaiden
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All weapons; favored weapon greatsword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Guardian, Healing, Protection, Travelers, War
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Divination, Necromantic, Summoning, Sun
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (weaponsmithing)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on blind-fighting; +1 bonus on
  acrobatics (tumbling)
- **Other Clergy Grants:** Clerics of Haela (unlike most dwarven clerics) may use any weapon, not just
  bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons.

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2 bonus on all saving throws while actively participating in melee combat |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Armor (self only) *or* Shield, choose one per use (10 mana) |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Aid *or* Strength (self only), choose one per use (20 mana) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Draw upon Holy Might *or* Lighten Load, choose one per use (20 mana) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Detect Weapons at will (no mana cost) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Prayer *or* Flame Strike, choose one per use (60 mana *or* 50 mana) |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Heal *or* Heroes' Feast, choose one per use (60 mana) |
