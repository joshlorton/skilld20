# Grumbar (Boss of Earth, Earthlord, Gnarly One, King of the Land Below the Roots, Boss of the Earth Elementals)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Grumbar
- **Titles/Epithets:** Boss of Earth, Earthlord, Gnarly One, King of the Land Below the Roots, Boss of the Earth Elementals
- **Portfolio:** Elemental earth, earth elementalists, solidity, changelessness, oaths
- **Symbol:** Mountains, often set against a purple field

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms, monastic orders
- **Organization:** Priests are organized into Holds of 21 priests each (seven specialty priests,
  seven monks, seven crusaders) plus as much laity as a Hold can support; the high priest of a Hold
  is always a specialty priest (earthwalker). Each Hold is composed of members of a similar
  alignment, so the faith is viewed differently region to region. In primitive or nomadic
  societies (such as among the Tuigan), Grumbar is instead served by unaffiliated shamans.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order): novices are called the Unspoken; upon
  taking the Oath of Landwalking they become full priests, known collectively as the Oathbound.
  1. Faithful Bedrock
  2. Granite Flagstone

  4. Buttress of the Faith

  7. Loyal Architrave
  8. Founding Fist

  Specialty priests are known as earthwalkers; monks are known as Cornerstones.

### Dogma

- The eternal Grumbar is perfect and unchanging; strive to be more like him, not to change or allow change to happen.
- One's given word is the bedrock upon which a stable society is built; to break an oath is to crack the foundation of civilization.
- Go forth and spread the word of Grumbar, showing through works the stability and safety he brings.
- Resist change -- especially radical change -- at every turn, whether in government, organizations, geography, or even one's own religion.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Preach publicly against spelljamming and, to a lesser degree, seeking out uncharted lands by sea,
  arguing there is more than enough of Faerûn, Kara-Tur, and Zakhara left to explore first.
- Take stances against changes to social codes, changes in government, and new landmarks (such as
  dams) that alter the face of the land.
- Wrestle with (and often quietly resent) the still-unexplained change Grumbar himself imposed on the
  church after the Godswar, when he converted all its clerics into specialty priests.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Daily devotion | Upon rising and before resting | The faithful thank Grumbar for keeping the earth stable and secure another day. |
| Oath of Landwalking | Taken upon entering the priesthood | A solemn vow forbidding travel by air except in the most dire circumstances, and restricting sea travel to journeys that clearly benefit the church. |
| Brotherhood of Earth | As earned, per specialty priest | A ceremony (preceded by a day of fasting and prayer) by which sufficiently skilled earthwalkers summon an elemental "brother" from the Elemental Plane of Earth and gift it gems and gold to carry back to Grumbar. |
| Midwinter Festival | Midwinter (annual) | Church leaders and elders gather to plan the faith's activities for the coming year; once set, these plans are never varied from until the next Midwinter. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Disciples of the Changeless Face -- the Grumbarryn monastic order; stoic and spartan,
  preserving knowledge of "how things are" so that changes can be detected and countered.
- (Formal) Sardonyx Knights -- the crusading order of the faith; guards holy sites against
  infidels, leads the church's few field actions, and punishes members who forswear their oaths.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Earthwalker
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon warhammer
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Heavy (any armor type, the bulkier the better); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Elemental Earth, Combat, Healing, Protection
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Elemental Fire, Elemental Water, Necromantic, Travelers, Wards
- **Magical Items:** anathema air-based or flying-effect items (flying carpet, ring of feather
  falling, cloak of the bat, etc.)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (stonemasonry)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on craft (gem cutting) or survival
  (mountaineering), pick one; +1 bonus on lore (elemental earth)
- **Other Clergy Grants:** Shamans of Grumbar (who serve independently of the Hold structure, in
  primitive/nomadic societies) gain Elemental Earth as an additional major sphere alongside their
  normal spheres -- this does not apply to Earthwalkers, who already have it natively.
