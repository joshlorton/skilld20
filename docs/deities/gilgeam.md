# Gilgeam (Dead) (The Great, Father of Victory, Supreme Ruler of Unther)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Gilgeam
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Great, Father of Victory, Supreme Ruler of Unther. Formally also styled
  Master of Wars and God of the Sky and the Cities, and nominal "Supreme Ruler" of Chessenta, Threskel,
  Chondath, Turmish, the Shaar, and the Yuirwood, though Unther's throne had held no real dominion over
  those lands for centuries.
- **Portfolio:** Strength, athletic prowess, the sky, battle, cities, the land of Unther -- all formerly
  held. Gilgeam was slain by Tiamat at the end of the Time of Troubles; see Church Summary for what
  became of his portfolio and clergy.
- **Symbol:** A clenched red fist backed by a golden sun on a black lozenge

## Church Summary

**Gilgeam is dead.** He was slain by Tiamat at the end of the Time of Troubles (see the shared
Mulhorandi & Untheric pantheon history), and his death effectively ended the Untheric pantheon as a
living faith. Everything below describes the church of Gilgeam as it existed and functioned while he
still lived and granted spells; it is preserved as historical/reference documentation, not as an active
church. Where the source itself confirms the church has collapsed or lost its powers, that is called
out explicitly rather than presented as ongoing.

- **Clergy (historical):** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders)
- **All Clergy (historical):** All clerics, specialty priests, and crusaders of Gilgeam received
  religion (Untheric), literacy (Untheric), and linguistics (Common) as bonus skills, and all knew
  Untheric as their native tongue. Gilgeam required every member of his priesthood to be a full-blooded
  Mulan human.
- **Organization:** Gilgeam's church was universally despised by the Untheric populace by the time of
  the Fall of the Gods -- his clergy were themselves brutal tyrants who administered Unther's every
  major civil, legal, and bureaucratic post, lived in luxury in Unthalass's Palace District, and
  enforced Gilgeam's demand for unquestioning obeisance. When Gilgeam was destroyed, the joyous populace
  sacked and burned nearly every temple of his faith; the clergy as an organized, spell-receiving church
  no longer exists. A small remnant of surviving priests fled to a hidden stronghold, the Citadel of
  Black Ash in the eastern Smoking Mountains, where they allied with a clan of fire giants and continued
  to chant dirges to their fallen lord while plotting revenge against Unther, Mulhorand, Chessenta, and
  the wider Realms. As of recently, this remnant has begun receiving priest spells again -- but the
  source is explicit that the power is not Gilgeam's: the survivors believe they have re-established
  contact with a living, merely-exiled Gilgeam, but their spells are in fact being secretly granted by
  Set, who is exploiting the deception to expand his own influence into Unther. In effect, no priest
  alive today receives spells from Gilgeam himself; any priest still nominally "of Gilgeam" active in
  the present day is, unknowingly, a priest of Set.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy (historical):** Novices were called Aspirants; the ranks of the full
  clergy, in ascending order, were:
  1. Watcher
  2. Enforcer
  3. Captain of the Third Rank
  4. Commander of the Third Rank
  5. Autarch of the Third Rank
  6. High Captain of the Second Rank
  7. High Commander of the Second Rank
  8. High Autarch of the Second Rank
  9. Lord High Captain of the First Rank
  10. Lord High Commander of the First Rank
  11. Lord High Autarch of the First Rank

  Priests of higher level received individual titles from Gilgeam personally.

### Dogma (historical)

- Life is hard, and the ability to endure hardship -- however unfair -- is the greatest virtue.
- Strength and athletic prowess prove one's fitness to survive hardship; promotions within the clergy
  went to the winners of athletic contests, which also served as a forum for challenges between rivals.
- "I am All Religion, I am All Worship; none may breathe in Unther without blessing me" -- unquestioning
  religious obeisance to Gilgeam was demanded of every citizen of Unther, though true faith was rare.

### Day-to-Day Activities (historical)

- Administer the day-to-day running of Unther as its primary landowners, nobles, and bureaucrats, with
  every whim satisfied by slaves and the national treasury.
- Enforce justice, largely on an "eye for an eye" basis, after having inherited that duty from the
  departed Enlil; in the faith's final two centuries this system broke down, and priests allowed nobles
  to seize property, break agreements, and commit murder in exchange for bribes of land, power, or
  wealth.
- Host routine tests of strength and athletic competition at temples and church grounds, used both to
  settle policy disputes between rivals and to advance within the church hierarchy.

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal, historical)* Prior to the Godswar, Gilgeam commanded a host of military orders. Only a
  fraction of the most powerful order survived his destruction: the Lords of War and Victory, an elite
  order of over 200 crusaders, reformed at the Citadel of Black Ash, where they guard the secret
  fortress's gates and drill on the Black Ash plains, wearing ebon-hued scale mail or full plate and
  full helms shaped in Gilgeam's likeness.

## Specialty Priests

**Gilgeam is dead.** The martinet write-up below documents the specialty priest class exactly as it
functioned while Gilgeam's church still received spells from him. No priest today draws these grants
from Gilgeam himself -- the tiny surviving remnant at the Citadel of Black Ash has recently begun
receiving spells again, but per the source this power is secretly Set's, not Gilgeam's (see Church
Summary). Presented here as historical reference only.

- **Name/Title:** Martinet (historical)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Mace, staff, staff sling, flail; favored weapons sword and axe -- drawn
  directly from the Epic of Gilgamesh itself (he draws a sword to strike Humbaba while Enkidu wields an
  axe, and separately wields the named legendary weapon Shamash's Radiant Axe)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Medium and Heavy (up to and including scale mail or bronze plate
  mail); no shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Combat, Elemental, Guardian, Law, Protection, Summoning, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Divination, Necromantic, Time, War, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in athletics (running)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on endurance
