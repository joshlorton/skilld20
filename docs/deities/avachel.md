# Avachel (Quicksilver)

This is a minor/stub entry. The source book gives Avachel only a single paragraph and explicitly
defers his full write-up to other sourcebooks (Cult of the Dragon and the Draconomicon for his aspect
as Hlal; Monstrous Mythology and On Hallowed Ground for his aspect as Aasterinian). He has no clergy,
symbol, church, vestments, specialty priest, or spell list of his own in this source -- he is a
companion/ally power, not an independently worshiped member of the Seldarine.

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Avachel
- **Titles/Epithets:** Quicksilver
- **Portfolio:** Not independently detailed in this source. Avachel is a male aspect of the draconic
  goddess Hlal (also known as Aasterinian), venerated by the Fair Folk as the boon companion of Erevan
  Illesere.
- **Symbol:** None given in this source.

## Notes

Avachel is described as an interloper power more properly seen as an ally of the Seldarine than a true
member. Elven myths hold he was once a great mercury wyrm who underwent apotheosis after sacrificing
himself to defend a band of green elves from an invasion of evil humans; other legends describe an
avatar of Hlal in mortal guise who joined the Trickster in similar exploits. He is good-natured and
impulsive, nearly Erevan's equal at getting into trouble, but a tireless defender of the Fair Folk,
particularly green elves. Avachel sends avatars to Evermeet and other Toril woodlands either as a
silver or green elf wielding an enchanted staff (any creature struck must save vs. spell or fall
asleep) or as a great mercury wyrm with all the attendant draconic powers.

His legendary former lair, Quicksilver's Lair, is mentioned in Erevan Illesere's entry as a hidden
cavern complex in the High Peaks now inhabited by a clutch of mercury dragons believed to be his
descendants.
