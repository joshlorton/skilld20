# Sebek (Lord of Crocodiles, the Smiling Death)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Sebek
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of Crocodiles, the Smiling Death
- **Portfolio:** River hazards, crocodiles, werecrocodiles, wetlands, rivers
- **Symbol:** A crocodile head surmounted by a horned, plumed headdress

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics and specialty priests of Sebek receive religion (pantheon), linguistics
  (Mulhorandi), and linguistics (Common) as bonus proficiencies. All of Sebek's clergy are werecrocodiles;
  those native to Unther and Chessenta additionally know their own homeland's tongue.
- **Organization:** Sebek is both feared and revered in rural Mulhorand, propitiated by fearful
  villagers rather than actively worshiped, except by twisted secret cults; his clergy is small and
  builds no permanent temples, only mud-and-reed shrine-huts in swamps, stagnant pools, and slow rivers.
  The clergy is dominated by specialty priests, known as swamplords, with clerics making up only about
  10%; clerics are capped at 5th level, while swamplords have no level cap. There is no fixed hierarchy
  of rank -- titles such as Glistening Jaws, Watery Death, Scales of Vengeance, Hungry Maw, and Bloody
  Teeth are awarded haphazardly or self-selected, and standing among the clergy is settled through
  ritual combat that continues until one combatant is killed or yields.

### Dogma

- Hunt or be hunted -- weed out the weak, terrify commoners into propitiating Sebek, and dominate your
  fellow priests.
- Hunt intruders into Sebek's domain and work to eventually turn the world into one vast swamp.
- Help Sebek gain the recognition he deserves as a powerful god to be feared; his priesthood will be
  amply rewarded with prey, wealth, and territory when he finally claims a rightful position in the
  Mulhorandi pantheon.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Bully fellow priests, hunt, and lure humans to their doom.
- Design schemes to seize power in Mulhorand, Unther, and Chessenta.
- Whisper praise to Sebek before every kill, and leave a small portion of the catch out at night for
  him to devour if he chooses.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Monthly Offering | Monthly | Villagers sacrifice animals (occasionally sentient creatures) at swamp shrines to appease Sebek; those slow to leave afterward risk falling prey to the crocodiles the sacrifice draws in. |
| The Kill-Debt | Monthly | Each werecrocodile priest must personally slay a sentient creature (of low intelligence or greater) or lose all priestly powers until they do so. |
| The Turning | Yearly | Each priest must create at least one new werecrocodile from the human population and guide them into Sebek's worship, so the cult continues to grow. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Informal) No knightly or military orders exist -- every werecrocodile serves Sebek directly and wars
  on humans who invade their rivers and swamps. Werecrocodiles have occasionally formed alliances of
  convenience with lizard men, lizard kings, marsh-dwellers, bullywugs, troglodytes, and boggles, though
  most such alliances collapse once one side or the other stops finding them useful.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Swamplord
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Mace, spear, staff, sling, staff-sling, khopesh, scimitar, dagger; favored
  weapon spear -- doubly confirmed, both by real-world 3.5e sourcing ("The Sorrowful Spear") and by
  real-world Egyptian mythology, which consistently depicts Sobek carrying a spear (and harpoons) as
  his attribute; this book's own flavor text claims specialty priests favor scimitars "in practice,"
  but that's uncorroborated by any outside source, so spear is treated as primary
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light and Medium (up to and including scale mail); no shields; a
  natural Armor Class of 1 while in crocodile form
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Chaos, Combat, Elemental (water and earth), Guardian, Healing, Plant,
  Protection
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Creation, Divination, Necromantic, Thought
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** None specified in the source beyond weapon training
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on direction sense; +1 bonus on swim

All swamplords are werecrocodiles, gaining that lycanthrope's abilities and immunities.
