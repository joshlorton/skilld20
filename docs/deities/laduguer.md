# Laduguer (The Exile, the Gray Protector, Master of Craft, the Slave Driver, the Taskmaster, the Harsh)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Laduguer
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Exile, the Gray Protector, Master of Craft, the Slave Driver, the Taskmaster,
  the Harsh
- **Portfolio:** Magical weapon creation, skilled artisans, magic, the gray dwarf race, protector of
  gray dwarves
- **Symbol:** A shield bearing a broken crossbow bolt motif

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders), Rogues
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, crusaders, and specialty priests of Laduguer receive the standard
  dwarven clergy grants (literacy (Dethek runes), modified turn undead) -- see
  `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** Laduguer's clergy is composed primarily of specialty priests (55%), with clerics
  (16%), crusaders (14%), fighter/clerics (8%), and cleric/thieves (7%) filling out the rest; where
  psionics are permitted in a campaign, roughly two-thirds of every group also multiclasses as
  psionicists. The priesthood is 99% gray dwarf and 95% male. Temples are grim, smoke-filled,
  unadorned halls hewn from solid rock, typically built directly atop mine shafts and combining
  armories, barracks, smithies, storerooms, and steeder stables; clerical guards, many mounted on
  steeders, oversee constant smithwork lit by great coal-burning forges.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Untempered (novice)
  2. Deep Adept
  3. Dark Craftsman
  4. Invisible Artisan
  5. Rune Weaver
  6. Grim Guardian
  7. Doom Knight (full priests collectively: Grimcloaks)
  8. Ardukes of the Gray Gloom (High Old Ones, who also hold individual titles)

  Specialty priests are known as thuldor (singular thuldar), a dwarvish word loosely translated as
  "those who endure."

### Dogma

- The children of Laduguer rejected the indolent, feeble gods of their forefathers and withdrew from
  their weak once-kin so as not to be tainted by their weakness.
- Strict obedience to superiors, dedication to one's craft, and endless toil are the only path to
  wealth, security, and power.
- A master craftsman's hands are his tools, and he always uses the most appropriate tool available.
- Nothing is ever easy, nor should it be; suffer pain stoically and remain aloof, for to show or even
  feel emotion is to demonstrate weakness.
- Those who are weak are undeserving and will suffer an appropriate fate.
- Adversity is Laduguer's forge, and the harsh trials the duergar must pass are his hammer blows --
  endure all and become stronger than adamantite.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve as the leaders, defenders, and elite artisans of gray dwarven society; unlike gold and shield
  dwarf cultures, the duergar make no distinction between religious and clan leadership.
- Command and serve in the military, and are ultimately responsible for the care, feeding, and training
  of steeders.
- Oversee the brewing of poisons, the infliction of torture, and the exploitation of slaves.
- Forcefully repel contact from other races, permitting trade only under tightly controlled
  circumstances kept far removed from duergar strongholds.
- Serve as skilled craftsmen, particularly of magical weapons; the older and more frail priests are
  typically the elite artisans of any gray dwarven community.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grimtidings | Midwinter (annual) | The only regular holy day. Duergar lay down their hammers to hear priests recount the trials the duergar have suffered since their voluntary exile, and a litany of those who have insulted Laduguer or the gray dwarves and against whom a promised, deadly revenge is recited. |
| Guerdon Revels | Declared by an Arduke of the Gray Gloom after a major victory or when prisoners (particularly gold or shield dwarves) are captured | Work continues, but gray dwarves are given a few moments to hear duergar warriors recount their heroics, examine plundered loot, and witness the torture and painful deaths of any prisoners. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Gray Lances of the Snarling Steeder -- an elite mounted order of duergar crusaders and
  fighter-priests, serving as the elite cavalry of gray dwarven armies and schooled in subterranean
  warfare across cave floors, walls, and ceilings; their most common opponents are drow mounted on
  riding lizards.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Thuldor (singular thuldar)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All weapons; favored weapon warhammer
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Divination, Elemental Earth, Guardian, Healing, Law, Protection, Sun
  (reversed only), Thought, War, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Creation, Necromantic
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in handle animal (steeders) [riding]; pick one: craft (blacksmithing),
  craft (mining), craft (armorer), craft (weaponsmithing)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on handle animal (steeders); +1 bonus
  on herbalism
