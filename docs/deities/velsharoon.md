# Velsharoon (The Vaunted, Archmage of Necromancy, the Necromancer, Lord of the Forsaken Crypt, Lord of the Forgotten Crypt, the Lich-Lord, Patron of Evil Liches)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Velsharoon
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Vaunted, Archmage of Necromancy, the Necromancer, Lord of the Forsaken Crypt,
  Lord of the Forgotten Crypt, the Lich-Lord, Patron of Evil Liches
- **Portfolio:** Necromancy, necromancers, lichdom, evil liches, undeath
- **Symbol:** A laughing lich skull wearing a silver crown on a solid black hexagon

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics and specialty priests of Velsharoon receive religion (Faerunian) as a bonus
  skill.
- **Organization:** Velsharoon is a newly ascended demipower (as of the end of 1368 DR) and largely
  unknown throughout Faerûn -- his faith is still small, disorganized, and lacks a single unified creed,
  though it is spreading via bardic ghost stories. His clergy is composed primarily of specialty priests
  (necrophants) and necromancer wizards in roughly equal numbers, plus a smaller cadre (about 20%) of
  clerics who are holdovers from the church of the former Lord of the Dead, Myrkul, and have little
  interest in serving Kelemvor or Cyric. Cultists gather in dusty crypts, abandoned mausoleums, and
  neglected graveyards; the faith's few true temples are built within ancient catacombs or necropolises
  as brooding stone mausoleums, dominated by laboratories, alchemical storerooms, and mortuaries rather
  than living quarters, and are guarded by tireless undead servitors. Novices and acolytes are called
  Pallbearers.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order, above Pallbearer):
  1. Soul Stalker
  2. Cryptguardian
  3. Dead Walker
  4. Bleeder
  5. Life Leech
  6. Spirit Sepulcher
  7. Seeker of the Seven Truths
  8. Necromaster

  Higher-ranking priests beyond these receive unique individual titles conferred by Velsharoon himself.

### Dogma

- Those chosen as Velsharoon's apostles are an elite fellowship of visionaries, worthy of respect for
  their bold excursions to the frontier of life and death.
- Life and death are twin faces of a single eternal existence; to surrender wholly to either is to
  resign yourself to obscurity.
- True power lies in the twilight zone between life and death -- seek to explore and extend the mortal
  condition, even mortal life itself, to extend knowledge of the world in all its complexity.
- Lesser beings cannot understand the importance of this research; let no one interfere with it, for the
  end result will justify whatever sacrifices the path requires.
- Knowledge is power, and knowledge of life, death, and undeath brings power over all beings -- power
  that can lift the church and its priests to dizzying heights.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Conduct necromantic research into life, death, and undeath, and create undead servitors, some unique;
  once a creation has served its purpose, many priests brand it with Velsharoon's symbol and set it
  loose to wander the Realms.
- Support the faith through grave robbing, embalming, and teaching.
- A few congenial low-level priests cure minor ills for the populace in exchange for funding, supplies,
  or information about the church's enemies.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Pact of the Everlasting | Upon reaching 9th level (or sooner, via an appropriate scroll spell) | An unholy rite in which Velsharoon's death pact is cast upon the priest, alongside numerous other foul incantations and the sacrifice of many good-aligned sentients. Prominent theologians claim it is only the first of seven rites revealed by Velsharoon on the path to immortality. |

The faith is young enough that widely varying, temple-specific (and even priest-specific) rituals are far
more common than any shared calendar of holy days.

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Order of One Thousand Nightmares -- nearly 200 nightmare-riding swordwraiths, once
  centuries-warring ghostly spirits of the Fields of the Dead, recently organized by Velsharoon's
  necrophant masters into a necromantic knighthood of growing size and unknown purpose.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Necrophant
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons; favored weapon quarterstaff (the
  Skull Staff of the Necromancer)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** None -- necrophants wear no armor
- **Major Spheres:** All, Creation, Healing, Necromantic, Protection, Summoning, Time, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, Elemental, Guardian, Numbers, Sun (reversed only)
- **Magical Items:** No limitations; additionally may use wizard items specifically suited to
  necromancers (such as staves of skulls)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in healing
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on literacy (Common); +1 bonus on
  literacy (Thorass)
