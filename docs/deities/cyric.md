# Cyric (Prince of Lies, Prince of Madness, the Dark Sun, the Black Sun, the Mad God, Lord of Three Crowns)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Cyric
- **Titles/Epithets:** Prince of Lies, Prince of Madness, the Dark Sun, the Black Sun, the Mad God, Lord of Three Crowns
- **Portfolio:** Murder, strife, lies, intrigue, deception, illusion
- **Symbol:** A white skull (sans jawbone) on a dark purple or black sunburst

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **Organization:** Chaotic and rife with internal feuds. The church absorbed most of the surviving
  clergy of Bane, Bhaal, and Myrkul after the Time of Troubles, and cabals of ex-Bhaalists,
  ex-Myrkulytes, Banites, and the "true" priests of Cyric (called the Sworn) constantly scheme
  against one another for personal power and advancement. Ranks and titles are disputed often and
  are not consistently applied.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** No fixed ascending hierarchy is observed -- superiors are
  challenged and titles disputed constantly. Popular (unranked) titles in use include: Dark Master,
  Hand of Cyric, Watchful Skull, and Dread Death.

### Dogma

- Death to traitors. Death to all who oppose Cyric.
- Bow down before the supreme power of Cyric, and yield to him the blood of all who do not believe.
- Fear and obey those in authority -- but if they are weak or pursue vague goals of goodness, slay
  them in Cyric's name.
- Battle against all clergy of other faiths, for they are false prophets who oppose the One True Way.
- Bring death to those who oppose the rightful church of Cyric and to those who seek to make or keep
  peace, order, and laws.
- All rightful authority comes from Cyric, and all other authority must be subverted.
- Do not break into open rebellion -- fell one foe at a time and keep all folk afraid, uneasy, and
  in constant strife under Cyric's spreading tyranny.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Spread strife and work murder everywhere to make folk fear and believe in Cyric.
- Support cruel, empire-building rulers and stir intrigue in every land, while avoiding outright war
  (which would only glorify Tempus).
- In practice, spend most of their time scheming against each other in an endless cabal-against-cabal
  struggle for personal power.
- Blood sacrifice is required for Cyric to hear any prayer of entreaty; local priests declare an
  impromptu "Day of the Dark Sun" (a high holy day) whenever they acquire something -- or someone --
  worth sacrificing.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Banedeath (discontinued) | Final day/night of Marpenoth | Formerly celebrated the purge of Bane-worship in Zhentil Keep; ceased to be observed and was struck from church records after the Keep's destruction. |
| Day of the Dark Sun | Impromptu, whenever declared | A high holy day declared locally whenever priests acquire a person or valuable enough to sacrifice to Cyric. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Company of the Ebon Spur -- a fighting order ordered into being by Cyric himself, but
  still without a leader; the two priests tasked with setting its admission standards are covertly
  plotting to kill each other rather than agree.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Strifeleader
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons and longswords; favored weapon longsword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Heavy (any armor type); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Combat, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic, Summoning, Sun,
  Time, War, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Elemental, Numbers, Protection
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in linguistics (two languages)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on disguise and ventriloquism; +1
  bonus on stealth

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 bonus on saving throws vs. illusion/phantasm magic |
| 1 | Unaffected by fear spells and other emotion-altering magic (still subject to charm/enchantment effects that don't directly alter emotion) |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Black Talon (20 mana) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Aerial Servant (2 mana -- 6th-level effect, 1/month = level × ⅓); acts as a magical assassin under the strifeleader's control; only one may be summoned per month and only one controlled at a time |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Dark Aura *or* Skull of Secrets, choose one per casting (40 mana) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Phantasmal Killer (4 mana -- 4th-level effect, 1/tenday = level × 1) |
| 15 | Instill Madness: touch ability identical to *feeblemind*, but the victim receives no Wisdom adjustment to the saving throw; usable at will, no mana cost |
