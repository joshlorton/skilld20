# Tempus (Lord of Battles, Foehammer)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Tempus
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of Battles, Foehammer
- **Portfolio:** War, battle, warriors
- **Symbol:** A blazing silver sword on a blood-red field

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **Organization:** Tempus is worshiped by warriors of every alignment and lineage, and his clergy may
  be found on both sides of the same conflict, since none can know whom the war god will favor. Priests
  tend to be human, male, and battle-loving, though the faith is open to all who have privately prayed
  to Tempus and received a sign of his favor. Among the Northmen of the Moonshae Islands and the
  barbarians of Icewind Dale, Tempus is served by shamans instead. Temples are usually walled military
  compounds rather than conventional shrines, and military-style ranks are common; ranks are assigned
  by church authorities based on service and need, with brevet (temporary) commands common in
  desperate situations, and are often superseded by duty-titles tied to a specific post (such as Battle
  Chaplain of a shrine or Trusted Sword, the seneschal of a temple). Leaders of a temple or crusade wear
  a heavy battle gauntlet as a badge of rank.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Warpriest
  2. Swung Sword
  3. Terrible Sword
  4. Lance of the Lord
  5. Shield of the God
  6. Battlelady/Battlelord
  7. Swordmaster/Swordmistress
  8. Lady/Lord of the Field

### Dogma

- Tempus does not win battles -- Tempus helps the deserving warrior win battles.
- War is fair in that it oppresses all sides equally; in any battle a mortal may be slain or rise to
  become a great leader.
- War should not be feared, but seen as a natural, human force -- the storm that civilization brings
  by its very existence.
- Arm all for whom battle is needful, even foes.
- Retreat from hopeless fights, but never avoid battle.
- Slay a foe decisively and bring a battle to a halt, rather than hacking down many over time and
  dragging out hostilities.
- Defend what you believe in, lest it be swept away, and remember the dead who fell before you.
- Disparage no foe and respect all, for valor blazes in everyone regardless of age, gender, or race.
- Fight honorably and tirelessly, but avoid craven tricks such as destroying homes, family, or
  livestock when a foe is away, or attacking from the rear (except when a small band does so against
  foes of vastly superior numbers).
- Consider the consequences of violence beforehand rather than rushing recklessly to war -- but know
  that those with smooth tongues or fleet feet who avoid all strife and never defend their beliefs do
  more harm than the most energetic tyrant or horde leader.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Keep warfare a thing of rules, respected reputation, and professional behavior, minimizing
  uncontrolled bloodshed and working to end feuds that spread beyond a single original dispute.
- Promote training and battle-readiness so that civilized holdings can survive monster raids and orc
  hordes in Faerûn.
- Deny the favor of Tempus to warriors (especially mercenaries) who employ poison, taint wells, sow
  fields with salt, kill noncombatants, torture, or wantonly slaughter innocents outside of war;
  publicize such crimes far and wide and see that the offenders atone or perish.
- Preserve the names of the honored battle-fallen on gravestones and other memorials, in prayers to
  Tempus, and in an annual chant at the March of the Dead, when priests of the war god walk the streets
  calling all folk -- worshiper and nonbeliever alike -- to the local Feast of the Moon.
- Collect and venerate the weapons and armor of famous, respected warriors, even if broken or
  deteriorated, for they retain something of the battlelust and energy of the deeds they took part in.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Eves and anniversaries of great battles | Varies by place | The holy days of the church of Tempus vary from place to place, tied to local battle history. |
| Feast of the Moon | Fixed annual date, honoring the dead | The single most important fixed date on the religious calendar. |
| Song of the Sword | At least once per tenday | Worshipers spill a few drops of blood (their own or a worthy foe's) and sing the Song of the Sword in Tempus's honor; a senior priest also leads this ceremony after dark for lay worshipers in most temples. |
| Feast of Heroes | Daily, at highsun | One of two ceremonies clergy perform every day, regardless of battle anniversaries. |
| Song for the Fallen | Daily, at sunset | The second of the two ceremonies clergy perform every day. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Order of the Broken Blade -- honors warriors and clergy injured in Tempus's service who
  can no longer fight on the front lines; members serve support roles at temples and shrines and swear
  a personal oath to defend their holy site to the death as a final line of defense.
- *(Formal)* Order of the Steel Fang -- an elite fighting order within the church whose members are
  often assigned the most hazardous duties, led by battle-hardened clergy.
- *(Informal)* Many mercenary companies and knightly fighting orders of crusaders maintain a
  connection to the church; one badge seen among Tempus's affiliated mercenaries is a rusty brown
  dagger, shown diagonally with its point to the upper right, dripping four drops of blood. No
  knightly orders of paladins serve Tempus.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Battleguard
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons, the spiked glove, plus one other weapon of choice;
  favored weapon battleaxe
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed unless a
  two-handed chosen weapon is in use
- **Major Spheres:** Animal, Chaos, Combat, Divination, Elemental, Healing, Necromantic, Protection,
  War, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** All, Guardian, Summoning, Sun, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** None
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon), craft (armorer), blind-fighting, crafting
  (charioteering), and craft (weaponsmithing) checks; +1 bonus on intimidation checks
