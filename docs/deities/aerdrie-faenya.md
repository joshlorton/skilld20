# Aerdrie Faenya (the Winged Mother, Lady of Air and Wind, Queen of the Avariel, She of the Azure Plumage, Bringer of Rain and Storms)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Aerdrie Faenya
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Winged Mother, Lady of Air and Wind, Queen of the Avariel, She of the Azure
  Plumage, Bringer of Rain and Storms
- **Portfolio:** Air, weather, avians, rain, fertility, avariel
- **Symbol:** A cloud with the silhouette of a bird

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including multiclassed half-elven clerics), mystics, and specialty
  priests of Aerdrie receive religion (elf) and literacy (Espruar) as bonus proficiencies. Most of
  Aerdrie's priests have a strong fear of confinement bordering on claustrophobia, suffering a -1
  penalty on initiative, attack rolls, and saving throws while confined (including in nearly all
  underground areas). Aerdrie's priests must sleep outdoors except during winter or bad weather.
- **Organization:** A small, loosely organized clergy centered on temples called aeries -- delicate
  crystalline spires on high hilltops or mountain slopes, bedecked with glass chimes, honeycombed with
  small caves and tunnels that let the wind whistle through the peak. Shrines are little more than
  alpine ledges accessible only to flying creatures. Specialty priests (halcyons) are only 20% of the
  clergy but hold nearly all high positions in the church; the rest are clerics (42%), air
  elementalists (30%), and mystics (8%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Eaglet / the Tethered (novice)
  2. Winged Brother / Winged Sister (full priest)

  Beyond full priest, titles vary widely by temple and are frequently unique to the individual;
  among the winged elves of Mount Sundabar, common honorifics include Aquiline Hunter, Cloud Walker,
  Feathered Dancer, Rain Bringer, Rising Thermal, Silent Screech, Sky Diver, Soaring Spirit, and Wind
  Chaser.

### Dogma

- The ever-changing reaches of the sky are the great gift of the Winged Mother.
- Take flight into her windswept embrace, and gambol amidst the everchanging clouds.
- Honor those who dwell with the Lady of Air and Wind, and cherish the birds who dance on her tresses.
- In change there is beauty, and in chaos there is the birth of new life.
- Ascend, soar, glide, dive, and ascend again, and relish the freedom the Winged Mother bequeaths.
- The air is the breath of life.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Maintain good relations with sentient avian races, such as giant eagles and aarakocra.
- Develop new spells and magic items that permit flight, since few elven priests of Aerdrie can fly
  unaided; seek out lost relics that grant the same.
- Raise winged steeds for elven aerial cavalries and tend cotes of exotic birds for elven gardens and
  fashion.
- Work with elven farmers and horticulturists to secure favorable weather for their crops.
- Destroy evil avians -- eblis, perytons, simpathetics -- as perversions of nature.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dance of Swirling Winds | Vernal and autumnal equinoxes (semiannual) | The wind is always strong wherever Aerdrie's followers gather. Celebrants offer beautiful feathers and dance an aerial ballet to wind instruments; those without wings may ride the wind as a gift of the goddess, tethered by rope to a flying dancer, before being swept for miles across the forest canopy and set down gently near home. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Wing of Plumed Kingfishers -- an aerial military order of moon and gold elven crusaders
  and rangers, subdivided by mount species (asperii, giant eagles, griffons, hippogriffs, pegasi).
  Only two branches survive: the Wing of the Green Isle (Evermeet) and the Wing of the Evereskan Eyrie
  (Evereska).

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Halcyon
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Blowgun, bow (with flight arrows), dagger, dart, javelin, horseman's mace,
  quarterstaff, spear, staff-sling; favored weapon quarterstaff
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (leather) or Medium (elven chain mail)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Chaos, Creation, Divination, Elemental (Air, Water), Protection, Sun,
  Travelers, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Combat, Healing, Summoning
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in handle animal (avians), 1 rank in craft (bowyer/fletcher)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on lore (nature) (avians); +1 bonus on
  musical instrument (wind instrument); +1 bonus on weather sense

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2 bonus on reaction rolls when dealing with avian and semiavian creatures (such as pegasi and giant eagles) |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Feather Fall (10 mana) *or* Murdock's Feathery Flyer (10 mana), choose one per use, 1/day |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Ride the Wind (20 mana) *or* Whispering Wind (20 mana), choose one per use, 1/day |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Fly, Gust of Wind, *or* Protection from Normal Missiles (30 mana each), choose one per use, 1/day |
| 7 | May shapechange into any real-world bird (hummingbird to ostrich size) up to 3 times/day, each a different species per day; heals 10-60% of current damage upon each change; gains the bird's movement, AC, and attacks but not its abilities dependent on Intelligence |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Air Walk *or* Control Winds (50 mana each) *or* Flight of Remnis (40 mana), choose one per use, 1/day |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Control Weather *or* Wind Walk (70 mana each), choose one per use, 1/day |
| 15 | May summon one 16-HD air elemental once per tenday (mana ~8), which serves for 1 hour and cannot be controlled by anyone else; if the halcyon is killed or knocked unconscious the elemental rampages against everyone but its summoner until the hour elapses |

## Aerdrian Spells (deity-specific)

Priests of Aerdrie may also cast the 4th-level priest spell calm winds and the 7th-level priest spells
conjure air elemental and whirlwind, all detailed in Faiths & Avatars under Akadi.

| Name | Est. Mana | Foundation | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speak with Avians | 10 | Animal, Divination | Lets the priest converse with any non-mindless normal or giant bird, though cooperation and honesty are not guaranteed. |
| Wind Blast | 30 | Elemental Air | Blasts a cone of hurricane-force wind from the caster's hand, damaging man-sized creatures and knocking smaller ones backward, possibly stunning them against a hard surface. |
| Flight of Remnis | 40 | Animal, Summoning | Summons nearby birds of prey (up to 32 Hit Dice, or 48 if giant eagles are available) to fight alongside the caster until the task is done or they are dismissed. |
