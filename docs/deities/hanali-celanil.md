# Hanali Celanil (the Heart of Gold, Winsome Rose, Archer of Love, Kiss of Romance, Lady Goldheart)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Hanali Celanil
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Heart of Gold, Winsome Rose, Archer of Love, Kiss of Romance, Lady Goldheart
- **Portfolio:** Love, romance, beauty, fine art and artists
- **Symbol:** A heart of gold

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including multiclassed half-elven clerics), mystics, and specialty
  priests of Hanali receive religion (elf) and literacy (Espruar) as bonus proficiencies. Very few of
  Hanali's clerics have a Charisma score below 15, though this is not a hard requirement.
- **Organization:** Widely (if quietly) venerated across nearly every elven subrace except the drow.
  Gold elves see her as overseer of their galas and romances; moon elves see her as the most beautiful
  face of Angharradh; green, winged, and sea elves see her as the embodiment of natural beauty in the
  forest, mountains, or undersea. Temples are bright and beautiful, ringed with fountains, springs, and
  gardens designed to facilitate amorous trysts -- endless shady paths, babbling brooks, quiet pools,
  leafy bowers, and flowering hedgerows -- with interior chambers open to sun, moon, and breeze; many
  double as museums or concert halls.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. The Beauteous (novice)
  2. Paramour (full priest)
  3. Dove
  4. Suitor
  5. Lover
  6. Libertine
  7. Soft Caress
  8. Heart's Desire
  9. Fiery Ardor

  High-ranking priests beyond Fiery Ardor carry unique individual titles. Specialty priests are called
  goldhearts.

### Dogma

- Life is worth living because of the beauty found in the world and the love that draws twin hearts
  together.
- Nurture what is beautiful in life, and let beauty's glow enliven and brighten the lives of those
  around you.
- The greatest joy is the rapture of newfound love and the tide of romance that sweeps over those
  wrapped in its embrace.
- Seek out and care for love wherever it takes root, and bring it to its fullest bloom so all may share
  in the joy and beauty it creates.
- Always give shelter and succor to young lovers, for their hearts are the truest guides to life's
  proper course.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Preside over marriage and rite-of-passage ceremonies for young elves, though marriage itself is not
  required -- Hanali's concern is love, not necessarily marriage.
- Cultivate beauty and love in all forms: tend fine gardens, amass collections of gems, crystal
  sculptures, and other fine art (favoring gold and crystal, particularly jewelry and statues, though
  beautiful art of any kind is admired).
- Dress finely and display personal beauty to its best advantage, as is expected of every priest of
  the Heart of Gold.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Secrets of the Heart | Monthly, under the full moon | Romantically involved participants are said to experience the full bloom of their affections, evaluating the strength of their feelings; celebrants' inner beauty visibly manifests as a rosy glow in cheeks and eyes for days after. Offerings of beautiful objects are made, artists unveil new work, and young lovers often pledge their troth secretly or proclaim it publicly, inviting Hanali's favor. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Chaperones of the Moonlight Tryst -- romantically inclined rogues and rangers who
  discreetly safeguard young elven lovers from those who would exploit their distraction or innocence,
  and who facilitate secret meetings or elopements between lovers of rival houses; usually works
  closely with the local temple of Hanali.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Goldheart
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, dart, flail, lasso, mace, mancatcher, net, quarterstaff, sling, short
  bow; favored weapon dagger (per published 3.5e sources -- notably absent from this book's own weapon
  list for goldhearts, whose avatar fights with unarmed touch attacks rather than a named weapon)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (leather) or Medium (chain mail, elven chain mail), shields
  allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Chaos, Charm, Creation, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic, Plant,
  Protection, Sun
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Wards, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in etiquette, 1 rank in herbalism
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on art; +1 bonus on dancing; +1 bonus on
  singing
