# Gond (Wonderbringer, the Lord of All Smiths, the Inspiration Divine, the Holy Maker of All Things)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Gond
- **Titles/Epithets:** Wonderbringer, the Lord of All Smiths, the Inspiration Divine, the Holy Maker of All Things
- **Portfolio:** Artifice, craft, construction, smithwork
- **Symbol:** A shining toothed wheel or cog with four spokes, in ivory, bone, or metal

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** Gondarism is the official state religion of Lantan, a hotbed of invention.
  Clerics (called Krii, Lantanna for "disadvantaged") outnumber specialty priests roughly 15:1
  across most of Faerûn, but the ratio nearly reverses in Lantan (about 20 Gondsmen per cleric,
  and most Lantanna merchants met abroad are Gondsmen). One cleric holds the post of Most Holy
  Avenue for Spreading the Faith, nominal supreme authority for all worshipers outside Lantan.
  Clergy call themselves the Consecrated of Gond, or "fellow Consecrates," and obedience to a
  superior is unquestioning despite Gondar acting independently in their day-to-day duties.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Wonderer (novice)
  2. Seeker Postulant (priest in training)
  3. Seeker after Small Things (confirmed priest)
  4. Greater Seeker
  5. Seeker of the Twelfth Order, counting down through Seeker of the Eleventh Order, Tenth Order, and so on to Seeker of the First Order
  6. High Seeker (title held by all senior clergy)
  7. Master (leader of a religious community, or one who tends a holy site)
  8. Artificer (personally rewarded and named by Gond for special service)
  9. High Artificer (supreme priest of the faith)

### Dogma

- "Actions count" -- the result matters most, not intention or talk.
- Strive to make new things that work.
- Become skilled at forging, casting, or tempering, and at joining and fastening things to fit any need.
- To venerate Gond is to continually question and challenge the unknown with new devices and items.
- Elegance and usefulness are the two legs any new making should stand on.
- Practice experimentation and innovation, and encourage these virtues in others through direct aid,
  sponsorship, and diplomatic support.
- Encourage farmers, hunters, and others to think of new tools and better ways of doing things.
- Keep records of one's strivings, ideas, and attempts, so others can continue the work.
- Observe, acquire, and safely store the makings of others, and share what is learned with fellow Consecrated.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Keep the formulas for smoke powder and various sealants, cleansers, and lubricants secret, selling
  small quantities while traveling.
- Sell buckles, brass bells, mortars and pestles, monocles, and lenses for income.
- Protect church trade secrets (such as the special storage jars for smoke powder) from rival makers
  in Calimshan and the Tashalar through sabotage, diplomacy, and financial influence.
- Establish caches, investments, and alliances while traveling, and collect samples of new inventions
  encountered.
- Assist inventors and innovators, filing regular reports to the nearest Master by messenger envoy.
- Settling permanently in one place is frowned upon unless the locale is a hub of ongoing innovation
  worth constant watching (such as Waterdeep, Athkatla, or Suzail).
- Making a handsome personal living is encouraged, as a demonstration of the rewards of following Gond.
- Much in demand as builders, especially of vaulted and buttressed temples for other faiths -- a
  lucrative but rivalry-generating trade.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ippensheir | The 12 days immediately following Greengrass | Named for Ippen, Gond's first great cleric (who sometimes appears as Gond's First Servant); clergy gather at a temple or holy site to share and show off inventions, with feasting, drinking, and revelry, often using teleport magic and the priesthood's gate network to attend multiple gatherings. |
| The Sacred Unmaking | Whenever a new tool or machine is made | Two copies are made of any new invention: one is hidden away for later display, the other is destroyed (preferably burned) while a prayer of offering to Gond is chanted, reinforcing his dominion over both constructive and destructive engineering. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Informal) Order of Puissant Stonemasons and Stonecarvers
- (Informal) Holy Order of Most Skilled Architects and Bridgemakers
- (Informal) Armorers of the Wonderbringer
- (Informal) Most Arcane Order of Gearmakers, Clockmakers, and Automationists
- (Informal) Society of Creative Castle Design and Construction
- (Informal) Industrious Brothers and Sisters of Carpentry, Cabinetry, Puppetry, and Toymaking
- The church of Gond has no knightly orders; the above are honorary trade societies that recognize
  Gondar work in a given specialty and control the leak of trade/church secrets to competitors.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Gondsman
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons, crossbows, and firearms such as the arquebus (where
  available); favored weapon warhammer
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Heavy (up to and including plate mail); no shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Combat, Divination, Elemental, Numbers, Protection, Sun, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Creation, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic
- **Magical Items:** No limitations; additionally may use all magical staves, wands, and rods
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (pick one: armorer, blacksmithing, carpentry,
  leatherworking, engineering, mining, pottery, stonemasonry, or weaponsmithing) or art (any one)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); pick one: art (any one), craft (any one)
