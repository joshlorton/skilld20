# Dumathoin (Keeper of Secrets under the Mountain, the Silent Keeper, the Mountain Shield)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Dumathoin
- **Titles/Epithets:** Keeper of Secrets under the Mountain, the Silent Keeper, the Mountain Shield
- **Portfolio:** Keeper of metals and other buried wealth, the earth's riches, ores, gems, minerals,
  mining, exploration, the shield dwarf race, guardian of the dead
- **Symbol:** A cut, faceted gem set inside the silhouette of a mountain; the physical holy symbol
  actually worn by priests is a miniature silver pick

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics), crusaders, and specialty priests of Dumathoin
  receive the standard dwarven clergy grants (literacy (Dethek runes), modified turn undead) -- see
  `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** All dwarves who live or venture underground or in the mountains, or who work
  directly with the earth's riches, worship (or at least appease) the Silent Keeper; miners often
  carry a small gemstone to attract his favor. Temples and shrines are common among shield dwarves
  (and merely uncommon among gold dwarves) but rare among the other subraces, built in the deepest,
  best-hidden natural caverns, ideally with veins of ore or gem-studded rock nearby. Altars are natural
  boulders or plain stone blocks. Dumathoin's priests also serve as the dwarves' morticians and tomb
  protectors, a role held since the days of the lost realm of Ammarindar. The clergy is nearly evenly
  split between specialty priests (45%) and clerics (44%), with fighter/clerics (6%) and crusaders (5%)
  filling out the rest; by subrace it is shield dwarf (80%), gold dwarf (18%), and gray dwarf (1%), and
  it is the one dwarven clergy that regularly admits nondwarves (humans, rock gnomes, stout halflings,
  svirfneblin) as clerics, crusaders, or fighter/clerics. The priesthood remains predominantly male
  (97%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. The Uncut (novice)
  2. Keeper of the Shield (full priest, upon taking the Silent Vow)
  3. Agate
  4. Onyx
  5. Amethyst
  6. Jargoon
  7. Garnet
  8. Topaz
  9. Opal
  10. Sapphire
  11. Diamond
  12. Beljuril (a High Old One, 7th level or higher)

### Dogma

- Walk the deep and silent ways of Dumathoin. Seek out the hidden gifts of the Keeper of Secrets under
  the Mountain.
- That which is hidden is precious, and that which is precious shall stay hidden.
- Seek to enhance the natural beauty of Dumathoin's gifts, and go with, not against, the contours of
  the deeps. Beauty is in the discovery and the crafting, not the holding.
- Keep the places of our dead inviolate and well tended; the noble ancestors of our race will neither
  be robbed nor mocked.
- Abide no undead creatures, especially those that take the form of dwarves, for they mock the
  creation of Moradin.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Uncover the buried wealth of the earth without marring the beauty of the ways beneath the surface or
  being overly greedy; supervise mining operations and maintain underground safety and security.
- Clean up the rubble of mining, grow luminous fungi and edible deep-mosses, and direct water through
  the earth to serve the underlife (dwarves included).
- Hunt for new veins of ore, new sources of useful fungi, and unexplored delves; identify underground
  dangers and how best to deal with them, and bargain with nonhostile Underdark races to avoid
  over-exploitation of shared resources.
- Serve as the primary morticians and tomb protectors of the dwarves, presiding over burials that
  always include washing the body, braiding personal, clan, and Dumathoin's own stone tokens into the
  deceased's beard, arming and armoring the corpse, and carving a memorial song into the coffin or
  tomb marker -- never sung aloud, on pain of a curse said to fall on whoever breaks that silence.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Deepstone Triad | Nights of the new moon, and the days to either side | Held holy because the moon itself is considered hidden deep beneath the surface during this span; gems and jewelry are sacrificed on temple altars, then pulverized with herbs and fungal secretions into a paste priests use to nourish plant growth and beautify (or conceal) the underways. |
| Splendarrsonn | Irregular, declared by a High Old One | A special holy day proclaimed when dwarves discover a major new lode, a lost subterranean treasure cache, or a significant new delve. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Knights of the Mithral Shield -- 300 Dumathan crusaders and multiclassed
  delvesonn/fighters based in Citadel Adbar, serving as elite honor guard sworn to protect the shield
  dwarves as Dumathoin is forever pledged to do.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Delvesonn
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapons mattock & maul
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Creation, Elemental Earth, Elemental Fire, Healing, Necromantic,
  Protection, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Guardian, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (blacksmithing), 1 rank in craft (pick one: armorer,
  weaponsmithing)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on literacy (Dethek runes); +1 bonus on
  craft (mining); +1 bonus on survival (mountaineering); +1 bonus on survival (mountains); +1 bonus on
  survival (Underdark)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Protected at all times as if by a ring of mind shielding; immune to ESP, detect lie, know alignment, and comparable psionic powers |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Detect Metals and Minerals *or* Fist of Stone (10 mana each), choose one per use |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Meld into Stone *or* Stone Shape (30 mana each), choose one per use |
| 7 | Identify at will (as a wizard of the same level; no mana cost) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Stoneskin (40 mana) |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Passwall (50 mana) |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Speak with Stones (180 mana; 6th-level spell, three times per day) |

In extremely rare cases a single-classed delvesonn especially favored by the Silent Keeper is chosen
to continue advancing past 14th level without the usual triple-experience penalty. Over the following
decade such a Chosen of Dumathoin slowly petrifies into living rock -- gray stone skin, a stalactite
beard, emerald-sparked eyes -- and becomes a priestly archlich, retaining full mobility and acting as a
normal dwarven priest despite the transformation.
