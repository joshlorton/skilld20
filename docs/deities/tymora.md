# Tymora (Lady Luck, the Lady Who Smiles, Our Smiling Lady, Tyche's Fair-Haired Daughter)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Tymora
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lady Luck, the Lady Who Smiles, Our Smiling Lady, Tyche's Fair-Haired Daughter
- **Portfolio:** Good fortune, skill, victory, adventurers, and adventuring
- **Symbol:** A shining, featureless disk of silver (in some temples represented instead as a
  floating, slowly turning sphere of everbright silver)

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** Highly independent -- each Tymoran temple is its own operation with its own
  clergy, reflecting the tastes of its high priestess or priest. A recent push by Daramos Lauthyr, High
  Priest of the Lady's House in Arabel (where Tymora's avatar manifested and remained during the Time
  of Troubles), to unify the church under a single grand patriarchal seat is being strongly resisted by
  the other temples. Both sexes and all races are equal in Tymora's eyes, though human women hold most
  of the higher ranks in practice; a few elves and half-elves serve despite a chilly reception from
  elven society. Mystics of Tymora serve both within temple ranks and as itinerant agents answering to
  no one but the goddess.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** Titles are used and changed with informality. "Lord Priest" and
  "Lady Priestess" are respectful forms of address for all clergy; "High" is prefixed for clergy senior
  in years or demonstrated power, who are referred to as "the High." Other special designations
  include "favored of Tymora" (one personally chosen by the goddess to enter her clergy), "fallen of
  Tymora" (one who has left her service and spurned chances for atonement), and "Atalara" (a priestess
  who has at some point been directly possessed by the goddess, marked afterward by deep blue body
  hair and bright silver eye pupils).

### Dogma

- Be bold, for to dare is to live -- "Fortune favors the bold."
- A brave heart and a willingness to take risks beat a carefully wrought plan nine times out of ten.
- Place yourself in the hands of fate (that is, in Tymora's hands) and trust to your own luck.
- The Lady's own luck never fails; any apparent misfortune she suffers is a deliberate test, though the
  faithful should not speak of this to outsiders.
- Show your good fortune -- and your acceptance of bad fortune -- as confidence in the Lady and in
  yourself.
- Chase your own unique goals; it is in this chase that the Lady lends her aid.
- Those with no direction or goals soon know the embrace of Beshaba, the Lady's dark sister, for those
  on no set course are at the mercy of misfortune, which is no mercy at all.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Urge folk throughout Faerûn to take chances and pursue their dreams rather than endlessly plan and
  dare nothing (this does not mean encouraging reckless whims or frivolity).
- Once counsel is given, aid those who have dared with healing spells and other magical support
  (sometimes surreptitiously), reinforcing the lesson that trusting in Tymora brings good fortune.
- Observe a greeting ritual among the faithful: touching silver holy-symbol disks together (often with
  an embrace) after exchanging watchwords of recognition. To possible worshipers: "Life is short. Live
  it as Tymora means it to be lived!" -- answered "Dare all, and trust in the Lady." Between known
  clergy, simply: "Defy," answered "Dare much."
- No fixed tithing or sacrifice practice is specified in the source; support for the church comes
  through its wealth and the gratitude of the adventurers it aids.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Midsummer | Midsummer (annual) | A wild, nightlong revel of reckless, mischievous derring-do and romantic trysts. Wandering clergy gather to meet with Harpers, allied faiths, and relatives, and many missions and plans are laid. |
| Starfall | 22nd of Marpenoth | The most holy Tymoran festival, marking the day of Tyche's destruction and Tymora's birth (a date the rival clergy of Beshaba dispute). Clergy who have earned advancement are formally acclaimed and presented with tokens and vestments appropriate to their new station. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* An ongoing relationship with the Harpers, the secret good-aligned society active
  throughout Faerûn.
- *(Informal)* The church sponsors some adventuring companies, and countless independent adventuring
  groups have dedicated themselves to Lady Luck after she smiled on them in a difficult moment.
- *(Informal)* Fellows of Free Fate ("Triffs") -- a fellowship within the church dedicated to
  countering the efforts of Beshaba, especially her assassins, the Black Fingers. Open to any clergy
  member who shows experience and dedication and is vouched for by a senior Fellow.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Luckbringer
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon shuriken
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Chaos, Charm, Creation, Divination, Healing, Necromantic, Protection,
  Summoning, Travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Guardian, Sun, Wards, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** None
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon) and gaming checks; +1 bonus on acrobatics checks
