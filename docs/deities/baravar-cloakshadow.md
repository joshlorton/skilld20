# Baravar Cloakshadow (The Sly One, Master of Illusion, Lord in Disguise, Bane of Goblinkin)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Baravar Cloakshadow
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Sly One, Master of Illusion, Lord in Disguise, Bane of Goblinkin
- **Portfolio:** Illusions, deceptions, traps, wardings
- **Symbol:** A cloak and dagger

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics, cleric/illusionists, and cleric/thieves) and
  specialty priests receive bonus ranks in religion (pantheon) and literacy (Ruathiek).
- **Organization:** Temples are disguised as ordinary businesses, hiding secret chapels behind doors
  guarded by tricks, traps, and illusions; interiors are cloaked in shifting illusions and
  omnipresent shadow meant to befuddle intruders. No fixed hierarchy of titles is discernible --
  priests employ a wide variety of titles that seem to change to suit the situation. Clergy is 60%
  rock gnome, 30% deep gnome, 10% forest gnome (60% male); composition is specialty priests (40%),
  cleric/illusionists (30%), illusionists (12%), cleric/thieves (10%), fighter/clerics (5%), and
  clerics (3%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. The Cloaked (novice)
  2. The Illusory (full priest -- no further fixed hierarchy; individual titles are adopted
     situationally and change frequently)

### Dogma

- The world is a dangerous place, and the only sure defense is to cloak oneself in shadows under a
  web of deception.
- Strive to master the art of illusion and the game of deceit, for therein lies security.
- Protect yourself and other gnomes; do not completely trust anyone who has not proven themselves.
- Hope for the best, but prepare for the worst in life and in others' behavior.
- If folk do you or yours ill, do not fear showing them the error of their ways through a few pointed
  jokes.
- In desperate times, use illusion and camouflage to make sure the right side wins -- yours.
- Do not flout laws openly, but do what is best for those in your care, whether or not that course is
  approved by those in authority.
- Devote yourself to your art and those you love with equal fervor, for one must have a reason to
  live beyond mere survival.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Refine the art of illusion; a sizable portion of the clergy adventures to find new illusion/phantasm
  spells and magical items.
- Work as researchers, ever-refining the craft of illusion.
- Serve communities as spies and investigative agents, teaching disguise, camouflage, and hiding.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Cloaking | Monthly, on the night of the new moon | Location and nature vary every time; it is a point of honor that the gathering is never detected as such by outsiders (leading to a common gnome joke that any unexplained gathering of two or more gnomes is "another meeting of the Illusory"). Priests offer illusions of things they have seen, heard, or sensed -- the greater the realism, the greater the god's pleasure. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Knights of the Shadowy Cloak -- cells in most gnome communities where Baravar is
  venerated, drawing specialty priests, illusionists, cleric/illusionists, fighter/illusionists, and
  illusionist/thieves; dedicated to driving away or exterminating goblinkin threats without drawing
  retaliation, often by directing the militant talents of nongnome allies in multiracial adventuring
  bands.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Hoodwinker
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, dagger, dart, hand crossbow, knife, lasso, short bow, short sword,
  sling, staff, staff-sling
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (leather, studded leather) or Medium (elven chain mail)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Chaos, Charm, Creation, Guardian, Healing, Protection, Thought, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, Divination, Sun, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in dagger
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on disguise; +1 bonus on survival
  (trapping/set snares)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | May select nonweapon proficiencies from the rogue group without penalty; may hide in shadows as a ranger of the same level; may cast wizard spells from the illusion/phantasm school (limited wizard spellcasting) |

| 3 | +2 bonus to saving throws vs. illusion/phantasm spells |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Improved Phantasmal Force *or* Invisibility, choose one per use (20 mana each) |
