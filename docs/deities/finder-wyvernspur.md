# Finder Wyvernspur (The Nameless Bard)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Finder Wyvernspur
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Nameless Bard
- **Portfolio:** The cycle of life (rot to rebirth), transformation of art, saurials
- **Symbol:** A white harp on a gray circle

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Bardic performers
- **All Clergy:** All clerics and specialty priests of Finder Wyvernspur receive religion (Faerûnian) as
  a bonus proficiency; this grant does not extend to bards or spellsingers.
- **Organization:** Finder ascended to godhood only recently, after slaying the evil god Moander, and is
  still establishing his following. Worship is currently limited to a handful of arrogant young bardic
  prodigies and artists, some of the saurials of the Lost Vale, a few music aficionados, and the halfling
  bard Olive Ruskettle. His actual ordained clergy at present consists of only two people: a saurial
  priestess serving the Lost Vale and a young human bard from Berdusk who is presently adventuring in the
  Heartlands. The clergy is expected to expand rapidly over the coming decades and eventually settle into
  a composition of roughly 44% specialty priests, 44% bards, 10% clerics, and 2% spellsingers. Saurials
  and halflings, not just humans, may become finders. No standard temple architecture has been settled on
  yet, but common themes are expected to include large performance spaces, good acoustics, and services
  built around musical accompaniment or interludes.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** None established yet -- the faith is too small and too new to have
  settled on formal titles.

### Dogma

- Art forced to remain immutable is a sign of stagnation and spiritual rot; to thrive, art (and the
  artist) must be willing to change and transform.
- Art has the power to influence people and politics; when it can be used to keep people free from
  social or political tyranny, that use is proper, though it should not be art's sole purpose.
- No distinction exists between secular and religious art -- all art is pleasing to Finder.
- Encourage artistic expression in everyone, child and adult, amateur and professional alike, while
  still admiring those who master formal disciplines of expression.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Help emerging artists find an opportunity -- a tavern willing to let a young bard perform, a shop
  willing to carry a struggling sculptor's work, a festhall in need of a dancer -- then step back and let
  the artist sink or swim.
- Train and mentor young talent, especially the saurial soul singers of the Lost Vale, in music and
  verse.
- Welcome pilgrims and curiosity-seekers who travel to places tied to Finder's mortal life.
- Compose and perform music and other art in Finder's honor, and encourage others -- particularly the
  young -- to create, including songs critical of the powerful.
- Tithe: gold offerings are accepted but not required; artistic works are considered the truer, more
  proper tithe.

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* The Shrine Keepers -- small pockets of followers who live near the shrines established
  throughout the Western Heartlands, tending, preparing, and protecting them from defilement.
- *(Informal)* The saurial soul singers of the Lost Vale -- trained by Finder's priestess and sometimes
  counted as an order of Finderites, though not all of them actually follow Finder.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Finder
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapon bastard sword ("Sword of Songs" per real-world 3.5e
  sourcing, matching the magic sword of dancing that Finder himself wields as his avatar's weapon)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light and Medium armor (up to and including chain mail); no shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Divination, Elemental, Travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Healing
- **Magical Items:** No limitations beyond the standard cleric/bard restrictions; anathema items
  restricted to wizards only
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in art (any) *or* performance (any instrument), choose one
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on direction sense; +1 bonus on
  performance (singing); +1 bonus on performance (harp)

Finders (this specialty priest track) are open to saurials and halflings in addition to humans.
