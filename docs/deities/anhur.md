# Anhur (General of the Gods, Champion of Physical Prowess, Supreme Marshal of All Armies, the Falcon of War, God of War, Thunder, Rain, and Storms)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Anhur
- **Titles/Epithets:** General of the Gods, Champion of Physical Prowess, Supreme Marshal of All Armies,
  the Falcon of War, God of War, Thunder, Rain, and Storms
- **Portfolio:** War, conflict, physical prowess, thunder, rain, storms
- **Symbol:** A khopesh with a hawk's-head pommel bound with a cord, or (an older form) a bird of prey;
  in his Untheric aspect as Ramathant, a lightning bolt through a storm cloud

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders)
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, specialty priests, and crusaders of Anhur receive religion (Mulhorandi),
  literacy (Mulhorandi), and linguistics (Common) as bonus skills, and all know Mulhorandi as their
  native tongue. All of Anhur's clergy must be humans of Mulhorandi extraction.
- **Organization:** Largely hereditary; almost all specialty priests belong to the House of Ramathant,
  human descendants of Anhur's divine incarnations. The clergy is split nearly evenly between clerics,
  specialty priests (militars), and crusaders. All priests are addressed publicly as "Lord Priest," or
  "Lord High Priest" at 9th level or higher, but internal titles vary widely temple to temple --
  Bloodletter, Warmaker, Honor Brother/Sister, Swordarm, Lionclaw, Defender, and Commander are all in
  use. Temples are built as impregnable citadels -- steep-roofed, stone-walled castles with extensive
  armories and dungeons stocked with nonperishable supplies. Anhur's cult was once second only to Re's
  in size; it fell from favor after Re's death amid rivalry with the priesthood of Horus-Re (who blame
  Anhur's clergy for Mulhorand's military losses and hire Chessentan mercenaries in preference to
  Anhur-worshiping native soldiers), but has been rebuilding vigorously, including converting many of
  those same Chessentan mercenaries.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** No single fixed hierarchy is given -- titles vary by temple (see
  Organization above); the only universal distinction is the "Lord Priest" / "Lord High Priest" (9th
  level+) form of address.

### Dogma

- Defend the territory of Mulhorand out to its historic boundaries; smite the realm's enemies and keep
  its people -- highborn or slave -- safe from evil.
- Obey honorable leaders and behave with honor, as a representative of Anhur.
- Show bravery and valor in combat, and protect brothers and sisters in arms.
- Follow a commander's wise advice as a parent's, but refuse any command to do ill -- such a refusal
  will be vindicated by a council of at least three peers.
- Protect the people of Mulhorand and the church's property as fiercely as a lioness defends her cubs.
- Beware the wiles of Set, whose spies are everywhere and whose taint turns brother against sister and
  parent against child.
- Since the Time of Troubles, counter the weather magic of the Red Wizards of Thay, particularly in
  Unther.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Spend mornings practicing martial skills, afternoons drilling Mulhorand's legions, and evenings
  repairing weapons and armor or studying strategy.
- Develop and execute unorthodox, daring plans to regain Mulhorand's former glory, and counter the
  political machinations of the priesthood of Horus-Re.
- Undertake ambitious defensive projects even at great cost -- the clergy considers a plan successful
  merely for having defended Mulhorand's borders, regardless of losses suffered in the attempt.

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Guardians of Skuld -- a joint order of guardians drawn from the priesthoods of Anhur,
  Osiris, and Isis, formalized since the Time of Troubles; leadership has reverted to Anhur's temple
  from Horus-Re's clergy.
- *(Formal)* Order of the Watchful Lion -- primarily crusaders (with some clerics and specialty
  priests), forming the front line of Mulhorand's defense; members often take extended leave to
  adventure beyond Mulhorand's borders and report back on developments abroad.
- *(Informal)* Hundreds of informal fraternities roughly corresponding to companies of troops in
  Mulhorand's armies.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Militar
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any weapon, though the lance is the only polearm permitted; favored weapon
  lance -- real-world Egyptian mythology consistently depicts Onuris/Anhur wielding a spear or lance
  and even names him "Lord of Lances"; superseding both the earlier D&D 3.5e citation (warhawk/falchion)
  and this book's own khopesh implication, and cleanly matching the lance already singled out as this
  track's one permitted polearm
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Combat, Elemental (air, water), Guardian, Protection, Sun, War, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Elemental (earth, fire), Healing, Necromantic, Time
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in khopesh or scimitar (pick one), 1 rank in charioteering, 1 rank in
  linguistics (Untheric)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on endurance

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2, "once per tenday" = 10 × level
× (1/10) = level × 1). Where no spell level is given but a class/character level is, use spell level =
that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Gains 2 bonus hit points per level of experience (on top of any Constitution bonus), until 9th level; also receives Constitution hit point adjustments as a warrior |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Stumble *or* Command, choose one per use (10 mana each), once per day |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Armor *or* Shield, choose one per use (10 mana each), once per day |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Bird of Prey (30 mana), once per day |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Thunderstroke (80 mana; twice per day) |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Flame Strike (50 mana), once per day |
