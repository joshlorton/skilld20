# Kossuth (the Tyrant Among Fire, the Firelord)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Kossuth
- **Titles/Epithets:** the Tyrant Among Fire, the Firelord
- **Portfolio:** Elemental fire, fire elementalists, purification through fire
- **Symbol:** A springing flame or a flaming orb

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** spellcasters, men-at-arms, monastic orders. Since the Time of Troubles, Kossuth has
  granted no new clerics -- formerly clerics existed, but the god now sees only specialty priests as
  fit to be closest to him.
- **Organization:** Priests are organized into two factions -- the Tendrils of Flame, who run temples,
  preach, and perform rituals/holidays and hold most of the church's power; and the Burning Braziers
  (the "Brazier Brigade"), the adventuring arm, often embittered former Tendrils who venture into
  unclaimed lands to spread the faith (and, pragmatically, look for weakly defended territory to
  exploit). Each temple is led by an Eternal Flame (a specialty priest), under whom serve three Most
  Fervid Fires, each overseeing two Numinous Blazes, each of whom oversees one of every lower rank
  down to Devoted Blaze. Promotion is by experience up to Devoted Blaze, then by recommendation of two
  higher-ranking priests when an opening occurs, subject to the Eternal Flame's absolute veto/override.
  Shamans serve nomadic or primitive communities independent of any temple.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Lightless (novice)
  2. Torch of the Faith
  3. Righteous Flame
  4. Devoted Blaze
  5. Zealous Pyre
  6. Pillar of Flame
  7. Fury of the Faith
  8. Flamebrother / Flamesister
  9. Inspired Forge
  10. Numinous Blaze
  11. Most Fervid Fire
  12. Eternal Flame of Kossuth

### Dogma

- Fire and purity are one and the same; the faith of Kossuth is innately superior to all others,
  especially the other elemental lords and above all Istishia.
- Change to a higher state is always accompanied by difficulty and personal pain -- "no pain, no gain."
- Follow the Promised above you, for they have proven their worth and achieved a higher state you too
  can find in Kossuth's service.
- Find the true vision, the final goal of your life, and pursue it utterly; give yourself totally to
  the cause, and it will return eternal rewards.
- Guide the teeming masses to the pure light of Kossuth so that he may reforge all life into its
  essential form, bringing complete order and harmony.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- The Lightless tend the temple's sacred fires so they never go out.
- Senior clergy plan ceremonies (weddings, funerals, fire-walkings), instruct novices, and tend to the
  temple's day-to-day growth, health, and wealth.
- Most priests pursue promotion relentlessly; temple leadership grants near-total personal license, and
  most leaders grow into small, power-hungry tyrants.
- Temples pursue conquest, land acquisition, wealth, and rulership, allying with whoever is expedient
  while enforcing a strict internal code.
- Tithe/offering: burnt offerings of garnets, topazes, citrine, fine iron or precious-metal smithwork,
  nuts, fine oils, aromatic resins, and meat -- Kossuth rewards generous and faithful givers more than
  newcomers or token offerings.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Oath of Firewalking | Ordination rite (not calendar-fixed) | Initiation granting a novice first-level spells as a full priest. The oath is tested again at each rise in rank via a longer, hotter walk over burning coals; doubt or hidden treachery is said to cause horrific burns or death on the coals, while the pure-hearted walk unscathed. |
| Daily Devotions | Every day, at sunrise and highsun | The faithful pray before meals, thanking Kossuth for the hidden fire of life energy that burns in all things. |
| The Unity of Fire | As needed, not calendar-fixed | Rite allowing specialty priests to call fire elementals from the Elemental Plane of Fire. Preceded by a day of constant chanting and prayer; the summoned elemental is given a meal prepared by the celebrant and a coffer of worked copper or precious metal full of coins, gems, and jewelry to carry back to Kossuth. |
| Eternal Flame's Birthdate Festival | Annual, on each temple's Eternal Flame's birthdate | Local festival where the Eternal Flame invites high/senior priests from other temples and local dignitaries, pampering and courting them as future allies. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Knights of the Fire Drake -- the crusading military order of Kossuth; guards holy sites,
  leads holy campaigns, and provides personal protection to Eternal Flames. Its members are the only
  priests permitted to wear armor during temple ceremonies.
- (Formal) Disciples of the Phoenix -- good-aligned monastic order of Faithful Flames.
- (Formal) Brothers and Sisters of the Pure Flame -- neutral-aligned monastic order of Faithful Flames.
- (Formal) Disciples of the Salamander -- evil-aligned monastic order of Faithful Flames.
- (Informal) The church maintains close ties to the Red Wizards of Thay, many of whom are Kossuthans;
  several zulkirs actively collaborate with the church on regional power plays and conquest.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Firewalker
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon spiked chain
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Medium armor, shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Elemental Fire, Combat, Healing, Summoning, Sun
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Elemental Air, Elemental Earth, Protection, War
- **Magical Items:** anathema water-based/underwater items
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in survival (fire-building)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on survival (pick one terrain from
  woodland, desert, steppe, mountain, or tropical); +1 bonus on craft (blacksmithing)
- **Other Clergy Grants:** Shamans of Kossuth gain Elemental Fire as an additional major sphere
  alongside their normal spheres -- this does not apply to Firewalkers, who already have it natively.
