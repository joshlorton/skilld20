# Yondalla (The Protector and Provider, the Nurturing Matriarch, the Blessed One)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Yondalla
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Protector and Provider, the Nurturing Matriarch, the Blessed One
- **Portfolio:** Protection, fertility, the halfling race, children, security, leadership,
  diplomacy, wisdom, the cycle of life, creation, family and familial love, tradition, community,
  harmony, prosperity
- **Symbol:** A shield with a cornucopia

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, mystics, and specialty priests also receive literacy (common) as a
  bonus nonweapon proficiency, in addition to the baseline religion (pantheon) grant.
- **Organization:** Formal temples are remarkably rare despite Yondalla's near-universal veneration;
  most worship happens in small shrines and in the home, and a community's "temple" is usually
  little more than the home of the local priest. Where true churches exist, they are carved into
  earthen hillsides resembling halfling burrows, blending fortification (armories, cisterns,
  granaries) with residential and worship space. Clergy is 55% hairfeet, 30% stouts, 15%
  tallfellows; nearly evenly divided between specialty priests (37%), mystics (33%), and clerics
  (30%); women (60%) slightly outnumber men (40%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Blessed Child (novice)
  2. Blessed Sister/Brother
  3. Sacred Guardian
  4. Revered Nurturer
  5. Blessed Mother/Father
  6. Eminent Prodigal
  7. August Warden
  8. Hallowed Provider
  9. Exalted Protector (full priest, "Revered Councilor")

  High-ranking priests also carry unique individual titles. Specialty priests are called horn
  guards.

### Dogma

- Those who seek to live in accordance with the way of the Provider will be blessed with a
  cornucopia of riches.
- Seek peace and comfort, for a life lived with both is true wealth.
- Although violence should never be welcomed, the Protector's aegis extends to those willing to
  fiercely defend their home and community.
- Lead through example, and know the activities of those you lead so you can help shoulder their
  burdens when need be.
- Treasure your family, for your parents gave you life and your children are your future.
- Care for the aged and the weak, for you never know when you may be one of the strong laid low.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Protect halfling communities from outside threats, serving as ever-vigilant sentinels over fields
  and burrows.
- Many double as secular leaders of their communities as well as religious authorities.
- Officiate weddings and (jointly with Urogalan's clergy) funerals.
- Teach collective and self-defense, concealment, agriculture, brewing, wine-making, gardening, and
  cooking, using granted spells to demonstrate or enhance such activities.
- Bring communities together through regular feasts, revels, and celebrations.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Safeday | Fifth day of each tenday | Families gather to offer up the fruits of the goddess's bounty and prepare a tenday's feast together while local clergy visit house to house leading brief devotions. In the late afternoon the community assembles for a symbolic walking tour ("joining Yondalla in her defense of the settlement") followed by a community-wide dinner feast and traditional storytelling; strangers are often welcomed to partake, though discreetly observed. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Wayward Wardens -- a loosely organized fellowship of wanderlust-stricken Yondallan
  priests who forgo settling with one community, instead coming to the defense of besieged or
  threatened halfling communities in need of additional protectors.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Horn Guard
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All weapons; favored weapon short sword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shield required at all
  times
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Astral, Combat, Creation, Divination, Elemental (Air, Earth,
  Water), Guardian, Healing, Law, Necromantic, Plant, Protection, Summoning, Sun, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in short sword, 1 rank in agriculture, 1 rank in lore (local history)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on lore (ancient history); +1 bonus on
  craft (cooking)
- **Other Clergy Grants:** All Yondallan clergy (not just horn guards) may also cast Reed Staff (a
  1st-level spell detailed in the Sheela Peryroyl entry) once per day.
