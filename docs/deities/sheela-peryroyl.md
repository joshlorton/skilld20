# Sheela Peryroyl (Green Sister, the Wise, the Watchful Mother)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Sheela Peryroyl
- **Titles/Epithets:** Green Sister, the Wise, the Watchful Mother
- **Portfolio:** Nature, agriculture, weather, song, dance, beauty, romantic love
- **Symbol:** A daisy

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** Temples are woven into the surrounding landscape -- built of earth, stone, and
  plants, containing both tended gardens and untamed thickets, usually sited in the heart of
  agricultural valleys ringed by wilderness. Clergy is 65% hairfeet, 10% stouts, 25% tallfellows;
  females (78%) greatly outnumber males (22%); the clergy is mostly druids (51%), mystics (23%),
  specialty priests (21%), and clerics (5%). Specialty priests are druids and greenfosters:
  greenfosters concentrate on halfling villages and farms, while druids go wherever needed.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Seedling (novice)
  2. Daisy Maid/Lad
  3. Seed Sower
  4. Nature Nurturer
  5. Plant Grower
  6. Crop Harvester
  7. Seed Pollinator
  8. Sun Shower
  9. Watchful Sister/Brother (full priest, "Green Daughter/Son", collectively "Green Children")

  High-ranking priests also carry unique individual titles.

### Dogma

- Living in harmony with nature requires a careful balance between the wild and the tame, the feral
  and the tended.
- The need to preserve wild growth is just as important as the need to till the fields and provide
  ready food.
- Seek to understand the natural processes that envelop and work within them.
- Nature can be adapted, but should be evolved, never forced; work within the framework of what
  already exists.
- Celebrating life requires one to live with passion and romance. Revel, feast, and thrive -- this is
  the zest of life.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Work closely with halfling farmers and settlers to preserve the balance between cultivating
  fertile land and leaving some areas wild.
- Tend gardens, developing new strains of crops and flowers, or protect wilderness regions from
  careless exploitation.
- Oversee the integrity of halfling lands, leading the annual calendar of seed-sowing and harvest
  festivals.
- Guide wild creatures to travel, live, or grow around settled halfling areas rather than through
  them.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gatherings | Twilight of every full moon (monthly) | Community-wide feasts celebrating the bounteous produce of the earth; all are expected to contribute, even if only a single stone for the soup (echoing the halfling "stone soup" folktale). |
| The Seeding (New Spring) | Around Greengrass, date varies by year | At dawn, Sheelite priests dispense seeds from temple stores while the entire community aids in sowing the first crops of the year. |
| High Harvest (the Reaping) | Around Higharvestide, date varies by year | Offerings of seeds and the fruits of the season's labor are brought to the temple for storage; evening and night revelry follows once the work is done. Both major festivals run roughly 10 days. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No affiliated knightly orders. Firm ties to halfling warrior orders serving Arvoreen (who defend
  fields and silos from despoilers) and to rangers of Mielikki (human, half-elven, or elven) who
  help preserve the wilderness.

## Specialty Priests

Sheela Peryroyl's specialty priests fall into two types -- Druids and Greenfosters -- per Appendix
1's note that "Sheela Peryroyl allows druids as one of her two types of specialty priests."

### Druids

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, sickle, dart, spear, dagger, scimitar, sling, staff; favored
  weapons sickle & quarterstaff
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (padded, leather, or hide), plus a nonmetallic (wooden,
  bone, or shell) shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Elemental, Healing, Plant, Sun, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as druids)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in herbalism
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on agriculture; +1 bonus on
  linguistics (pick two from: brownie, centaur, dryad, elvish, gnome, korred, nixie, pixie, satyr,
  sprite, treant, unicorn)

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

No unique granted-power list; the Druids of Sheela otherwise follow the standard Player's Handbook
Druid class abilities.

### Greenfosters

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Bow, club, crossbow, dagger, dart, flail, knife, net, staff, sickle,
  sling, staff-sling, whip; favored weapons sickle & quarterstaff
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (padded, leather, hide, or studded leather); no shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Creation, Elemental (Air, Earth, Water), Healing, Plant, Sun,
  Travelers, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Divination, Guardian, Necromantic, Protection
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics and druids)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in herbalism, 1 rank in lore (weather)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on agriculture; +1 bonus on handle
  animal; +1 bonus on lore (local history)

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Can analyze and identify domestic grains and garden plants native to Faerûn on sight -- what is growing, how far along in the harvest year, its condition (healthy, diseased, drought-stressed), and even who planted and tends it |
| 1 | Casts all Plant sphere spells as if two levels higher |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Speak with Domestic Animals (as Speak with Animals, domesticated animals only, 60 mana) *and* Speak with Plants (120 mana), each three times per day |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Entangle *or* Reed Staff, choose one per use (10 mana), once per day |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Sheela's Entangle (20 mana) *or* Plant Growth (30 mana), choose one per use, once per day |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Hold Plant *or* Plant Door, choose one per use (40 mana), once per day |
| 10 | Speak with Domestic Animals and Speak with Plants at will (no mana cost) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Anti-Plant Shell (5th-level wizard spell, 15 mana) *or* Anti-Animal Shell (18 mana), choose one per use, three times per tenday |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Charm Plant (7th-level wizard spell, 7 mana) *or* Sunray (7 mana), choose one per use, once per tenday |
