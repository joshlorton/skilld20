# Jergal (Lord of the End of Everything, the Forgotten One, the Scribe of the Doomed)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Jergal
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of the End of Everything, the Forgotten One, the Scribe of the Doomed
- **Portfolio:** Fatalism, order in death, proper burial, guardian of tombs, protector of the names of
  the dead
- **Symbol:** A jawless skull and a writing quill resting on a scroll

## Church Summary

**Jergal is alive, but a shadow of what he once was.** He was once a Greater Power and Lord of the Dead
in his own right -- some sages believe he also held Bhaal's and Bane's portfolios in that era and was
venerated as Nakasr by the Netherese survivor states -- but he voluntarily relinquished the office of
Lord of the Dead centuries ago, becoming Seneschal of Bone Castle under his successor Myrkul, and
continuing in that seneschal role (unaffected by which god holds the office) through Myrkul's death,
Cyric's tenure, and now Kelemvor's. He is a demipower today with only a handful of living worshipers,
though a reportedly substantial population of loyal, still-intelligent mummified priests persists in
long-sealed tombs. Nothing below should be read as historical or non-functional: Jergal is confirmed to
still grant spells to his surviving clergy, living and undead alike, and currently serves at Kelemvor's
side at the Crystal Spire.

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Monastic orders (monks)
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, specialty priests, and monks of Jergal receive religion (Faerunian) as a
  bonus skill.
- **Organization:** Jergal's few living worshipers are joined by several score priests who reportedly
  still survive as mummies and greater mummies in long-sealed tombs, retaining their living intelligence
  and the ability to cast priest spells; most are lawful evil, though a favored few lean lawful neutral.
  Historically his priests existed only in strict, lawful, militaristic societies that did not venerate
  Deneir or any of the goodly gods, where they served as scribes, funerary workers, and morticians, and
  kept careful records of births, deaths, and taxes for their rulers. His few temples are lifeless stone
  mausoleums or dusty crypts where animals and plants never live long, and where the sentients who toil
  daily age and weaken but never die before their appointed time. The clergy have always been evenly
  split between clerics, monks, and specialty priests (doomscribes); they are collectively known as the
  Scriveners of Doom, and otherwise eschew titles or ranks beyond the head of each temple.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** No general hierarchy of ranks exists -- the faith deliberately
  eschews titles. The general clergy are simply Scriveners of Doom; the sole distinct title is First
  Scrivener of Doom, held by the high priest of each individual temple.

### Dogma

- Each being has an eternal resting place chosen for it at the moment of its creation; life is the
  process of seeking that place and eternal rest.
- Existence is but a brief aberration in an eternity of death. Power, success, and joy are as transitory
  as weakness, failure, and misery.
- Only death is absolute, and then only at its appointed hour.
- Seek to bring order to the chaos of life, for in death there is finality and a fixedness of state.
- Be ready for death, for it is at hand and uncompromising.
- Life should be prolonged only when it serves the greater cause of the death of the world.
- Undeath is not an escape or a reward; it is simply a duty of the chosen few who serve the Lord of the
  End of Everything.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Mummified Scriveners of Doom labor eternally in forgotten crypts, scribing the fates of the living on
  cracked parchment as whispered to them by Jergal, sometimes served by nonsentient zombies or skeletons
  (never sentient undead); they attack or ignore intruders depending on whether the intruder's appointed
  hour has come.
- Living Scriveners of Doom maintain vast archives of scrolls listing how the dead under their purview
  passed away and where they were sent in the afterlife.
- In Thay, where Jergal's small living cult is comparatively prominent, most clergy are employed by
  individual Zulkirs or Red Wizards to oversee slave records -- a task that conveniently grants them
  access to records on large numbers of sentient beings.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Sealing | After recording each creature's demise | A small required ritual: a light dusting of ash and powdered bone is sprinkled over the newly inscribed record, blotting the ink and marking another step toward the world's end. |
| Night of Another Year | 30th of Nightal (year's end) | Clergy cease their endless toil for a single night. Every name recorded that year is read aloud from the scrolls, which are then burned with the cry "One Year Closer!" before work begins anew. |

### Affiliated Orders

- The Jergali church has shrunk too small to need or maintain a fighting branch or other affiliated
  orders today; all creatures die at their appointed time whether or not the clergy assists.
- *(Formal, historical, now extinct)* Companions of the Pallid Mask -- Jergali priests who specialized in
  combating or commanding undead, eliminating unsanctioned or troublesome undead and supervising the
  church's nonsentient undead work crews.
- *(Formal, historical, now extinct)* Hand of Jergal -- an elite group of fanatic priests who avenged
  slights against the church at a high priest's direction, acting against those who raised or
  resurrected the dead without paying tribute to Jergal, or who violated a tomb under the church's
  protection.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Doomscribe
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons; favored weapon scythe
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Divination, Law, Necromantic, Summoning, Thought, Time, Sun
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, Elemental, Healing (reversed only), War
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in literacy (Common)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on literacy (Thorass)
