# Berronar Truesilver (The Revered Mother, the Mother Goddess, Matron of Home and Hearth, Mother of Safety, Truth, and Home)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Berronar Truesilver
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Revered Mother, the Mother Goddess, Matron of Home and Hearth, Mother of
  Safety, Truth, and Home
- **Portfolio:** Safety, truth, home, healing, dwarven home life, records, traditional clan life,
  marriage, familial love, faithfulness/loyalty, honesty, obligations, oaths, the family, protector of
  dwarven children
- **Symbol:** Two silver rings, interlocking, worn on a steel or silver chain around the neck

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics), crusaders, and specialty priests of Berronar
  receive the standard dwarven clergy grants (literacy (Dethek runes), modified turn undead) -- see
  `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** Widely and unreservedly respected across dwarven culture -- none question the
  dedication of the Revered Mother's priests, though some younger Wanderers see the church (alongside
  Moradin's) as clinging too tightly to old ways. Temples stand both above and below ground: an
  aboveground temple is a stone circle with scattered small fires among gems and metal sculptures on
  poles; an underground temple is a cavern carpeted floor-to-wall in tended moss, lichen, and
  luminescent fungi. The clergy is composed mostly of specialty priests (42%) and clerics (38%), with
  a handful of fighter/clerics (5%) and crusaders (5%); by subrace it is gold dwarf (54%), shield dwarf
  (45%), and a scattering of jungle and gray dwarves (1%). Female dwarves still make up nearly all the
  priesthood (98%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Daughter/Son of Berronar (novice)
  2. Revered Sister/Brother (full priest)
  3. Hearth-mistress/Headmaster
  4. Homesteader
  5. Lorekeeper
  6. Faithkeeper
  7. Fidelite
  8. Sacred Heart
  9. Keeper of the Truesilver (a High Old One, 7th level or higher)

### Dogma

- The Children of Moradin are shaped on the Soul Forge and ever warmed by the embrace of the Revered
  Mother.
- Tend the hearth and home, drawing strength and safety from truth, tradition, and the rule of law.
- Join with friends, kin, and clan in common purpose.
- Do not succumb to the misery of greed or the evils of strife; always bring hope, health, and cheer
  to those in need.
- Once an oath is made, Berronar watches over its keeping -- to break it is to grieve her sorely.
- Children must be cherished and guarded well from harm, for they are the future of the race.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve as guardians and protectors of dwarven clans; maintain lore records and family histories.
- Further the good health and character of all dwarves: heal the sick and injured, treat and eradicate
  disease, develop antidotes to dwarfsbane and other poisons, and encourage truthfulness, obedience to
  law, and governance of greed and goldlust.
- Never ignore a dwarf in need of aid -- if a Revered Sister/Brother lacks the magic to cure, they
  seek out whoever can, or provide all nonmagical care possible.
- Act, informally, as the pillars of dwarven society: knitting together families, educating and
  nurturing the young, and maintaining orderly clan governance -- often wielding more real influence
  than a hold's titular ruler, despite rarely holding formal leadership.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Midwinter Day | Midwinter (annual) | Celebrated underground with rituals beginning in chanted prayer, an address from the Keepers of the Truesilver, a report of the priesthood's works, and a rising unison prayer; if sick dwarves are present, the assembled clergy perform unison healing (Berronar's Touch), a manifestation rather than a cast spell, which always dulls pain and sometimes cures wounds, disease, blindness, insanity, lycanthropy, or worse. |
| Midsummer Night | Midsummer (annual) | The more elaborate of the two main holy days, held aboveground; otherwise as Midwinter Day. |
| Ring Exchange (Betrothal) | Whenever a couple commits to marriage | Lawful good dwarves exchange everbright silver rings, treated by dwarven smiths and blessed by Berronar's priests; if either party enters the rite with deceit in their heart, Berronar's power crumbles one or both rings during the blessing. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* The Legion of Silver Helms -- a military order of crusaders and fighter/clerics based at
  the abbey of Earthhearth, defending the Firecaverns of the Deep Realm.
- *(Formal)* Berronar's Valkyries -- small bands of elite crusader and fighter/cleric dwarven women who
  observe dwarven battles from a vantage point and charge to the rescue only when warriors are in
  danger of being overrun, ensuring the (predominantly male) army returns home alive.
- *(Formal)* Order of the Silver Knightingale -- a loosely structured order of physicians, medics,
  clerics, and healing specialty priests (mostly female) who accompany warriors into battle to
  minister to the wounded and dying rather than fight, then disperse to their clans in peacetime.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Faernor (singular faernar)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Battle axe, club, crossbow, flail, hand axe, mace, and morning star;
  favored weapon mace
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Combat, Creation, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Law, Plant,
  Protection, Sun, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Astral, Elemental Earth, Necromantic
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in endurance
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on literacy (Dethek runes); +1 bonus on
  healing; +1 bonus on herbalism
