# Vhaeraun (the Masked Lord, the Masked God of Night, the Shadow)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Vhaeraun
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Masked Lord, the Masked God of Night, the Shadow
- **Portfolio:** Thievery, drow males, territory, evil activity on the surface world
- **Symbol:** A black half-mask

### Symbol -- Midjourney prompt


## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms, rogues
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including cleric/thieves, a multiclassed combination allowed to drow
  priests of Vhaeraun), crusaders, and specialty priests of Vhaeraun receive religion (drow), religion
  (elven), and reading/writing (drowic) as bonus proficiencies -- see `drow-priests-general.md`. Must
  be male, with the rare exception of suborned priestesses of Lolth (masked traitors, see below). May not cast any spell from the summoning sphere or the school of summoning
  except to summon Vhaeraun's own avatar or request his favor (such as blessing of Vhaeraun); spells
  purely of the conjuration school remain permitted.
- **Organization:** The clergy is collectively known as the Masked; novices are the Uncloaked, and all
  other clergy are Nightshadows. Titles vary widely by temple; examples include Ascendant Darkness,
  Black Moon, Dark Mantle, Deep Rogue, Enveloping Night, Raven's Caw, Shadow Hunter, Silent Sable, and
  Twilight's Herald, with unique individual titles for high-ranking priests. Specialty priests are
  called darkmasks, and traitorous priests of Lolth secretly in Vhaeraun's service are called masked
  traitors. The clergy is made up entirely of dark elves, over 99% of them male, drawn from specialty
  priests (55%), thieves including nonpriest multiclassed thieves (25%), clerics (10%), cleric/thieves
  (7%), crusaders (2%), and masked traitors (1%). In the Underdark, temples are deep caverns cloaked
  in overlapping darkness spells, often natural amphitheaters with ceilings studded with beljurils
  arranged to resemble stars. On the surface (the "Night Above"), temples are shallow woodland caves
  cloaked by dense forest canopy, usually near small communities of surface-dwelling drow.

### Dogma

- The shadows of the Masked Lord must cast off the tyranny of the Spider Queen and forcibly reclaim
  their birthright and rightful place in the Night Above.
- The existing drow matriarchies must be smashed, and Lolth's warring practices done away with, so the
  drow are welded into a united people rather than a squabbling gaggle of rival Houses.
- Vhaeraun will lead his followers to reign supreme over the other, lesser races on the surface, with
  true equality between males and females.
- Encourage, lead, or aid bands of drow (and allied chaotic evil creatures) in thievery; instigate
  plots, intrigues, and events that continually increase drow influence and power in the surface Realms.
- Manipulate trade, creatures, and intrigues to lessen the power of rival drow priests (particularly
  Lolth's), and continually foment rebellion or disobedience among drow males.
- Aid drow thieves in need -- heal them, bail them out, or forcibly rescue them, even if female.
- Physically aid drow men oppressed or attacked by drow women in any circumstance, and avenge
  cruelties done against drow men.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Practice passive opposition to Lolth's priests; also active on the surface, where some preach a
  heresy of elven-race unity working toward common dominion.
- Specialize in intrigue, trickery, and treachery, and foment disobedience and rebellion among males;
  often disguise their allegiance while within drow communities.
- Encourage contact and marriage with other elven races, since half-drow tend to breed true back into
  the drow race, inexorably raising drow numbers on the surface.
- Every priest works to establish some sort of permanent drow settlement on the surface world, and to
  support that settlement's needs personally or (preferably) make it self-supporting.
- Poison use, manufacture, and experimentation is common; especially effective spells, poisons, and
  tactics devised by a priest are to be shared with the Masked Lord, and thence with all clergy.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nightly Devotions | Whenever the clergy's aims are furthered | Priests utter prayers to Vhaeraun whenever they accomplish something in service of his aims; offerings of the wealth and weapons of vanquished foes (or the regalia of captured female drow priests) are melted in black, bowl-shaped altars. |
| The Masked Lord's Embrace | Midwinter Night (annual) | The most sacred day of the year: each worshiper cloaks themselves in a region of magical darkness and levitates at its center for a full 24 hours, contemplating Vhaeraun's teachings and scheming to advance his goals in the coming year. The Masked Lord grants the necessary extended duration to both spell-like effects as a special boon for this rite. |
| New Moon Stag Hunts | Nights of the new moon | Midnight hunts ranging over miles of shadowy woodland; packs of Vhaeraun's worshipers, mounted on riding lizards, run down a noble hart and sacrifice its antlers and still-beating heart to the Masked Lord. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Informal, growing) The Dark Dagger -- drow venerating Vhaeraun, individually powerful but few in
  number, active in Skullport, Turmish, the Vilhon Reach, Amn, and Calimshan, and increasingly
  infiltrating coastal cities around the Sea of Fallen Stars. They habitually use poison (to which
  they are largely immune, through lifelong incremental dosing), take covert control of local thieving
  guilds, hire skilled agents, establish hidden temples, and recruit disaffected half-elves and humans
  -- aided by the fact that Vhaeraun's symbol is identical to that of Mask, the human god of thieves.

## Specialty Priests

Vhaeraun's church recognizes two distinct specialty-priest tracks: darkmasks, his ordinary male
clergy, and masked traitors, priestesses of Lolth who secretly also serve Vhaeraun. Their granted
powers and progressions are detailed separately below; both share the Vhaeraunan spell list at the
end of this entry.

### Darkmasks

- **Name/Title:** Darkmask
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any, plus mandatory proficiency in a black-edged bladed weapon. The book
  favors daggers, short swords, and long swords among the clergy generally, without naming one
  explicit favored weapon; later published sources give short sword (Shadowflash) as Vhaeraun's
  favored weapon
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light or Medium (padded, leather, studded leather, drow chain
  mail, or elven chain mail); no shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Chaos, Charm, Combat, Divination, Elemental (Air, Earth), Guardian, Healing,
  Necromantic, Sun (reversed)
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Elemental (Water, Fire), Protection, Time, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in disguise, 1 rank in herbalism (in addition to the mandatory
  black-edged bladed weapon proficiency above)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on alertness; +1 bonus on Blind-fighting

### Masked Traitors

Masked traitors are priestesses of Lolth (of any clergy class -- cleric, crusader, or specialty
priest) who secretly also serve Vhaeraun, worshiping Lolth on the surface and the Masked Lord
underneath. Their base statistics, weapon and armor proficiencies, and spheres are otherwise
identical to whichever type of Lolth clergy they impersonate (see [lolth.md](lolth.md)); only the
deviations below are unique to the masked traitor.

- **Name/Title:** Masked Traitor
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** As the type of Lolth clergy impersonated
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** As the type of Lolth clergy impersonated
- **Major Spheres:** As the type of Lolth clergy impersonated
- **Minor Spheres:** As the type of Lolth clergy impersonated
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** None beyond those of the impersonated Lolth clergy class
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); retains linguistics (high drow) from their
  suborned service to Lolth

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

All of the following are granted immediately upon entering Vhaeraun's service, rather than at a
specific later level.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Gains access to all spells known to Vhaeraun's faith, including several unique to masked traitors -- chaotic combat (usable only on herself or another Vhaeraun faithful, regardless of class), create holy symbol, divine inspiration, mindnet, reversion, and seclusion (not detailed in this source) |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Blessing of Vhaeraun (20 mana), once per day |
| 1 | May cast Deceive Prying once at will immediately upon entering Vhaeraun's service; thereafter it must be prepared normally like any other spell |
| 1 | Receives useful information from Vhaeraun in dreams; he often (falsely) hints at rewarding exceptional service with immortality or the role of his consort |
| 1 | Suffers no penalty for acting in full or bright light (her eyes are shaded by Vhaeraun's power); this does not protect drow armor or weapons from sunlight's normal degrading effect |

If a masked traitor's true loyalty to Vhaeraun is discovered, Lolth refuses to grant her further
spells (though any already memorized are kept), and she continues to advance as a cleric or specialty
priest of Vhaeraun outright if she survives to flee Lolth's worshipers.

Priests of Vhaeraun may also cast Conceal Item (3rd-level, detailed above under Lolth) and Air Walk
(5th-level, Player's Handbook).
