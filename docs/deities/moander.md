# Moander (the Darkbringer, the Rotting God, the Jawed God, the Great Dread God)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Moander
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Darkbringer, the Rotting God, the Jawed God, the Great Dread God
- **Portfolio:** Rotting death, decay, corruption
- **Symbol:** An upright male human right hand, fingers outstretched, with an open human female fanged
  mouth, lips parted as though speaking, set in its palm

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** spellcasters (post-destruction, surviving cults retain only specialty priests and lay
  members)
- **Organization:** No true central hierarchy, especially after Moander's death. Historically, the
  supreme Faerûnian priest was the Mouth of Moander; senior clergy of a temple were High Minions; a
  temple's leader was its Master Minion; all others were simply Minions of Moander, since the faith
  used no other individual titles -- all were slaves and puppets of the god.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Minion of Moander (general clergy)
  2. High Minion (senior temple clergy)
  3. Master Minion (temple leader)
  4. Mouth of Moander (supreme Faerûnian priest -- historically Dalchatha Maereegh)

  There was a 1% chance that any high priest of Moander would be transformed into an undead skulk upon
  death, becoming an Undying Minion -- a special fate outside the normal rank progression.

### Dogma

- Feed Great Moander with fresh corpses of your own making.
- Hew down strong plants and trees to feed it.
- Keep the Abomination and the lands through which it passes as warm as possible.
- Seek not to question the ways and words of Moander, lest you be stricken by the Eating From Within.
- Go forth and possess beings of power and influence for Moander.
- Slay, and let the rot cover all.
- Fight against cold with fire and magic.
- Fear Moander, and obey.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Minions scoured the land for malformed life (mongrelmen, diseased plants and beasts) and brutish,
  destructive beings (orcs and the like) to feed to Moander.
- Cultists strove to spread intelligent vegetable life -- algoids, shambling mounds, gibbering
  mouthers, and vegepygmies (russet mold) -- throughout the Realms.
- Minions built the god endless new bodies (triangular pyramids of decaying vegetation, dung, and
  rotting corpses), since any body Moander animated decayed and had to be replaced.
- Tithe/offering: daily, each Minion had to slay something or gather vegetable matter in the god's
  name, rendering the gatherings up either to build Moander a new body or simply to encourage rot and
  decay. Monthly, each Minion had to extend the god's influence by spreading rumors of its power and
  bringing a seed of Moander into contact with at least one new being while whispering its name.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Balefire | The first of Hammer (annual) | Servants built huge bonfires to hold back the winter cold, at which Moander always manifested to thank them, deliver sermons, and charge them with missions for the cold months, when the Darkbringer retreated to lava-warmed caverns in the Underdark. |
| Anniversary of the Defeat | Midnight, the sixth of Kythorn (annual) | Surviving cultists gather at the Hill of Fangs shrine west of Westgate to plead for the Jawed God's return to the Realms. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Eyes of the Darkbringer, or "Darkeyes" -- a secretive brotherhood of rogues placed as
  spies (and occasionally assassins) in most of Faerûn's major cities, warning the cult of brewing
  threats.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Darkbringer
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** all; favored weapon sharded cudgel (treat as horseman's mace), alt. scimitar
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Any (Light, Medium, or Heavy all permitted), plus shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Elemental, Healing, Necromantic, Plant, Summoning
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Chaos, Time, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** None
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on herbalism; +1 bonus on lore (fungi &
  decay)
