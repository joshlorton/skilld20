# Amaunator (The Yellow God, the Keeper of the Eternal Sun)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Amaunator
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Yellow God, the Keeper of the Eternal Sun
- **Portfolio:** Bureaucracy, contracts, law, order, the sun, rulership
- **Symbol:** A sun with a face on the solar disk

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, monastic orders
- **Organization:** Extremely hierarchical and rulebound, historically wielding great secular power in
  Netheril. Each temple (called a "Court") is overseen by a Righteous Potentate, under whom serve seven
  Monastic Abbots, each responsible for one supplemental duty (upkeep of temple grounds and farms;
  upkeep of the church interior; upkeep of the church exterior; procurement of spiritual necessities;
  procurement of physical necessities such as food and supplies; upkeep and expansion of the law
  library; and cultivation/supervision of itinerant monk missionaries). Under each Abbot serve seven
  High Jurists, who carry out assigned duties; lower clergy ranks beneath them are lost to history.
  Itinerant monks are the only clergy not tied to temple duties most of the time, though they must
  report back to their assigned temple or shrine at least once a year.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Court (novices in training)
  2. Lower clergy (specific titles lost to time)
  3. High Jurist (one of seven serving under each Monastic Abbot)
  4. Monastic Abbot (one of seven under a Righteous Potentate, each with a supplemental duty)
  5. Righteous Potentate (high priest and ruler of a temple/"Court")

  Itinerant monks form a separate track alongside this ladder, traveling the world preaching the peace
  found in law rather than serving fixed temple duties.

### Dogma

- The law is the law; it keeps order in society, and without it civilization unravels into chaos.
- Amaunator represents the sure function of law: as certainly as the sun rises each morning, the law
  can deal fairly with any dispute or crime.
- Learn the law and live it; obey its every letter and clause, for knowledge of law's intricacies is
  freedom to act with righteous impunity.
- Keep track of superiors' decisions so the body of precedent grows and Amaunator's unity of purpose
  is made manifest to all.
- Serve your superiors faithfully and be rewarded faithfully; shirk your duty and meet the harsh hand
  of reproof.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Constantly drill one another in law, and practice and rehearse legal argument in courtrooms whenever
  possible, in order to master the nuances of law and legislature.
- Investigate crime scenes and take part in drafting new laws for the locale.
- Serve in court as judges, present cases, and hear legal arguments and disputes.
- Arbitrate merchant disputes over contracts, agreements, and trade practices for pay, serving as
  arbitrators of commercial and personal claims not worthy of higher authorities' attention.
- Tithe/offering: whenever a devout follower successfully exploits a contract, wins a court case, or
  helps pass a new law, the priest gives thanks to Amaunator by burning magically preserved oak leaves
  and incense in his honor.

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Most Transcendent Affiliation of Paradisiacal Pens -- an aloof, arrogant order of
  bonded scribes who travel the world preaching the holiness of law and order, building
  amphitheater-sized archives that preserve the laws of every land and city they encounter.
- *(Formal)* Syndicate of Celestial and Righteous Lawmakers -- a military/knightly order of roughly 70
  warriors and paladins who honor Amaunator's love of law while softening his less benevolent
  tendencies into a more humanitarian reading; their shields are enchanted with potent variants of
  *continual light* to blind lawless foes and serve as a lasting light source.
- *(Informal)* Brotherhood of the Sun -- an association of itinerant monks (symbol: a sunburst) who
  bring Amaunator's teachings to peasants and common folk and help preserve order across the land.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Sunlord / Sunlady
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon light mace
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, medium, & heavy armor, shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Creation, Elemental Fire, Elemental Air, Law, Summoning, Sun, Time
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Elemental Earth, Healing, Necromantic, Numbers, Protection, Thought
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in literacy (reading/writing)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on etiquette; +1 bonus on perception
  (reading lips); +1 bonus on lore (law)
