# Iyachtu Xvim (The Godson of Bane, the Godson, the son of Bane, the New Darkness, the Cruel Master)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Iyachtu Xvim
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Godson of Bane, the Godson, the son of Bane, the New Darkness, the Cruel
  Master
- **Portfolio:** Tyranny, hatred
- **Symbol:** A pair of bright, glowing green eyes superimposed on the palm of an upright,
  human-shaped hand of utter blackness (older symbol: a pair of green, glowing eyes peering out of
  darkness)

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **Organization:** Lay followers and priests alike are called Xvimists; priests specifically are
  Xvimlar, and the elite specialty priests are authlims. Xvimlar exult in destruction and casually
  dealing pain (kicking passersby, slamming doors on people, hurling stones at random are not uncommon
  acts). Temples are run strictly hierarchically under a High Tyrannar; crusaders (still few in
  number) report directly to a temple's high priest (usually a Ruinlord or Tyrannar), with one
  exclusive unit answering directly to the High Tyrannar. The church favors underground temples,
  especially ones converted from abandoned temples of Bane, though grand temples have also been
  (re)dedicated in Thay, notably the House of Iyachtu Xvim in Bezantur.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order; identical regardless of class or gender):
  1. Vermin (novices, an intentionally demeaning nickname)
  2. Oppressor (3rd level or below)
  3. Hatemaster (4th-8th level)
  4. Ruinlord (9th-15th level)
  5. Tyrannar (16th level or higher)
  6. High Tyrannar (supreme head of the entire church; currently one individual, Fzoul Chembryl)

### Dogma

- Xvim delights in death and destruction, and his devoted must emulate him -- nothing worthy is
  accomplished without the destruction of something else.
- Followers must be as committed to destruction, oppression, and cruelty as Xvim himself.
- Favor a crushing pecking order that rewards those who shove others aside for personal gain; those at
  the top prey on those at the bottom, who either survive and grow stronger or perish, having proven
  too weak to belong.
- "Obey or die in pain and utter destruction."
- Enslave or slay the weak, ensuring they know their suffering is in Xvim's name and by his will.
- Cause pain and fearful obedience in others whenever prudent; be a cruel, heartless tyrant.
- Slay the priests of other gods whenever it can be done without being identified.
- Capture tyrants and deliver them to senior clergy to be given unto Xvim; capture wizards and bring
  their magic to the church, or deliver them to Xvim's most senior servants to be transformed into
  guardian creatures.
- Spread fear of Xvim over all the lands; destroy whatever and whoever bars his will, ensuring word of
  his power spreads while few survive to describe the deed in detail.
- Destroy all witnesses to secret acts, but leave survivors to spread word of Xvim's power after
  casual, public destruction.
- There is delight in destruction -- feel it, and indulge in it.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Xvimlar roam Faerûn, inspired by their Cruel Master's emergence into open godhood.
- Challenge and slay clergy of Cyric, their acknowledged enemy, wherever found.
- Urge still-faithful Banite priests to convert to Xvim's worship, or perish at Xvimlar hands.
- Xvim ensures High Tyrannar Fzoul Chembryl's loyalty by possessing him at regular intervals to
  thoroughly explore his thoughts and memories, prizing his cunning and experience with religious
  intrigue.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Coming | Varies by temple; no fixed calendar date yet | Celebrates Xvim's emergence into godhood; a firm, unified date is expected to be set eventually. |

Beyond this, priests are expected to pray to Xvim whenever they take a life in his name (even mid-battle
or flight) and to bow in prayer morning and evening before a high altar. High rituals involve horrific
sacrifices of intelligent creatures and depraved gladiatorial combats between goaded, starved beasts and
desperate unarmed captives, staged amid fragile glass items that can be shattered into weapons that harm
both wielder and target. Temples are also rumored to hold chanting dances in which priests work
themselves into frenzies and attack slaves or lesser priests barehanded until one combatant falls
bleeding.

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Brothers of the Black Fist -- a still-forming knightly order to which the faith's
  crusaders belong, commanding fighters drawn to Xvim's discipline and decisive leadership.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Authlim
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons (nonmissile only) and javelins; favored weapon gauntlet
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Heavy armor, shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, combat, divination, elemental, guardian, necromantic, summoning, thought,
  war
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, healing (reversed forms only), law, sun (reversed forms only)
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in languages (pick one: beholder, bugbear, gargoyle, common giant,
  gnoll, goblin, High Shou, kobold, kuo-toan, Midani, minotaur, Mulhorandi, ogre, orc, sahuagin,
  Undercommon, Untheric, urd, yuan-ti)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on blind-fighting; +1 bonus on
  languages (pick a second, from the same list); +1 bonus on intimidation

## Xvimlar Spells (deity-specific)

Iyachtu Xvim learned to grant many of the spells his father Bane was capable of granting; any spell
exclusive to the church of Bane may also be used by the church of Iyachtu Xvim, at the DM's discretion.

| Name | Est. Mana | Foundation | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mace of Xvim | 20 | Combat | Conjures a short-lived, nearly weightless glowing mace that strikes with a strong attack bonus and forces struck items to save or shatter. |
| Reaving Blades | 40 | Combat | Summons two whirling blades of force that fly at the caster's command, slashing a target (or two separate targets) twice per round before vanishing after two rounds. |
| Battletide | 50 | Combat | Slows every creature within a 20-foot radius of the caster (with a save each round to break free) while hasting the caster to double movement and attacks for the duration. |
