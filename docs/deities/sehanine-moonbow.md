# Sehanine Moonbow (Daughter of the Night Skies, Goddess of Moonlight, the Lunar Lady, Moonlit Mystery, the Mystic Seer, the Luminous Cloud, Lady of Dreams)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Sehanine Moonbow
- **Titles/Epithets:** Daughter of the Night Skies, Goddess of Moonlight, the Lunar Lady, Moonlit Mystery, the Mystic Seer, the Luminous Cloud, Lady of Dreams
- **Portfolio:** Mysticism, dreams, death, journeys, transcendence, the moon, the stars, the heavens, moon elves
- **Symbol:** A full moon with a moonbow (an opaque, milky crescent); the priesthood's physical holy symbol is a moonbar crystal carved into a small flat disk about three inches across, worn on a silver or mithral chain

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including multiclassed half-elven clerics), crusaders, mystics, and specialty priests receive religion (pantheon) and literacy (Espruar) as bonus skills; all priests of Sehanine may additionally pray for and receive the 1st-level wizard spell Sleep as one of their 1st-level spells if they desire (casting time 4 instead of 1).
- **Organization:** Removed from the daily concerns and outward expression of everyday life, and little known outside elven society; closely held and deeply cherished among elves, for the Luminous Cloud envelops and binds together all the Tel'Quessir. Temples, open only to elves and a few pious half-elves, are soaring white-stone (often marble) monuments shaped to suggest imminent flight, centered on a perfectly circular chapel open to the night sky or covered by a retractable/transparent dome, surrounded by gardens and hedgerow mazes tracing the paths of the heavens; megaliths near long-standing temples form stone circles used to track the moon and stars. The clergy is moon elves (54%), gold elves (34%), wild elves (5%), half-moon elves (4%), half-gold elves (1%), half-wild elves (1%), and a handful of dark elves, sea elves, winged elves, and other half-elves (1%); made up of specialty priests (40%), mystics (35%), clerics (20%), and crusaders (5%); nearly evenly split between female (52%) and male (48%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Mooncalled (novice)
  2. Heavenly (full priest)
  3. Stargazer
  4. Moondancer
  5. Sky Seer
  6. Vision Seeker
  7. Omen Teller
  8. Dream Walker
  9. Transcendentalist
  10. Reverent Dreamer

  High-ranking priests hold unique individual titles. Specialty priests are known as starsingers.

### Dogma

- Life is a series of mysteries whose secrets are veiled by the Luminous Cloud.
- As the spirit transcends its mortal bounds and new mysteries are uncovered, a higher form is achieved and the cycle of life continues.
- Through contemplation and meditation, communion with the Lady of Dreams is achieved.
- Through dreams, visions, and omens revealed in sleep or reverie, the Daughter of the Night Sky unveils the next step and destination on the endless journey of mystic wonder that is life and death and life.
- Revere the mysterious moon, who draws forth the tides of being from us all.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve as spiritual counselors to elves and half-elves seeking transcendence through journeys of enlightenment.
- Act as shepherds and protectors of the dead: organize and administer funeral rites and guard the remains of the fallen.
- Seek out and destroy undead creatures, held blasphemous by Sehanine -- with the notable exception of baelnorn and other good-aligned undead who voluntarily prolong their existence to serve their kin.
- Weave and maintain the illusions guarding elven homelands and sanctuaries, and divine potential threats to them.
- Adventuring priests focus on retrieving lost arcane and magical knowledge, especially concerning illusion and divination; others bring news of the Retreat to isolated elven enclaves and help prepare them for the journey, though Sehanine's priests offer no guidance during the journey itself, which comes through direct intuitive revelation from the goddess.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lunar Hallowings | Monthly, beneath the full moon | Marked by personal meditation and collective entrance into a communal trance; Sehanine sometimes manifests through her worshipers, knitting their spirits together in a true sharing of minds. Concludes with a joyous freeform dance lasting until dawn. |
| Mystic Rites of the Luminous Cloud | Annually, on the Feast of the Moon | Similar to the Lunar Hallowings, but the assembled worshipers are enveloped in a mantle of shimmering silvery light that rises and darts across the heavens, revealing sacred mysteries appropriate to each participant's spiritual development, before returning to earth. |
| Transcendence | Whenever an elf is called to depart the mortal world (unpredictable) | The elf receives a vision from Sehanine showing where their journey must begin, and a telltale opaque milky crescent (the moonbow) appears in their eye; on rare occasions other elves join in a shared trance called the Circle of Transcendence, sharing memories and knowledge in direct telepathic communion. |
| Ceremony of Recovery | Following a violent or accidental death where the spirit is not utterly destroyed | Sehanine's priests spend one or more days in meditation and mystic communion, channeling the lost spirit through their own link to the goddess so it may transcend to Arvanaith; the priest's eyes display the telltale moonbow for the ritual's duration. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Knights of the Seven Sacred Mysteries -- defends elven homelands from N'Tel'Quess invaders and retrieves lost tomes of elvish lore and elven artistry from fallen realms; composed mostly of moon and gold elves and half-elves, including crusaders, clerics, fighters, and rangers. The order has seven secret tiers (Knight of the First Mystery through Knight of the Seventh Mystery), and advancement can take a century or more per mystery revealed; no half-elf has ever risen above Knight of the Fourth Mystery.
- *(Informal)* Sentinels of the Moonbow -- a small fellowship of rangers who watch over animals that may hold the reincarnated spirits of elves of ages past, and who track and destroy blighting undead.
- *(Informal)* Veiled Choir -- a mysterious, rarely-glimpsed sisterhood of elven mystics renowned for prophecy revealed in song; their oracular sanctuaries can never be found by deliberate seeking, only by fate, and none are located on Evermeet.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Starsinger
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Bow, javelin, quarterstaff, sickle, sling, staff-sling; favored weapon quarterstaff
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic, Protection, Summoning, Sun, Travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Numbers, Thought, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (bowyer/fletcher)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on astrology; +1 bonus on navigation
