# Corellon Larethian (Creator of the Elves, the Protector, First of the Seldarine, Protector and Preserver of Life, Ruler of All Elves, Coronal of Arvandor)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Corellon Larethian
- **Titles/Epithets:** Creator of the Elves, the Protector, First of the Seldarine, Protector and
  Preserver of Life, Ruler of All Elves, Coronal of Arvandor
- **Portfolio:** Magic and elven magic (especially elven High Magic), music, arts, crafts, war, the
  elven race (especially gold elves), poets, poetry, bards, warriors
- **Symbol:** A crescent moon

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including multiclassed half-elven clerics and elven fighter/clerics),
  crusaders, and specialty priests of Corellon receive religion (elf) and literacy (Espruar) as bonus
  proficiencies.
- **Organization:** Venerated by nearly all Tel'Quessir, even those who do not specifically worship
  him, but his clergy is somewhat removed from elven society and governance -- the guardian and
  creative aspects of his nature dominate his priesthood's place in elven life far more than his
  position as Ruler of All Elves. Temples are rare (elves are individualistic in his worship); shrines
  are little more than clearings with a good view of the sky. His temples are shaped from great natural
  geological formations -- shallow caves entered from above, natural amphitheaters, great rock spires --
  woven through with trees and plants into natural cathedrals of stone and growth.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Faerna (novice)
  2. Faernsuora (full priest)
  3. Aegisess (Protector)
  4. Adoness (Peacekeeper)
  5. Kerynsuoress (Holy Warrior)
  6. Ivae'ess (Lightbringer)
  7. Avae'ess (Joy-bringer)
  8. Syolkiir (Wildstar)
  9. Lateu'suoress (Crescent-Moonblessed)
  10. Araegisess (Great Protector)
  11. Aradoness (Great Peacekeeper)
  12. Ark-erynsuoress (Great Holy Warrior)

  The highest-ranking priests are collectively known as the Cormiira (Blessings of Corellon) but each
  carries a unique individual title.

### Dogma

- The Tel'Quessir are both wardens and sculptors of magic's endless mysteries.
- Through Art and Craft, bring forth the beauty that envelops, and let the spirit gambol unfettered.
- The song of joy and the dance of freedom shall ever soar on the wings of those who dare take flight.
- Guard against the slow death of stultifying sameness by seeking out new experiences and new ways.
- Ward against those who seek only to destroy in their inability to create and commune with the
  natural and mystical world.
- Be ever vigilant in force of arms and might of magic against any return of the banished darkness.
- Be strong in heart against the corruption from within that allowed the Spider Queen to foment the
  chaos and evil of the Crown Wars.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve in the defense and artistic development of elven communities; mediate disputes among the Fair
  Folk or between elves and other sylvan deities.
- Serve in elven armed forces, defending elf-claimed territories and training fellow elves in combat
  and magic.
- Instruct elven artisans and craftsfolk in the skills of creating works of wondrous beauty, and
  practice such arts personally.
- Act as diplomats and arbitrators between clergies, elven subraces, classes of elven society, and
  elven communities -- often working behind the scenes to keep governments functioning smoothly, rather
  than ruling directly.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lateu'quor (the Forest Communion of the Crescent Moon) | Once per lunar month, when the crescent moon illuminates the night sky | Corellon's faithful gather in moonlit glades to offer praises through music, song, dance, and their most beautiful creations; true works of art are sometimes carried up to Arvandor, others kept in Corellon's shrines. Revels occasionally erupt into spontaneous cooperative magic that reshapes the landscape (creating a new sacred site) or coalesces into an item of unearthly beauty later enchanted as a holy relic. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal, historical)* Knights of the Golden Wyrm, the Blade of Sahandrian, the Fey Staghorns, the
  Swords of the Seldarine -- notable orders of ages past.
- *(Formal, Evermeet)* Wings of Yathaghera, Knights of the Alicorn, Weavers of Bladesong, Vassals of
  the Reverend Ones -- pledged to defend the Green Isle.
- *(Formal, mainland)* Swords of Evereska -- the most notable surviving mainland order, defending the
  alpine vale of Evereska.
- *(Informal)* Fellowship of the Forgotten Flower -- a loosely structured organization recovering lost
  elven relics from long-abandoned elven realms.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Feywarden
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any (must include long sword and long bow); favored weapon long sword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Heavy (any armor and shields)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Combat, Creation, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic,
  Protection, Sun, War, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Chaos, Summoning, Plant, Thought
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in art, 1 rank in spellcraft
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on musical instrument; +1 bonus on
  singing
