# Talona (Lady of Poison, Mistress of Disease, Mother of All Plagues)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Talona
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lady of Poison, Mistress of Disease, Mother of All Plagues
- **Portfolio:** Disease, poison
- **Symbol:** Three golden amber teardrops on a purple equilateral triangle, point upward

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** An underground church, strongest wherever plagues are rampant; the faithful are
  often blamed for causing the very plagues they exploit. Priests (Talontar) favor ritual facial
  tattoos and scarification. Priests of 2nd level or less are probationary initiates; only at 3rd
  level are they formally inducted. Malagents make up about 45% of the clergy, clerics 40%, and
  mystics 15%.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** No formal ascending ladder is given. Malagents are addressed as
  "Most Fatal Horror" (informally "Fatals"); other Talontar are addressed as "Most Debilitating
  Holiness"; senior clergy informally call juniors "Young Venom" regardless of relative age.

### Dogma

- Let pain be as pleasure to the faithful of Talona; she works upon you from within, and in weakness
  and wasting is her strength.
- She is forever and always with you, whomever you or the rest of the world believes in or serves.
- Let all living things learn respect from Talona and pay homage to her in goods and fervent
  worship, and her dedicated priests will intercede so that she will not claim them -- this time.
- Go and work in Talona's name, and let your doings be subtle or spectacular, but make them known as
  the will of the Mother of All Plagues.
- Life and death are in balance, but death is the more powerful and should be paid proper homage.
- Death is the true power, and the lesson that waits for all; if it falls to the faithful to drive
  home the point with the tip of a dagger, so be it.
- The great equalizer of disease teaches the falsely invincible -- through wealth, sword-arm, or
  spells -- respect and humility.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Sell poisons, antidotes, and medicines while traveling Faerûn as quietly as possible.
- Seek out new diseases and afflictions and spread rumors to augment Talona's reputation.
- Build personal immunity to poisons and diseases through magic and inoculation.
- Treat the diseased, serve as food tasters for paranoid rulers and wealthy nobles, and bury the
  plague-dead.
- Cause a plague in any realm or city-state that casts out or punishes a Talontar, to exact
  "Talona's price" for the insult.
- Some unscrupulous Talontar target wealthy folk with disease so the church can seize their wealth
  upon death, keeping rightful heirs at bay with fear of contagion.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Thrice-daily prayers | Morning, highsun, evening (daily) | Timing need not be precise. |
| Daernuth | Every 12 days | Holy festivals open to nondevotees, who are encouraged to pray and give offerings to be spared disease. Priests display lepers and disfigured victims being cured, demonstrate immunity by touching filthy objects to fresh ritual wounds, and sell poisons/antidotes/medicines while senior clergy diagnose and prescribe for fees of 50-100 gp. |
| Annual Ascension | The daernuth closest to Higharvestide | Initiates are formally inducted into the priesthood via horrifying private ceremonies of ritual scarring and sacred tattooing. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No military or knightly orders serve Talona, but several secret cabals do.
- *(Informal)* The Plague Rats -- an elite organization of thieves, assassins, and wererats active
  throughout the Western Heartlands and the North, based secretly in the Rat Hills.
- *(Informal)* The Plague-mother's Children -- a guild of thugs active in Chondath and the Vilhon
  Reach, degenerated from a former crusader order into an extortion racket (crusaders are no longer
  counted among Talona's faithful).
- *(Informal)* Remnants of Castle Trinity -- a trifold order of wizards, priests, and warriors founded
  during the Time of Troubles by the wizard Aballister at Talona's bidding, armed with the Chaos
  Curse; the stronghold was collapsed and ruined, but a secretive fellowship of survivors may remain.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Malagent
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapons unarmed strike & dagger
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** Astral, Chaos, Combat, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic, Summoning
- **Minor Spheres:** All, Charm, Protection
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** None
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon), herbalism, and dagger checks; +1 bonus on
  stealth checks when administering poison unnoticed

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +4 bonus to all saving throws vs. poison |
| 1 | Immune to nonmagical disease; +4 bonus (and halved percentage chance, round down) on checks to resist magical disease |
| 1 | Normal saving throw allowed against potions that would not normally allow one |
| 1 | +4 bonus to herbalism checks when brewing poisons |
| 3 | Can identify pure poisons by smell |
| 5 | Can identify poisons in drinks by taking a harmless tiny sip |
| 7 | Can identify poisons in food by taking a harmless tiny bite |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Poison Touch (20 mana) *or* Spread Contagion (40 mana), choose one per use |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Cause Disease (30 mana) |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Cloud of Pestilence (40 mana) *or* Stinking Cloud (20 mana), choose one per use |
