# Ubtao (Creator of Chult, Founder of Mezro, Father of the Dinosaurs)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Ubtao
- **Titles/Epithets:** Creator of Chult, Founder of Mezro, Father of the Dinosaurs
- **Portfolio:** Creation, the labyrinth of life, jungles, the Jungles of Chult, the Tabaxi, dinosaurs
- **Symbol:** A maze

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All specialty priests, druids, and shamans of Ubtao receive religion (Chultan) as a
  bonus proficiency. Unlike most faiths, Ubtao's clergy includes no clerics at all.
- **Organization:** Three distinct priestly traditions serve Ubtao, each venerating a different facet
  of his fragmented being: mazewalkers (specialty priests) venerate Ubtao directly as Creator of Chult;
  jungle druids venerate the essence of the jungle he made; and spiritlords (shamans) venerate the
  myriad spirits of the dead, animals, and nature that are fragments of his greater self. All three are
  considered equally valid worship of Ubtao. Mazewalkers hold a slight majority in the city of Mezro,
  while jungle druids and spiritlords dominate the remote peninsula; most individual Tabaxi practice a
  hybrid of all three traditions. Priests of every tradition eschew formal titles beyond their
  profession, though clans sometimes grant affectionate titles of respect. The only true temple, the
  Maze of Life (also called simply the Temple of Ubtao), stands in Mezro and welcomes clergy and
  worshipers of all three traditions; it holds no fixed organized services.

### Dogma

- Ubtao created the jungle as a great test for his people -- a maze to be passed through on the way to
  a heavenly afterlife -- and each individual life is itself represented by its own maze.
- Upon death, the faithful are called before Ubtao and told to draw the maze of their own life; success
  admits them to Ubtao's home, while failure returns them to the world as a ghost or ghoul.
- Practice drawing your personal maze constantly throughout life, adding to it as you grow.
- Dinosaurs, the Children of Ubtao, are agents of fate -- success or misfortune against them merely
  reflects one's place in Ubtao's regard.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Mazewalkers see to their clan's spiritual welfare, teach history and lore, counsel on major life
  decisions, mediate disputes, and help uphold the law of Mezro.
- Spiritlords smooth their clan's passage through the spirit-infested world, ensuring no ancestor or
  elemental power is offended by a missed ritual or sacrifice, and coax favors and knowledge from
  capricious spirits.
- Jungle druids help scattered clans fit into the web of jungle life, often serving as clan healers and
  passing on knowledge of animals (including dinosaurs) and plants; they commonly train what few
  domestic animals the Tabaxi keep.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| (No fixed calendar of holy days) | Continuous, highly localized | Tabaxi religious practice consists of countless small ceremonies tied to specific ancestors, the season, an upcoming hunt or journey, or a particular task such as burying the dead, rather than a shared ritual calendar. Small, portable stone altars are used only for these occasions and are otherwise treated as ordinary rocks. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) The Barae of Mezro -- seven immortal champions personally chosen by Ubtao to defend the holy
  city, shielded from age and sickness (though not from death in battle or murder) for as long as they
  serve; if slain, Ubtao selects a replacement through a private trial known only to those who undergo
  it. Many rural Tabaxi clans venerate the barae as intercessors with Ubtao himself and trace their
  lineage to the original seven.

## Specialty Priests

Ubtao has three priest traditions with their own granted powers: jungle druids, mazewalkers (the
specialty priests proper), and Tabaxi shamans (spiritlords).

### Jungle Druids

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Blowgun, club, dart, dagger, kerrie, scimitar, spear, yklwa; no favored
  weapon specified in the source, and none found in real-world published material
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (padded, leather, or hide, typically dinosaur hide); wooden
  or hide (typically dinosaur hide) nonmetallic shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Elemental, Healing, Plant, Travelers, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Combat, Divination, Protection
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as druids)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (nature), 1 rank in lore (dinosaurs), 1 rank in herbalism
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on healing; +1 bonus on survival
  (jungle); +1 bonus on linguistics (pick one from: aarakocra, aldani, bullywug, dwarf, goblin, lizard
  man [and its ptera-man dialect], locathah, muckdweller, sahuagin, troglodyte, Tabaxi, yuan-ti)

Otherwise follow the standard druid class, per the Player's Handbook.

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Can move through thick jungle without leaving a trail, at full movement rate |
| 3, 6, 9... | Gains an additional language slot every 3 levels for learning the tongues of tropical forest and swamp creatures |
| 7 | Can shapechange into a normal (non-giant) reptile, a small Chultan dinosaur, a bird, or a mammal, up to three times per day (each form usable once per day), restricted to animals native to the Jungles of Chult |

### Mazewalkers (Specialty Priests)

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons; favored weapon heavy pick
  ("Tyrannosaur head") -- not named explicitly in this source, but confirmed via real-world 3.5e
  sourcing
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Charm, Combat, Divination, Healing, Plant, Protection, Thought
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Elemental, Guardian, Necromantic, Sun, Time, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (Chultan history), 1 rank in direction sense, 1 rank in
  linguistics (Tabaxi)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on survival (jungle); +1 bonus on art
  (singing); +1 bonus on literacy (Thorass)

Mazewalkers must be human and Tabaxi (barring direct divine intervention for any other race).

### Tabaxi Shamans (Spiritlords)

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, kerrie, spear, yklwa; no favored weapon specified in the source, and
  none found in real-world published material
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (hide, typically dinosaur hide); shield (hlang) permitted
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Elemental, Healing, Plant, Protection, Summoning, Sun, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, Divination, Necromantic, Travelers, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in herbalism, 1 rank in lore (local history)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on art (woodcarving); +1 bonus on
  survival (jungle)

Spiritlords must be human and Tabaxi (barring direct divine intervention for any other race). Upon
becoming a spiritlord, a shaman carves a personal spirit mask that permanently houses their first
spirit ally (always a spirit of the dead or an animal spirit); that spirit no longer needs the usual
summoning ceremony and is always available.

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | The mask spirit can cast one priest spell of the spiritlord's own choosing (from their accessible spheres, 1st level or less) once per day, in addition to its normal minor-spirit powers |
| 1 | +1 bonus on surprise rolls while the spirit mask is within 30 feet |
| 4 | Mask spirit gains a second chosen spell (2nd level or less), once per day |
| 7, 10, 13... | Mask spirit gains an additional chosen spell (3rd level or less), once per day, every 3 levels thereafter |
