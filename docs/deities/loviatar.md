# Loviatar (Maiden of Pain)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Loviatar
- **Titles/Epithets:** Maiden of Pain, one of the Dark Gods
- **Portfolio:** Pain, hurt, agony, torment, suffering, torture
- **Symbol:** A black nine-stranded whip, its ends bloody and barbed (in elder days, a chalk-white,
  slim female human left hand, fingertips downward, with three drops of blood dripping from each
  fingertip)

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** spellcasters, monastic orders.
- **Organization:** A tightly organized priesthood dominated numerically and in rank by women (both
  human and half-elven). Specialty priests ("Pains") form a separate, itinerant arm serving as the
  church's envoys, secret agents, and inquisitors, held in great regard by the clerics. Mystics are
  often attached to small bands of Pains; monks live only in their own monasteries/abbeys but owe
  fealty to whichever temple their abbey is hierarchically attached to. The church is currently
  independent overall, aiding other evil churches only when convenient, and holds particular hatred
  for the churches of Ilmater and Eldath, whose specialty priests resist Loviatar's pain-inflicting
  abilities.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Kneeling Ones (novices/postulants)
  2. Taystren
  3. Adept (in Pain)
  4. Sister/Brother (in Torment)
  5. Supremar
  6. Caressor (of Terrors)
  7. Whiplass/Whiplar
  8. Paingiver
  9. Whipmistress/Whipmaster
  10. High Whipmistress/Whipmaster
  11. Branded (of the God)
  12. Truescar

  (The last two titles are reserved for those who have headed a temple, abbey, or monastery and
  distinguished themselves, then taken up an itinerant life spreading Loviatar's will -- often
  leading bands of "dark adventurers.")

### Dogma

- The world is filled with pain and torment; the best one can do is suffer the blows that cannot be
  avoided and deal pain back to those who offend.
- True pleasure is only won through pain; the strong are those who taste pain and strive on.
- Kindnesses are the best companions to hurts, and increase the intensity of suffering.
- Let mercy -- sudden abstinence from causing pain, or unlooked-for healing -- come over you seldom,
  but at whim, to increase the Mystery of Loviatar's Mercy.
- Unswerving cruelty will turn all folk against you.
- Act alluring, and give pain and torment to those who enjoy it as well as to those who deserve it
  most or would be most hurt by it.
- The lash, fire, and cold are the three pains that never fail the devout.
- Spread these teachings whenever punishment is meted out.
- Pain tests all, but gives strength of spirit to the hardy and the true.
- There is no true punishment if the punisher knows no discipline.
- Wherever a whip is, there is Loviatar -- fear her, and yet long for her.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Tirelessly cause suffering, both widespread and personal -- the clergy's "Prime Charge."
- Range from brutal (flogging a captured band of enemies until they flee) to subtle (breaking hearts
  and friendships among nobles through disguised seduction and staged scandal).
- Keep activities energetic and numerous, but not so high-profile that local authorities move to
  imprison or thwart them on sight.
- Cultivate striking beauty and good acting (or illusion magic to appear so), and study the ways of
  folk to know exactly how to cause them the most pain.
- Whisper of Loviatar's ever-present power in the darkness after inflicting pain.
- Always pray for battle spells before appearing in public, and remain alert.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Kneeling Prayer | Morning and evening, daily | Performed after striking oneself once with a whip. |
| Consecration Rites | As needed | Consecrate wine, whips, holy symbols, and other items both practical (potions of healing) and horrific (torture implements); also mark advancement in priestly rank. |
| Rite of Pain and Purity | All four seasonal festivals | A circle dance of chanting clergy performed atop barbed wire, thorns, or broken glass, urged to greater efforts by the whips of high-level priests and the drumming of lay worshipers. A red radiance rises above the ring; Loviatar may manifest within it if she has a mission, is especially pleased, or wishes to show displeasure. If she does not appear, the rite ends after half an hour and the priests heal themselves. |
| Candle Rites | Every twelfth night (unless preempted by a Rite of Pain and Purity) | Clergy sing, chant, and dance around lit candles, passing parts of their bodies through the flames repeatedly; the rite ends when the highest-ranking priestess present extinguishes her candle with consecrated wine. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No affiliated knightly orders.
- (Formal) Disciples of the White Rod -- the monastic order of Loviatar's monks, named for the token
  granted to their founder by the goddess, kept as a relic at their home abbey near Calimport.
- (Informal) Way of Transcendence -- an eccentric philosophy/order followed by the faith's mystics,
  who refuse to disclose its actual tenets.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Pain
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons, plus whip and scourge; favored weapon scourge
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Medium armor (pleated ceremonial scale mail preferred), shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Combat, Elemental, Healing, Law, Necromantic, Sun
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Divination, Guardian, Summoning, Time, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (leatherworking), 1 rank in scourge
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on endurance; +1 bonus on rope use; +1
  bonus on whip; +1 bonus on intimidation
