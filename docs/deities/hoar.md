# Hoar (The Doombringer, Lord of Three Thunders, the Poet of Justice, Hurler of Thunders)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Hoar
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Doombringer, Lord of Three Thunders, the Poet of Justice, Hurler of Thunders
- **Portfolio:** Revenge, retribution, poetic justice
- **Symbol:** A black-gloved right hand holding a coin with a two-faced head, or three lightning bolts,
  or three deep rolls of thunder

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders)
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, specialty priests, and crusaders of Hoar receive religion (Faerunian) and
  religion (Untheric) as bonus skills.
- **Organization:** Hoar is propitiated far more than he is formally worshiped -- inhabitants of the
  Realms commonly credit fitting acts of justice to his hand, and the helpless and weak sometimes
  entreat him for vengeance, but few worship him in set services at shrines or temples. His few temples
  are plain, severe stone edifices, usually built in high, hidden places where their inhabitants can
  secretly brood and plot vengeance against all who have slighted them. Roughly 40% of his clergy are
  clerics, 30% are crusaders, and 30% are specialty priests (doombringers). The clergy is splintered
  into a multitude of backstabbing factions with centuries-old hatreds and constantly shifting
  alliances; titles vary by faction.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order, Heartlands factions):
  1. Eye of Irony
  2. Hand of Doom
  3. Fist of Vengeance
  4. Claw of Revenge
  5. Fateful Eye of Irony
  6. Fateful Hand of Doom
  7. Fateful Fist of Vengeance
  8. Fateful Claw of Revenge

  Senior priests beyond this ladder are known as Lords of Thunderous Vengeance.

### Dogma

- Uphold true and fitting justice, and maintain the spirit of the law, not merely its letter.
- Fitting recompense will always accrue for one's actions -- violence meets violence and evil is repaid
  with evil, but good also comes to those who do good.
- Walk carefully the line between seeking retribution and pursuing evil for evil's own sake; that path
  is seductive and leads only to downfall.
- Vengeance must be sought for all injustices, and all punishments must fit the crime -- revenge is
  sweetest when sharpened with irony.
- All attacks must be avenged. Those who do not respond to attacks against themselves or what they hold
  dear only invite future attacks.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Wander from town to town as itinerant clergy (true temples are rare in the western Realms), praying
  for Hoar's intercession on behalf of those who seek or fear vengeance, in exchange for a small fee.
- Charlatans posing as Hoar's clergy, or priests who neglect prayers they have promised to make, receive
  a fitting punishment by Hoar's own hand.
- Seek out victims of injustice, hear their stories, evaluate their veracity, and track down
  perpetrators to inflict a fitting punishment -- "essential justice." No injustice is too large or too
  small.
- Regarded by most town watches and Tyrists as vigilantes, but seen by common folk as champions of the
  downtrodden and underdogs.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Personal anniversaries | Individual, ongoing | Each priest is encouraged to celebrate the anniversary of their sweetest act of revenge, and to mark the anniversary of each as-yet-unavenged injury, insult, or slight with quiet contemplation (bordering on brooding) of strategies to enact it. Priests are also expected to remember those who have helped them and contemplate lasting rewards for them. |
| The Penultimate Thunder | 11th of Eleint | Marks the Doombringer's long-anticipated victory over Ramman; celebrated with great feasts of game, bread, fruit, and mead. |
| The Impending Doom | 11th of Marpenoth | Daylong ceremonies of rumbling drums, vigorous oaths, and exhausting acts of purification; celebrates justices yet to be meted out, revenges yet to be carried through, and good deeds that call to be remembered. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Hunters of Vengeance -- a small, informal order of bounty hunters and vigilantes active
  throughout the Heartlands and the North, whose actions are spoken of with admiration and dread long
  after they move on.
- *(Formal)* Fellowship of Poetic Justices -- an order of bards and crusaders founded after the Time of
  Troubles, dedicated jointly to Hoar and Tyr and supported by clergy from both faiths; seeks to spread
  tales of ironic justice throughout the Realms.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Doombringer
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any (if fitting the intended vengeance); must carry at least one blunt, one
  piercing, and one slashing weapon at all times; favored weapon javelin ("Retribution's Sting," a
  javelin of lightning) -- matches this book's own account of Hoar's avatar hurling an inexhaustible
  quiver of lightning javelins
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Combat, Law, Guardian, Necromantic, Summoning, Travelers, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Divination, Healing, Protection, Time
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in a blunt weapon, 1 rank in a piercing weapon, and 1 rank in a slashing
  weapon (one of each, as soon as possible); 1 rank in survival (hunting)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on blind-fighting; +1 bonus on survival
  (tracking is a survival skill action); +1 bonus on linguistics (pick two: dwarvish, elvish, gnome,
  halfling, orc, Untheric)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2, "once per tenday" = level × 1).
Where no spell level is given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2,
rounded up.

| 1 | Once per day may incite a focused rage against a specific named target of righteous justice: +2 on attack and damage rolls and +2 on all saves against that target, lasting a turn; cannot flee from the target while raging (no mana cost) |

| 1 | May speak a Prayer of Revenance over a dying being who begs for vengeance, raising the chance the deceased rises as a revenant (25% if Constitution 18 and Wisdom or Intelligence 17+, otherwise 5%) |
