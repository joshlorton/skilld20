# Set (Lord of Evil, Defiler of the Dead, Lord of Carrion, Father of Jackals, Brother of Serpents, Outcast of the Gods, King of Malice)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Set
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of Evil, Defiler of the Dead, Lord of Carrion, Father of Jackals, Brother of
  Serpents, Outcast of the Gods, King of Malice
- **Portfolio:** Evil, the desert, desert storms, destruction, drought, night, rot, predators and
  carrion-eaters, snakes, hate, malice, betrayal, evil magic, ambition, poison, murder
- **Symbol:** A coiled cobra

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics and specialty priests of Set receive religion (pantheon), literacy
  (Mulhorandi), and linguistics (Common) as bonus proficiencies, and know Mulhorandi as a native tongue.
  All priests of Set must shave their heads even outside Mulhorand, where the practice is otherwise the
  cultural norm.
- **Organization:** Set has numerous followers throughout Mulhorand and a growing cult in Unther,
  feared and relentlessly denounced by the other Mulhorandi priesthoods. Before the Godswar, all of
  Set's priests had to be of Mulhorandi extraction; since the Time of Troubles that restriction has
  been lifted. Temples are always hidden, often belowground, twisted parodies of whatever powerful local
  temple they emulate in ornamentation. By the pharaoh's decree, all of Set's priests are publicly
  called Eaters of Carrion. Approximately 60% of the clergy are specialty priests, known as nighthunters,
  with the remainder clerics -- though since the Godswar Set has refused to promote any cleric above the
  rank of Jackal, and their numbers are slowly dropping.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Supplicant
  2. Snake
  3. Scorpion
  4. Crocodile
  5. Jackal
  6. High Snake
  7. High Scorpion
  8. High Crocodile
  9. Lord High Jackal

### Dogma

- Destroy the priesthoods of Horus-Re and Osiris, bring Set to his rightful place as god-king of
  Mulhorand, and spread his cult throughout the Realms.
- Sacrifice both wealth and sentient lives to Set.
- The ends justify the means -- life is a struggle to be won by the powerful and the ruthless.
- Loyalty to comrades is admirable so long as it advances Set's cause; those who serve him well will be
  justly rewarded when he accedes to his rightful throne.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Undermine and overthrow the theocracy of Mulhorand through ruthless and subtle plans.
- Oversee the activities of thieves, assassins, and other evil beings, financing plots and mocking the
  beliefs of rival faiths; several orders of assassins are headed by Set's clergy.
- Never betray a member of the "brotherhood of evil," and always work to promote it.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rite of Victory | Whenever a major victory is achieved in Set's name | Set's clergy keep no fixed calendar of holy days -- important ceremonies and sacrifices are performed spontaneously to mark triumphs. |
| The Test of Venom | Upon a Supplicant's induction into full clergy | Aspirants walk through a pit of poisonous snakes or scorpions; survivors are admitted to the priesthood. |
| The Reconsecration | As opportunity allows, on defiled tombs | Grave-robbing Settite priests desecrate a burial chamber, raise an unholy altar to Set, loot its treasure, and animate the dead as undead horrors -- a rite that enrages the clergy of Osiris and Nephthys, who must then resanctify the site. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Informal) The Fangs of Set -- a wide-ranging network of assassins, spies, and thugs grown from a
  disrupted adventuring company (originally led by Hodkamset, Nekiset, and Hamsetis), permeating every
  level of Mulhorandi society and working to rule Mulhorand from behind the scenes.
- (Informal) Numerous small bands of mercenaries, assassins, and thieves sponsored by Set's clergy, most
  short-lived before being hunted down, and ultimately reporting back to the Fangs of Set regardless.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Nighthunter
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons, composite bow, dagger, dart,
  blowgun, knife, khopesh, scimitar, spear, whip; favored weapon spear ("Spear of Darkness," confirmed
  via real-world 3.5e sourcing; matches this book's own avatar weapon)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Astral, Charm, Combat, Elemental, Guardian, Necromantic, Protection,
  Summoning
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Healing, Sun (darkness spells only), Thought, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** None specified in the source
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on disguise; +1 bonus on tumbling

Only humans and half-orcs of Mulhorandi extraction can become nighthunters, and all must shave their
heads even outside Mulhorand. Nighthunters can backstab as thieves of equal experience level.
