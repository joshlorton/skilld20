# Callarduran Smoothhands (Deep Brother, Master of Stone, Lord of Deepearth, the Deep Gnome)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Callarduran Smoothhands
- **Titles/Epithets:** Deep Brother, Master of Stone, Lord of Deepearth, the Deep Gnome
- **Portfolio:** Stone, the deep underground, the Underdark, mining, the svirfneblin
- **Symbol:** A gold ring with a star pattern

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics, cleric/illusionists, and cleric/thieves) and
  specialty priests receive a bonus rank in religion (pantheon).
- **Organization:** Temples are built in natural caverns smoothed over centuries by priests rubbing
  the rough stone with bare hands. At the center stands a stalagmite altar inlaid with hundreds of
  tiny rubies, said to hold power drawn from the Elemental Plane of Earth -- including the ability
  to animate itself as a 24-Hit-Die earth elemental if the temple is attacked. Nearly the entire
  clergy is deep gnome (97%, with 2% rock gnome and 1% forest gnome), and the priesthood is
  male-only. Composition is cleric/illusionists (45%), specialty priests (40%), fighter/clerics (8%),
  clerics (5%), and cleric/thieves (2%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. The Unworked (novice)
  2. First Facet
  3. Second Facet
  4. Third Facet
  5. Fourth Facet
  6. Fifth Facet
  7. Sixth Facet
  8. Seventh Facet
  9. Eighth Facet
  10. Ruby (highest common rank; individual high priests hold unique titles)

### Dogma

- Callarduran led his chosen people into the deepest depths of the earth so that they might discover
  the joyous beauty of rubies and other gems.
- Beware the dangers of Deepearth, and guard against evil races -- especially the drow -- who employ
  any means necessary to seize what is not rightfully theirs.
- Protect and serve the interests of your community.
- Preserve and nurture the depths that give you and your people life.
- Celebrate the intricacies of minerals, especially gems, and their many forms, uses, and subtle
  variations -- including their latent magical potentials.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Stay ever vigilant against drow incursions into deep gnome territory; many lead small war bands
  against the drow to exterminate them before they threaten nearby svirfneblin enclaves.
- Teach magic, particularly the schools of elemental earth and illusion/phantasm, spreading such
  knowledge throughout the community.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Festival of the Ruby | Midsummer day | Marks Callarduran's hiding of rubies and other gems in the earth for deep gnomes to find, symbolized by the Great Red Ruby (the setting sun) sinking below the horizon. |
| Festival of the Star | Midwinter night | Deep gnomes gather at a subterranean lake or pool to watch a specially bred phosphorescent fungus in the cavern roof light up like stars reflected in the water, reaffirming their ties to the surface world. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Wardens of the Webspinners -- warriors, warrior/priests, and priests trained in tactics
  against the drow, with chapters in most gnome cities that must regularly contest territory with the
  Spider Queen's followers; many members are deep gnome burrow wardens (6th level or greater
  fighters).

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Earthblood
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (leather, studded leather) or Medium (chain mail); shield
  permitted
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Combat, Creation, Elemental (Earth), Guardian, Healing, Protection,
  Summoning, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Elemental (Air, Fire, Water), Necromantic, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in battle axe, 1 rank in craft (stonemasonry)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on craft (gem cutting); +1 bonus on
  craft (mining)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2 bonus to all saving throws against spells cast by drow spellcasters |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Strength of Stone (10 mana) |
| 3 | Deep gnomes can summon an earth elemental (as their innate racial ability) once per day with a 25% chance of success (5% for rock gnomes); the type summoned is determined randomly, weighted toward smaller elementals with a chance of a xorn or outright failure |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Meld into Stone *or* Stone Shape, choose one per use (30 mana each) |
| 5 | Can subtract 5% per level (maximum 50%) from the magic resistance of drow targeted by their spells |
| 6 | Summoning chance from Rank 3 increases to 65% for deep gnomes, 15% for rock gnomes |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Dig *or* Stoneskin, choose one per use (40 mana each) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Lower Resistance *or* Wall of Stone, choose one per use (50 mana each) |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Move Earth *or* Stone Tell, choose one per use (60 mana each) |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Animate Rock *or* Earthquake, choose one per use (70 mana each) |

## Callardurian Spells (deity-specific)

Priests of Callarduran may also cast detect metals and minerals (1st level, detailed under Geb in
*Powers & Pantheons*), strength of stone (1st level, detailed under Moradin), and depress resistance
(3rd level, detailed under Shevarash) -- these are added to the standard priest spell list rather
than granted as free castings, so they are not repeated here.

| Name | Est. Mana | Foundation | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Animate Stalactite | 10 | Elemental Earth | Temporarily awakens a stalactite to act as a piercer, dropping to impale prey once it detects them; the animation ends (and the stalactite shatters if it misses) after a single attack. |
| Ruby Axe | 30 | Combat | Causes the caster's hand axe or battle axe to glow with a faint red edge, becoming magical (if it wasn't already) and gaining a growing attack and damage bonus with caster level, cumulative with the weapon's own enchantment. |
| Stone Form | 40 | Elemental Earth | Transforms a willing recipient into living stone, granting an improved Armor Class and immunity to nonmagical attacks, but leaving them vulnerable to any spell that affects stone. |
