# Ilmater (The Crying God, the Lord on the Rack, the One Who Endures, the Broken God)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Ilmater
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Crying God, the Lord on the Rack, the One Who Endures, the Broken God
- **Portfolio:** Endurance, suffering, martyrdom, perseverance
- **Symbol:** A pair of white human hands bound at the wrists with blood-red cord (older symbol: a
  blood-stained rack)

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, monastic orders
- **Organization:** Novices undergo a gentle, exploratory conversation with a senior priest followed
  by a dinner where they are given a trance-inducing wine, during which friendly clergy and wizards
  use mind-scrying spells to verify the novice holds no dedication to evil or conflicting loyalty.
  Once accepted, a novice is robed and becomes one of "the Adorned" -- the collective term for all
  clerics, specialty priests, and monks of the faith. Monastic orders usually dwell apart in
  monasteries and abbeys, though some monks also live in temples to teach or provide protection.
  Ilmatari are loosely ranked under the Revered Mother or Father of the temple/abbey/monastery they
  report to, with monasteries and abbeys often forming a second, informal tier tied to a specific
  temple.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Brother / Sister (any of the Adorned)
  2. Revered Brother / Revered Sister (senior clergy)
  3. Father / Mother (head of a temple, abbey, or monastery)

### Dogma

- Help all who hurt, no matter what they are; the truly holy take on the suffering of others.
- If you suffer in Ilmater's name, he will be there to support you.
- Stick to your cause if it is right, whatever the pain and peril.
- Stand up to all tyrants, resisting injustice in ways both great and small, and allow no injustice to
  go unchallenged.
- There is no shame in a meaningful death.
- "Persevere in the face of pain. Heal the sick, the wounded, and the diseased. Comfort the dying, the
  griefstricken, and the heartsick."
- Take on the burdens and pain of others.
- Champion the causes of the oppressed and unjustly treated, and give shelter and kind counsel to the
  lonely, the lost, and the ruined.
- Pursue the service of Ilmater, and he will provide -- leave gross riches and the acquisition of all
  but medicines to others.
- Take up the tasks no others dare.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Share what they have with those in need; counsel the upset and give healing and tender care to the
  injured.
- Speak for the oppressed, guide the lost, feed the hungry, and shelter the homeless.
- Gather herbs and make medicines at all times in preparation for disasters to come.
- When war is expected, gather in strength with litters, shovels, tents, splints, bandages, and wagons
  of medicines and healing potions.
- Bury the dead, treat the diseased, and give food, drink, and firewood to the poor.
- Do not stand in the way of others' chosen paths or condemn them for it, even a warrior's wish to die
  in battle -- instead assist them toward their own goal in the most honorable way available.
- Tour the wealthier cities and settlements of Faerûn soliciting donations to support the church.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Turning | At the side of any dying person (not calendar-fixed) | The most important ritual of the church: every priest's duty is to bring a dying person to turn to Ilmater for comfort and receive his blessing before they expire. |
| The Rest | As needed, by special Plea (not calendar-fixed) | A tenday dispensation from serving Ilmater's dictates, granted after a Plea; usually requested when emotionally exhausted, though church leaders sometimes use it to send trusted clergy on missions Ilmater would otherwise frown upon. |

No calendar-related holy days or festivals are observed; instead, daily prayer to Ilmater at least six
times a day governs every day of service.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Painbearer
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons and the scourge; favored weapon unarmed strike (open
  hand)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** None (unarmored); no shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, charm, creation, guardian, healing, law, necromantic, protection, travelers
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, elemental, summoning, sun, wards, weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in healing
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on endurance; +1 bonus on herbalism; +1
  bonus on diplomacy when counseling the suffering or grieving

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is (e.g. "at 12th level, priests gain the ability to..."), use
spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +4 bonus to saves involving endurance or resisting pain/suffering; +2 bonus on Constitution ability checks in such situations (e.g. swimming, drowning); trained to recognize disease, injury, or affliction at a glance (via studied illusion drills during training) |
| 1 | Can survive without food or water for a number of tendays equal to level (attack/damage rolls suffer -1 per 3 tendays without food/water thereafter, but the painbearer does not die) |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Remove Fear *or* permanently dispel a baneful pain/suffering/hopelessness emotion effect by touch, usable on self plus up to two others per day (30 mana) |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Holy Might, usable without needing rest afterward (20 mana) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Favor of Ilmater (30 mana) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Endurance of Ilmater (40 mana) |
| 7 | Can brew healing potions starting at 7th level rather than the normal 9th |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Commune with Ilmater, 1/tenday (5 mana) |
| 12 | Can be voluntarily possessed by a beneficial servant of Ilmater while suffering extreme punishment, torture, or pain -- the servant absorbs all pain inflicted (no physical damage) until the suffering passes or a dispel magic drives it out |
