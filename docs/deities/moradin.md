# Moradin (The Soul Forger, Dwarf-Father, the All-Father, the Creator)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Moradin
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Soul Forger, Dwarf-Father, the All-Father, the Creator
- **Portfolio:** Dwarves (survival, renewal, and advancement), creation, smithing of all sorts,
  craftsmanship, war, the dwarven race, protection, metalcraft, stonework (stonemasonry, tunneling,
  construction), engineering, dwarven engineers
- **Symbol:** Hammer and anvil

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders)
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, crusaders, and specialty priests of Moradin receive the standard dwarven
  clergy grants (literacy (Dethek runes), modified turn undead) -- see `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** Moradin's clergy is nearly evenly split between specialty priests (47%) and clerics
  (43%), with a handful of crusaders (5%) and fighter/clerics (5%). The priesthood draws on gold
  dwarves (50%) and shield dwarves (48%), with a scattering of jungle and gray dwarves, and remains 94%
  male. Temples are always carved from solid rock (never natural caverns), built around vast smithies
  and a great ever-burning forge-hearth; should that fire ever be extinguished, the temple is abandoned
  or torn down stone by stone and rebuilt elsewhere.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Unworked (novice)
  2. Adept of the Anvil
  3. Hammer of War
  4. Artisan of the Forge
  5. Craftsman of Runes
  6. Artificer of Discoveries
  7. Smith of Souls (full priests collectively: Forgesmiths, or the Tempered)
  8. High Forgesmiths (High Old Ones, who also hold individual titles)

  Specialty priests are known as sonnlinor, a dwarvish word loosely translated as "those who work
  stone."

### Dogma

- The Soul Forger is the father and creator of the dwarven race; honor the All-Father by emulating
  both his principles and his workmanship in smithcraft, stoneworking, and other tasks.
- Wisdom is derived from life tempered with experience.
- Advance the dwarven race in all areas of life; innovate with new processes and skills, testing and
  refining them until they are pure.
- Found new kingdoms and clan lands, defending those that already exist from threats within and
  without.
- Lead the Stout Folk in the traditional ways laid down by the Soul Forger; honor your clan leaders as
  you honor Moradin.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Strive to restore the dwarven race to strong numbers and a position of influence in Faerûn, by
  founding new dwarven kingdoms and raising dwarven standing within human-dominated society.
- Preside over a wide range of formal ceremonies (consecration of forges, temples, and other buildings,
  the crowning of monarchs, and so on) and the education of the young, especially in history.
- Maintain genealogies and historical archives, cooperating with the clergy of Berronar.
- Adventuring is encouraged in the priesthood, but only when it directly serves the interests of the
  dwarven race.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Forge Gathering | Monthly, at the full or crescent moon (varies by dwarven culture) | Clergy gather around the forge to celebrate the All-Father, making offerings of common or worked precious metal that are melted down and reformed for the clergy's use, amid chanting, kneeling, and bare-handed handling of red- and white-hot objects (which Moradin prevents from harming the truly faithful). |
| Declared Holy Days | Any time, at the discretion of a High Forgesmith | Local celebrations of a notable dwarven event. |

*Worship at any gathering closes with a rising, quickening unison chant ("The dwarves shall prevail,
the dwarves shall endure, the dwarves shall grow!") repeated until the plain smith's hammer on the
forge's largest anvil rises of its own accord and descends again with a thunderous ring, denoting the
listening god's will or pleasure.*

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Hammers of Moradin -- an elite military order dominated by crusaders and fighter/clerics,
  with chapters in nearly every dwarven stronghold and members drawn from every dwarven clan. The
  Hammers command dwarven armies and serve as an elite strike force against anything from orc hordes to
  great wyrms to fiends; a Grand Council of reigning monarchs and senior Hammers convenes in times of
  great crisis to plot strategy and divine Moradin's will.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Sonnlinor
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All weapons; favored weapon warhammer
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Creation, Divination, Elemental, Guardian, Healing, Law, Protection,
  Sun, War
- **Minor Spheres:** Astral, Necromantic, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in war hammer; pick one: craft (armorer), craft (weaponsmithing)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on craft (blacksmithing); +1 bonus on
  craft (stonemasonry)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Animate Weapon, Command, *or* Strength of Stone, choose one per use (10 mana) |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Protection from Paralysis *or* Spiritual Hammer, choose one per use (20 mana) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Dispel Magic *or* Stone Shape, choose one per use (30 mana) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Stonefire (40 mana) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: True Seeing, four times per day (200 mana) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Flame Strike (50 mana) |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Defensive Harmony *or* Soul Forge, choose one per use (50 mana) |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Elemental Aura, five times per tenday (45 mana) |
