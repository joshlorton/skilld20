# Valkur (The Mighty, Captain of the Waves)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Valkur
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Mighty, Captain of the Waves
- **Portfolio:** Sailors, the safety of ships, favorable winds, naval combat
- **Symbol:** A shield showing a thundercloud with three lightning bolts descending from it

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics and specialty priests of Valkur receive religion (Faerunian) and seamanship
  as bonus skills.
- **Organization:** Valkur's church is small but influential along the coasts, though little known
  inland; where Umberlee's church holds sway, sailors sometimes dare not even speak his name for fear of
  angering her. The clergy is roughly 40% clerics and 60% specialty priests (wavetamers), and the balance
  keeps shifting toward the latter as Valkur's rising popularity is credited largely to their efforts.
  Temples always stand in city port districts, right on the wharf -- partially open-air above ground,
  with tall ironwood mast-like columns hung with fluttering sails, and below ground laid out like a
  ship's hold, stocked with rations donated to sailors and families in need. Each temple ranks its
  priests strictly, but temples rarely recognize each other's rankings. Titles vary by region; along the
  Sword Coast, the following hierarchy (ascending) is typical, with novices called swabs:
  1. Gob
  2. Seaman
  3. Sailor
  4. Boatswain
  5. Third Mate
  6. Second Mate
  7. First Mate
  8. Captain
  9. Commodore
  10. Admiral

  Regardless of level, the highest-ranking priest of any given temple is called High Captain.

### Dogma

- There is nothing more invigorating than challenging the elements -- the wind and spray on one's face,
  the deck pitching underfoot, is the greatest feeling in the world.
- If humankind is to expand its reach, daring men and women must defy the odds and dare the impossible;
  the thrill of exploration is sweeter than wine or rum.
- There is always risk, but a life without risk is empty. Life is to be lived, and the consequences be
  damned.
- Loyalty runs first to one's mates, then to one's ship, and only then to Valkur himself.
- Do not rely on Valkur's hand to always pull the faithful from difficulty -- that is coddling, and robs
  them of the room to grow. He helps most those who find their own solutions and lets his aid make those
  plans work.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Captain sailing ships along the Sword Coast and the Sea of Fallen Stars, or serve as officers aboard
  someone else's.
- Serve as privateers for their homeland rather than as pirates, when a fighting role is called for.
- While in port, provide care and small monetary support to sailors down on their luck and to families
  who have lost their breadwinner at sea.
- Run shipyards, administer merchant fleets, or sponsor (if not wholly fund) seaborne exploration and
  long-distance trading expeditions for the challenge and opportunity they offer.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Shattering | Early spring, exact date varies year to year (revealed by Valkur to his High Captains) | Marks the end of winter and the start of the sailing season, celebrated even where winter ice never troubled travel. New sails are unfurled throughout port districts, and at least one newly christened ship departs on its maiden voyage crewed by the clergy. |
| Ship ceremonies | Ongoing, at every christening, departure, arrival, and homecoming | Valkur's clergy perform over a dozen distinct ceremonies tied to ships and voyages; these are the church's main source of operating funds, paid for by merchants seeking his favor on high-risk voyages. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Marines of the Seven Seas -- a modest war-fleet sponsored by Valkur's temples, stationed
  along the Sea of Swords and the Sea of Fallen Stars, dedicated to hunting down dragon turtles, giant
  octopi, krakens, morkoths, and other sea monsters that prey on shipping.
- *(Informal)* Sailors of the Heavenly Seas -- a sect of spelljammers who left Faerûn to sail the void
  between the stars and rarely return, regarding Valkur more as a god of travel and exploration than of
  the sea specifically.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Wavetamer
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons, plus cutlass, harpoon, net, and
  trident; favored weapon cutlass (matches this book's own Captain's Cutlass and 3.5e sourcing directly)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light and Medium (up to chain mail); no shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Combat, Divination, Elemental (Air and Water), Healing, Protection, Sun,
  Summoning, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Necromantic, War, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics and fighters)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in cutlass, 1 rank in swim, 1 rank in direction sense
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on navigation (a further +1 applies as a
  minor blessing Selûne passes on to Valkur's faithful, given the two deities' friendship); +1 bonus on
  seamanship; +1 bonus on lore (weather); +1 bonus on literacy (Common)
