# Urdlen (The Crawler Below, the Evil One)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Urdlen
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Crawler Below, the Evil One
- **Portfolio:** Greed, bloodlust, blood, evil, hatred, uncontrolled impulse, spriggans
- **Symbol:** A blood-soaked skull -- the holy symbol priests actually carry; Urdlen's own sign is a
  plain white mole, and many priests also keep a blood-fed white mole as a living token of their god

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics, cleric/illusionists, and cleric/thieves) and
  specialty priests of Urdlen receive religion (pantheon) as a bonus nonweapon proficiency; none of
  Urdlen's clergy can turn undead, but all of them command undead, as a cleric two levels below their
  own.
- **Organization:** Urdlen's cult is despised and feared by the Forgotten Folk, a particular threat to
  rock and deep gnomes and a lesser scourge even to forest gnomes; spriggans both venerate and fear the
  Crawler Below, and most are counted among its followers. The cult is opposed by good-aligned dwarves,
  gnomes, and halflings, and is little known to other surface races, who tend to fold tales of its
  rampages into generic horror stories from below; subterranean races are more likely to have been
  directly assaulted by Urdlen's avatar or its followers. Temples are rare -- Urdlen's followers seldom
  set aside their destructive urges long enough to build one -- and what few exist are little more than
  blood-spattered shrines in lightless natural caverns, dominated by a crude stone altar stained with
  the lifeblood of countless sacrifices. Novices are the Unblooded; full priests are Deep Crawlers, each
  also bearing a unique personal title. The clergy includes rock gnomes (60%), deep gnomes (18%), forest
  gnomes (12%), and spriggans (10%); it is 70% male, and composed of specialty priests (60%),
  cleric/thieves (15%), clerics (12%), fighter/clerics (10%), and cleric/illusionists (3%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Unblooded (novice)
  2. Deep Crawler (full priest; individual priests also hold unique personal titles)

  Specialty priests are known as bloodstalkers.

### Dogma

- Succumb to bloodlust. Seize power directly. Hate, covet, crush, despoil, and kill.
- Revel and exult in orgies of death and destruction; that which lives or was made by life must be
  murdered or destroyed, for that is the ultimate end of all things, and to deny it is to deny the
  truth of existence.
- The strong survive, and the weak are their cattle.
- Do what Urdlen wants first; second, do whatever you want. Give in to every evil impulse, for there is
  no use in covering over the truth of your nature.
- Propitiate the Crawler Below with sacrifice so that it does not come for you itself.
- Existence is a cosmic joke before the death that comes at Urdlen's claws; sharing the cruel ironies
  and harsh humor of existence with others only helps toughen them for what is coming.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Wage unending war from subterranean warrens on communities of the Forgotten Folk, particularly the
  clergies of the other gnome gods.
- Steal, deface, or destroy objects of value -- particularly gems and works of art -- when not hunting
  other creatures.
- Share Urdlen's love of evil, deadly pranks directed against all creatures, gnomes included.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Feeding | Regular ritual, no fixed interval given | Priests appease Urdlen by spilling the blood of their prey on the ground and burying it, alongside jewels and valuable metal goods ruined by breaking, tarnishing, or melting before also being buried. |
| Night of Blood | Midwinter | Followers gather in subterranean caverns to offer blood sacrifices in appeasement of the god's wrath. If Urdlen is displeased by the volume of blood or the value of the despoiled goods offered, it may appear and slay all the assembled worshipers in an orgy of unbridled destruction. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Small spriggan family bands are the closest thing to organization Urdlen's cult manages
  -- its followers are otherwise too chaotic and consumed by bloodlust to form long-lasting bands, let
  alone a militant order, gathering at most into ad hoc raiding parties that rarely stay together for
  more than a few raids.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Bloodstalker
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any slashing and piercing weapons; favored weapon claws of Urdlen (claw
  bracer)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Chaos, Combat, Elemental Earth, Healing, Protection, Sun (reversed
  only), War
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Elemental Fire, Necromantic, Summoning
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** None
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on Blind-fighting
