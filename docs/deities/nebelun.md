# Nebelun (the Meddler)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Nebelun
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Meddler
- **Portfolio:** Invention, tinkering, and gnome artificers, as venerated within the gnome pantheon
- **Symbol:** Not given separately in this entry -- see the note below

*This is a genuinely thin, special-case entry in the source material, and this file deliberately
does not force it into the full deity template. In the Realms, "Nebelun the Meddler" is simply the
name the Forgotten Folk use for Gond the Wonderbringer -- Gond assumed Nebelun's aspect and appeared
to the gnomes in gnome form on the shores of Lantan during the Time of Troubles. The source text
explicitly redirects readers to Gond's full entry in Faiths & Avatars rather than detailing a
separate church, dogma, day-to-day activities, holy days, affiliated orders, vestments, or specialty
priest for Nebelun -- all of that content already exists at [gond.md](gond.md), and this file does
not duplicate it. A new, small, emerging cult reportedly believes Nebelun and Gond are in fact
separate gods, but the source material gives this belief no distinct priesthood, ritual, or
mechanical shape. Gond/Nebelun is counted as a member of both the gnome pantheon and the human
Faerûnian pantheon.*

### Notable Devotee

- Nadul Da-Roni, a former hermit gnome living in the wilds outside High Horn, gained fame over the
  past decade marketing his mechanical inventions through *Aurora's Whole Realms Catalog*. He
  unwittingly built a small following of gnome inventors devoted to Nebelun as a result.
