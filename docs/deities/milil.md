# Milil (Lord of Song, the Lord of All Songs, Guardian of Singers and Troubadours, the One True Hand of All-Wise Oghma)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Milil
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of Song, the Lord of All Songs, Guardian of Singers and Troubadours, the
  One True Hand of All-Wise Oghma
- **Portfolio:** Poetry, song, eloquence
- **Symbol:** A silver harp, often depicted as a fire-stringed instrument whose body is made of silvery
  leaves

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** spellcasters, bardic performers
- **All Clergy:** Required to take singing and musical instrument as nonweapon proficiencies,
  regardless of class.
- **Organization:** An organized faith; all churches nominally answer to the Patriarch of Song in
  Waterdeep, though his influence wanes with distance. About half the priesthood are clerics, the rest
  specialty priests with a scattering of bards, mystics, and spellsingers. Relations between clerics
  and specialty priests are good, if occasionally strained by tuneservants using enthrall and
  suggestion powers to raise their own status.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order, general clergy -- "Sorlyn"):
  1. Mute One (novice)
  2. Chanter
  3. Chorister
  4. Soloist
  5. Lead Voice
  6. First Voice
  7. Songmaster
  8. Glorian (used by all senior clergy in addition to any temple office they hold)

  Temple offices include Castellan, Master Tutor, Master Wind, Master Serenader, Master Librarian,
  Master Instrumentalist, Prior, and Patriarch. Specialty priests (Tuneservants) address one another as
  "Harmonian," regardless of rank.

### Dogma

- Life is a song: strive always to make it more beautiful.
- Destroy no music nor instrument, nor stop a singer before the tune is done.
- Listen to the world around you as well as filling it with your own sound.
- One singer's music is another's noise, so still no bad music if its making is joyful.
- Spread the teaching of song and musicianship always; sing to Milil every day.
- Encourage the training, use, and preservation of music at all times and in all possible ways.
- Awaken a love of song in all folk you can, and offer its performance freely around the campfire or on
  the trail.
- Cease not in your own seeking for new tunes, new techniques, and new instruments to master.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Sorlyn spend their time learning lyrics, tunes, and how best to perform them on a slowly expanding
  repertoire of instruments, both in temple and on the road.
- They write down original compositions and learned works, and use the singing stone spell to record
  tunes for those not yet born, caching copies both hidden and in temple vaults.
- Sorlyn tutor all who profess faith in Milil (or pay for training), and judge or adjudicate bardic
  contests and disputes.
- Tuneservants are more adventurous: they roam the roads rescuing or protecting minstrels and bards who
  fall on hard times or into peril, accompany other adventurers to compose ballads of their deeds, and
  recover music and instruments from ruins and tombs.
- Tithe/offering: no coin tithe is described. Devout worshipers call out a Song of Praise to Milil at
  least once a day and after every victory or boon.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Song of Praise | Daily, and after victories | A call sung out to Milil in gratitude or celebration. |
| Sunrise Song | Daily, upon waking | A personal song, or a softly voiced temple chorus, sung upon awakening. |
| Song of Sorrowing | At the funeral of any faithful of Milil | Solemn, beautiful polyphonic chord-singing. |
| Song of Welcoming | Upon a new member joining the faith | Sung to welcome them formally. |
| Call to the Flowers | Greengrass | Sung by all the faithful. |
| The Grand Revel | Midsummer | A feast, dancing, and roistering marked by parody and wickedly satirical song. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* The Harmonious Order -- a knightly order of personable (and sometimes swaggering)
  fighters, paladins, and bards who guard temples and holy sites alongside the clergy and pursue quests
  or good works in Milil's name; tuneservants love to accompany them.
- *(Informal)* Ties to Those Who Harp (the Harpers) -- Milil's silver harp symbol is not meant to
  directly link him to the Harpers' crescent-moon-and-harp emblem, but the church maintains friendly
  ties with them regardless.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Tuneservant (addressed as "Harmonian")
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon rapier
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Any (Light, Medium, or Heavy all permitted), plus shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Creation, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic,
  Protection, Summoning, Thought
- **Minor Spheres:** Elemental, Sun, Weather, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in singing, 1 rank in poetry
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon), musical instrument (pick one), art (songwriting), and
  diplomacy; +1 bonus on linguistics (pick one language)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.
