# Istishia (The Water Lord, God-King of the Water Elementals)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Istishia
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Water Lord, God-King of the Water Elementals
- **Portfolio:** Elemental water, water elementalists, purification through cleansing, wetness
- **Symbol:** A cresting wave or a drop of water

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **Organization:** The faith has four cooperating (if occasionally quarreling) sects: the Church of
  the Magnificent Storm ("Stormers," who revere water's cleansing power in storms), the Church of the
  Sacred Sea (who treat large bodies of water as Istishia's body and protect ships/ports), the Church
  of Watery Paths (who venerate rivers and streams as Istishia's veins), and the Church of the Eternal
  Transformation (who see the water cycle as a symbol of the soul's journey between planes). Priests
  are rotated to a new posting roughly every five years (extendable by petition to a maximum of ten),
  overseen by the fiercely-contested Elder Oversight Committee. The faith's hereditary head is the
  Delphine Regent (currently a 12-year-old aquatic elf, Aquaril Sethanilar); actual day-to-day control
  rests with a council of seven regional heads called the Primatus. A priest who has slain or defeated
  a high-ranking enemy of the church (usually a priest of Kossuth) may add the honorific "True" to the
  front of their title.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Searcher (novice)
  2. Essential Servant (full priest)
  3. Spring of the God
  4. Tidal Messenger
  5. Cephalian
  6. Full Flood (senior priest)
  7. Monsoon
  8. Typhonic Oracle (head of a temple or large shrine)
  9. Grand Oracle (senior/elder head of a temple)
  10. Stratus Primae (leader of a region's temples and shrines)
  11. Delphine Regent (hereditary leader of the entire faith)

### Dogma

- Everything is interconnected and cyclical.
- Every element has its place, but water triumphs over all in the end -- though it may be transformed
  by its environment, it retains its essential nature and ultimately transforms the environment it is
  in.
- "Earth dissolves through water, fire is extinguished even by steam, and air becomes clouds and then
  rain, completing the eternal cycle."
- Istishia is the great equalizer and leveler of the elements.
- Avoid personal combat and direct confrontation with unbelievers; the truths embodied by the Water
  Lord will eventually convince all, if allowed to go where they will.
- Work quietly along less-obvious paths to accomplish goals, following the path of least resistance,
  just as water takes the easiest course to the sea.
- "Do not try to be what you are not; rather, excel at what you are and carry this message of personal
  excellence to the world. Be flexible but not unreasonable."
- The mysteries of life are to be enjoyed and puzzled over, but some answers come only in the next
  world.
- The cycles of life are mirrored by the cycles of fate; be prepared to pay the price or reap the
  reward for the actions of your past or future.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Novices are taught to throw pottery -- both a practical income source for the church and a teaching
  tool demonstrating the interaction of the elements and the mutability of water.
- Keep waterways pure and clean; wantonly polluting rivers, streams, seas, and oceans is considered an
  insult to Istishia.
- Work toward better communication among the aquatic races, and between the aquatic races and surface
  dwellers.
- Priests accept offerings of water-hued fine fabrics, gemstones from clear to deep emerald or
  sapphire, fine inks or dyes, and books of unsolved riddles or mathematical problems; Istishia always
  sends a representative (anything from a priest to a fish to a water elemental) to accept these gifts
  from devout worshipers.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Spring Tide / Neap Tide festivals | Spring tide and neap tide (recurring) | New members are dedicated to Istishia by being boisterously tossed into the sea or a lake, or by diving into fountains or pools; aquatic and surface communities exchange visits via magical accommodations during these fêtes. |
| Waveriding | Upon a specialty priest first gaining the skill to summon a water elemental (individual rite) | An hour-long responsive reading and chant precedes the summoning; the elemental carries the celebrant on a ride across the waves, then is gifted garlands of flowers, fine wine, and crafted jewelry, pottery, or sculpture to carry back to Istishia's realm. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Cavaliers of the Seven Seas -- crusader order of natural water-breathers.
- *(Formal)* Cavaliers of the Ever-Changing Truth -- crusader order of natural air-breathers.
- *(Formal)* Order of Cryptic Transformational Purity -- the faith's mystic order, symbolized by a
  human shapechanging into a fish; members are nicknamed "fishtishians" or "the Water Lord's fishmen."

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Waterwalker
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons plus nets, harpoons, and tridents; favored weapon
  warhammer
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Medium armor, shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, animal, creation, elemental water, healing, protection, weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, elemental air, elemental earth, necromantic, numbers, time
- **Magical Items:** anathema fire
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in swim
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on languages (pick one aquatic tongue:
  sea elvish, koalinth, kuo-toan, locathah, nereid, nixie, merman, morkoth, sahuagin, sirine, triton);
  +1 bonus on craft (pottery); +1 bonus on survival (aquatic/nautical) -- navigation is a survival
  skill action; can pray for elemental water school arcane spells in place of priest spells (cast as a
  spellcaster of the same level, occupies a priest spell slot), and can always read elemental water
  arcane spells/scrolls as if under read magic
- **Other Clergy Grants:** Shamans of Istishia gain Elemental Water as an additional major sphere
  alongside their normal spheres (this does not apply to Waterwalkers, who already have it natively);
  Istishian mystics craft special candles that burn even underwater and are not extinguished by
  immersion in liquid.
