# Zandilar

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Zandilar
- **Note:** Not detailed as an independent deity in this sourcebook -- a minor-power stub only. The
  entire text of this entry reads: "Zandilar (ZAN-dih-lahr) is the only god of the Yuir that was not
  absorbed as an aspect of one of the Seldarine. See the entry for Sharess in the 'Faerunian Pantheon

  not this book or *Faiths & Avatars*.
