# Akadi (Queen of Air, the Lady of Air, Lady of the Winds, Queen of Air Elementals)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Akadi
- **Titles/Epithets:** Queen of Air, the Lady of Air, Lady of the Winds, Queen of Air Elementals
- **Portfolio:** Elemental air, air elementalists, movement, speed, flying creatures
- **Symbol:** A white cloud, often upon a blue background

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms. Since the Time of Troubles, Akadi has had no clerics remaining
  in her priesthood -- only specialty priests, with small orders of mystics and crusaders added
  afterward to round out the church's capabilities.
- **Organization:** Extremely loose and fluid. Priests are almost never settled, wandering from town
  to town and rarely staying past a single day. Few permanent temples exist. Clergy divide informally
  by philosophy into the Whisper (work behind the scenes) and the Roar (direct and visible in their
  involvement) -- not a rank distinction. "Churches" are small, shifting cliques built around a
  charismatic leader, rising and falling and splintering constantly, which has given Akadi's clergy a
  reputation for unreliability.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Fledgling (novice)
  2. Winged One (full priest, achieved through a personally designed rite of empowerment)
  3. Air of the Goddess
  4. Breeze of Light
  5. Zephyr (senior priest)
  6. Mistral
  7. Sirocco (leader of a large "church")
  8. Whirlwind (leader of a very large church, or a priest of great experience)

  A priest who has slain or soundly defeated a notable enemy of the church (typically a high-ranking
  priest of Grumbar or a powerful earth-based creature) may prefix "High" to their title.

### Dogma

- Find one's own enlightenment; truth exists only in what interests you, and once interest fails, so
  does the chance for further spiritual growth in that place or pursuit.
- Move constantly from activity to activity, place to place, growing through changing experience.
- Pay little heed to resistance to the church's teachings -- such obstacles wear down over time.
- Members and priests of the faith must never be fettered or imprisoned.
- Be as flexible as Akadi; pursue everything that interests you in turn.
- Look at problems from as many viewpoints as possible to find the most creative answer.
- View life as an art form to be created anew each day, to your benefit and Akadi's.
- The freedom to move and soar on the breeze of life is an inherent right of every living thing.
- Fly forth, spread Akadi's word, and show through your works the fresh new life she brings.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Listen to the wind, travel beneath the stars, and pursue whatever personal interest currently holds
  the priest's attention.
- Speak to others of Akadi's glories at every opportunity, without stopping to argue doctrine.
- Spelljamming is looked on favorably, since it spreads Akadi's word to distant places.
- Some priests grow obsessively involved in "life experiments" -- breeding faster hawks or hounds,
  testing how creatures adapt to fierce windstorms, devising artificial wings -- then abandon the
  project the moment it stops being fun or interesting, no matter the time already invested.
- Tithe/offering: worshipers burn precious incense that wafts to Akadi on the wind; this is the
  primary way the faithful show devotion and, some say, the only way to reliably earn her favor.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Daily dawn and dusk prayers | Every day | The faithful rise before sunrise to whisper prayers on the morning breeze, and stay up to watch the moon rise (or glide the day's dying thermals) while speaking evening prayers. |
| The Fellowship of Flight | Whenever an airwalker reaches sufficient skill to summon an air elemental | An hour of solemn prayer precedes the summoning; the answering elemental is wafted with incense, sung to, sprinkled with flower petals, and given poems praising Akadi to carry back to her realm. |
| Midsummer Festival | Midsummer (annual) | The church's only calendar-fixed festival. The faithful travel for tendays beforehand to the ruins of Blaskaltar in the Shaar, site of the first known shrine to Akadi, where the chant of the faith's heroes is recited and new names are added to the memorial roll of those who died in the past year. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Knights of the Wind's Four Quarters -- the order of Akadian crusaders; members pursue
  personal quests and errands for church elders and carry on an ongoing vendetta against the church
  of Grumbar.
- *(Informal)* Companions of the Summer Wind -- order of mystics, generally good-aligned.
- *(Informal)* Disciples of Spring's Breeze -- order of mystics, mainly neutral.
- *(Informal)* Alliance of Midwinter's Teeth -- order of mystics, evil-aligned.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Airwalker
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any one chosen melee weapon, plus all missile or thrown weapons (no
  firearms); a nonweapon proficiency penalty applies to a thrown weapon used in melee unless it is the
  sole chosen melee weapon; favored weapon heavy flail (the whirlwind)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light & medium armor, shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Elemental Air, Healing, Protection, Travelers, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, Divination, Elemental Fire, Elemental Water, Necromantic
- **Magical Items:** anathema earth-based magic items (e.g. *ring of elemental earth command*)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in handle animal (airborne mount, pick one)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on acrobatics (tightrope walking); +1
  bonus on athletics (speed) -- direction sense is a survival skill action

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2 bonus on saves vs. air- or wind-related spells and gaseous/cloud-based breath weapons (save results improved one step) |
| 1 | Never takes falling damage so long as the fall is no more than 10 feet per level (effectively feather fall at will, even while unconscious) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Wraithform (30 mana) |
| 5 | Can summon one 8-HD air elemental once per day; it serves faithfully for up to one hour or until the summoner is killed/knocked unconscious, at which point it rampages until the hour ends |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Fly (30 mana) |
| 9 | Foundation bonus: Cloudkill (50 mana) |
| 9 | Can summon one 12-HD air elemental once per tenday, under the same conditions as the 8-HD summons |
| 13 | Can summon one 16-HD air elemental once per tenday, under the same conditions |
| 15 | Can move through the Elemental Plane of Air without need for food or water |
| 16 | Gains access to 8th-level wizard spells (requires Wisdom 18+ and Intelligence 16+) |
| 18 | Gains access to 9th-level wizard spells (requires Wisdom 18+ and Intelligence 18+) |

Airwalkers may also pray for wizard spells from the elemental air school in place of priest spells
(occupying a priest spell slot of the same level), cast as a mage of equal level; no more than
three-quarters of their total available spells (rounded down) may be taken this way. Airwalkers
working together can pool their levels to summon a single elemental of greater Hit Dice than either
could alone.
