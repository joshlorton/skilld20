# Felarathael

This is a minor/stub entry. The source book gives Felarathael only a single sentence directly ("See
the entry for Corellon Larethian for details"), with the rest of his description folded into Corellon
Larethian's own entry. He has no independent clergy, symbol, church, vestments, specialty priest, or
spell list -- he is a servitor spirit of Corellon, not an independently worshiped member of the
Seldarine.

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Felarathael
- **Titles/Epithets:** None given in this source; sometimes mistakenly referred to as a demipower
  (a status the source explicitly denies).
- **Portfolio:** Not independently detailed. Felarathael is one of Corellon Larethian's two primary
  servitors -- identical twin solar spirits, alongside Lashrael -- held by some of the Fair Folk
  (particularly gold elves) to be demipowers in their own right.
- **Symbol:** None given in this source.

## Notes

Felarathael and Lashrael resemble tall, shining, androgynous elves clad in gleaming white robes, most
often seen delivering Corellon's messages or defending elves who are threatened. Where Lashrael is
given to emotional extremes -- speaking with great conviction, joy, or sorrow, and fighting
ferociously without asking or giving quarter -- Felarathael is the image of rational detachment,
treating all situations with logic and calm reason, always speaking in a slow, measured, reassuring
voice, and fighting with unhurried skill.

When either Lashrael or Felarathael strikes a victim in combat, they may inflict one of the following
effects in lieu of normal damage: the victim falls asleep (no saving throw), is randomly teleported up
to 10 miles, is polymorphed into a woodland animal, or suffers amnesia.
