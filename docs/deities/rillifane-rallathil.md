# Rillifane Rallathil (the Leaflord, the Wild One, the Great Oak, the Many-Branched, the Many-Limbed, Old Man of the Yuirwood)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Rillifane Rallathil
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Leaflord, the Wild One, the Great Oak, the Many-Branched, the Many-Limbed, Old Man of the Yuirwood
- **Portfolio:** Woodlands, nature, wild elves, druids
- **Symbol:** An oak tree; the priesthood's physical holy symbol is an acorn enclosed in amber

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including multiclassed half-elven clerics), mystics, and specialty priests (including multiclassed half-elven specialty priests) receive religion (pantheon) and literacy (Espruar) as bonus skills.
- **Organization:** Organized regionally and divided into branches by priest type. Druids (the bulk of the clergy, sometimes called skinwalkers) tend to the forests and their inhabitants, fiercely contesting any further reduction of what remains. Clerics serve as ambassadors, working outside Sy-Tel'Quessir communities to teach other races (and other elven subraces) how to live in harmony with nature. The few mystics act much as individual druids do, outside the formal druidic circles. In times of war, regional leaders unite all branches of the faith and the Sy-Tel'Quessir warriors into a single force. The clergy is green elves (52%), moon elves (26%), lythari (8%), gold elves (6%), half-elves (4%), voadkyn/wood giants (3%), and other elven races (1%); dominated by specialty priests (75%), with clerics (20%) and mystics (5%); nearly evenly split between male (51%) and female (49%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Acorn (novice)
  2. Oakheart (full priest)
  3. Felsul
  4. Silverbark
  5. Laspar
  6. Hiexel
  7. Blueleaf
  8. Phandar
  9. Duskwood
  10. Shadowtop
  11. Weirwood

  High-ranking priests hold unique individual titles; druids additionally carry titles reflecting their place within their circle's own hierarchy. Specialty priests are known as druids, or (among green elves) skinwalkers.

### Dogma

- The Great Oak draws energy from all living creatures and nourishes, sustains, and protects them from outside threats.
- Live in harmony with the natural world, allowing each living being to serve out its natural purpose.
- As the Leaflord's countless branches, his faithful serve as his mortal agents in the natural world.
- Defend the great forests from those who would ravage their riches and leave only destruction behind.
- Contest both the quick and the slow death of Rillifane's bounty; hold strong like the great oaks against those who see only their own immediate needs.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Druids tend the health of the forests and their dwellers, fiercely contesting further deforestation.
- Clerics serve as ambassadors, teaching other races and elven subraces to better dwell in harmony with nature.
- Mystics act largely as individual druids do, apart from the formal druidic circles.
- All branches unite regionally in wartime, alongside Sy-Tel'Quessir warriors.
- Act as deadly enemies of sport hunters and those who harm trees maliciously; hunt down and destroy sentient plants twisted into perversions of nature (hangman trees, obliviax, death's head trees, black willows, serpent vines, and evil treants including dark trees), and hold particular hatred for the priests of Malar.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Budding | Vernal equinox | A joyful celebration of new life through dance and song, preceded by an extended fast; a ritual hunt of an ancient, noble hart provides the venison that breaks the fast, honoring Rillifane's bounty and the natural cycle of life. |
| The Transformation | Autumnal equinox | Marks the arrival of autumn's vibrant colors; elves seeking spiritual rebirth or a major change in their lives gather to celebrate the Leaflord's promise that the trees will bloom again. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Order of the Oakstaff -- the collective name for loose fellowships of elven rangers affiliated with individual druid circles; rangers are not part of the formal clergy hierarchy but serve as the faith's militant arm.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Druid (skinwalker, among green elves)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, dagger, dart, long bow, scimitar, short bow, sickle, sling, spear, staff; favored weapon quarterstaff
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (leather, hide, or tree bark armor); wooden shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Elemental, Healing, Plant, Sun, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Thought, Time
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (nature)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on herbalism
