# Deep Duerra (Queen of the Invisible Art, Axe Princess of Conquest, Daul of Laduguer)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Deep Duerra
- **Titles/Epithets:** Queen of the Invisible Art, Axe Princess of Conquest, Daul of Laduguer
- **Portfolio:** Psionics (the Invisible Art), conquest, expansion, duergar warriors, duergar
  psionicists
- **Symbol:** A shattered skull (the exact race varies, but usually illithid, drow, or dwarven); the
  physical holy symbol is a two-inch silver orb carved to resemble a cracked illithid skull, worn on a
  steel chain through the skull's ear holes

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, crusaders, and specialty priests (including multiclassed combinations of
  all three) of Deep Duerra receive the standard dwarven clergy grants (literacy (Dethek runes),
  modified turn undead) -- see `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** A gray dwarf (duergar) faith, little known outside the Underdark -- even shield
  and gold dwarves rarely recognize her name, and illithids in particular loathe the gray dwarves who
  dare turn the Invisible Art against them. Temples are hewn in symmetric, sound-muffling patterns from
  solid rock, doubling as armories, barracks, and command centers, with an empty throne on a central
  dais reserved for an avatar's use. The clergy is almost entirely gray dwarves (99%), split among
  specialty priests (25%), psionicists (22%), crusaders (18%), clerics (11%), fighter/specialty priests
  (9%), fighter/clerics (8%), and cleric/thieves (7%) -- roughly three-quarters of every group also
  multiclass as psionicists. The priesthood remains predominantly female (97%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Close-minded (novice)
  2. Mindaxe (full priest)
  3. Psionic Blaster
  4. Mind Thruster
  5. Ego Whipper
  6. Id Insinuator
  7. Psychic Crusher
  8. Thought Conqueror
  9. Axe Princess/Prince of the Invisible Art (a High Old One, 7th level or higher)

### Dogma

- The children of Laduguer shall conquer the earth and stone from which they sprang, and the voids in
  which they dwell.
- The seizing of new lands, new wealth, and new servitors is the manifest destiny of those who mine
  the Night Below.
- Magic is weak, unreliable, and unsubtle compared to the powers of the mind, unless bequeathed and
  steadied by the will of the gods.
- By means of the Invisible Art, the duergar shall destroy or enslave all who rely on petty magics to
  survive.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve as capable leaders using cunning strategy to defeat enemies in an endless quest for power.
- Command elite strike forces of duergar warriors and junior Ladugueran and Duerran priests, tasked
  with scouting and seizing new tunnels (as distinct from the Ladugueran clergy, who mainly defend
  existing duergar cities, homelands, and mines).
- Monitor the thoughts of slaves and gray dwarven masters alike, alert for disloyalty, and instruct
  young duergar in developing their natural psionic talents and the arts of war and Underdark
  survival.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Rallying | Midwinter eve | Followers assemble to commemorate the previous year's conquests and proclaim intentions for the year ahead, chanting grim war-songs and pounding hammers against stone; the ceremony culminates in the display of a newly seized enemy skull from a race whose territory is targeted for the coming year. |
| The Melding | 5th of Mirtul | Skilled duergar psionicists gather to join minds in a ritual contact with Duerra herself, briefly forming a common mind rivaling the great Elder Brains of the illithids -- many advances in duergar technology and psionics trace back to insights gained in past Meldings. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* The Mindstalkers of the Invisible Art -- a secretive network of Duerran
  psionicist/priest cells seeking to unite the scattered duergar realms of the Northdark under one
  collective consciousness; they direct much regional gray dwarven trade and have begun culling
  surface-dwellers with wild psionic talent from the slave markets of Skullport to breed into the
  duergar race.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Norothor (singular norothar)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapon battle axe
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Medium or Heavy (chain mail or dwarven plate mail)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Divination, Elemental Earth, Guardian, Healing, Law, Thought, War
- **Minor Spheres:** Necromantic, Numbers, Protection, Summoning, Sun (reversed only), Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations; additionally may use devices normally restricted to psionicists
  and warriors
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in blind-fighting
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on literacy (Dethek runes); +1 bonus on
  handle animal (steeder riding)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Morale *or* Thought Capture (10 mana each), choose one per use |
| 3 | Detects psionics at will (as a 1st-level wizard spell; no mana cost) while concentrating; no other action but slow movement is possible while using it |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Augment Psionics *or* Spiritual Hammer, which appears as a spectral axe (20 mana each), choose one per use |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Deflect Psionics (30 mana) *or* Rigid Thinking (40 mana), choose one per use |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Augment Psionics (20 mana) *or* Cure Critical Wounds (50 mana), choose one per use |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Mind Blank (80 mana), once per day |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Lower Resistance (160 mana; 8th-level spell, twice per day) |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Mindshatter (80 mana), once per day |

## Duerran Spells (deity-specific)

| Name | Est. Mana | Foundation | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steal Psionic Strength | 10 | Thought | Steals psionic strength points from a psionic target (who may save to resist) and transfers them to the caster, to be spent before dissipating. |
| Augment Psionics | 20 | Thought | Grants a touched psionic creature a temporary pool of bonus psionic strength points, spent before their normal reserve. |
| Deflect Psionics | 40 | Thought | Forces psionic attacks directed at the caster to instead strike a random nearby creature (favoring whoever has the most psionic strength points) unless the attacker succeeds at a save. |

**Judgment call:** Deep Duerra's CLERGY field lists "psionicists" alongside clerics, crusaders, and
specialty priests. Psionicist is not one of the classes named in the standard pooling rule, so it was
folded into Spellcasters on the reasoning that Duerran psionicist-priests cast priest spells
side-by-side with their psionic powers rather than existing as a separate non-caster tradition; flagged
for review. The norothor's psionics-specific bonus proficiencies (contact, harness subconscious,
rejuvenation, and mental armor, all from *Skills & Powers*) were dropped as psionics-subsystem crunch
with no equivalent in this project.
