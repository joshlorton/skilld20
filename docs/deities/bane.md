# Bane (The Black Lord, Lord Bane, the Lord of Darkness, the Black Hand, The Dark One)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Bane
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Black Lord, Lord Bane, the Lord of Darkness, the Black Hand, The Dark One
- **Portfolio:** Strife, hatred, tyranny
- **Symbol:** An upright black hand, palm out and fingers together, usually on a red field

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **Organization:** Rigidly hierarchical and rule-bound; Banites are cruel folk who relish exercising
  power over others within a strict chain of command. The church was long split between the Orthodox
  (mostly clerical) and Transformed (more heavily wizardly) factions, a schism worsened by the
  ambitions of Fzoul Chembryl of Zhentil Keep. Novices are addressed as "slave" until named by Bane
  from a speaking altar or in a dream vision, at which point they become full priests. Banite clergy
  bow, kneel, or kiss the boots of superiors depending on relative rank; outside the faith's presence
  they address each other as "Brother/Sister Faithful" (or "Dread Brother/Sister" for a much higher
  ranking superior).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Slave (novice, prior to being personally named by Bane)
  2. Watchful Brother / Watchful Sister (full priest)
  3. Deadly Adept
  4. Trusted Servant
  5. Willing Whip
  6. Hooded Menace
  7. Black Fang
  8. Striking Hand
  9. Vigilant Talon
  10. Masked Death
  11. Dark Doom
  12. Higher Doom
  13. Deeper Mystery (a general title held by all clergy of 12th level and higher)

  Individual, self-bestowed titles used among those of Deeper Mystery rank (requiring confirmation by
  a higher-ranking priest before formal recognition) include Vigilator, Lord/Lady of Mysteries,
  Lord/Lady of the Hand, Imperceptor, Dark Imperceptor, Grand Bloodletter, High Inquisitor, and (the
  one non-self-bestowed title) High Imperceptor, the nominal supreme living servant of Bane.

### Dogma

- Those who cross the Dark One meet their dooms earlier and more harshly than those who merely
  worship other gods and still eventually fall before Bane's church.
- Submit to the word of Bane as uttered by any Banite clergy member who outranks you.
- Spread the Dark Fear of Bane.
- Serve no one but Bane; fear him always, and make others fear him even more than you do.
- The Black Hand always strikes down those who stand against it, in the end.
- Defy Bane and die -- or in death, find loyalty, for he will compel it.
- Submit to his will, since true power can only be gained through service to him.
- It is the doom of those unguided by Bane to let power spill through their hands.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Rise to power in every realm, guild, village, town, court, war band, fellowship, and society, and if
  necessary lead a band, city-state, or kingdom to war to conquer its neighbors, bringing ever more
  territory under Bane's sway.
- Spread hatred, strife, and destruction -- but under clear duress and control, not unbridled chaos;
  domination is preferred to debauchery, and carefully limited discord to unrestrained anarchy.
- Cruelty, torture, and mayhem are tacitly encouraged, though those caught at it must pay the price
  unless they have spread enough fear that none dare speak or act against them.
- Obey superior Banite clergy at all times and in all things, while working subtly and patiently to
  bring the faith's members into positions of power everywhere.
- Personal prayers to Bane are uttered before going into battle and before eating or drinking anything,
  thanking him for allowing the worshiper to live to taste the fare.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

No specific holy days named in the source. No rituals of Bane corresponded to calendar dates, seasons,
or lunar progressions -- rituals were held whenever a congregation's leading clergy member declared
they would be, under a wide variety of names. Services were held in places of darkness (often simply
outdoors at night, in dark chambers, caverns, or ruins) around the Black Altar -- a plain black-draped
or obsidian stone block, or any surface over which a floating black stone Hand of Bane hovered (or, in
its absence, an empty black throne facing the altar) -- and involved rolling drums, chanting, singing,
and the sacrifice of intelligent life. Sacrifices had to be humiliated, tortured, and made to show fear
before dying to be acceptable to Bane.

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Knights of the Black Gauntlet -- a military order of crusaders and helmed horrors based in
  Mintar on the Lake of Steam, ruled by Teldorn Darkhope, Lord Knight Imperceptor of the Dark Lord.
  Founded when a ghostly black hand delivered Teldorn and a company of black knights into the ruling
  satrap's throne room; recruits from across the South are transformed into incorruptible, disciplined
  champions of evil through a rite involving dark promise spells and other magics.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Dreadmaster (a genderless title)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons except missile weapons, plus javelins; favored weapon
  morningstar
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, medium, & heavy armor, shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Divination, Elemental, Guardian, Healing, Law, Necromantic,
  Summoning, War
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Numbers, Sun (darkness-creating or detrimental effects only)
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in etiquette
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on linguistics (pick two: beholder,
  bugbear, gargoyle, common giant, gnoll, goblin, High Shou, kobold, kuo-toan, Midani, minotaur,
  Mulhorandi, ogre, orc, sahuagin, Undercommon, Untheric, urd, yuan-ti); +1 bonus on intimidation
