# Gorm Gulthyn (Fire Eyes, the Golden Guardian, the Sentinel, Lord of the Bronze Mask, the Eternally Vigilant)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Gorm Gulthyn
- **Titles/Epithets:** Fire Eyes, the Golden Guardian, the Sentinel, Lord of the Bronze Mask, the
  Eternally Vigilant
- **Portfolio:** Guardian and protector of all dwarves, dwarven guardians, defense, watchfulness,
  vigilance, duty
- **Symbol:** A shining bronze or brass mask with two eyeholes of flame; the physical holy symbol is a
  miniature bronze shield worn as a medallion on a burnished steel chain

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics), crusaders, and specialty priests of Gorm
  receive the standard dwarven clergy grants (literacy (Dethek runes), modified turn undead) -- see
  `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** Well regarded across dwarven society for unswerving dedication to defense, though
  seen as stern and humorless by many; other lawful-minded races (followers of Arvoreen, Gaerdal
  Ironhand, or Helm) hold Gorm in high regard, while more chaotic folk write him off as the archetypal
  dour dwarf. Temples are plain, unadorned stone caverns whose altar is a stone bench (or an old,
  unoccupied tomb casket) set before a massive locked door representing a place a dwarf might have to
  guard; chapels typically adjoin an armory, training hall, and barracks near the entrances to dwarven
  halls. The clergy runs evenly between shield dwarves (48%) and gold dwarves (52%), with no known gray
  or jungle dwarf members, and is dominated by specialty priests (41%), split roughly evenly among
  clerics (22%), crusaders (20%), and fighter/clerics (17%); the priesthood is 95% male.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Watchful Guard (novice)
  2. Vigilant Host (full priest, collective term)
  3. Lockout of the First Rank
  4. Scout of the Second Rank
  5. Sentry of the Third Rank
  6. Sentinel of the Fourth Rank
  7. Defender of the Fifth Rank
  8. Guardian of the Sixth Rank
  9. Lord/Lady Protector (a High Old One, 7th level or higher)

### Dogma

- Never waver in your duty to Gorm's sacred charges. Defend, protect, and keep safe the children of
  the Morndinsamman from the hostile forces of the outside world.
- Be always vigilant and ever alert, so that you are never surprised.
- If need be, be prepared to pay the greatest price so that the clan and the community survive, and
  your name will be honored for generations.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Guard clanhold entries, the Gates on the borders of the Deep Realm, and all temples of Gorm.
- Serve as protectors and bodyguards for all dwarves, especially the young and their parents.
- Instruct dwarven warriors in alertness, blind-fighting, and weapons-skill, tutoring dwarves in every
  proficiency useful to a guardian.
- Protect the dwarves assigned to a priest's care above all else; higher-ranking priests may choose
  whom they protect, but sacrificing one's own life for the charge is "Gorm's greatest price," known
  to every priest of Gorm.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Any festival of the Calendar of Harptos | Every general Realms festival | All are sacred to Gorm's priesthood. Guardians gather for the salute, a ritual of rhythmically grounding weapons together with a responsively chanted prayer; offerings are weapons used (even broken) in the service of guardianship, anointed with the offerer's own tears, sweat, and blood. At the height of the ritual, the temple's inner door sometimes opens by the god's power to release instructive visions, or small gifts (scrolls, potions, weapons, armor, maps) -- any recipient wearing or using such a gift gains a morale bonus for a time. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Numerous knightly orders founded in Gorm's name across the centuries, including the
  Twin-Bladed Axes of Fire, the Silent Sentinels, the Guardian-Knights of Gorm, the Vigilant
  Halberdiers, the Company of the Scarlet Moon, the Fellowship of the Stern Gauntlet, and the Order of
  the Smoking Shield.
- *(Formal)* The Sacred Shields of Berronar's Blessed -- one of the oldest and most revered Gormite
  orders, found in nearly every clanhold, charged with guarding nurseries of dwarven children and
  their parents and with recovering dwarven youths kidnapped for slavery.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Barakor
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapon battle axe
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Law, Protection, Sun, War, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Charm, Creation, Elemental Earth, Necromantic, Summoning
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (pick one: armorer, weaponsmithing)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on literacy (Dethek runes); +1 bonus on
  alertness; +1 bonus on blind-fighting
- **Other Clergy Grants:** Clerics of Gorm (unlike most dwarven clerics) may use any weapon, not just
  bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons.
