# Sharindlar (Lady of Life, Lady of Mercy, the Merciful, the Bountiful, the Shining Dancer)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Sharindlar
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lady of Life, Lady of Mercy, the Merciful, the Bountiful, the Shining Dancer
- **Portfolio:** Healing, mercy, romantic love, fertility, dancing, courtship, the moon
- **Symbol:** A flame rising from a steel needle

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics and specialty priests of Sharindlar receive the standard dwarven clergy
  grants (literacy (Dethek runes), modified turn undead) -- see `dwarven-priests-general.md`.
- **Organization:** Sharindlar's clergy is nearly evenly divided between specialty priests (58%) and
  clerics (42%), drawn from gold dwarves (49%), shield dwarves (48%), and small numbers of jungle and
  gray dwarves; the priesthood remains 99% female. Temples are pillar-free great halls doubling as
  ballrooms, often partly open to the sky, with fountains, pools, and formal gardens, small guest
  chambers for the many visitors, and a library of runestones recording genealogies, courting rites,
  formal dances, astronomy, and medicinal/herbal lore.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Chaste (novice)
  2. Dancing Tresses
  3. Golden Allure
  4. Healing Touch
  5. Merciful Smile
  6. Loving Heart
  7. Fruitful Mother/Father (full priests collectively: Merciful Maidens/Youths)
  8. Sons/Daughters (Dauls) of Sharindlar (High Old Ones, who also hold individual titles)

  Specialty priests are known as thalornor (singular thalornar), a dwarvish word loosely translated as
  "those who are merciful."

### Dogma

- Be merciful in speech and deed. Bring relief and healing where needful.
- Temper anger and hostility with constructive and charitable endeavor.
- The children of Moradin must live in safety and propagate.
- Maintain and encourage the traditional rites of courting and marriage.
- Celebrate the endless, joyous dance of life by living it to the fullest.
- Sharindlar restores the fertile seed of dwarven life, while Berronar protects the fruit.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Dispense healing and mercy to dwarves and others in need, from hospices in dwarven strongholds and by
  traveling to isolated holds scattered through surface and subterranean wilderness.
- Work, with the assistance of Berronar's clergy, to reverse declining dwarven birthrates -- especially
  among the shield dwarves of the North.
- Maintain and teach dwarven courting rites (traditional dances, ritual forms of address) and strive to
  bring young dwarves together, particularly across clan lines.
- In some surface-dwelling cultures (High Shanatar, Besilmer), Sharindlar's oversight of fertility
  extends to agriculture and animal husbandry, developing hardier crops and livestock breeds.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Waxing Moon Gathering | The night the moon begins to wax (after the new moon), each month | Clergy gather to pray to the Lady of Life. |
| Greengrass Rite | Greengrass | As the Waxing Moon Gathering; the more secret rituals (fertility-focused) take place in hidden caverns beside a pool of water, with dancing, prayer, and a sacrifice of gold heated and poured, mixed with the celebrants' own blood, into the water. |
| Midsummer Rite | Midsummer Night | As the Greengrass Rite. |
| Full Moon Rite | Every full moon | As the Greengrass Rite; healing rituals performed by two or more priests over the sick or injured involve sprinkling them with water from the Lake of Gold while whispering the goddess's secret names, with a chance to maximize healing magic, double natural rest recovery, or halt disease/poison/parasites for several days. |

*Even Sharindlar's name, whispered or thought silently by the faithful, calms upset or pain-wracked
dwarves of any faith, letting them sleep.*

### Affiliated Orders

- No formal martial orders. *(Informal)* Maidens of Midwifery -- roughly one in five of Sharindlar's
  other priests, who serve small dwarven communities independently as midwives, often also acting as
  physician, matchmaker, and brewer of aphrodisiacs and fertility elixirs.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Thalornor (singular thalornar)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Bludgeoning weapons and knives; favored weapon whip (the whip itself falls
  outside the thalornor weapon list per the source text -- it's carried over from Sharindlar's avatar,
  who fights with one -- but is kept here as the stated favored weapon)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Charm, Creation, Healing, Necromantic, Plant, Sun, Time
- **Minor Spheres:** Elemental, Guardian, Protection
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in herbalism
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on performance (dancing); +1 bonus on
  etiquette; +1 bonus on healing
