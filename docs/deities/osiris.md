# Osiris (Lord of Nature, Judge of the Dead, Reaper of the Harvest, the White Crown)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Osiris
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of Nature, Judge of the Dead, Reaper of the Harvest, the White Crown
- **Portfolio:** Vegetation, death, the dead, justice, harvest
- **Symbol:** White crown of Mulhorand with a crossed ceremonial crook and flail beneath it

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (paladins, rangers)
- **All Clergy:** All of Osiris's clergy, including paladins and rangers, know Mulhorandi as a native
  tongue.
- **Organization:** Osiris's active clergy is small, though every citizen of Mulhorand follows his
  teachings in daily life -- most venerate him simply by living according to maat, thanking him at
  harvest time, and invoking his name at a loved one's death. All of Osiris's clergy must be humans of
  Mulhorandi extraction; the priesthood is largely hereditary, and nearly all paladins and specialty
  priests belong to the House of Osriant, human descendants of Osiris's ancient incarnations. Clerics
  make up 30% of the clergy, rangers 12%, paladins 8%, and specialty priests (divine arbitrators) the
  remaining 50%; clerics and specialty priests additionally receive religion (pantheon), literacy
  (Mulhorandi), and linguistics (Common) as bonus proficiencies. The public addresses all of Osiris's
  clergy as "Lord Magistrate" (or "Lord High Magistrate" for 9th level and above); paladins are
  collectively known as the Pinnacles of Maat, and rangers as the Eyes of Truthful Nature. Civil
  disputes are settled by tribunals of Osiris's priests for a fee, and any judge who suspects wrongdoing
  may convene an independent inquiry with up to two fellow judges, reporting findings to the vizier
  (Horus-Re's high priest) and, recently, to Pharaoh Horustep III directly. The clergy also guards the
  Land of the Dead's burial crypts, maintains their wards, presides over every burial, and works with
  the clergy of Nephthys on mummification and the reconsecration of desecrated tombs.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Seeker of Truth
  2. Guardian of the Dead
  3. Dark Harvester
  4. Magistrate
  5. High Seeker
  6. Deathwatcher
  7. High Harvester
  8. High Magistrate
  9. Deathlord

### Dogma

- Honor the dead and the places where they rest; perform all burial rituals and aid in the mummification
  of the noble or wealthy dead.
- Never take part in or condone the looting of a grave or the removal of treasure from a fallen body --
  such sacrilege means instant loss of all priestly powers, and dying while in that disgrace makes you an
  accidental mummy.
- Use your powers to assist in the harvest, aiding common folk and slaves so enough food is stored for
  the year ahead.
- Live by the code of maat: goodness, honor, and order are the natural state; evil, treachery, and chaos
  are unnatural. Judge without partiality -- treat the known as the unknown, the near as the distant, and
  never withhold judgment out of anger without cause.
- When honor is lacking in the application of Mulhorand's laws, Osiris's fair judgment is the law.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Travel from town to town as itinerant justices and magistrates, hearing cases and rendering judgment;
  the accused bears the burden of proving innocence, not the accuser.
- Convene tribunals to settle civil disputes over property and marriage, for a fee.
- Guard the Land of the Dead: maintain burial crypts, renew fading wards, and watch for tomb robbers,
  reporting any desecration to the church of Nephthys for vengeance.
- Preside over every burial service and perform the last rites that usher the deceased into the
  afterlife; cooperate with Nephthys's clergy on tomb construction and mummification.
- Convene independent inquiries into suspected political corruption when warranted.

| The Passing to Eternal Contentment and Justice | At each burial | The rite of enshroudment and burial, performed in High, Middle, or Low form for the royal family, nobles, or commoners respectively. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) The Order of the Risen Scepter -- paladins and lawful good rangers slain in honorable combat
  by clergy of Set who violated the principles of maat to kill them; such fallen are raised back
  stronger than before, with maximum hit points per die, a +1 bonus to all saving throws, and no loss of
  Constitution from the raising.
- (Formal) The Brotherhood of Those Who Smile in the Face of Death (the Deathgrins) -- an order of
  paladins descended from the survivors who drove Sebek's werecrocodile cult from Mulhorand and destroyed
  the city of Sekras; serve as Osiris's sword-arm against evil throughout the Old Empires, and since the
  Godswar have spent great effort (and many lives) trying to destroy the cult of Tiamat in Unther.
- (Informal) The Guardians of Skuld -- drawn jointly from the priesthoods of Anhur, Osiris, and Isis to
  guard the capital city of Skuld from invaders and treachery; formalized since the Time of Troubles and
  currently led from the temple of Anhur.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Divine Arbitrator
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Flail, hammer, mace, staff, staff sling, sling; favored weapon flail
  ("Just Reward" per real-world 3.5e sourcing, matching this book's own ceremonial flail +3 wielded by
  Osiris's avatar)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Astral, Combat, Creation, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic, Plant,
  Protection, Sun, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Divination, Summoning, Time
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in linguistics (Untheric), 1 rank in herbalism
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on literacy (Mulhorandi); +1 bonus on
  linguistics (Common); +1 bonus on blind-fighting
- **Other Clergy Grants:** Paladins of Osiris, while within the Mulhorandi pantheon's sphere of
  influence, may cast the spell test of maat at will in place of their normal detect evil ability (test
  of maat is documented under Horus-Re, outside this file's scope).

Only humans of Mulhorandi extraction can become divine arbitrators.
