# Sseth (The Great Snake, the World Serpent, the Sibilant Death)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Sseth
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Great Snake, the World Serpent, the Sibilant Death (also known regionally as
  Squamata the Black in Samarach and Thindol, and Amphisbaena in the Tashalar and Lapaliiya)
- **Portfolio:** Intelligent snakes, yuan-ti, ophidians, renegade spirit nagas, somnolence, poison
- **Symbol:** A flying snake with fangs bared

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders)
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, crusaders, shamans, and specialty priests of Sseth receive religion
  (Faerûnian) or religion (Chultan) as a bonus proficiency, whichever matches their region of origin.
- **Organization:** Clergy (the Ssethssar) is dominated by specialty priests known as serphidians,
  with clerics about 20% and crusaders 10%; a few remote human beast-cults venerating Varae, along with
  primitive ophidian tribes, are instead led by shamans. Temples are dark stone edifices overgrown by
  jungle or subterranean caverns, lined with columns depicting snakes devouring humans, built without
  stairs (favoring ramps and poles instead), and centered on a chapel dominated by a massive pit of
  writhing poisonous serpents. Wizards in the church's service are known as Heways. Despite their
  chaotic nature, the Ssethssar keep a strict rank hierarchy.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Adder
  2. Pit Viper
  3. Asp
  4. Krait
  5. Cobra
  6. Anaconda
  7. Rattler
  8. Boalisk
  9. Python

  Higher-ranking priests are collectively known as Elder Serpents, each bearing a unique personal title
  revealed to them by Sseth himself.

### Dogma

- Serpents are descendants of the original World Serpent and sacred manifestations of the Great Snake;
  true wisdom and dominion over the Realms come from embracing Sseth and entwining oneself in his coils.
- Life is transformation -- an evolution through a series of forms toward embrace of the
  all-encompassing serpent of the world.
- Reclaim your birthright, molding your image in the World Serpent's shadow.
- Spend long periods in torporific contemplation to acquire wisdom and patience; only after extensive
  intelligence-gathering and reflection should the children of Sseth act -- but when they do, they must
  act decisively and without mercy against the Prey.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Stalk and hunt prey, whether jungle wildlife or entire human realms.
- Court government officials into an ever-widening sphere of corruption.
- Breed new varieties of serpents adaptable to hostile climes, in preparation for the eventual conquest
  of the Realms.
- Patiently accumulate power and knowledge in service of long-term plans.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Sagacious Slumber | A month-long hibernation overlapping Midwinter and much of Alturiak | Preceded by an obscene revel consuming huge amounts of (rumored still-living) food, sometimes including rebellious slaves. |
| The Shedding | Immediately following the Sagacious Slumber; the most sacred Ssethssar holy day | Priests shed their snakeskin (or ritually shed clerical raiment, if full-blooded human) during an evil rite involving the sacrifice of at least one sentient being, typically followed by a burst of energetic activity to extend the church's power. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Informal) The Viper's Fangs -- a secretive order of assassins, spies, and thugs (yuan-ti purebloods
  and humans) who have infiltrated noble houses, trading costers, mercenary companies, government
  offices, and adventuring bands throughout the Realms, gathering information and eliminating threats
  to the Ssethssar's plots.
- (Informal) The Coiled Cabal -- a highly secretive order of yuan-ti wizards (abominations, half-breeds,
  and purebloods) based in the Forest of Wyrms, building its strength in secret for the day the yuan-ti
  nation reclaims its place in the Realms.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Serphidian
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; no favored weapon specified in the source, and none found in
  real-world published material
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Medium (scale mail); shield permitted
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Charm, Combat, Divination, Healing (reversed forms favored), Plant
- **Minor Spheres:** Chaos, Elemental (Air, Earth, and Water only), Guardian, Protection, Sun,
  Summoning, Thought
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (Age of Serpentes history), 1 rank in astrology, 1 rank in
  linguistics (Yuan-ti)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on blind-fighting; +1 bonus on disguise

Serphidians may be human, ophidian, spirit naga, or yuan-ti (abomination, half-breed, or pureblood, but
never histachii) -- or any other race with at least partial reptilian ancestry. All gain slitted,
snake-like pupils upon entering the priesthood (concealable by magic, but revealed by true seeing).
