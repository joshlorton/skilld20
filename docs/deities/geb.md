# Geb (Lord Earth, King of the Riches Under the Earth, Father Under the Skies and Sands)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Geb
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord Earth, King of the Riches Under the Earth, Father Under the Skies and Sands
- **Portfolio:** The earth, miners, mines, mineral resources
- **Symbol:** A mountain

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics and specialty priests of Geb receive religion (Mulhorandi), literacy
  (Mulhorandi), and linguistics (Common) as bonus skills, and all know Mulhorandi as their native
  tongue.
- **Organization:** Largely hereditary; almost all clerics and specialty priests belong to the House of
  Gebthant, human descendants of Geb's divine incarnations. A few dwarves and gnomes worship Geb, and
  since the Time of Troubles a handful of gold dwarves have been admitted to the clergy. Only about 15%
  of the clergy are specialty priests (earthchildren), as Geb has been slow to convert his priesthood.
  Temples are always built underground in large natural caverns, expanded and decorated to showcase
  cavern formations (stalactites, stalagmites, columns, flowstone) and underground pools and streams.
  Priests are addressed publicly as "Lord Priest" or "Lord High Priest" (9th level+), though priests of
  rival, more prestigious faiths often drop the honorific. Internal titles vary by temple -- Miner,
  Majer, Deepdigger, Earthclaw, Earthheart are all in use. Geb's worship peaked during the First Empire
  but his priesthood never engaged in politics and has since been marginalized; today the faith is
  treated with condescending respect by the rest of the Mulhorandi pantheon and its own worshipers
  alike.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** No single fixed hierarchy is given -- titles vary by temple (see
  Organization above).

### Dogma

- Know the earth: explore its beauties and reveal its hidden secrets, but to highlight their mysteries,
  not merely to exploit their monetary value.
- Protect those who work the earth with respect, and punish those who ravish it of treasure without
  regard.
- Become one with the earth to know Geb, who abides within it.
- Seek out new veins of ore and gems, fund and construct new mines that reveal Geb's creations, and
  assist miners in safely recovering minerals and gems from the depths.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Work side by side with miners digging tunnels, with prospectors panning for gold, and with
  blacksmiths forging weapons and armor.
- At least once per year, search out and locate a new vein of ore or precious stones, or lose priestly
  abilities until doing so.
- Senior priests travel to the cities to seek funding from the clergy of Nephthys for new mines and
  quarries.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Hidden Gift / the Bountiful Joy | Every evening and the following morning | Twin daily rites: a priest buries one precious stone or mineral in the dirt while reciting evening prayers, then excavates it the following morning and offers it up to Geb with morning prayers. |
| The Unwrapping | 1st of Mirtul (the Time of Melting) | Celebrates mountain streams filling with alpine snowmelt, often exposing new caves and veins to mine. |
| Day of Drawing Down | Last day of Nightal | A solemn ceremony marking those who died in the earth and mountains during the year; the entrance of at least one abandoned mine shaft is collapsed to commemorate the occasion. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Fellowship of the Mithral Mountain -- most adventuring priests of Geb belong; united in
  the search for a near-mythical mountain laden with veins of mithral.
- *(Informal)* Brotherhood of the Pick -- a society including most of Mulhorand's miners, serving as a
  union of sorts for negotiating with the nobility.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Earthchild
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons, footman's pick; favored weapon
  the was-scepter (light mace) -- real-world Egyptian iconography shows Geb carrying a was-scepter, a
  shaft with a distinct animal-head striking end and a forked, cloven-hoofed base, which reads as a
  stylized mace far more than a plain quarterstaff; supersedes the earlier D&D 3.5e citation
  (quarterstaff, "Stonemantle") with a shape closer to the real object, and still fits cleanly within
  this book's own bludgeoning-weapons list
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Medium and Heavy (any predominantly metal armor; leather-type armor
  excluded)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Elemental (earth), Guardian, Healing, Protection, Summoning, Time,
  Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, Creation, Elemental (air, fire, water)
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (mining), 1 rank in survival (mountaineering)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on appraisal or craft (gem cutting), pick
  one; +1 bonus on endurance
- **Other Clergy Grants:** Dwarves may become earthchildren (though dwarven culture frowns on the
  choice); gnomes may be admitted in the future, though none have been thus far due to strong gnome
  cultural peer pressure against it.
