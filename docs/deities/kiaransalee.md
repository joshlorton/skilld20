# Kiaransalee (Lady of the Dead, the Revenancer, the Vengeful Banshee)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Kiaransalee
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lady of the Dead, the Revenancer, the Vengeful Banshee
- **Portfolio:** Undead, vengeance
- **Symbol:** A female drow hand wearing silver rings

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All crusaders and specialty priests of Kiaransalee receive religion (drow), religion
  (elven), and reading/writing (drowic) as bonus proficiencies -- see `drow-priests-general.md` (the
  source notably does not extend this grant to Kiaransalee's necromancers, despite them also being
  clergy). Must be female dark elves, living or undead.
- **Organization:** Collectively known as the Crones of Thanatos. Novices are called the Commanded;
  all other clergy are called Nighthags. Titles vary widely by temple; those used at the Acropolis of
  Thanatos include Bones of the Dead, Flesh of the Zombie, Terror Touch of the Ghoul, Chill Touch of
  the Shadow, Raking Claws of the Wight, Life Leech of the Wraith, Rot of the Mummy, and Spirit
  Harvest of the Spectre, with unique individual titles for high-ranking priests. Specialty priests
  are called yathrinshee and make up 80% of the clergy; necromancers make up 12% and crusaders 8%.
  Many of Kiaransalee's faithful are eventually transformed into undead servitors -- by their own
  hand, by another priest's hand, or, rarely, by the goddess's own hand -- with the most favored
  becoming banshees, liches, vampires, or (most favored of all) kiaranshee, banshees who retain their
  spellcasting power.
- Worship happens in secrecy, hidden within cities dedicated to Lolth: simple shrines are black
  marble sarcophagi carved with the dead rising to take revenge on the living, and a priest venerates
  the Lady of the Dead by lying within her personal sarcophagus, holy symbol clasped in both hands
  across her breast.

### Dogma

- Death comes to all, and cruel vengeance will be exacted on those who waste their lives on the petty
  concerns of this existence.
- True power comes only through the unquestioning servitude of the once-dead, mastery over death, and
  the eventual, earned stature of one of the ever-living in death.
- Hunt, slay, and animate those who scorn the Revenancer's power.
- Answer any slight a thousandfold, so that all may know the coming power of Kiaransalee.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Priests are rare and secretive, usually found in small drow communities or special enclaves.
- Act as agents of vengeance, plotting revenge on those who have slain, harmed, or insulted the
  priesthood in any way.
- Regularly go out on missions to kill others for corpses to animate, or to steal the corpses of the
  recently buried.
- Take a prominent role in persecuting the slaves of the drow.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Minor Devotions | Monthly | Each priest performs a handful of small personal devotions to Kiaransalee; no fixed ceremony is prescribed. |
| The Graverending | Midwinter Eve (annual) | The Crones of Thanatos' single great holy day. Each priest individually animates as many undead as she can. These Vengeance Hunters are consumed with thoughts of revenge against their killers and unerringly seek them out for the next 24 hours; if destroyed they do not rise again, and any that survive the day return to their graves. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Legion of Vengeful Banshees (the Banshee Knights) -- an order of Kiaransaleen crusaders
  based in the Acropolis of Thanatos, dedicated to hunting and destroying Tenebrous's undead tanar'ri
  servants (visages) on the surface and in the Underdark; fanatically dedicated to the destruction of
  every visage, regardless of collateral cost.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Yathrinshee
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any, plus mandatory proficiency in dagger; favored weapon dagger (Cold
  Heart, per later published sources -- this book does not state a favored weapon for Kiaransalee's
  priests)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** None allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Combat, Divination, Elemental (Earth), Guardian, Healing (reversed
  only), Necromantic, Sun (reversed only)
- **Minor Spheres:** Astral, Chaos, Elemental (Fire), Protection, Summoning
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in spellcraft, 1 rank in singing (in addition to the mandatory dagger
  weapon proficiency above)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on lore (the dead/undead)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Immune to the special attack forms of undead creatures (level drain, energy drain, ability drain, magic jar, aging, and similar), when initiated by an undead being; always protected as though by negative plane protection (does not protect against physical damage from an undead creature) |
| 1 | Can cast wizard spells from the necromancy school, using the limited specialty-priest wizard-spellcasting rules |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Animate Dead (30 mana), once per day, animating one corpse per two levels of experience |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Chill Touch (1st-level wizard) *or* Invisibility to Undead (1st-level priest), choose one per use, once per day (10 mana) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Vampiric Touch (3rd-level wizard) *or* Speak with Dead (3rd-level priest), choose one per use, once per day (30 mana) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Contagion *or* Enervation (4th-level wizard spells), choose one per use, once per day (40 mana) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Cure Critical Wounds (5th-level priest, self only), once per day (50 mana) |
| 10 | May heal an undead creature by touch for 2d4 points of damage +1 per level (no mana cost) |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Heal (6th-level priest, self only), once per day (60 mana) |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Energy Drain *or* Wail of the Banshee (9th-level wizard spells), choose one per use, once per day (90 mana) |
| 20 | Foundation bonus: Slay Living (reverse of the 5th-level priest spell raise dead, 10 mana) *or* Destruction (reverse of the 7th-level priest spell resurrection, 14 mana), choose one per use, twice per tenday |
