# General & Emerald Enclave Holy Spells

Non-deity-specific spells from *Prayers from the Faithful* (TSR 9545) -- available to priests of
multiple faiths rather than tied to one deity's holy book.

## General Spells

| Name | Est. Mana | Foundation | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purify Self | 10 | All | Cleanses the caster and their vestments of dirt and grime, restores clothing to its original condition, and heals a single point of damage if no other curative magic has yet been applied. |
| Omen | 20 | All, Divination | Lets the priest request a symbolic yes-or-no sign of their deity's approval or disapproval for a planned action, with the chance of receiving an answer improving at higher caster levels. |
| Continual Faerie Fire | 30 | Weather | Permanently coats a chosen area in a harmless colored glow that outlines any creature, invisible being, undead, or active magic that touches or enters it. |
| Glyph of Warding: Telatha | 30 | Guardian | A variant glyph of warding favored by Lathander's church that, when triggered, bursts with sunrise-like brilliance to stun the intruder and risk blinding them permanently. |
| Hold Metal | 40 | Charm | Freezes all ferrous metal objects and armor within the area of effect in place for the duration, immobilizing anyone wearing enough iron or steel. |
| Censure | 40 | All | Marks a touched creature that fails its save with a sigil invisible to itself but obvious to the caster's co-religionists, who then shun, distrust, and drive it away. |
| Control Giant Insect | 40 | Animal | Lets the priest remotely puppet a single giant or magically enlarged insect, its body mimicking the caster's own movements to perform complex tasks. |
| Float | 50 | Creation | Grants an object weighing up to 500 pounds a slow magical flight that the caster can steer, or hold motionless, through concentration. |
| Hard Water | 50 | Elemental Water | Turns a volume of ordinary water into a rock-hard solid for the duration, safely trapping anything embedded within it without causing harm. |
| Break Limb | 60 | Necromantic, Healing | Lets the priest snap a touched target's arm or leg (crippling its use) while dealing damage; its reverse form instead mends already-broken bones. |
| Create Undead Minion | 70 | Necromantic | An evil-aligned rite that transforms a living or freshly dead subject into an undead servant -- its type scaling with the caster's level -- bound to the caster's control. |

## Emerald Enclave Spells

| Name | Est. Mana | Foundation | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Faith Magic Zone | 40 | Protection, Wards | Creates a wide zone that penalizes the casting of quick wizard spells and spell-like magic item effects while granting creatures inside a bonus against wizard magic. |
| Retarget | 50 | Protection | Lets the caster seize a spell cast during the duration and redirect it to a different target in range, negating it instead if the new target is out of range. |
| Windlance | 50 | Elemental Air | Launches the caster and anyone nearby high into the sky on a burst of wind as an escape method, letting the caster shapeshift mid-flight for a safe landing. |
