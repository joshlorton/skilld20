# Karsus (Dead) (The Momentary God, The Archwizard)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Karsus
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Momentary God, The Archwizard
- **Portfolio:** Folly of overweening arrogance and ambition, unending hubris -- Karsus was destroyed by
  his own hubris in the same instant he achieved godhood, roughly two thousand years before the present
  day, during the fall of ancient Netheril; see Church Summary for what became of his "clergy."
- **Symbol:** None. Karsus's brief cult never adopted a formal holy symbol; the closest equivalent was
  the ever-bleeding stone heart enshrined at the Shrine of the Undying Heart, built atop the red stone
  butte that is Karsus's fallen, petrified body.

## Church Summary

**Karsus is dead, and no organized worship of him exists anywhere in the Realms today.** He was a mortal
archwizard of ancient Netheril who achieved godhood for a single instant before dying in the same moment
his hubris destroyed his own empire. He never lived long enough as a god to grant a single spell to any
follower, living or dead. Everything below documents the short-lived, mortal-founded cult that arose in
his name centuries after his death, presented as historical curiosity, not as an active or ever-truly-
functioning church.

- **Clergy (historical):** None. Karsus never had clerics or specialty priests in any real sense -- the
  source is explicit that his followers were "lay followers of any class except priests, paladins, or
  rangers," chiefly minor magelings of little talent, and that the cult itself was exposed within about
  a decade as "a sham lacking any divine patron." No spells were ever granted by Karsus to a mortal
  follower.
- **All Clergy (historical):** All lay followers of Karsus received lore (Netherese history) as a bonus
  skill, if instructed by a well-established cult member familiar with the era.
- **Organization:** Karsus is all but unknown in the Realms today; the elves and half-elves of the
  eastern High Forest regard him as an embodiment of human arrogance and recklessness. After Netheril's
  fall, Netherese refugees following a series of "dream visions" built the city of Karse in -298 DR at
  the foot of the red stone butte formed by Karsus's fallen body, and founded a cult clinging to the
  belief that Karsus could somehow restore Netheril's former glory. With the lich Wulgreth's secret
  assistance, the cult was exposed as having no true divine patron; Karse was destroyed in the ensuing
  fighting and abandoned, and the cult died out within roughly a decade -- before it ever established a
  formal hierarchy of titles.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy (historical):** None were ever established; members were known
  informally as Heartbloods.

### Dogma (historical)

- The fall of Netheril was humankind's folly and pride, punished by the wrath of the gods.
- Karsus's arrogance destroyed Netheril, but the very act which caused the collapse transformed the
  Archwizard into a being both human and divine.
- Karsus would be reborn to lead humankind into a new age of magic, built on the ashes of the old but
  tempered by experience.

### Day-to-Day Activities (historical)

- Administer the city of Karse and mine the rich metal deposits of the neighboring Star Mounts.
- Perform an unending series of unsuccessful rituals designed to reawaken Karsus and usher in his
  promised rebirth of Netheril.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies (historical)

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Reawakening rituals | Monthly, form changing each time | Inspired by cult leaders' claimed "dream visions"; typically featured extreme displays of emotion and rolling chants synchronized to the endless beating of Karsus's undying heart. These rituals never succeeded. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal, historical, extinct)* Order of Thaumaturgists -- wizards within the cult of Karsus who
  researched new spells in the wake of Mystryl's death and the resulting change in the nature of magic;
  some of their spell tomes may still survive in shattered towers scattered about the Dire Wood.

## Specialty Priests

**No specialty priest class ever existed for Karsus, at any point in his history.** Karsus held divine
power for only a single instant before dying, and never granted a spell to any mortal. The source
material itself contains no specialty-priest stat block for him at all -- only a note that his lay
followers were drawn from "any class except priests, paladins, or rangers." The Heartblood cult of
ancient Karse believed itself to serve a true god, but per the source was conclusively exposed as
following no divine patron whatsoever. There is nothing to document here beyond that historical fact.

### Rank Progression

**Not applicable.** With no specialty priest class and no divine grants ever bestowed, there is no rank
progression to record.
