# Bhaal (Lord of Murder)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Bhaal
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of Murder
- **Portfolio:** Death, especially violent or ritual death
- **Symbol:** A white, face-on human skull surrounded by a counterclockwise orbit of many streaming
  teardrops

### Symbol -- Midjourney prompt

```
heraldic emblem, a stark white human skull facing forward centered on a black background, encircled by a counterclockwise ring of streaming teardrop shapes trailing like comet tails, painted heraldry style, flat vector-like clean linework, bone-white and jet-black palette with a hint of dried-blood red, grim funerary symbol, holy symbol, high detail --ar 1:1
```

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** a disorganized web of strictly local hierarchies rather than a single unified
  church. Bhaalists/Bhaalyn in urban areas tended to be clerics, while remote-area worshipers (such
  as those of the Moonshaes) tended to be specialty priests known as deathstalkers. Bhaal was
  murdered by Cyric during the Time of Troubles, and nearly all of his former urban clergy have since
  converted to worshiping Cyric outright, adopting his vestments, ceremonies, religion-specific
  spells, and specialty-priest abilities; only isolated, dwindling pockets of true Bhaal-worship
  persist in remote rural areas under aging priests. Urban temples were dark, spartan dungeons
  beneath city streets, often containing crypts of past murder victims; rural holy sites were
  primitive hilltop shrines dominated by blood-stained sacrificial altars ringed with stones carved
  into teardrop-shaped skulls.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Slaying Hand (ordinary clergy, collectively called Deathdealers)
  2. Cowled Death (one of the nine most senior clergy of a temple or community)
  3. First Murder (assistant to a Primate or Primistress)
  4. Primate / Primistress (head of a temple or fortress)
  5. High Primate / High Primistress (leader of an area or faction of Bhaal-worship)

### Dogma

- Every murder committed strengthens holy Bhaal; murder is viewed as both pastime and duty.
- Deal death at least once every tenday, during the darkest period at the heart of the night; if
  imprisonment or other circumstances prevent this, murder twice for each death missed.
- Ensure that, before they die, murder victims know who is killing them and that their death is in
  Bhaal's name.
- Make all folk fear Bhaal -- let killings be especially elegant, grisly, or deceptively easy, so
  that onlookers are awed or terrified.
- Tell folk that gold given to the church can persuade the Lord of Murder to overlook them for a day.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Spend nights performing murders and days preparing for murder: procuring or caring for weapons,
  traveling to the site, praying to Bhaal for success, sleeping under guard to stay fully rested for
  the hours of darkness, eating, training, and praying for spells.
- Many serve as assassins, bounty hunters, and mercenaries, often within organized brotherhoods.
- Kill for its own sake, but avoid indiscriminate murder -- much thought goes into planning both the
  deed and its consequences; the church works to eliminate rivals while sparing economically or
  socially important individuals.
- Tithe/offering: frighten common folk into placing offerings of coin and valuables before Bhaalists.
- High Primates spend much of their time scheming to manipulate nearby rulers, inhabitants, and
  organizations toward ends the church desires.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Slaying Rite | Whenever a priest kills in Bhaal's name | The core ritual of the faith: the priest intones "Bhaal awaits thee, Bhaal embraces thee, none escape Bhaal," then draws Bhaal's symbol beside the corpse in the victim's blood and smears it on their own hands, from which it vanishes if the rite is done correctly. |
| Day's Farewell | Nightly, before sleep | A mass prayer ritual held in temples each night as clergy retire. |
| Rank Ascension Rite | Whenever a priest is promoted | The ascending priest leaves the temple and returns only after slaying someone with bare hands alone, then privately relates the killing to a senior priest; if the signs are favorable, a full ceremonial rank ritual follows involving a living sacrifice to Bhaal. |
| Feast of the Moon | Annual | The only calendar-fixed rite of the church: the faith's dead are remembered and famous slayings retold, such as the tale of Uthaedeol the Blood-drenched's killing of King Samyte of Tethyr. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Shadow Thieves of Amn -- a guild of thieves and assassins sponsored by Bhaal's
  clergy; still exists though Bhaal does not.
- *(Informal)* Citadel of Assassins, Galena Mountains -- another Bhaal-sponsored brotherhood of
  assassins and thieves.
- *(Informal)* Brethren of the Keen Strike -- a society comprising all specialty priests
  (deathstalkers) of Bhaal; its senior members are the only Bhaalyn permitted to wield ceremonial
  daggers in actual combat or slayings rather than ritual alone.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Deathstalker
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning and piercing weapons; favored weapon dagger
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light and Medium armor; no shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Combat, Elemental, Healing, Law, Necromantic, Summoning
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Creation, Guardian, Time
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** None specified in the source
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on survival (hunting and tracking are
  both survival skill actions); +1 bonus on dagger (ceremonial dagger); +1 bonus on stealth
