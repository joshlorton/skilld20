# Thoth (Lord of Magic, Scribe of the Gods, Knower of All Secrets, the Keeper of Knowledge, King of Knowledge)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Thoth
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of Magic, Scribe of the Gods, Knower of All Secrets, the Keeper of
  Knowledge, King of Knowledge
- **Portfolio:** Neutral magic, scribes, knowledge, science, engineering, secrets, invention
- **Symbol:** The head of an ibis below an ankh

### Symbol -- Midjourney prompt

```
heraldic emblem, the head of an ibis in profile mounted atop an ankh, rendered in polished gold and lapis-blue linework on an ivory field, elegant flowing heraldic style, holy symbol of knowledge and magic, high detail --ar 1:1
```

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, specialty priests, and wizards of Thoth receive religion (pantheon),
  literacy (Mulhorandi), and linguistics (Common) as bonus proficiencies, and know Mulhorandi as a
  native tongue. A wizard must learn the wizard spells read magic and read Southern magic before
  entering the clergy.
- **Organization:** Thoth's followers are few in number but widely respected. His temples double as
  libraries and universities, built with huge atriums, towering columns, and immense domes, each new
  temple expected to push the boundaries of conventional Mulhorandi architecture. All of Thoth's
  clergy must be humans of Mulhorandi extraction; the priesthood is largely hereditary, and nearly all
  specialty priests belong to the House of Tholaunt, human descendants of Thoth's ancient incarnations.
  Before the Time of Troubles, all clergy needed at least 5th-level wizardry before entering the
  priesthood, keeping the church small; since the Godswar that requirement has loosened, novices may
  now become simple clerics, and mages or specialist wizards of any level (other than necromancers) are
  accepted. Specialty priests, called scriveners, are now specially multiclassed wizard/priests rather
  than dual-classed. The public addresses all of Thoth's clergy as "Lord High Wizard." Wizards currently
  make up 55% of the clergy, a share expected to keep growing.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Notary
  2. Diviner of Mysteries
  3. Engineer
  4. Recorder of Knowledge
  5. Scrivener of Secrets

### Dogma

- Learn all you can, and help spread wisdom throughout the world.
- Research magic, protect the secrets of Thoth, and spread magic throughout Mulhorand.
- Explore new invention and better ways of doing things, pushing the limits of engineering,
  architecture, and magical item construction.
- Protect Mulhorand from the traitorous Red Wizards of Thay and undermine their efforts at every turn.
- Use weaponry as little as possible -- magic is the true weapon of Thoth's clergy.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Spend mornings studying; spend most afternoons researching magic, serving as scribes, clerks, or
  librarians for the church or state, or teaching the young.
- Particularly pious young priests spend evenings assisting the poor and unschooled as scribes and
  tutors.
- Hold the contents of any dictated message in strictest confidence, as a religious trust between the
  scribe and the speaker; delivering messages is left to messengers, not scribes.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Ceremony of Introspection | Winter and summer solstices | Priests fast for a full day, then immerse themselves in an icy bath, symbolizing a desire for true understanding and a clear-headed return to the world. |
| Sigil of Beginning | Before any major engineering, writing, or magical undertaking | The clergy draw Thoth's symbol on the backs of their hands and over their hearts; scribes also inscribe his symbol atop every fresh page of papyrus before writing anything else on it. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) The Order of the Ibis Feather -- a sacred fellowship of 77 scribes, many scriveners of 9th
  level or higher, who serve the pharaoh of Mulhorand directly, recruited from the elite of Gheldaneth's
  Scribes' Guild and from Thoth's clergy.
- (Formal) The Brothers and Sisters of the Quill (the Quillians) -- wizards and specialty priests who
  adventure beyond Mulhorand's borders to recover lost knowledge and destroy those who wrongfully seek
  or acquire the secrets of Thothian mage script.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Scrivener
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Dagger, dart, knife, staff; favored weapon the was-scepter (light mace) --
  like Geb, Thoth is shown in real-world Egyptian iconography holding a was-scepter (alongside his more
  common scribe's palette), whose distinct animal-head striking end and forked base read as a stylized
  mace rather than a plain quarterstaff
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** None (unarmored)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Creation, Divination, Elemental, Guardian, Healing, Protection,
  Necromantic, Numbers, Summoning, Sun, Time
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Plant, Thought, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as wizards or clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in astrology, 1 rank in engineering, 1 rank in art (pick one: drawing,
  illumination, papermaking)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on literacy (Untheric); +1 bonus on
  literacy (ancient Rauric); +1 bonus on literacy (Thorass); +1 bonus on spellcraft

Scriveners must be humans of Mulhorandi extraction and multiclassed wizard/priests (mage or any
specialist wizard except enchanter or necromancer) -- a combination normally barred to humans but
permitted by Thoth as a special exception. They receive the wizard spells read magic and read Southern
magic upon entering the priesthood.

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2, "once per tenday" =
level × 1). Where no spell level is given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that
class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Detect Lie, requires hearing the speaker (no mana cost), at will |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Speak with Birds (10 mana), once per day |
| 5 | Intelligence and Wisdom each permanently increase by 1 point, to a maximum of 18 |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Magic Missile (10 mana) *or* Fly (30 mana), choose one per use, once per day, cast at the scrivener's wizard level |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Commune, with Thoth specifically (5 mana), once per tenday |
| 15 | Intelligence and Wisdom each permanently increase by 1 point again, to a maximum of 19 |
| 20 | Foundation bonus: Permanency, without the usual loss of Constitution (negligible mana under the standard formula at this frequency), up to three times per year |
