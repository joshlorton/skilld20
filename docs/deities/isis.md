# Isis (Bountiful Lady, Lady of All Love, Lady of Rivers, Mistress of Weather, Mistress of Enchantment)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Isis
- **Titles/Epithets:** Bountiful Lady, Lady of All Love, Lady of Rivers, Mistress of Weather, Mistress
  of Enchantment
- **Portfolio:** Weather, rivers, agriculture, love, marriage, good magic
- **Symbol:** A silver lunar disk bearing an ankh and a star, surrounded by horns (also used alone: an
  ankh and a star, or an eye and a teardrop)

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, specialty priests, and mystics of Isis receive religion (Mulhorandi),
  literacy (Mulhorandi), and linguistics (Common) as bonus skills. As Mulhorandi, they all also know
  Mulhorandi as their native tongue. All of Isis's clergy must be female and of Mulhorandi extraction,
  and every member -- cleric, specialty priest, or mystic alike -- must be multiclassed as a
  wizard/priest (a special exception to the usual human multiclass restriction). The human wizard half
  of the pairing may be a mage or any specialist wizard except a necromancer, though enchanter is
  favored; the rare half-elf clergy (always of gold or moon elf ancestry, under 1/10 of 1% of the
  clergy) pair their priest class with mage, conjurer, diviner, enchanter, or transmuter instead. Isis's
  clergy wear no armor of any kind.
- **Organization:** About 55% of Isis's clergy are multiclassed clerics, 30% are multiclassed specialty
  priests (skyweavers), and 15% are multiclassed mystics. All priestesses are addressed publicly as
  "Lady Priestess," or "Lady High Priestess" at 9th level or higher; within the clergy, titles such as
  Seedgiver, Bountiful Sower, Lady of the Harvest, Joylove, Endless River, Rainmaker, Spellweaver, Lady
  of the Planting, and Joyous Uniter are used, varying by temple. Isis is the most beloved deity of the
  common people, worshiped by the lost, those seeking guidance, women entering marriage, all whose
  hearts are touched by love, farmers, and young mothers. Temples are architecturally varied but always
  built with crop-carved pillars, rooms left open to the weather, ever-flowing fonts sprung from solid
  rock, and shadowed gardens for young lovers.

### Dogma

- Encourage love, affection, and marriage among the people of Mulhorand and Unther.
- Sow the seeds of happiness, family, and food wherever you go, and offer wise counsel to those in need.
- Use magic only to benefit the people of the Old Empires -- above all by shaping the weather to serve
  them and crafting magical items and charms for the common good.
- Protect the heroes of Mulhorand and all who are in love.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Oversee the agricultural production of the nation's farms, especially during planting season, using
  weather-control magic to ensure a bountiful harvest.
- Administer most marriage ceremonies in Mulhorand and Unther, work as matchmakers, serve as
  go-betweens for lovers, and counsel young mothers on raising their children.
- Fashion charms of Isis for the deserving and magical items to serve good causes or ease the burdens of
  the common people.
- Celebrate the Shift in the Winds with quiet thanks each time the weather turns for the better, drink
  deeply and give thanks whenever crossing a river, and bless every meal before the first bite.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Greengrass | Annual (planting) | The clergy give thanks to Isis for her aid with the planting; a day of joyous celebration, wild revels, and moonlit trysts joined by everyone involved in farming. |
| Higharvestide | Annual (harvest) | The clergy give thanks to both Isis and Osiris for their help with the harvest; celebrated the same way as Greengrass. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) The Sisters of Life -- serve in the Guardians of Skuld alongside the clergy of Anhur and
  Osiris, protecting the capital city.
- (Informal) The Shield of the Lady -- a fighting order of wizards, clerics, and specialty priests who
  join adventuring companies across the Old Empires and beyond, shielding Mulhorand's heroes from
  barbarism and evil in service of the goddess's will, whether or not they realize it.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Skyweaver
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons plus dagger, dart, knife, lasso,
  net; favored weapon the papyrus baton -- Isis is consistently depicted holding a papyrus-form staff
  (the wadj) in one hand and the ankh in the other, which rules out a full two-handed quarterstaff and
  points to a shorter, one-handed baton or wand instead; superseding the earlier D&D 3e citation
  (an ankh-and-star punching dagger, which was never actually attested as a weapon in real iconography
  -- Isis is shown holding the ankh, not fighting with it)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** None (no armor of any kind permitted, not even elven chain mail)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Animal, Charm, Combat, Creation, Divination, Elemental, Guardian,
  Healing, Numbers, Plant, Protection, Necromantic, Sun, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Time
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (whichever of the cleric or wizard restriction set is more
  beneficial in a given case)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in agriculture, 1 rank in herbalism, 1 rank in spellcraft, 1 rank in lore
  (weather)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on linguistics (Untheric)

Only human or half-elf women of Mulhorandi extraction can be skyweavers (half-elves always of moon or
gold elf ancestry); like all of Isis's clergy, they must be multiclassed wizard/priests.

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2, "twice per day" = level × 20,
"once per tenday" = level × 1). Where no spell level is given but a class/character level is, use spell
level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | 5% innate magic resistance, +5% per experience level thereafter, to a maximum of 50% |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Read Magic (no mana cost), permanently known and freely castable |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Charm Person (1 mana), once per tenday |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Dispel Magic (30 mana), once per day |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Air Walk (50 mana) *or* Vestment (30 mana), choose one per use, once per day |
| 9 | Foundation bonus: Regenerate (7 mana), once per tenday |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Control Weather (70 mana), once per day |
| 13 | Never fails a saving throw caused by a magical attack (spell, scroll, potion, or item) -- still takes any damage involved, but always resolves as if the save succeeded |
