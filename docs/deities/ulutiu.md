# Ulutiu (Slumbering) (The Lord in the Ice, the Eternal Sleeper, Father of the Giants' Kin)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Ulutiu
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Lord in the Ice, the Eternal Sleeper, Father of the Giants' Kin
- **Portfolio:** Glaciers, frozen seas, polar environments, arctic dwellers
- **Symbol:** A necklace of blue and white ice crystals

## Church Summary

**Ulutiu is not dead -- merely slumbering.** He drifts in a voluntary, eon-long sleep in the Astral Plane
alongside the remains of his lover Othea, and has shown no interest in the Realms since her death. But
unlike deities who have truly died, he remains "sufficiently alive" (per the source) to go on granting
spells to his few followers in Faerûn's arctic north. The church described below is a small, remote, and
functioning one -- not a historical relic -- though it is also a shrinking one: two of the deities of Cold
(Auril) and giants (Annam) are actively encroaching on Ulutiu's people even as he sleeps through it.

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All shamans and specialty priests of Ulutiu receive religion (Faerunian) and religion
  (giant) as bonus skills.
- **Organization:** Ulutiu has no unified priesthood, no true temples, and no cross-community hierarchy --
  his two peoples, the Nakulutiuns of the Great Glacier and the Ice Hunters of the Savage Frontier (who
  split off from the Nakulutiuns, then known as the Iulutiuns, nearly 3,000 years ago), venerate him in
  unconnected and very different ways. Each Nakulutiun village is ruled outright by an autocratic
  specialty priest called the urit (male or female) and three lieutenant specialty priests called
  yaaurit; Nakulutiun specialty priests are collectively known as nakurits. Ice Hunter communities are
  led more by consensus, through two priestly types filling the same civic role: beast-cult shamans, who
  directly channel one of the four Ice Hunter totem great spirits (Clever Oomio the Gray Seal,
  Grandfather Walrus, Great White Bear, and Pindalpau-pau the Reindeer Mother), and iceguardians, Ice
  Hunter specialty priests who blend priestly and arcane (elemental water) magic. There are no temples in
  the conventional sense -- worship happens at small village shrines, typically in the home of the
  senior priest, centered on unmelting ice-block altars enclosing sacred fetishes.

### Dogma

- No person is the superior of another, except Ulutiu's priests, who are his representatives and rightful
  leaders.
- Animals share the same emotions, thoughts, and morals as people, though Ulutiu compels them to express
  these differently; because animals are his children, disrespecting them risks his wrath.
- All people and animals carry a life essence, pokulu, that passes to Ulutiu at death and becomes part of
  him.
- *(Nakulutiuns only)* Ulutiu has set a social order for his faithful in which everyone knows and accepts
  their place.
- *(Nakulutiuns only)* Keep apart from other peoples of the Great Glacier, such as the Iulutiuns and
  Angulutiuns -- they are heathens, and long contact with them leads the faithful astray.
- *(Nakulutiuns only)* Magic outside of that granted by Ulutiu is blasphemous; wizards are to be shunned
  or driven from Nakulutiun villages.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Nakurits rule their villages as autocratic, at times harsh, leaders, but are rarely self-interested;
  villagers trust that Ulutiu himself guides their edicts.
- Ice Hunter priests (shamans and iceguardians) lead more by consensus. They teach clan children, induct
  youths into adulthood, keep clan history and tradition alongside practical survival knowledge, and lead
  quiet resistance where Luskan's influence encroaches on their communities.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Kaiurit Announcement | First day of spring | *(Nakulutiuns only)* After a month of solitary evening prayer, the urit declares the past year's kaiurit (special edicts) void and proclaims two or three new ones, received as fragments of Ulutiu's own dreams. |
| Thrice-Daily Prayer | Dawn, midday, and sunset, every day | A minute of silent prayer -- eyes covered by the hands, face turned to the sky. Observed by both Nakulutiuns and Ice Hunters. |
| Kaitotem | Year-long, beginning at the birthing season | *(Ice Hunters only)* An infant of the tribe's totem species is captured unharmed and raised in comfort by the priesthood for a year, then departs, possessed by the totem spirit; how well it was treated determines Ulutiu's favor for the year to come. |

### Affiliated Orders

- None. Neither the Nakulutiuns nor the Ice Hunters maintain warrior, knightly, or honorary orders --
  status is instead set by rigid social ranking: community leaders (Ulutiu's priests) first, then
  married or widowed adults, unmarried adults, children, and finally the ukeu (the elderly, infirm, or
  family-less, who enter a respected but unequal voluntary servitude with any family that will have
  them).

## Specialty Priests

Ulutiu has three specialty-priest traditions with their own granted powers: Nakulutiun nakurits, Ice
Hunter beast-cult shamans, and Ice Hunter iceguardians.

### Nakurits

- **Name/Title:** Nakurit (urit if a village's ruling priest, yaaurit if one of an urit's three
  lieutenants)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Short bow (and ekaa and trukaa arrows), dagger, gaff, garnok (and darts),
  harpoon, iuak, knife, net, ritiik, spear, trident; favored weapon harpoon (per 3.5e sourcing: the
  Harpoon of the Cold Sea, statted as a longspear or shortspear -- consistent with this book's own
  harpoon/spear-heavy weapon list)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (up to leather or hide); no shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Charm, Elemental (Water), Guardian, Law, Summoning, Protection,
  Travelers, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Healing, Necromantic, Plant
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (Ulutiuns and Ulutiu), 1 rank in lore (Great Glacier), 1 rank in
  craft (fishing), 1 rank in lore (weather), 1 rank in survival (fire-building), 1 rank in survival
  (trapping/set snares)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on charioteering (dog sled); +1 bonus on
  endurance; +1 bonus on survival (arctic); +1 bonus on survival (tracking)

Nakurits must be human Nakulutiuns. They are paranoidly distrustful of non-Nakulutiuns and rarely
cooperate with them for long.

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day

Where no spell level is given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2,
rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 bonus on attack rolls against all non-Nakulutiuns |
| 1 | Receives double the normal bonus spells granted for a high Wisdom score |
| 1 | Totally immune to cold, including cold-based spells and other magically generated cold effects |
| 1 | Can lay hands on a touched creature to deal 2 points of damage per priest level (save vs. spell for half), once per day |
| 3 | +3 bonus on saving throws vs. spells cast by wizards (not priests of any sort) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Commune (7 mana -- 5th-level spell, once per week = 10×5×⅐), once per week |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Commune (50 mana), once per day -- but only for the urit (village leader), and only during the month before the yearly kaiurit is announced |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Command (no mana cost; at-will, but no more than once every turn/10 rounds); Nakulutiuns treat such commands as Ulutiu's own word and voluntarily fail their saves barring extraordinary circumstances |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Prayer (30 mana), once per day |
| 10 | +2 bonus cold damage (beyond Strength or other magic) on successful melee or missile attacks against non-Nakulutiuns |
| 12 | Cold damage bonus against non-Nakulutiuns increases to +4 |
| 14 | Cold damage bonus against non-Nakulutiuns increases to +6 (maximum) |

### Ice Hunter Beast-Cult Shamans

- **Name/Title:** Beast-Cult Shaman
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Short bow (and ekaa, trukaa, and flight arrows), spear, net, harpoon, gaff,
  machete, garnok (and darts), trident, knife, dagger, iuak, ritiik; favored weapon harpoon (per 3.5e
  sourcing, see Nakurits above)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (up to leather or hide); no shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Summoning, Protection, Travelers, Wards (plus totem-specific spheres,
  see Totem Variants below)
- **Minor Spheres:** Healing, Plant (plus totem-specific spheres, see Totem Variants below)
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (fishing), 1 rank in lore (weather), 1 rank in survival
  (fire-building), 1 rank in charioteering (dog sled), 1 rank in survival (trapping/set snares)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on endurance; +1 bonus on survival
  (arctic); +1 bonus on survival (tracking)

Ice Hunter shamans must be Ice Hunter humans. Their alignment and access to additional spheres depend on
their tribe's totem great spirit; see Totem Variants below.

### Iceguardians

- **Name/Title:** Iceguardian
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Short bow (and ekaa, trukaa, and flight arrows), harpoon, net, gaff, garnok
  (and darts), knife, dagger, iuak, ritiik, trident; favored weapon harpoon (per 3.5e sourcing, see
  Nakurits above)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (up to leather or hide); no shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Elemental (Water), Summoning, Protection, Travelers, Wards (plus
  totem-specific spheres, see Totem Variants above)
- **Minor Spheres:** Healing, Plant (plus totem-specific spheres, see Totem Variants above)
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in craft (fishing), 1 rank in lore (weather), 1 rank in charioteering (dog
  sled), 1 rank in survival (trapping/set snares), 1 rank in lore (glaciers above the Spine of the
  World), 1 rank in lore (Ice Hunter people and Ulutiu)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on endurance; +1 bonus on survival
  (arctic); +1 bonus on survival (tracking)

Iceguardians must be Ice Hunter humans, and must never start a fire themselves (though they may enjoy the
benefit of one started by others) or lose all iceguardian abilities until they atone. Their alignment and
access to additional spheres depend on their tribe's totem great spirit; see Totem Variants above.
