# Leira (Lady of the Mists, Mother of Illusionists, the Guardian of Liars, the Mistshadow)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Leira
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lady of the Mists, Mother of Illusionists, the Guardian of Liars, the Mistshadow
- **Portfolio:** Deception, illusion
- **Symbol:** A triangular plaque of cloudy, swirling gray mists, point downward

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** spellcasters.
- **Organization:** Leira died shortly after the Time of Troubles, betrayed to Cyric by her onetime
  friend Mask; Cyric absorbed her portfolio and now secretly grants spells to her clergy in her guise,
  a fact most Leirans neither know nor care about. The church is loose and chaotic, with an estimated
  ratio of roughly 20 clerics to 45 specialty priests to 35 illusionists. Because compulsive lying is
  doctrine, official titles change constantly and mean nothing reliable -- a "high pontiff" one tenday
  may be an "acolyte" the next.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** No fixed ascending rank list -- titles are deliberately random
  and ever-changing (ranging from "Supreme High Lord of the Heights and Depths" to "Most Holy
  Guttersweeper"), and the same individual may hold a different title every tenday. Specialty priests
  are informally distinguished as mistcallers.

### Dogma

- The Lady can be anything, anywhere, that is not what it seems.
- Make folk everywhere doubt what they believe and see, in order to restore Holy Mystery to the world.
- Give as well as take; raise hope as well as dash it.
- Never tell the truth when less will do, and never use a half-truth when a lie would serve better.
- Speak truth only to fellow worshipers of Leira, and only between two utterances of "By the
  Mistshadow."
- Cherish and further illusions and rumors, for distortion and legend are what make folk happy and
  life alluring.
- Hiding a thing gives it value by the very act of cloaking it.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Spread false rumors and craft illusions for fees; engineer hoaxes that encourage, then dramatically
  reveal, false beliefs.
- Act as masters of disguise, renting costumes and applying makeup (for stiff fees) to those in some
  kind of trouble.
- Work as actors-for-hire in others' deceptions -- impersonating spouses, agents, brigands, escorts,
  thieves, or even rival clergy.
- Run personal swindles and cons for enrichment and entertainment; a tenet of the church requires that
  anyone a Leiran robs must later be aided by Leiran hands to offset the loss.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Prayers | Every morning and every moonlit night | Performed lying face-down on the ground. |
| Fog/Mist Walks | Whenever fog or mist is encountered | Clergy walk and chant praises to Leira, who may answer as an echoing whisper from the mists. |
| Daily Altar Ceremony | Daily, at temples | A brief ceremony allowing nonbelievers to make placating offerings and lay worshipers to be heard and guided. |
| The Unmasking | On entering the priesthood, rising in rank, or as penance | The bare-faced supplicant walks down ranks of candle-holding priests, between reflecting pools of water and mirrors. |
| The Invocation | As needed, to call the goddess directly | Chanting priests swing censers to create thick smoke within which she may appear and speak. |
| The Conclaves | Six times yearly, at shifting locations and times | Secret gatherings where, by holy writ, only truth is spoken; used to share information, transfer clergy between temples, and vote on the faith's most important missions. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No formal knightly or military orders are confirmed -- the church itself contradictorily claims (on
  different days) to have many, none, or to secretly control them all, making the truth unknowable.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Mistcaller
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon war fan (tessen) -- a real historical
  weapon that folds closed for concealment, hiding a weapon inside something harmless-looking
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Any armor type or shield is technically permitted, but wearing any
  armor at all entirely negates spellcasting and spell-like ability use -- mistcallers function
  unarmored in practice.
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Creation, Divination, Healing, Protection, Summoning, Time,
  Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Chaos, Combat, Elemental, Guardian, Necromantic
- **Magical Items:** No limitations; additionally may use items/effects involving illusions or
  deceptions normally restricted to wizards
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in disguise
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on linguistics (pick one modern
  language); +1 bonus on literacy (Ruathlek); +1 bonus on deception (ventriloquism); +1 bonus on
  sleight of hand

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

True seeing (and any device using it) is entirely forbidden to clerics and mistcallers of Leira alike;
using it drives the caster irrevocably insane.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2 bonus to saves vs. illusion/phantasm spells and spell-like abilities |
| 2 | May cast illusion/phantasm wizard spells in place of priest spells, as a mage of half actual level (round down); capped below 8th/9th-level wizard spells |
| 2 | May cast purely illusion/phantasm spells from scrolls without a read magic spell |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Impenetrable Falsehood (20 mana) |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Phantasmal Force or Audible Glamer, choose one per use (10 mana) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Misdirection (20 mana -- 1/day, +1 additional use per 5 levels beyond 5th) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Shadow Magic (50 mana) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Demi-Shadow Magic (60 mana) and Disbelief (60 mana), both usable once per day |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Mislead (60 mana) |
| 20 | Foundation bonus: Permanent Illusion (18 mana -- 6th-level effect, 3/tenday = level×1.8) |
