# Ao (Lord Ao, the Hidden One)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Ao
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord Ao, the Hidden One
- **Portfolio:** Creation of deities, maintenance of cosmic balance
- **Symbol:** None. Ao has no holy symbol described in the source.

### Symbol -- Midjourney prompt

Ao has no holy symbol in the source material, so no image prompt is generated for this section.

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Ministers, who may be of any class except the priest classes. Ao has no true
  priesthood -- ministers receive no spells or special powers of any kind from him.
- **Organization:** Cults worshiping Ao directly sprang up immediately after the Time of Troubles and
  mostly evaporated once it became clear Ao does not answer prayers, offer protection, or grant
  spells. The only enduring group is a Waterdeep cult that behaves more like a debating society than a
  church, meeting at the Cynosure (a former temple, now a public forum and rental hall) and acting in
  accordance with the tenets its members believe Ao espoused -- chiefly, maintaining balance among the
  Realms' deities and faiths. A second group in Zazesspur, Tethyr, invokes Ao's name but is secretly
  backed by Cyric, not Ao.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** None described. The cult of Ao has no priests, only lay
  ministers, and no ordination or rank structure is given in the source.

### Dogma

- Ao is the over-power, but what does that actually mean?
- Does Ao himself worship or answer to some other being -- and if so, what would that being's function be?
- If Ao did not create the Realms, only the powers of the Realms, then who did, if anyone?
- (More activist cult members hold that) it is the cult's responsibility to ensure that every faith in
  the Realms actively supports its deity's assigned portfolio.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Debate the meaning of Ao's nature and the words he spoke during his sole appearance during the Time
  of Troubles.
- Debate whether Ao himself serves some even higher over-power.
- No tithe, sacrifice, or offering practice is described -- Ao is not known to want or need anything
  from mortals.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gods' Day | 15th of Marpenoth (Waterdhavian holiday) | Celebrates the anniversary of the end of the Godswar and the day Lord Ao appeared on Mount Waterdeep. The cult of Ao sponsors a day-long party at the Cynosure in Waterdeep, followed by a solemn evening prayer service. |

*(This is added to the standalone [holy-days-calendar.md](holy-days-calendar.md).)*

### Affiliated Orders

- No formal or informal affiliated orders mentioned, beyond the Waterdeep cult itself (already
  described above). A nominal "cult of Ao" exists in Zazesspur, Tethyr, but it is secretly backed by
  Cyric and is not a genuine affiliate of Ao's worship.
