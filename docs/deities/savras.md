# Savras (The All-Seeing, the All-Seeing One, Lord of Divination Magics, He of the Third Eye, the
Diviner)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Savras
- **Titles/Epithets:** The All-Seeing, the All-Seeing One, Lord of Divination Magics, He of the Third
  Eye, the Diviner
- **Portfolio:** Divinations, divination magic, diviners, fate, truth, truth-speakers
- **Symbol:** A crystal ball in which dance countless eyes of many different types

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, monastic orders
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, specialty priests, and monks of Savras receive religion (pantheon) as a
  bonus skill (specialist wizard diviners in his service do not receive this grant).
- **Organization:** Savras is little known in the Realms outside the coterie of diviners and
  fortunetellers, and those outside his faithful who do know of him typically hold him in poor regard --
  his penchant for speaking unwanted truths has earned him more foes than allies, and the clergy of
  Azuth long contributed to sullying his name (only moderating their invective in recent years). Shrines
  and temples are dominated by a giant sacred statue of Savras carved from white marble, seated atop an
  onyx dais in meditative repose with palms upturned and a crystalline third eye set in the brow; halls
  are typically titanic-columned, accessed through iris-like circular portals, and their walls inscribed
  with hieroglyphics depicting visions of the distant past or future. During Savras's long imprisonment
  in the Scepter of Savras, his clergy dwindled to a handful of specialist wizard diviners, as his
  clerics received no answer to their prayers for centuries. Since his release following the Time of
  Troubles, he has added mainly specialty priests (sibylites) and monks. His clergy, collectively known
  as divinators, currently runs about 65% specialty priests, 15% monks, 5% clerics, and the remainder
  specialist wizard diviners. Novices are Truth Seekers; higher-ranking priests, a tradition begun
  during the centuries of Savras's imprisonment, hold their own unique personal titles beyond the
  standard ladder.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Truth Seeker (novice)
  2. Truth Speaker
  3. Savant
  4. Scholar
  5. Sage
  6. Clairvoyant
  7. Soothsayer
  8. Prophesier
  9. Prophet
  10. Oracle

### Dogma

- The blindness of mortals is the origin of all folly.
- Search for the truth in all things great and small, and conceal nothing.
- Speak only the truth -- lies and misdirection, even for benign motives, are the root of all sorrow.
- Be not paralyzed by indecision, but take no action without first analyzing its implications; hasty
  decisions are rarely more beneficial than well thought-out strategies, revised as necessary.
- Mortals who rely only on their two common eyes are essentially blind; Savras grants the third blessed
  eye of foresight and hindsight, letting mortals share in the omniscience of the gods.
- It is not wrong to use Savras's knowledge to help yourself and your church, but be cautious in
  furthering the goals of others, as part of their agenda may be hidden.
- Seek the hidden motive before you act, and damage not the whole of the realm in which you live out
  your mortal life.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Begin and end each day with extended sessions of peaceful meditation.
- Foretell the future or study the past, and deal with the consequences of what is learned.
- Hold extended strategy sessions analyzing the implications of foreseen events and planning
  accordingly.
- Some wander the Realms uttering prophecies; others withdraw to remote locations to become oracles.
- A few serve as truth speakers for the legal systems of various cities and kingdoms, acting as expert
  witnesses, magistrates, or judges.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Vision | Feast of the Moon | Every devout follower observes 24 hours of continuous meditation (in a sauna, steam bath, or amid incense, depending on the temple); each participant is rewarded with a vision from Savras, and is expected to act on it or risk his wrath. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* The Sibylline Sisterhood -- an affiliation of female oracles, mostly low-level sibylites
  (though a few are powerful adventurers), who wander western Faerûn dispensing prophecies and visions
  for a fee that varies with the recipient's wealth; their visions are usually accurate but often so
  clouded as to be helpful only in hindsight.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Sibylite
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons; favored weapon dagger ("the eye of
  Savras")
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** None
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Divination, Healing, Law, Numbers, Sun, Thought, Time
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Guardian, Necromantic, Protection, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations; additionally may use any magical item that allows scrying or
  divination of any type (such as crystal balls)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (pick one: ancient history, local history)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on astrology; +1 bonus on blind-fighting
