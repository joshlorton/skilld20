# Gaerdal Ironhand (The Stern, Shield of the Golden Hills)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Gaerdal Ironhand
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Stern, Shield of the Golden Hills
- **Portfolio:** Vigilance, combat, martial defense
- **Symbol:** An iron band

### Symbol -- Midjourney prompt

```
heraldic emblem, a plain forged iron arm-band shown in profile, steel-gray and dull iron palette, subtle rivet details, austere flat heraldic linework, holy symbol, high detail --ar 1:1
```

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics, cleric/illusionists, and cleric/thieves) and
  specialty priests receive a bonus rank in religion (pantheon).
- **Organization:** Temples are fortified subterranean strongholds, austere and adorned only with the
  shields of the fallen; small statues of Gaerdal are commonly erected at the entrances to gnome
  settlements as a reminder of daily vigilance. Clergy is 75% rock gnome, 15% forest gnome, 10% deep
  gnome, and 85% male. Composition is specialty priests (60%), fighter/clerics (24%), clerics (13%),
  cleric/illusionists (2%), and cleric/thieves (1%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Eyes of Shield (novice)
  2. Stern Watcher
  3. Stern Observer
  4. Stern Guard
  5. Stern Sentinel
  6. Stern Sentry
  7. Stern Guardian
  8. Stern Defender
  9. Stern Protector (highest common rank; individual high priests hold unique titles)

### Dogma

- The best defense is unswerving vigilance.
- Defend and protect communities of the Forgotten Folk against all invaders, both obvious and
  hidden.
- Never cease to hone the skills of battle and war, and pass such talents on to gnomes at large.
- Be fair to those in your charge, and ask of them nothing that you would not do yourself.
- Follow orders to preserve the chain of command, but never be blind to the consequences of your
  actions -- question any order that seems to run counter to the common good, for it may unmask a
  spy, a traitor, or a magically cloaked invader.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve as close to a warrior caste as gnomes have, often doubling as administrators and judges.
- Erect and tend small statues of Gaerdal at major entrances to gnome settlements.
- Make life difficult for the followers of Baravar Cloakshadow, and to a lesser extent Baervan
  Wildwanderer and Garl Glittergold, as their closest approach to enjoyment.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Great Clang | Every tenth Tenhammer (a "Tenhammer" is the faith's name for a tenday, marked by striking a hammer against a metal shield once per day of guard duty) | The cult of Gaerdal assembles to pay homage through battle hymns and rhythmic chants. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Shields of the Golden Hills -- a strictly organized militant order with semiautonomous
  chapters in most large gnome communities, divided into four branches (specialty priests, fighters,
  fighter/clerics, and clerics); defends the Forgotten Folk and serves as the champions and officers
  of any larger gnome force raised in time of need.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Sternshield
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Any (Heavy)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Law, Protection, Summoning, War,
  Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Elemental (Earth), Necromantic
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in war hammer, 1 rank in blind-fighting
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on perception (alertness); +1 bonus on
  endurance

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

## Gaerdalian Spells (deity-specific)

Priests of the Shield of the Golden Hills may also cast blessed watchfulness (1st level), alert
allies (2nd level), and iron vigil (2nd level) -- all detailed in the Gorm Gulthyn entry of the
dwarven pantheon -- added to the standard priest spell list rather than granted as free castings
(though sternshields also receive the first and third as free daily castings, above).

| Name | Est. Mana | Foundation | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deafening Clang | 10 | Combat | Enchants a metal item so that, on command, striking it against a hard surface produces a deafening clang that can deafen nearby creatures who fail a save; usable up to three times or 10 rounds after casting. |
| Nature's Eyes | 30 | Animal, Divination | Attunes the priest to the senses of animal-intelligence creatures in the area, making the caster instantly aware of intruders' approximate positions (and immune to surprise) so long as enough wildlife is present to notice them. |
| Wave of Telekinesis | 40 | Combat | Hurls objects and creatures within a wide arc directly away from the caster with a single violent thrust; affected creatures are only thrown if they fail a save, and damage is capped at 1 point per caster level. |
