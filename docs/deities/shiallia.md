# Shiallia (Dancer in the Glades, Daughter of the High Forest, Sistem Goddess, the Lady of the Woods)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Shiallia
- **Titles/Epithets:** Dancer in the Glades, Daughter of the High Forest, Sistem Goddess, the Lady of
  the Woods
- **Portfolio:** The High Forest, Neverwinter Wood, woodland glades, woodland fertility, growth,
  korreds (especially young korreds)
- **Symbol:** A golden acorn

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Bardic performers (spellsingers)
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, druids, specialty priests, and mystics of Shiallia receive religion
  (Faerûnian) as a bonus skill. Her clergy are usually female and may be human, elf, half-elf, halfling,
  or korred; all human priests of Shiallia must be female.
- **Organization:** Collectively known as the Sisters of Life and Mercy (with a few Brothers of Life
  and Mercy among them), Shiallia's clergy is small and given to migratory wandering -- her priests
  follow long paths wherever natural life needs a helping hand, then move on, though they almost always
  return later to check on and cultivate what they began. Prior to the Fall of the Gods the clergy was
  evenly split between clerics (typically found on the edges of the High Forest) and mystics/druids (who
  wandered its deepest reaches); since the Time of Troubles most initiates have become the specialty
  priests known as woodwives, and the balance is now roughly even among all four types. Formal titles
  are shunned: younger priestesses are addressed as Daughter, peers as Sister, and senior priestesses as
  Mother, while males are Brother, Son, or Elder Brother (never Father). Worship is limited to the
  proximity of the High Forest; there is no central temple, aside from the Golden Oak in Silverymoon and
  the Glade of Life at the headwaters of the Unicorn Run (a site shared with the faiths of Chauntea,
  Eldath, Lurue, and Mielikki).

### Dogma

- The only true goal of any living thing is to procreate.
- Nature dictates the shape of the world, for good or ill; survival is the only true concern of the
  creatures who inhabit it.
- Death is not to be feared, for it is part of the natural cycle -- but life, and especially new life,
  is to be encouraged and nurtured wherever possible.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Plant, nurture, and call upon the weather, tending to the ill and injured -- not only among forest
  creatures, but extending the same care to humans and demihumans who enter or live within the forest.
- Watch over lost children and the foolish who wander the High Forest unaware of its dangers, earning
  the clergy the folk-name the Silent Helpers.
- Bless weddings held upon the change of seasons with faerie charm magic and romance, creating a
  fantasy atmosphere under the stars.

### Affiliated Orders

- Shiallia sponsors no military or knightly orders of her own.
- (Informal) The Shields of Hope -- an elite sisterhood of female swanmay rangers sworn within the
  church of Mielikki to protect the Sisters of Life and Mercy, escorting Shiallia's clergy in groups of
  three through the High Forest's most dangerous regions.
- (Informal) The Harpers -- on friendly terms with Shiallia's followers; Master Harpers within the High
  Forest can speak with any forest animal or plant there and dance with korreds without danger anywhere
  in the Realms, believed to be a mark of Shiallia's blessing.

## Specialty Priests

Shiallia's specialty priests fall into two types: druids and woodwives.

### Druids

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Cudgel (club), sickle, dart, spear, dagger, scimitar, sling, staff, shears;
  favored weapon staff (per real-world 3.5e sourcing, "Forest's Friend," a quarterstaff -- matching
  Shiallia's own avatar, whose walking stick is a staff of the woodlands +4)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (padded armor only), plus a nonmetallic (wooden, bone, or
  shell) shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Charm, Combat, Elemental, Healing, Necromantic (no reversed), Plant,
  Time, Wards, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Protection, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as druids)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (nature), 1 rank in herbalism
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on survival (woodland) -- tracking is a
  survival skill action; +1 bonus on linguistics (pick two from: dryad, korred, satyr, sylph, treant,
  unicorn)

Shiallia's druids are either half-elves of gold elf, moon elf, or wild elf ancestry, or human females.

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cannot cast raise dead or resurrection (necromantic sphere); otherwise follows the standard Player's Handbook Druid class abilities |

### Woodwives

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Cudgel (club), dagger, dart, net, shears, sling, staff; favored weapon staff
  (per real-world 3.5e sourcing, "Forest's Friend," a quarterstaff)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (padded armor only), plus a nonmetallic (wooden, bone, or
  shell) shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Charm, Elemental, Healing, Necromantic (no reversed), Plant,
  Summoning, Sun, Wards, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Divination, Guardian, Protection, Time, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics and druids)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (nature), 1 rank in herbalism
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on healing; +1 bonus on survival
  (woodland) -- tracking is a survival skill action; +1 bonus on linguistics (pick three from: dryad,
  korred, satyr, sylph, treant, unicorn)

Gold elves, moon elves, wild elves, forest gnomes, halflings, half-elves of gold elf, moon elf, or
green elf ancestry, human females, and korreds can become woodwives.
