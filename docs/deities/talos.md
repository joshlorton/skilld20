# Talos (The Destroyer, the Raging One, the Stormstar, the Storm Lord)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Talos
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Destroyer, the Raging One, the Stormstar, the Storm Lord
- **Portfolio:** Storms, destruction, rebellion, conflagrations, earthshakings, and vortices
- **Symbol:** Three lightning bolts, each of a separate color, radiating from a central point

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** Feared far more than worshiped -- Talos has few open followers, and his church
  survives as a pernicious, largely underground presence throughout Faerûn, outlawed in many
  countries. Clergy are itinerant for the most part, traveling to preach of coming dooms and
  disasters (predictions that often come true, since the clergy or Talos himself sometimes causes
  them). Specialty priests (stormlords) outnumber clerics roughly 80/20; clerics are mostly found as
  adventurers and free operatives outside the scattered church cells, which are led by stormlords.
  Public temples, where they exist, tend to be built as castles or walled compounds able to serve as
  defensible strongholds against angry mobs.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Storm Supplicant
  2. Weatherwise
  3. Talon (full, confirmed priest)
  4. Lord/Lady of Fury
  5. Eye of the Storm
  6. Reaver
  7. Stormherald (high priest)
  8. High Stormherald
  9. Weathermaster/Weathermistress

  The uppermost three ranks (Stormherald, High Stormherald, and Weathermaster/Weathermistress) are
  honors bestowed and personally confirmed by Talos himself, not titles priests dare assume on their
  own -- Talos destroys those who speak against his will. The spells of a Stormherald relating
  directly to natural forces (such as call lightning and flame strike) deal double normal damage.
  High-level Talassan priests also know a secret 7th-level spell that can strip an inferior priest of
  all spell powers for a day, a threat used sparingly to enforce obedience.

### Dogma

- Talos is the dark side of nature, the uncaring and destructive force that waits to strike at any
  time.
- Life is a combination of random effects and chaos, so the devout should grab what they can, when
  they can.
- Preach the might of Talos endlessly to all, warning of the fury only he can command.
- Walk unafraid through storms, forest fires, earthquakes, and other disasters, for the power of Talos
  protects the faithful -- and let others see this, so unbelievers come to believe in his true power.
- Make all fear Talos by showing the destruction that he and his servants can cause.
- Pray to him energetically; only such observance protects against the furies of gales, hailstorms,
  winds, floods, droughts, blizzards, and hurricanes.
- Such destructive forces may be hurled at one's foes if Talos deems a place or person worth
  defending, but one cannot ignore or ever cease worshiping him.
- Proclaim Talos's message to all and show everyone the destruction even his lowliest servants can
  cause.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Travel the world spreading word of Talos's might, recruiting new worshipers either through fear or
  through their own love of wielding raw power.
- Indulge, as fatalistic examples to all, in acts of random or spiteful destruction while traveling,
  and make an example of any who stand up to them or try to bar their passage.
- Some priests pillage, burn, and steal as enthusiastically as any brigand; hamlets that successfully
  fight off Talassan clergy are often visited again a season later by a larger gathering intent on
  slaughtering everyone and laying the place waste.
- Talos does not mind priests indulging personal desires for wealth, food, luxury, and wanton
  behavior, so long as they call up a storm or commit some random, spectacular act of violence roughly
  once every tenday (toppling a tower is always effective).
- As a result, some clergy have taken up lives of brigandage -- posing as lunatics to spread Talos's
  word as ordered, while using disguises the rest of the time to scout rich prizes to strike at.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Calling Down the Thunder | Not calendar-fixed; performed as opportunity allows | The most sacred Talassan ritual: the slaying of an intelligent being by lightning, in return for a special boon from the Storm Lord -- usually a spell normally beyond the priest's ability, but sometimes a deed such as a storm sent against a chosen locale or foe. |
| The Fury | Not calendar-fixed | A berserk attack on people and property while howling Talos's name, bracketed by prayer at start and end. Considered most holy performed by a lone priest, though against tough opposition clergy attack together or use illusions to draw off defenders. |
| Annual festivals (Greengrass, Midsummer, and others) | Standard Faerûnian festival dates | Talassans mark all of Faerûn's annual festivals with rituals that call down lightning or call up storms. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No formal knightly or military orders -- many brigand bands, reavers, and raiders pay tribute to
  Talos and his priesthood, but none are organized enough to be called a true affiliated order.
- *(Informal)* Lords of the Tempest -- a rumored group of wizards specializing in exotic combinations
  of elemental magic, said to owe allegiance to Talos.
- *(Informal)* Circle of Rust and the Worm -- a cabal of crazed sages and mystic spellcasters of both
  religious and secular disciplines, intent on bringing about the end of the world, who have taken
  Talos as their patron.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Stormlord (rank titles as listed above apply equally to clerics and stormlords)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapons spear & javelin
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Astral, Chaos, Combat, Elemental, Healing, Necromantic, Summoning,
  Sun, War, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Divination, Protection, Time
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (weather)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon) and intimidation checks

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Immune to extremes of heat and cold; can never die of exposure even naked and wet, and can walk through natural fires unharmed (though clothing/equipment is damaged); still takes normal damage from cold- and fire-based magic and breath weapons |
| 1 | Immune to all forms of lightning and electrical damage |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Shocking Grasp (10 mana) |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Call Lightning (30 mana) *or* Ride the Wind (20 mana), choose one per use |
| 5 | Water Walk at will (no mana cost) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Control Winds (50 mana) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Lightning Bolt (30 mana); cannot be cast while wearing any metallic armor (including studded leather) |
| 10, 13, 16, 19 | Gains one additional daily casting of Lightning Bolt at each of these levels |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Control Weather (70 mana); may only shift conditions to a worse state, never an improved one |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Earthquake (7 mana -- 7th-level spell, 1/tenday = 10×7×(1/10)) |
