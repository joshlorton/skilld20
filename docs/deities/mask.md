# Mask (Master of All Thieves, Shadowlord, Lord of Shadows)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Mask
- **Titles/Epithets:** Master of All Thieves, Shadowlord, Lord of Shadows
- **Portfolio:** Thieves, thievery, shadows
- **Symbol:** A black velvet mask, tinged with red

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** spellcasters, rogues
- **Organization:** The priesthood is independent in each major city or region, so that action against
  one thieves' guild or temple cannot spill over and affect others. The clergy is roughly 30% clerics,
  40% specialty priests, and 30% thieves. Temples are often connected to a local thieves' guild by
  underground tunnels, or (in cities with competing guilds) sited underground as recognized neutral
  ground.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order): all clergy address each other as "Brother/Sister
  Shadow" regardless of rank.
  1. Unproven (novice)
  2. Proven Brother/Sister
  3. Veteran Brother/Sister
  4. Master Brother/Sister
  5. Lord/Lady Master (leader of a temple)

  Non-specialty-priest high priests carry the separate title Hidden One.

### Dogma

- All that occurs within shadow is Mask's purview.
- Ownership is nine-tenths of what is right -- if a person currently has something, it is theirs;
  previous ownership does not count.
- The day belongs to the quick, the smooth-tongued, and the light-fingered.
- Wealth rightfully belongs to those who can acquire it. Strive to end each day wealthier than you
  began it, but steal what is most vital, not everything to hand.
- Honesty is for fools, but apparent honesty is valuable. Make every untruth plausible, and prefer a
  misleading impression to an outright lie whenever you can.
- Subtlety is everything -- a bribe is the least subtle of the tools Mask gives. Manipulate rather than
  force, so the victim believes the decision and the deed were entirely their own.
- Never do the obvious, except to conceal a deeper deed; spin secrets atop secrets calmly, not with the
  sly excitement of untutored youth.
- Trust in the shadows, for the bright way makes for easy targets.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Maskarran tend to their plots, and provide support to (and collect tithes from) individual thieves
  and thieves' guilds.
- Each temple sets its own policy toward "rogue" thieves outside the faith: some aid them but covertly
  inform the local guild, some refuse aid and inform, others aid whoever pays and inform no one.
- Maskarran are among the wealthiest clergies in the Realms, second only to Waukeen's before the Time
  of Troubles -- but they spend their stolen wealth rather than hoard it, buying agents, bribing
  officials, and manipulating events from behind the scenes.
- Tithe/offering: at the monthly Ritual of the Unseen Presence, stolen coinage is melted down in
  altar-flanking braziers or crucibles as an offering. At daily Sunset Prayers, junior clergy proffer
  coins to senior clergy -- but the highest-ranking priest present gives their coin to the
  lowest-ranking priest present, in recognition of Mask's wry humor at making the high low and the low
  high.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ritual of the Unseen Presence | At least monthly, at every full temple | Chanted verse, hymns, and melted-coin offerings acknowledging Mask's constant, unseen regard for his faithful. Guarded by striking shadows spells cast by senior clergy to slay or drive off eavesdroppers. |
| Sunset Prayers | Daily | Lay worshipers and Maskarran kneel and proffer coins before the Altar of the Masked God; junior clergy give to senior clergy, except the highest-ranking priest present, who gives to the lowest-ranking. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No formal knightly orders.
- *(Informal)* Circle of the Gray Ribbon -- clergy who pull off a particularly daring heist or
  intricate manipulation are admitted by acclamation at a secret yearly meeting.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Demarch (male) / Demarchess (female)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons, plus knife, dagger, and hand crossbow; favored weapon longsword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light only (leather, padded leather, or studded leather) or Medium
  (elven chain mail); no shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Combat, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Protection, Sun, Time
- **Minor Spheres:** Elemental, Necromantic, Summoning, Thought, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations; additionally may use devices normally restricted to thieves
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in disguise
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon), forgery, and stealth; +1 bonus on linguistics
  (pick one language); +1 bonus on perception -- reading lips is a perception skill action

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

|---|---|
| 1 | Uses standard thief skill base scores (Dexterity/race/armor adjusted) with no initial discretionary points; gains 20 points to allocate per level thereafter (max 15 in a single skill); understands and uses thieves' cant |
