# Baervan Wildwanderer (The Masked Leaf, the Forest Gnome, Father of Fish and Fungus)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Baervan Wildwanderer
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Masked Leaf, the Forest Gnome, Father of Fish and Fungus
- **Portfolio:** Forests, travel, nature, forest gnomes
- **Symbol:** A raccoon's face

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including fighter/clerics, cleric/illusionists, and cleric/thieves) and
  specialty priests receive a bonus rank in religion (pantheon).
- **Organization:** Loosely organized around forest clearings rather than formal temples, preferably
  sylvan glades ringed by ancient oaks; long-standing shrines grow into woodland chapels as the
  circling trunks close together and their canopy forms a natural roof. Baervan is the patron of
  forest gnomes but is loved by all gnome subraces (clergy is 65% forest gnome, 33% rock gnome, 2%
  deep gnome; nearly evenly split between genders). Most clergy wander far afield, usually
  accompanied by a raccoon or giant raccoon companion. Full priests are collectively called
  Wildwanderers; the clergy breaks down as specialty priests (45%), cleric/thieves (35%), clerics
  (12%), cleric/illusionists (6%), and fighter/clerics (2%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Acorn (novice)
  2. Chipmunk
  3. Squirrel
  4. Opossum
  5. Hedgehog
  6. Marten
  7. Red Fox
  8. Lynx
  9. Wolf (highest common rank; individual high priests hold unique titles)

### Dogma

- The great forests await those Forgotten Folk daring enough to venture forth from their burrows;
  wander the woodlands in search of excitement and sylvan beauty.
- Befriend and protect the creatures of the forest.
- Care for and nurture the woodlands where you live.
- Be ever curious, and follow life wherever it may lead.
- Defend your community and yourself against the incursions of goblinkin and other brutish races.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Wander far afield, typically accompanied by a raccoon or giant raccoon companion.
- Work to protect nature and the gnomes who dwell in harmony with it.
- Actively drive off evil creatures, particularly spriggans.

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Wild Wayfarers -- a loosely organized band of rogues, priests, and cleric/thieves found
  in both city and sylvan settings, sharing a love of travel and adventure; many serve their home
  communities as far-ranging scouts who report on emerging threats.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Fastpaw
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, crossbow, dagger, hand/throwing axe, knife, sling, spear, short bow
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light (padded, leather, hide, or other nonmetallic armor); no shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Charm, Elemental (Earth, Water), Healing, Plant, Sun, Travelers, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Combat, Divination, Necromantic
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in spear, 1 rank in lore (nature), 1 rank in survival (trapping/set snares)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on herbalism; +1 bonus on survival
  (forest)
