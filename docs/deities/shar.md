# Shar (Mistress of the Night, the Lady of Loss, Nightsinger)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Shar
- **Titles/Epithets:** Mistress of the Night, the Lady of Loss, Nightsinger
- **Portfolio:** Dark, night, loss, forgetfulness, unrevealed secrets, caverns, dungeons, the Underdark
- **Symbol:** A black disk with a border of deep purple

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **Organization:** The church is composed largely of underground cells rather than an overt,
  uniformed body working from temples. Every full priest serves a direct superior, an overpriest
  responsible for a large area, and knows their "Own Secret" (the personal name Shar gave them and the
  dark deed they performed to win it). Cells are organized around small congregations led by a single
  priest; many priests operate in the same area but work independently, so that the failure of one
  cell does not doom the others. Clergy address each other as "Brother/Sister Night" and superiors as
  "Mother/Father Night."
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Adept of the Night (novice)
  2. Watcher (least senior ordained priest)
  3. Hand of Shar (battle-tested priest who leads priest-adventurers or oversees several cells)
  4. Darklord/Darklady (senior priest able to proclaim local policy)
  5. Nightseer (overseer of all faithful in a realm or other large area)
  6. Flame of Darkness (archpriest, or personally trusted servant of the goddess)

### Dogma

- Reveal secrets only to fellow faithful.
- Never follow hope, nor turn to promises of success.
- Quench the light of the moon (Selûne's faithful, holdings, deeds, and magic) whenever found; hide
  from it when it cannot be prevailed against.
- The dark is a time to act, not to wait.
- Do not hope; do not strive to better your lot in life or plan ahead, except in matters directly
  overseen by the clergy of the Dark Goddess.
- Consorting with actively serving worshipers of good deities is a sin, unless done to exploit them in
  business or to corrupt them into Shar's service.
- Never speak out against the clergy of Shar, nor interrupt their devotional dances for any reason.
- Prove your faith through obedience to the clergy and by carrying out at least one dark deed ordered
  by a priest each year -- or by bringing at least one being to worship the Dark Goddess.
- Lower clergy must obey superiors in all matters short of orders that would lead to their own death.
- Ensure some of the dark desires of worshipers are fulfilled, to win new followers and keep the
  faithful loyal.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Pursue practical, local goals to further the power of the priesthood and its worshipers, rather than
  openly opposing other faiths (save that of Selûne).
- Work covertly to bring down governments, particularly within cities, promoting general lawlessness
  and strife.
- Publicize Shar's patronage of avengers so the desperate and despairing turn to her for revenge
  rather than to the weakened demipower of vengeance, Hoar.
- Sponsor thieving guilds and hedonistic clubs of all sorts, and encourage political intrigue
  everywhere.
- Avoid sponsoring widespread war and slavery -- Shar wants to gain followers, not see their lives
  thrown away for no gain.
- Work through and promote shadowy cabals and secret organizations that appeal to human desires for
  exclusivity, secrecy, and mystery (over fifty false cults have arisen this way in the past two
  decades).

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rising of the Dark | Feast of the Moon (annual) | The Sharran name for the Feast of the Moon; the faithful gather under cover of the wider holiday to witness a blood sacrifice and learn the clergy's plots for the winter ahead. |
| Nightfall | Nightly | A brief invocation, a dance, a charge or instructions from the goddess, and a revel of eating, drinking, and dancing; lay worshipers must attend at least one (or dance to the goddess themselves) and report a small act of wickedness every tenday. |
| Coming of the Lady | Nightfall performed on moonless nights | Every congregation must additionally carry out some significant act of vengeance or wickedness in the Dark Lady's name. |
| Kiss of the Lady (Kissmoot) | Irregular, scheduled by the clergy of Old Night | The most important ceremony of the priesthood -- a horrific night-long revel of slaying and dark deeds that ends with a feast at dawn. |

### Affiliated Orders

- The church sponsors no fighting or knightly orders; crusaders are attached to particular Sharran
  cells and temples, not the faith in general.
- (Informal) Dark Justiciars -- a rumored honorary order or secret society for clergy who have killed
  a priest of Selûne.
- Many thieves' guilds have informal connections to Sharran cells, using each other for plots
  mercilessly.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Nightcloak (formerly called nightbringers)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons; favored weapon chakram
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Medium (up to and including chain mail); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Combat, Divination, Guardian, Necromantic, Protection, Sun
  (darkness-creating spells only), Time, Thought
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Elemental, Healing
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in performance (dancing)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on blind-fighting; +1 bonus
  on survival (direction sense is a survival skill action)

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first. Where no spell level is given but a class/character level is, use spell level =
that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Can see as well in natural or magical darkness as in light (does not grant true infravision or heat-sensing); +1 bonus to hit, damage, and saving throws while in darkness (even self-created; not cumulative with other darkness modifiers, but replaces them if less beneficial) |
| 1 | -1 penalty to hit, damage, and saving throws in the light of a full moon |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Darkness, gaining one additional daily use for every 2 levels beyond 1st (10 mana) |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Blindness (20 mana) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Continual Darkness, gaining one additional daily use for every 5 levels beyond 5th (30 mana) |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Forget (20 mana) |
| 9 | Foundation bonus: Nightmare (50 mana) |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Shadow Door (50 mana), Eyebite (60 mana), or Veil (60 mana), choose one per use |
