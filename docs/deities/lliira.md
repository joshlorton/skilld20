# Lliira (Our Lady of Joy, Joybringer, the Goddess of Joy, Mistress of the Revels)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Lliira
- **Titles/Epithets:** Our Lady of Joy, Joybringer, the Goddess of Joy, Mistress of the Revels
- **Portfolio:** Joy, happiness, dance, festivals, carefree celebration, contentment, release,
  hospitality, freedom/liberty; and, held in regency from the missing Waukeen: trade, money, wealth
- **Symbol:** Three six-pointed stars arranged in a triangle with their points touching -- orange
  uppermost, yellow at lower left, red at lower right (an older variant shows the three stars in a
  descending diagonal row; her altars and temple doors also bear a secret sigil of a fat log on a
  fire, accompanied by an illusory scent of cinnamon or roasting meat)

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** spellcasters, bardic performers.
- **Organization:** No organized hierarchy or chain of command exists between temples, though
  relations among clerics, specialty priests, spellsingers, and mystics are excellent. Each temple is
  led by a Master of the Revels, assisted by a High Prior, a Lorespeaker, a Seneschal, and a
  Quartermaster. The whole church defers informally to the Grand Rapturemother (currently Chlanna
  Asjros of the Palace of Holy Festivals in Selgaunt), who issues nonbinding policy as "Words of the
  Goddess" and oversees the church's considerable investments. Since the Time of Troubles, Lliira has
  held Waukeen's portfolio in trust as her missing friend's regent, and the church has absorbed many of
  Waukeen's former clergy and temples along with a new, mildly mercantile streak.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** No fixed ascending personal rank ladder -- clergy address each
  other informally as "Brother" or "Sister," call themselves "the True," and call novices and laity
  "the Tested" and nonbelievers "the Unseeing." Specialty priests are called joydancers; any member of
  the clergy may be addressed generically as a joybringer. Temple leadership titles, in ascending
  order of authority, are: Quartermaster/Seneschal/Lorespeaker/High Prior (roughly equal, supporting
  roles), Master of the Revels (temple head), and Grand Rapturemother/Rapturefather (sole head of the
  entire church).

### Dogma

- Believe in the ability, potential, and talent of the individual, and celebrate life and its
  diversity.
- Spread joy wherever and whenever possible; allow no one to be sad when mirth or comfort could be
  given to them.
- Strive to brighten the hearts and minds of all folk met, not merely friends and fellow believers.
- Exult in life, and find joy in all things; out of grief and despair, wrest joy, and join in the
  dance.
- Celebrate and honor deaths and the dead -- the best mourning is laughing remembrance.
- Seek joy always by working to bestow it on others.
- Hide no true feelings, but dance them out if it is not prudent to speak them aloud.
- Gather into celebrations the lost, the lonely, the exiled and outlaw, and the shunned, and even your
  foes: festivals are for all.
- Let folk follow their own desires, and respect their choices.
- Learn what folk find funny and what lightens their hearts, and in doing so come to know yourself and
  how best to serve Lliira and all intelligent beings.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Throw the best parties in Faerûn, perceiving and fulfilling what people actually need to be happy
  (conversation, companionship, or something to smash) without being told.
- Perform small heart-lifting deeds daily and keep up a steady stream of jokes so people stay open and
  unguarded around them.
- Explain to any who ask exactly how offerings are distributed among the needy.
- Their smoothest diplomats lobby rulers and wealthy merchants on how useful a happy, content populace
  is, soliciting steady financial support for the church.
- Manage and invest church donations shrewdly (a skill ex-Waukeenar clergy particularly excel at),
  funding good works and making the church one of the larger landholders in Faerûn.
- Tithe/offering: regular donations from wealthy patrons courted by clergy, plus steady small gifts
  from the poor and bequests from grateful peasants' wills.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Holy Festivals / Lesser Revels | Any major calendar holiday or local festival (essentially constant) | Opens with fanfare, songs, tumbling, and dancing, then a feast; what follows varies by occasion -- speeches, solemn ritual, comedy, romance, or friendly contests like tug-of-war, knock-the-knight-down, or pun duels. Offerings of food or wealth are "held up to the goddess," then buried, burned, or given to those unable to attend. |
| Swords Cast Down | Opens the most holy ceremonies | Two or more weapons are cast to the ground amid chanting and covered with fresh flowers (picked, or conjured by hired druids/mages in winter). |
| Personal Dance Prayer | Personal, whenever desired | The faithful pray alone, dancing in a meadow, garden, or other beautiful setting while whispering or singing to the goddess; a rainbow appearing during the prayer is a powerful good omen. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No knightly or military orders exist, "for obvious reasons."
- (Informal) Order of the White Violet -- an honorary order celebrating dance performed with great
  poignancy.
- (Informal) Order of the Leaping Stag -- an honorary order celebrating dance that skillfully evokes
  the nature of an animal.
- (Informal) The church maintains a fundamental tie to the Harpers, the secret organization working
  for freedom and good throughout Faerûn.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Joydancer
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Lasso & net (bludgeoning weapons only in extreme circumstances); favored
  weapon shuriken
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, medium & heavy armor; shield use unspecified in source
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Charm, Creation, Elemental, Healing, Necromantic, Protection, Sun,
  Travelers, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Plant, Summoning, Thought, Time
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in acrobatics (tumbling)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on performance (dancing); +1 bonus on
  juggling; +1 bonus on acrobatics (tightrope walking); +1 bonus on diplomacy
- **Other Clergy Grants:** The church's sole Grand Rapturemother/Rapturefather (currently Chlanna
  Asjros) can levitate and dance on air, or walk three feet off the ground, at will -- a personal
  grant tied to that single office, not to Joydancers generally.

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

All of a joydancer's granted abilities below are revoked if they use a damaging weapon other than net
or lasso, until they spend a full day in meditation and absolution followed by an all-night party.

|---|---|
