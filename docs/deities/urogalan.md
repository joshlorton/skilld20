# Urogalan (He Who Must Be, the Black Hound, Lord in the Earth, the Protector, the Shaper)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Urogalan
- **Titles/Epithets:** He Who Must Be, the Black Hound, Lord in the Earth, the Protector, the Shaper
- **Portfolio:** Earth, death, protection of the dead
- **Symbol:** A black dog's head silhouette

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** A small priesthood, propitiated by many halflings but joined by few, as symbols
  of the Black Hound are considered unlucky by most of the Small Folk despite the priesthood's
  respected services. Temples are shallow basins open to the sky, natural caves, and halfling-dug
  catacombs, floored in soft dirt around a limestone altar; more often a "temple" is little more
  than a single-priest shrine tended from a nearby community. Clergy is 25% hairfeet, 65% stouts,
  10% tallfellows; nearly evenly divided between specialty priests (54%) and clerics (46%), and
  between men (55%) and women (45%). The priesthood is informally split into two overlapping
  orders: the Wardens of the Dead (protection of gravesites and safe passage of spirits) and the
  Children of the Earth (honoring the earth and defending against dangers from below).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Earthling (novice)
  2. Earth Embracer
  3. Soil Digger
  4. Clay Potter
  5. Diarun Smith
  6. Grave Guardian
  7. Crypt Sentinel
  8. Vault Marshal
  9. Barrow Warden
  10. Black Hound (full priest, "Vassal of the Black Hound")

  High-ranking priests also carry unique individual titles. Specialty priests are called
  grimwardens.

### Dogma

- Earth is the giver and the receiver of life, providing shelter, food, and wealth to those whose
  toes embrace it.
- The sacred soil is to be revered as the mantle of Those Who Have Been and the shelter of Those Who
  Will Be.
- Death is to be embraced as the natural end of life, and in doing so gives honor to life.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Preside over internment of the dead and caretaking of graves; administer last rites, preside over
  burials, and memorialize the fallen.
- Maintain much of the history of the Small Folk, keeping genealogies and deeds of those who have
  "gone to the fields of green."
- Safeguard sacred tokens of the deceased used in ancestor-worship rituals, where practiced.
- Consecrate the foundations or first diggings of buildings and new burrow complexes.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Earthrisings | Nights of the full moon | Priests, followers, and halflings who lost a loved one within the past month gather in natural earthen basins to propitiate He Who Must Be, offering uncut gems, diarun, and clay statuettes on a central flat stone while singing dirges and chanting elegies to the pounding of bare feet. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Wardens of the Dead -- protect halfling gravesites and ensure the peaceful transition
  of spirits to the afterlife.
- *(Informal)* Children of the Earth -- honor the ground from which halflings draw their livelihood
  and defend against dangers emerging from below.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Grimwarden
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All weapons; favored weapon flail (double-headed flail)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Combat, Divination, Elemental (Earth), Guardian, Healing, Necromantic,
  Protection, Summoning, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Law, Sun, Time
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in flail, 1 rank in lore (ancient history), 1 rank in craft
  (stonemasonry)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on craft (gem cutting); +1 bonus on
  lore (undead); +1 bonus on lore (the planes); +1 bonus on craft (pottery)
