# Gwaeron Windstrom (Master of Woodland Signs, Master of Tracking, Mouth of Mielikki)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Gwaeron Windstrom
- **Titles/Epithets:** Master of Woodland Signs, Master of Tracking, Mouth of Mielikki
- **Portfolio:** Tracking, interpreting woodland signs, rangers of the North
- **Symbol:** A dark red "S" overlaid with a small, five-pointed white star on its top half and a large,
  four-clawed animal print in brown on its bottom half

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (rangers)
- **All Clergy:** All rangers of Mielikki, including those specifically sworn to Gwaeron's service,
  receive religion (Faerûnian) as a bonus proficiency.
- **Organization:** The source is explicit that "there is not an organized Gwaeronan faith distinct from
  the church of Mielikki." Gwaeron has no independent priesthood, temples, or hierarchy of his own --
  he is a demipower who serves Mielikki directly (alongside Lurue and Shiallia) and is venerated by
  rangers and trackers of the North who interpret woodland signs and hunt outlaws or game. His actual
  following consists entirely of the Fellowship of Stalkers of the Silent Path: single-classed human and
  half-elven rangers who are already full members of Mielikki's clergy (specifically her Needles branch;
  see `mielikki.md` for that church's real hierarchy, ranks, and holy days) and who have additionally
  sworn personal service to Gwaeron as their patron within that role. Shrines and temples of Mielikki in
  the North -- such as the one in Mornbryn's Shield -- often include a small side altar to Gwaeron; where
  he receives a standalone shrine, it is typically a single boulder inscribed with his symbol, found
  mainly in the High Forest and Cormanthor.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy:** None of Gwaeron's own -- Stalkers use the ordinary rank titles of
  Mielikki's Needles branch (see `mielikki.md`), not a separate Gwaeronan hierarchy.

### Dogma

- Gwaeron's teachings mirror Mielikki's, whom he serves: intelligent beings can live in harmony with the
  wild without requiring the destruction of one in the name of the other.
- Embrace the wild and do not fear it -- the wild ways are the good ways. Keep the balance and learn the
  hidden ways of all life.
- Do not allow trees to be needlessly felled or the forest to be burned; live in the forest and be a part
  of it, not in endless battle against it.
- Protect forest life and strive to keep the balance that indiscriminate fire-users, woodcutters, and
  hunters break; live in harmony with the woods and teach others to do so.
- Punish and frustrate those who hunt for sport rather than food and who practice cruelty on wild
  creatures.
- Take the role of protector seriously -- keep in check the numbers of malicious wild creatures and
  humanoids who would distort the Balance just as thoughtless, careless civilization does.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Walk the trails and wilderness of the North practicing tracking, becoming attuned to the environment,
  and observing the activity of the "monster" races.
- Track down fugitives from justice, elusive predators (human and beast alike), or lost travelers when
  called upon by the church of Mielikki or hired by local rulers.
- Join militias, mercenary companies, or adventuring companies, typically serving as scouts.
- In times of famine, travel to regions where game is scarce and use their tracking and hunting skills to
  provide food for those in danger of starving.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| (None independent of Mielikki) | -- | Gwaeron has no holy days of his own; his followers observe the holy days of Mielikki's church (see `mielikki.md`). The one Gwaeron-specific practice is personal rather than calendar-based: after a successful stalk, a Stalker of the Silent Path (or anyone invoking Gwaeron's name) leaves a circle of six right-foot prints in the ground, heels nearly touching, toes facing outward. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Fellowship of Stalkers of the Silent Path -- composed exclusively of single-classed human
  and half-elven rangers of Mielikki's clergy who have sworn personal service to Gwaeron. They remain
  full members of Mielikki's regular church hierarchy while also serving as her faith's tracking,
  scouting, and hunting arm, much as Gwaeron himself serves Mielikki.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Stalker of the Silent Path (Ranger)
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapon greatsword (two-handed sword) per real-world 3.5e
  sourcing, matching Gwaeron's own avatar weapon Flameheart, a two-handed sword +1, flame tongue, in
  which he is specialized
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); ranger special abilities suffer
  penalties in armor heavier than studded leather (i.e. above Light)
- **Major Spheres:** Not applicable -- Stalkers follow the standard Ranger class from the Player's
  Handbook rather than a priest sphere list, identical in this respect to Mielikki's own Needles branch
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Divination, Plant, Travelers
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as rangers)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in survival (woodland)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); pick three: lore (nature), herbalism, survival
  (hunting), survival (trapping/set snares), linguistics (elvish), linguistics (gnome), linguistics
  (halfling)

As rangers of Mielikki's Needles branch, Stalkers also retain the at-will analyze balance ability
documented under Mielikki's own Rangers track (see `mielikki.md`); it is not restated here since it is
not unique to Gwaeron.

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

## Gwaeronan Spells (deity-specific)

All clergy of Gwaeron also have access to the applicable religion-specific spells of Mielikki (see
`mielikki.md`).

| Name | Est. Mana | Foundation | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Natural Attunement | 20 | Animal, Plant | Attunes the recipient fully to their surroundings, granting bonuses to surprise, initiative, and saving throws; grants tracking ability to the untrained, removes the usual penalty for non-rangers who already track, and lets rangers track unusual trails (flying, teleporting, or otherwise "impossible" quarry) at a penalty, with a second chance to recover a lost trail. |
