# Beshaba (Maid of Misfortune, Black Bess, Lady Doom, Maid of Misrule, Tyche's Unpleasant Daughter)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Beshaba
- **Titles/Epithets:** Maid of Misfortune, Black Bess, Lady Doom, Maid of Misrule, Tyche's Unpleasant
  Daughter
- **Portfolio:** Random mischief, misfortune, bad luck, accidents
- **Symbol:** A rack of sharp-pointed black antlers on a red triangular field

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **Organization:** the priesthood splits roughly in half between clerics and specialty priests
  (doommasters), with only a smattering of mystics, most of whom dwell apart in remote regions. The
  faith is led entirely by priestesses locked in an endless, vicious struggle for personal supremacy;
  male clergy tend to be underpriests or serve as the professional assassins of the Black Fingers,
  while nearly all high-ranking clergy are female. Shrines are usually undecorated but for a
  discreet symbol or rack of horns painted red; doommasters favor underground facilities with a
  gothic flavor, whether excavated or converted from natural caverns.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Bewildered (novices)
  2. the Unfortunate (full priests)
  3. Finger of Fear
  4. Hand of Strife
  5. Higher Hand of Strife
  6. Hand of Gloom
  7. Higher Hand of Gloom
  8. Hand of Horror
  9. Higher Hand of Horror
  10. Hand of Despair
  11. Higher Hand of Despair
  12. Mistress/Master of Dread
  13. Nails of the Lady (head of a major temple)

### Dogma

- Bad things happen to everyone; only by following Beshaba may a person be spared the worst of her
  effects.
- Too much good luck is a bad thing -- to even things out, the wise should plan to undermine the
  fortunate.
- Whatever happens, it can only get worse.
- Novices are charged simply to fear Beshaba and revere her.
- All clergy must spread word across Faerûn to obey Beshaba and make offerings to appease her, lest
  all taste firsthand the curse "Beshaba provides" -- misfortune, in generous supply.
- Make others worship her, and in turn they will be spared the ill luck she can bring.
- Never falsely advise anyone in how to worship Beshaba, on pain of being cast out and cursed with
  misfortune for the rest of their days.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Spread fear of the goddess by starting talk of Beshaba's power and her latest wickedness.
- Instruct commonfolk in how to make offerings to appease her, or how to join her clergy if they
  would rather be protected against misfortune entirely.
- Indulge personal tastes for random cruelty and sadism along the way.
- Manipulate simpler folk mysteriously into service -- from food, shelter, and companionship to
  weapons to use against rivals within Beshaba's own church and against the clergy of other faiths.
- Tithe/offering: an offering of something valuable is held in flame until at least partially
  consumed while Beshaba's name is called out, followed by a kneeling prayer of praise and entreaty;
  she is said to look more kindly on those who let themselves burn their fingers somewhat in the
  process. Priests additionally make a daily offering by setting fire to brandy, wine, or spirits
  while dipping a black antler tine into the mixture and uttering her name, followed by prayer.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Fire Offering | Daily | Priests set fire to brandy, wine, or spirits while dipping a black antler tine into the mixture and speaking Beshaba's name, followed by prayer. |
| Nightly Prayer | Every night, outside under a dark sky | A personal prayer for guidance; the goddess often answers with nightmare visions later that night. If a priest is forcibly confined, at least an attempt at this prayer is still expected. |
| Midsummer/Shieldmeet Revels | Midsummer and Shieldmeet (annual) | Devotees mark Beshaba's nature as Maid of Misrule with wild revels of destruction and deliberate rudeness. |
| The Passing | Upon the death of a member of the clergy | A rare moment of dignity: the body is floated down a river amid candles in a rite meant to raise it as undead and teleport it to a random location in the Realms to wreak sudden havoc. Senior clergy scry from afar to witness the resulting damage. |
| The Marking | Upon a priestess's ascension in rank | Drum music, dancing over flames, and the permanent branding or tattooing of the priest being promoted, who must bear the pain without the benefit of spell or potion. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Black Fingers -- a secret society of assassins comprised of male members of
  Beshaba's clergy alongside evil thieves and fighters.
- No formal knightly or military orders are mentioned.
