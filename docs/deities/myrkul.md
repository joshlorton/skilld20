# Myrkul (Lord of Bones, Old Lord Skull, the Reaper, Lord of the Dead)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Myrkul
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lord of Bones, Old Lord Skull, the Reaper, Lord of the Dead
- **Portfolio:** The dead, wasting, decay, corruption, parasites, old age, exhaustion, dusk, autumn
- **Symbol:** A white human skull face-on against a black field, or a reaching white skeletal hand in
  white on a black field (in recent years, either symbol usually shown on an inverted black shield
  bordered continuously with white, stylized human finger bones)

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** spellcasters
- **Organization:** A small, unpopular faith of morbid loners who mostly worked alone and concealed
  their identities, though relations between its branches were unusually good since no single leader
  held enough power to move against the others. Myrkulytes addressed one another as "Death" or "Most
  Holy Death" (the latter for senior/elderly priests), prefixed before a name or title (e.g. "Death
  Williamson," "Most Holy Death Trollslayer"). Temples were massive mausoleums built above subterranean
  necropolises, wreathed in smoke from ever-burning crematoriums and patrolled by undead guardians. The
  faith is now defunct: Myrkul's portfolio passed first to Cyric and then to Kelemvor, and most surviving
  Myrkulyte clergy have shifted allegiance to one or the other.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Daring One (novice; addressed as "Daring Death [name]")
  2. Night Walker
  3. Bone Talker
  4. Shroud Wearer
  5. Crypt Carver
  6. Bone Dancer (leads the bone dance ritual, a variant animate dead used to conjure guardian undead)
  7. Ritual Consecrator (dedicates altars, scythes, and magical-item materials to the god's service)
  8. Undead Master
  9. Withering Lord (taught wither, a prayer duplicating a staff of withering)
  10. Deathbringer (gained quench the spirit, a priestly finger of death)
  11. Elder Doom (title used by all clergy who ascend in rank and influence beyond rule over a single temple)

### Dogma

- Make folk fear and respect death and the power of almighty Myrkul, so that none stand against the
  church or try to thwart its activities.
- Spread the word that touching a priest of Myrkul brings death.
- Teach that those in Myrkul's service have perfect patience and can be trusted utterly -- and conduct
  themselves accordingly.
- Teach the tales of past and future "doombringers" -- mortals who avenge dead friends, masters, and
  blood kin, and who slay those who scoff at or hold other gods supreme over the Lord of Bones.
- Know Myrkul and fear him: his embrace is for all and is patient but sure; the dead can always find
  you; his hand is everywhere -- there is no door he cannot pass, nor guardian who can withstand him.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Bury the dead and conduct funerals for fees.
- Serve as hirelings for the disposal of plague victims and dealings with infected lycanthropes and the
  diseased, aided by the clergy's resistance to disease.
- Comfort the dying while treating death as natural and inevitable, never something to be run from.
- Assist dying folk in drafting decrees, wills, and cryptic verses to guide the living or point them
  toward hidden treasure -- "the sacred hand that reaches from the grave."
- Accept a "skull fee" (arranged with the dying before death, never with a living intermediary) to act
  as an agent or avenger for the deceased, carrying out tasks they could not complete in life.
- Rarely resurrect the dead directly; instead it was commonplace and acceptable for a Myrkulyte to
  escort a body to another faith's temple for resurrection, funded with money the deceased had left for
  that purpose.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Dusking | Daily, at dusk (personal prayer any time during darkness) | A ritual of bones, cremated ashes, and grave dust centered on a floating, magically glowing skull above a black, bone-decorated altar; offerings from the non-devout are accepted; tolling bells mark the ritual's opening, its close, and each offering made. |
| Feast of the Moon (the Day the Dead Are Most With Us) | Annual | The faith's only calendar-fixed ritual. The dead's essences were believed to rise as unseen ghosts seeking descendants to warn or guide (never by speech). Celebrated in chant, prayer, and hymns, culminating at midnight in the Flagons of the Fallen, where wine is set alight by magic so visiting spirits could be warmed briefly in their "eternal chill." |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Knights of the Undying Dragon -- an ancient order of undead crusaders serving as Myrkul's
  swordarm: 12 death knight commanders (mounted on nightmares), each commanding a company of 12 skeletal
  warriors, each of which commands a platoon of 12 night riders (mounted on gaunts). Based in the
  dungeons of the long-vanished Castle of Al'hanar in the Eastern Shaar; emerges roughly once a century
  to battle the dracolich Everlasting Wyrm and its spawn. With Myrkul's death, the order has not yet
  decided whether to pledge to Cyric, to Kelemvor, or to go to war until every member is finally laid to
  rest as tribute to their fallen patron.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Gray One (informally "fingerbone")
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All simple weapons, scimitars, and scythes; favored weapon scythe
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Heavy (any armor), plus shield
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Chaos, Combat, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic, Summoning, Time
- **Minor Spheres:** Charm, Divination, Elemental, War
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (astrology)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on intimidation

### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: **mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day.** Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first, e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2. Where no spell level is
given but a class level is (e.g. "at 15th level..."), spell level = class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Gray ones command and destroy triple the normal number of zombies and skeletons |
| 1 | Unaffected by the debilitating game effects of disease or parasites (not immune -- a fatal disease still runs its course and can still kill, but imposes no penalties until then) |
| 1 | +1 bonus on saving throws vs. death magic (does not apply when using or defending against the Hand of Myrkul) |
| 1 | Necrology: ability check vs. Wisdom to identify a specific undead creature and, with a further check, recall its weaknesses and immunities |
| 1 | Netherworld Knowledge: ability check vs. Wisdom-3 to know the cosmology of the Outer Planes and identify extraplanar creatures and their weaknesses/immunities |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Feign Death (30 mana -- 3rd-level spell, 1/day = level×10) |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Animate Dead (30 mana -- 3rd-level spell, 1/day = level×10) *or* Corrupt (40 mana -- 4th-level spell, 1/day = level×10), choose one per use |
| 9 | Foundation bonus: Wither (50 mana -- 5th-level spell, 1/day = level×10) |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Summon Minor Death (5 mana -- spell level 5, from class level 10÷2, 1/tenday = level×1) |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Hand of Myrkul (8 mana -- spell level 8, from class level 15÷2 rounded up, 1/tenday = level×1). A six-round window to land a touch attack forcing a death-magic save on the target or the gray one suffers the same fate; the power is extinguished for a tenday after any use |
