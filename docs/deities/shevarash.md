# Shevarash (the Black Archer, the Night Hunter, the Arrow Bringer)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Shevarash
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Black Archer, the Night Hunter, the Arrow Bringer
- **Portfolio:** Hatred of the drow, vengeance, military crusades, loss, revenge
- **Symbol:** A broken arrow above a teardrop; the priesthood's physical holy symbol is a broken arrowshaft dipped in drow blood and blessed by a priest of Shevarash

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All crusaders and specialty priests (but not rangers) receive religion (pantheon) and literacy (Espruar) as bonus skills.
- **Organization:** A little-known cult, even among the Fair Folk, except in communities where drow raids are common. Elves pity those who join out of grief more than they understand the all-consuming hatred that drives the faith, and many elven theologians doubt the spirits of fallen Shevarashan priests can ascend to Arvanaith at all. Almost unknown among other surface races, but deep-dwelling dwarves, deep gnomes, and myconids give succor to Shevarash's followers and appreciate their tireless crusade against the drow and other evil Underdark powers. Temples double as nigh-impregnable forts at cave mouths connecting elven forests to the Underdark, built to withstand long sieges, walls adorned only with trophies seized from fallen drow; makeshift shrines are sometimes raised in the Underdark itself. The clergy is green elves (33%), moon elves (32%), gold elves (29%), and half-elves of those subraces (6%); evenly divided among specialty priests (34%), crusaders (33%), and rangers (33%); with equal numbers of male and female members.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Haunted (novice)
  2. Dark Avenger (full priest; further advancement is marked by unique individual titles recounting each priest's tally of slain foes, rather than a fixed rank ladder)

  Specialty priests are known as dhaeraowathila ("drowbane").

### Dogma

- The greatest enemy of the Seldarine is Lolth, who sought the corruption of Arvandor and the overthrow of the Creator.
- The greatest enemy of the Fair Folk is the drow, the debased followers of the Spider Queen long ago enmeshed in her dark web.
- Redemption and revenge may be achieved only through the utter destruction of the drow and the dark powers they serve.
- Only then may the joy of life begin anew. Hunt fearlessly!

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Drill, design tactics for Underdark warfare, and guard known entrances to the Underdark.
- Participate in hit-and-run raids and major assaults against drow-held territory.
- Join adventuring bands exploring the Underdark, since the cult is small and every additional sword against the drow is welcomed.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Midwinter Night | Midwinter (annual) | Observed in memory of the Dark Court Slaughter; those joining the clergy are inducted this night, shouting vows of unceasing vengeance into the night and swearing (as Shevarash himself once did) never to laugh or smile again until the Spider Queen and the other dark drow gods are slain and their followers destroyed. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No formal or informal affiliated orders -- the church is itself a military cult, and its scattered fighting bands adopt their own monikers (as any military unit would) rather than operate as a distinct suborganization.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Dhaeraowathila
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapons longbow & broadsword
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Chaos, Combat, Elemental (Earth), Guardian, Healing, Necromantic, Protection, Summoning, Sun, Travelers, War
- **Minor Spheres:** Animal, Charm, Creation, Divination
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (drow history); 1 rank in linguistics (Undercommon)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on blind-fighting; +1 bonus on survival (tracking -- tracking is a survival skill action); +1 bonus on linguistics (drow silent speech)
