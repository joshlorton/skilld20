# Khalreshaar

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Khalreshaar
- **Note:** Not an independent deity in this sourcebook -- a minor-power/alias stub only. Khalreshaar
  (Kal-REH-shay-are) is the name by which Mielikki is known and worshiped on Evermeet, the Green Isle.
  In this aspect she is said to serve Rillifane Rallathil rather than Silvanus, delivering messages and
  running errands for the Leaflord when speed is essential. Since the Time of Troubles, a growing cult
  (composed primarily of half-elves) has begun venerating Khalreshaar as the first truly half-elven
  power, on the strength of myths claiming Mielikki is the daughter of Silvanus and Hanali Celanil --
  a claim that dismays many full-blooded elves. The source text directs readers to the entry for
  Mielikki in *Faiths & Avatars* for full church, priesthood, and spell details (see
  [mielikki.md](mielikki.md)); no separate Khalreshaar-specific church, priesthood, or spell material is
  given in this book.
