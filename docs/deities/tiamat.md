# Tiamat (the Dragon Queen, the Chromatic Dragon, Nemesis of the Gods, the Dark Lady, Queen of Chaos, the Undying Queen, Bane of Bahamut, the Avaricious)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Tiamat
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Dragon Queen, the Chromatic Dragon, Nemesis of the Gods, the Dark Lady, Queen of Chaos, the Undying Queen, Bane of Bahamut, the Avaricious
- **Portfolio:** Evil dragons, chromatic dragons, evil reptiles, greed, Chessenta
- **Symbol:** A five-headed dragon (Tiamat), or a red dragon against a black mountain (in her Tchazzar aspect)

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders)
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, specialty priests, and crusaders of Tiamat receive religion (Untheric), religion
  (Faerûnian), literacy (Untheric), and linguistics (Common) as bonus proficiencies. Those of Untheric
  descent additionally know Untheric as a native tongue.
- **Organization:** Clergy is split evenly between specialty priests and clerics (about 40% each), with the
  remaining 20% crusaders. Specialty priests come in two distinct types: wyrmkeepers, found throughout
  Faerûn (especially Unther), and wyrmlairds, found only in Chessenta and dedicated to Tiamat's Tchazzar
  aspect specifically (many lower-ranking wyrmlairds don't even realize Tchazzar has been subsumed by the
  Dragon Queen). Temples are built in the former lairs of long-dead dragons -- vast natural caverns
  dominated by frescoes of Tiamat and heaped with wealth to be sacrificed to her, guarded by traps and at
  least one resident juvenile or young adult chromatic dragon. The most prominent temples contain a
  permanent gate to Avernus.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Wyrmfodder (acolyte)
  2. Custodian of the Copper Chalice
  3. Defender of the Silver Shield
  4. Warden of the Electrum Mail
  5. Guardian of the Gold Scepter
  6. Keeper of the Platinum Crown
  7. Scale of the White Wyrm
  8. Horn of the Black Beast
  9. Wing of the Green Gargantua
  10. Talon of the Blue Baatoran
  11. Breath of the Red Ravager

  Higher-ranking priests are collectively known as the Dark Scaly Ones.

### Dogma

- Rival powers of every creed and pantheon are inherently tyrannical, seeking only power behind honeyed
  words -- Tiamat alone is strong enough to defy the gods and overthrow their rule.
- Work tirelessly toward the day the Dragon Queen banishes the gods from Faerûn and unites the Realms
  under her.
- Follow her commands unquestioningly, and be willing to sacrifice yourself in her service.
- Power is acquired through the accumulation of wealth and magic, and power demands respect.
- Chromatic dragons everywhere are Tiamat's spawn and are to be venerated and paid homage.
- When Tiamat claims her throne, her draconic children shall rule as dukes and her clergy shall serve as
  their mortal vassals.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Acquire an ever-growing hoard of wealth for the faith through theft, assassination, vandalism, and arson.
- Sabotage the faiths and works of other powers.
- In Unther and Chessenta, seize as much temporal power as possible; in western Faerûn, infiltrate and
  subvert the Cult of the Dragon.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Tithing | Daily | Each priest offers a small tithe (a few coins or a gem) hidden in cupped hands with a prayer; a 10% chance the offering vanishes when unfurled, taken as a sign of especial blessing. |
| The Rite of Respect | As needed, whenever approaching any spawn of Tiamat | A ritual of abasement and appeasement; performing it grants no mechanical protection, but failing to perform it flawlessly is sure to draw the dragon's ire. |
| Festival of Vengeance | First full moon after Midsummer (Unther only) | Marks Tiamat's defeat of Gilgeam; Unthalass descends into sanctioned anarchy, rioting, and the settling of old scores, while rival clergies scramble to contain the destruction. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Serpent Guards -- warriors and crusaders who guard the cult's temples, hunt down and kill those
  who probe too deeply into its affairs, and wage an ongoing campaign of assassination and theft against
  individuals and churches seeking to restore order to Unther.
- (Formal) Knights of the Five-Thorned Rose -- an elite order of high-ranking crusaders drawn from the
  Serpent Guards, dedicated to destroying metallic dragons (the spawn of Bahamut); maintains extensive
  dragon-lore libraries and plans centuries-long hunts against individual good-aligned wyrms.

## Specialty Priests

Tiamat has two specialty priest types: wyrmkeepers (found throughout Faerûn) and wyrmlairds (found only
in Chessenta, dedicated to her Tchazzar aspect).

### Wyrmkeepers

Prior to the Time of Troubles, only humans of Untheric descent could become wyrmkeepers; since the
Godswar, Tiamat accepts humans of any lineage.

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons, long sword; favored weapon heavy
  pick (Dragon Head) -- not named explicitly in this source, but confirmed via real-world 3.5e sourcing;
  long sword remains explicitly permitted alongside it
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Charm, Combat, Divination, Guardian, Law, Necromantic, Protection,
  Time, Wards, Weather
- **Minor Spheres:** Elemental, Healing, Summoning
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (Untheric history), 1 rank in literacy (Auld Wyrmish)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on handle animal (dragon riding); +1 bonus
  on linguistics (pick one chromatic draconic tongue)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day

given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Cause Fear (reversed form of the 1st-level priest spell Remove Fear, 10 mana), once per day |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Feather Fall (10 mana), once per day |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Fear (as the 4th-level wizard spell, 40 mana) *or* Fly (as the 3rd-level wizard spell, 30 mana), choose one per use, once per day |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Dragon Scales (40 mana), once per day |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Fire Breath (10 mana), four times per day |
| 13 | Choose immunity to one damage type: fire, cold, acid, lightning, or poison (including poisonous gas) |
| 16 | Can summon a young adult dragon (species chosen by Tiamat, typically local) to serve as a mount, no more than once per decade and only if unserved by a wyrm for the past three years; the dragon is free-willed and must be placated and bargained with, not commanded |

### Wyrmlairds (Specialty Priests of Tchazzar)

Only humans of Chessentan descent can become wyrmlairds.

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** All bludgeoning (wholly Type B) weapons, long sword, two-handed sword;
  favored weapon two-handed sword (explicitly worn and wielded by Tchazzar's priests per their vestments)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Charm, Combat, Creation, Divination, Guardian, Healing, Necromantic,
  Protection, Time, Travelers, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Elemental, Summoning
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (Wars of the City-States of Chessenta)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on handle animal (dragon riding); +1 bonus
  on lore (pick one Chessentan city-state's local history); +1 bonus on literacy (Auld Wyrmish)

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | +4 bonus on saving throws vs. fire or magical fire of any kind |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Hold Person (as the 2nd-level priest spell, 20 mana), once per day |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Suggestion (as the 3rd-level wizard spell, 30 mana), once per day |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Fire Breath (10 mana), four times per day |
| 21 | Can summon a young adult red dragon to serve as a mount, same conditions as wyrmkeepers (once per decade, only if unserved for three years; free-willed, not controlled) |
