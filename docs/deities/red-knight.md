# Red Knight (Lady of Strategy, Grandmaster of the Lanceboard)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Red Knight
- **Titles/Epithets:** Lady of Strategy, Grandmaster of the Lanceboard
- **Portfolio:** Strategy and planning
- **Symbol:** A red knight chess piece with stars for eyes

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders), Monastic orders (monks)
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, crusaders, monks, and specialty priests of the Red Knight receive
  religion (pantheon) as a bonus skill.
- **Organization:** Only in recent memory has the Red Knight emerged as a demipower distinct from
  Tempus; most folk still view her as a follower or aspect of the Lord of Battles, and she is venerated
  by Tempus's followers in a secondary position to him. A small priesthood distinct from the Foehammer's
  own has arisen only since the Time of Troubles, grudgingly tolerated by Tempurans and apparently
  encouraged by Tempus himself, perhaps as a counterbalance to his enemy Garagos the Reaver. Villagers
  and city dwellers see the Red Knight as an ally of civilization, letting small numbers of brave
  defenders triumph through superior intelligence and foresight against overwhelming hordes. She has
  but a few shrines, nearly all housed within temples of Tempus; her chapels are dominated by chess
  imagery, with floors inlaid as a lanceboard (chessboard) of black-and-white or red-and-white marble,
  guarded by stone guardians carved in the shapes of chess pieces. Her clergy, the Red Fellowship, grew
  out of a Tempuran monastic order that specialized in planning and strategy, and has only organized
  into a distinct faith since the Time of Troubles, most active since the crusade against the Tuigan
  Horde (1361 DR). The priesthood is evenly divided among clerics, crusaders, monks, and specialty
  priests (holy strategists), and is organized under a strict rank hierarchy; higher-ranking priests
  precede their title with their numeric rank within the clergy (e.g. the eighth-highest-ranking priest
  is the "Eighth Lord Knight of the Red Standard"), though this practice may fade as the clergy grows.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Page
  2. Squire
  3. Knight
  4. Knight Quartermaster
  5. Knight Commander
  6. Knight Captain
  7. Lord Knight
  8. Lord Knight Commandant
  9. Lord Knight of the Red Standard

### Dogma

- War is won by those with the best planning, strategy, and tactics, regardless of the apparent odds;
  any fool can snatch defeat from the jaws of victory with fortune's aid, but only a master strategist
  can ensure a lasting victory.
- War is a series of battles -- losing one does not mean the war itself is lost.
- Seek out your opponent's weaknesses and recognize your own; avoid their strengths and play to yours,
  focusing your own strengths on their vulnerabilities.
- In times of war, prepare for peace; in times of peace, prepare for war.
- Seek out your enemy's enemies as allies, and be prepared to compromise.
- Life is an endless series of skirmishes punctuated by occasional outbreaks of war -- be ready, and
  always have a contingency plan.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve in armies throughout the Realms, often as high-ranking commanders of elite squads.
- Teach at war colleges across the Realms as respected instructors in strategy and tactics.
- Serve as quartermasters skilled at securing supplies and maintaining supply lines through hostile
  territory.
- Author treatises on military strategy.
- Off duty, hone the mind through abstract games of skill (avoiding games of pure chance), sharpening
  the ability to plan, develop parallel lines of thought, and read an opponent's intentions.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Retreat | Midwinter's day (annual) | A solemn day-long retrospective on the previous year's campaigns; strategies are discussed, battles analyzed, and the accumulated lore folded into the church's teachings. |
| The Queen's Gambit | First day of Tarsakh | A day of feasting and gamesmanship, with day-long tournaments of chess and go; victors receive recognition, titles of merit, promotions, and sometimes a gift from the temple armory. |

*(The clergy of the Red Knight also observe the holy days and ceremonies of the church of Tempus.)*

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Formal)* Order of the Red Falcon -- a small fellowship of crusaders, warriors, and a few paladins
  serving the Red Knight and High Lady Bloodhawk from the Citadel of Strategic Militancy. Many serve as
  instructors at the newly founded Red War College or train officers in armies throughout the Realms;
  on rare occasions the order is summoned back to the Citadel and led into combat by Lady Bloodhawk
  herself.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Holy Strategist
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; favored weapon longsword ("Checkmate" per real-world 3.5e sourcing)
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light, Medium, and Heavy (any armor); plate mail, field plate, or
  full plate preferred
- **Major Spheres:** All, Charm, Combat, Divination, Healing, Law, Protection, Travelers, War
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Guardian, Necromantic, Thought, Wards
- **Magical Items:** No limitations (same as clerics)
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in etiquette, 1 rank in heraldry, 1 rank in handle animal (horse)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on craft (engineering); +1 bonus on
  gaming; +1 bonus on survival (hunting)
