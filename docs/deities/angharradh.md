# Angharradh (the Triune Goddess, the One and the Three, the Union of the Three, Queen of Arvandor)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Angharradh
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Triune Goddess, the One and the Three, the Union of the Three, Queen of
  Arvandor
- **Portfolio:** Spring, fertility, planting, birth, defense, wisdom
- **Symbol:** Three interconnecting circles laid out in a triangle that points down

## Church Summary

Angharradh is both a separate goddess (the One) and the fused aspect of three other Seldarine
goddesses (the Three): Aerdrie Faenya, Hanali Celanil, and Sehanine Moonbow. She is venerated nearly
exclusively by moon elves and half-moon elves; other subraces worship her three aspects as separate
goddesses and rarely give homage to the Union of the Three itself.

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms
- **All Clergy:** All clerics (including multiclassed half-elven clerics), crusaders, druids, mystics,
  and specialty priests of Angharradh receive religion (elf) and literacy (Espruar) as bonus
  proficiencies.
- **Organization:** Most of Angharradh's clergy are affiliated with one aspect of the Three and their
  activities reflect that association; specialty priests of the Three are considered members of both
  that aspect's clergy and Angharradh's. A minority (10%) are totem sisters, female moon elf druids who
  practice totemic magic, craft charms, and serve as tribal wise women, attending births, blessing
  children, and counseling warriors before battle. Temples resemble those of the Three, either favoring
  one goddess's style or blending all three, and always display both the symbol of the One and the
  Three and the symbols of the individual goddesses.
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Triune Seeker (novice)
  2. Trimorph (full priest)

  Beyond full priest, priests use whatever titles are appropriate to the aspect of the Three they
  primarily venerate (see Aerdrie Faenya and Hanali Celanil above; Sehanine Moonbow is covered
  elsewhere).

### Dogma

- Through unity and diversity there is strength.
- Be ever vigilant against She Who Was Banished, and work together in defending the lands of the Fair
  Folk from those who would work evil.
- Celebrate the One and the Three for their collective purpose and individual expressions of life.
- Through the melding of widely different skills and interests, creativity, life, and artistry are
  nurtured and new ideas are discovered.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve the Triune Goddess through the activities of whichever of the Three (Aerdrie, Hanali, or
  Sehanine) the individual priest is affiliated with.
- Totem sisters craft totemic charms for their tribe, attend births, bless children, aid with planting
  and harvest, and bless warriors before battle; successful totem sisters are sought out by other
  tribes for advice.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Melding of the Three | Shieldmeet (quadrennial) | The only holy day celebrated exclusively in Angharradh's own name (elsewhere celebrated more generally as Cinnaelos'Cor, the Day of Corellon's Peace). The Teu-Tel'Quessir sing hymns to the Triune Goddess and often assemble to invoke great feats of cooperative magic. |

### Affiliated Orders

- No knightly orders are specifically affiliated with Angharradh; members of orders affiliated with
  Aerdrie Faenya, Hanali Celanil, or Sehanine Moonbow sometimes worship their order's patron as an
  aspect of Angharradh instead.

## Specialty Priests

Angharradh has two specialty-priest tracks: circle singers, who are simultaneously specialty priests of
one of the Three, and totem sisters, who are effectively druids.

### Circle Singers

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Varies -- circle singers receive all the benefits of, and abide by the
  restrictions of, the specialty priest track of whichever aspect (Aerdrie Faenya, Hanali Celanil, or
  Sehanine Moonbow) they primarily venerate; see that deity's specialty priest entry.
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Varies (as above)
- **Major Spheres:** Varies (as above)
- **Minor Spheres:** Varies (as above)
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** Varies (as above)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on singing; otherwise varies (as above)

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up. The named
spells below are not detailed in this source; their spell levels come from the parenthetical noted
alongside each in the source text.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | May cast cooperative magic together with other priests of Angharradh, Aerdrie Faenya, Hanali Celanil, or Sehanine Moonbow |
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Ring of Hands (mana ~1), once per tenday |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Mystic Transfer *or* Sanctify (mana ~2 each), choose one, once per tenday |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Line of Protection *or* Unearthly Choir (mana ~3 each), choose one, once per tenday |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Focus, Fortify, *or* Uplift (mana ~4 each), choose one, once per tenday |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Meld *or* Thoughtwave (mana ~5 each), choose one, once per tenday |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: The Great Circle *or* Spiritual Wrath (mana ~6 each), choose one, once per tenday |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Spirit of Power (mana ~7), once per tenday |

### Totem Sisters

Totem sisters of Angharradh are effectively druids; their class abilities otherwise follow the standard
Druid rules, plus totemic magic (a rune-magic-like system detailed in Elves of Evermeet, not covered in
this source).

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Any; recommended long bow and spear
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light or Medium (hide, leather, padded, studded leather, or chain
  mail), shields allowed
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Charm, Elemental, Healing, Plant, Weather, Sun
- **Minor Spheres:** None
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** None beyond baseline
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on art (general)

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Must be a female moon elf; not allowed to multiclass; can use totemic magic, a rune-magic-like system (see Elves of Evermeet, not detailed in this source) |

No further unique granted-power list; standard Player's Handbook Druid class abilities otherwise apply.

## Angharradhan Spells (deity-specific)

Angharradh has no unique spells of her own beyond those granted through circle singers above; her
priests may otherwise cast any spell unique to Aerdrie Faenya or Hanali Celanil (see those entries).
Spells unique to Sehanine Moonbow, her third aspect, are covered in the companion batch of this
conversion project.
