# Labelas Enoreth (the Lifegiver, Lord of the Continuum, the One-Eyed God, the Philosopher, the Sage at Sunset)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Labelas Enoreth
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Lifegiver, Lord of the Continuum, the One-Eyed God, the Philosopher, the Sage at Sunset
- **Portfolio:** Time, longevity, the moment of choice, history
- **Symbol:** A setting sun; the priesthood's physical holy symbol is a semicircular gold disk carved to resemble the setting sun

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters
- **All Clergy:** All clerics, mystics, specialty priests, and chronomancers receive religion (pantheon) and literacy (Espruar) as bonus skills.
- **Organization:** A small but dedicated following found in most elven cultures; the counsel of Labelas's priests is sought whenever far-reaching decisions must be made. The Ar-Tel'Quessir in particular revere the Lifegiver for the philosophical, farseeing nature of both god and clergy. Temples are monuments unbowed by time -- built amid the branches of ancient forest giants or carved from weathered mountain stone -- each dominated by a massive golden sundial inlaid in the floor and windowed to catch the setting sun, and each maintaining a library, some housing the greatest collections of elven lore in the Realms. The clergy is gold elves (40%), moon elves (30%), wood elves (12%), sea elves (10%), half-elves of those ancestries (7%), and other elves/half-elves (1%); divided between clerics (77%), specialty priests (20%), mystics (2%), and chronomancers (1%); with more women (61%) than men (39%).
- **Ranks/titles within the clergy** (ascending order):
  1. Tyros (novice)
  2. Time Sentinel (full priest)
  3. Observer
  4. Recorder
  5. Librarian
  6. Lorist
  7. Scholar
  8. Historian
  9. Sage
  10. Philosopher
  11. Wizened (collective title for high-ranking priests, each of whom also holds a unique individual title)

  Specialty priests are known as chronologians.

### Dogma

- The march of time is inexorable, but the Lifegiver's blessing lets the children of Corellon live long, fruitful lives unmarked by the passage of years.
- Record and preserve the lessons of history, and draw lessons from all that has unfolded.
- In the end, the sun always sets before the next day dawns anew.
- Follow Labelas's teachings, and time is on your side.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Serve as keepers of elven history and lore, searching out hidden facts of the past.
- Compile, protect, and record sacred knowledge for the instruction of future generations.
- Act as philosophers and teachers, educating the young and promoting and acquiring knowledge.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Marking of Time | Daily, at sunset | The faithful gather in small groves near temples to mark the passage of another day, uttering prayers and reciting all they have learned that day, recorded by the lorekeepers of Arvandor. |
| Blessing of a Birth | The first sunset after a birth -- only when the priest first receives an advance vision instructing them to do so | Considered a great honor and a sign the child will live a long, fruitful life; a bless spell is cast on the infant amid prayers to Labelas. |

### Affiliated Orders

- *(Informal)* Order of the Setting Sun -- a fellowship of elf and half-elf archaeologists, bards, historians, lorekeepers, and sages who preserve and rediscover the relics and knowledge of elven cultures that have passed into history.
- *(Formal)* Knights Paradoxical -- an elite order of warriors, wizards, and priests who guard the integrity of the timestream against chronomancers and others who would alter history; they guard legendary time gates and hunt down copies of time conduit spells.

## Specialty Priests

- **Name/Title:** Chronologian
- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Club, dagger, knife, quarterstaff, sling, staff-sling; favored weapon quarterstaff
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Light or Medium (leather armor or elven chain mail)
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Astral, Charm, Divination, Necromantic, Numbers, Plant, Protection, Sun, Thought, Time
- **Minor Spheres:** Healing, Weather
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in literacy (Common)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on astrology; +1 bonus on lore (elven history)
