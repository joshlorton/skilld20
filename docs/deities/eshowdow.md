# Eshowdow (The Shadow Giant, Devourer of Honor, Courage, and Nobility, Betrayer of the Eshowe, Ravager of the Tabaxi)

## Deity Info

- **Name:** Eshowdow
- **Titles/Epithets:** The Shadow Giant, Devourer of Honor, Courage, and Nobility, Betrayer of the
  Eshowe, Ravager of the Tabaxi
- **Portfolio:** Shadows, destruction, revenge, cowardice, ignominy, fear
- **Symbol:** A black, vaguely man-shaped shadow with long claws

## Church Summary

- **Clergy:** Spellcasters, Men-at-arms (crusaders)
- **All Clergy:** All shamans, specialty priests, and crusaders of Eshowdow receive religion (Chultan)
  as a bonus proficiency. Unlike most faiths, Eshowdow's clergy includes no clerics at all.
- **Organization:** Worship is almost entirely confined to the Eshowe, a once-proud tribe now reduced
  to lurking remnants in the Valley of Lost Honor, joined by a scattering of traitorous Tabaxi outcasts
  and Batiri goblins. Temples exist more on the Demiplane of Shadow than the Prime Material Plane --
  low, brooding step-pyramid complexes raised with wall of shadow magic in areas of perpetual gloom,
  riddled with portals to the Demiplane of Shadow. The clergy (the Eshewtak) is dominated by shamans
  (over 65%), followed by specialty priests known as shadow stalkers (about 30%), with the remainder
  crusaders. There is no formal hierarchy of titles; power is settled through personal prowess,
  assassination, or formal duel, and each priest is given a unique personal name upon induction,
  said to come from Eshowdow himself.

### Dogma

- Ubtao broke the bond between his own body and spirit in creating the world, and in doing so turned
  to evil, choosing the Tabaxi to serve as his mortal overseers and enslavers of the Eshowe.
- Every creature is divorced from its spirit at creation; only by following Eshowdow's teachings can
  spirit and body be reunited and a higher state of existence attained.
- Never trust the Tabaxi -- they must ultimately be eliminated from Chult.
- Direct confrontation is wise only when the timing is perfect; otherwise favor sabotage, the killing
  of spies and stragglers, subversion of the innocent, and the covert elimination of Tabaxi authority
  figures, since such methods pose the least risk to the Eshowe and turn the enemy's fear against
  itself.
- On pain of death, never reveal the location of the Great Shadow's Mansion to any nonbeliever.

### Day-to-Day Activities

- Administer every aspect of the slowly rebuilding Eshowe nation's spiritual, political, and cultural
  life.
- Gather intelligence on and plan attacks against the Tabaxi; many sojourn on the Demiplane of Shadow
  seeking lost magic usable against their foes.
- Balance the competing demands of secrecy and revenge, while managing deep internal factionalism and
  long-simmering rivalries within the priesthood itself.

### Holy Days / Important Ceremonies

| Name | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Unearthing | Varies year to year; always gloomy, cloudy weather, sometimes a brief solar eclipse visible only in the Valley of Lost Honor | Marks the disinterment of the Shadow Giant and his promise of revenge against the Tabaxi. |
| The Day of All Sighs | 66 days after the Unearthing | A somber day of mourning the Eshowe's ancient defeat by Ubtao and the Tabaxi, and rededication to eventual vengeance. Many Eshowe undergo the Shadow Binding this day, a ritual (via Summon Shadow Spirit) meant to reunite a sundered spirit with the body -- the truth of the rite is far more horrifying than the Eshowe dare contemplate. |

### Affiliated Orders

- (Formal) Kenswa-Tokashow ("Children of the Ebon Hand") -- crusaders, warriors, and rogues organized
  into three-member Triads; the elite military arm of the Eshowe, ambushing lone Tabaxi, destroying
  livestock and farms, and gathering intelligence throughout the Jungle of Chult.
- (Informal) Abuya-Deimshowa ("Speakers of the Shadow") -- a secretive cabal of senior shadow mages
  (Abuyakas) who advise the Eshowe clan chiefs; initiates must survive a month alone on the Demiplane
  of Shadow. Conservative and patient, they frequently clash with the Kenswa-Tokashow over policy.

## Specialty Priests

Eshowdow's clergy has two priest tracks with their own granted powers: shamans (dowas) and specialty
priests (shadow stalkers). Both must be humans of Eshowe descent and must undergo the Summon Shadow
Spirit rite upon entering the priesthood.

### Dowas (Shamans)

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Short-handled hand axe, blowgun, club, kerrie, spear, yklwa; no favored
  weapon specified in the source, and none found in real-world published material
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Leather, hide, or Eshowe scale mail armor; wooden or hide
  (typically dinosaur hide) shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Animal, Summoning, Sun (reversed forms of reversible spells only),
  Necromantic, Travelers, Protection, Wards
- **Minor Spheres:** Creation, Healing, Plant
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in lore (Eshowe history), 1 rank in survival (jungle), 1 rank in
  linguistics (Tabaxi)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on herbalism; +1 bonus on healing

Dowas commune only with spirits of dead Eshowe, never animal or nature spirits (which they consider
minions of Ubtao). Minor spirits they contact can additionally invoke chill touch, spook, scare,
spectral hand, Lorloveim's creeping shadow, spirit armor, or wraithform; major spirits can additionally
invoke enervation, fear, shadow monsters, shadow door, shadow magic, shades, or shadow walk -- these
supplement the spirit's normal powers rather than replacing them.

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

No unique granted-power list beyond the spirit-contact abilities above; dowas otherwise follow the
standard shaman class progression.

### Shadow Stalkers (Specialty Priests)

- **Weapon Proficiencies:** Short-handled hand axe, blow gun, club, dagger, kerrie, machete, scimitar,
  spear, yklwa; no favored weapon specified in the source, and none found in real-world published
  material
- **Armor & Shield Proficiencies:** Leather, hide, or Eshowe scale mail armor; wooden or hide
  (typically dinosaur hide) shields
- **Major Spheres:** All, Astral, Chaos, Charm, Combat, Necromantic, Plant, Summoning, Sun (reversed
  forms of reversible spells only)
- **Minor Spheres:** Divination, Healing, Protection, Travelers, War
- **Magical Items:** No limitations
- **Skills Required:** 1 rank in performance (singing), 1 rank in survival (jungle), 1 rank in lore (Eshowe
  history), 1 rank in linguistics (Tabaxi)
- **Skill Bonuses:** +1 bonus on religion (pantheon); +1 bonus on blind-fighting; +1 bonus on disguise

Shadow stalkers can hide in shadows as a thief of the same level. Spells from the Sun sphere and any
granted power below are keyed to prevailing light: opponents' saves against them worsen from +2 in
bright daylight/continual light down to -4 in total darkness (weak daylight/dusk is unmodified); the
shadow stalker's own saves are never affected by lighting.

#### Rank Progression (skill ranks 1-20)

Mana formula: mana = 10 × spell level × uses per day. Non-daily rates normalize to a per-day
equivalent first (e.g. "2 per tenday" = 10 × level × (2/10) = level × 2). Where no spell level is
given but a class/character level is, use spell level = that class level ÷ 2, rounded up.

| Rank | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation bonus: Spook (10 mana), once per day |
| 3 | Foundation bonus: Darkness, 15' Radius *or* Scare (20 mana each), choose one per use, once per day |
| 5 | Foundation bonus: Spirit Armor *or* Wraithform (30 mana each), choose one per use, once per day |
| 7 | Foundation bonus: Fear *or* Shadow Monsters (40 mana each), choose one per use, once per day |
| 10 | Foundation bonus: Shadow Magic *or* Demishadow Monsters (50 mana each), choose one per use, once per day |
| 13 | Foundation bonus: Demishadow Magic *or* Shades (60 mana each), choose one per use, once per day |
| 15 | Foundation bonus: Shadow Walk (70 mana), once per day |
| 18 | Foundation bonus: Shadowform (80 mana), once per day |
