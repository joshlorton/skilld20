# SKILLd20 Ch09 -- Material Components

Material components are physical substances consumed or expended during casting to modify a Foundation's attributes or effects. Component effects are applied before Heightening changes.

Components are categorized as:
- **Foundation Components**: Apply only to specific Foundations where listed.
- **Universal Components**: Apply to any Foundation sharing a specified Trait. See Universal Components section below.

---

## Foundation Components

Foundation components are listed in each Foundation's entry. The examples below are drawn from the Animate Dead Foundation.

### Animate Dead -- Material Components

| Attribute | Actions | Component | Effect | Description | Price |
|-----------|---------|-----------|--------|-------------|-------|
| Area | 1 | Material | Increase burst radius to 15' | A pinch of bone dust from a previously animated creature | 1 gp |
| Targets | 1 | Material | Use HP as mana (1 hp = 2 mana) | 1 or more hit points of the caster's own blood | -- |
| Saving Throw / Effect | 1 | Material | Save at --1/3 creature's level; animated creature gains +1/3 creature's level | A piece of bone or scrap of flesh from the target creature | By creature level |
| Duration | 2 | Material | Increase control duration by 1 step (1 rnd → 1 min → 10 min → 2 hr → 1 day) | Black onyx gem(s) | 45 gp |
| Sustain | 3 | Material | Halve the mana cost of the Renew Control sustain action | Black onyx gem(s), one per animated creature level | 15 gp per gem |

**Black Onyx:** A smooth, opaque black gemstone used in funerary jewelry and necromantic work across many cultures. Its use in death magic predates recorded history. Sold by lapidaries, jewelers, and apothecaries in regions with active death cults or undead populations.

**Bone Dust:** Ground remains of a previously animated creature. The animated creature must have been genuinely animated through necromantic magic, not simply dead -- inert bone has no effect. Harvested during or immediately after destruction of an animated undead.

**Caster's Blood:** The caster opens a vein deliberately as part of the casting action. The HP cost is the component cost; there is no gold price. Healing the wound afterward is a separate action.

**Bone or Flesh Scrap:** A piece of bone or preserved flesh from the same creature being targeted for animation. Must come from the specific corpse or skeleton being animated -- generic bone has no effect. This component is consumed by the casting.

## Universal Components

Universal components apply to any Foundation bearing a specified Trait. They are not listed per Foundation. A caster who knows a universal component may use it with any qualifying Foundation.

> **Future State:** The Universal Components system is not yet finalized. The entries below are documented examples. Full rules for universal component access, discovery, and use will be developed in a future chapter.

> **Editor Note:** Universal components are not yet supported in the Foundation editor. They will be tracked in this chapter until editor support is implemented.

### Universal Necromancy Components

These components apply to any Foundation with the **Necromancy** trait.

---

**Morion**

*Rare -- 150 gp*

A few pieces of morion: black to very dark smoky quartz, semi-precious, semi-transparent. Found in igneous and metamorphic formations in cold highland regions. Named for its resemblance to the black morion helmet of earlier centuries.

**Effect:** Increase the DC to counterspell or dispel the affected spell by 1.

The quartz's dense crystalline structure resists magical interference. Necromancers who work in contested territory -- where rivals or clerics actively attempt to unravel their workings -- prize morion for keeping animated servants intact under suppression attempts.

---

**Nightspar**

*Rare -- 100 gp (powdered)*

Powdered nightspar: a rare crystalline mineral, deep black with faint silver veining, found in arid and volcanic regions. Occurs in columnar formations. The powder is produced by grinding raw nightspar crystals with a mortar of non-ferrous metal; iron disrupts the compound.

**Effect:** Substitute for up to 6 mana or 50 gp worth of unavailable material components required by the affected Foundation.

Nightspar carries a residual necromantic affinity -- it resonates with death-aspected magic without being a death product itself, making it an unusually versatile substitute in necromantic work. Alchemists in cities with active trade in magical components typically stock it in small quantities. Remote or landlocked regions may have none at all.

*Powdered nightspar is consumed on use. Raw nightspar crystals must be ground before use; this requires 10 minutes and appropriate tools.*
