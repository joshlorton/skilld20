## Cantrip Threshold (Revised)

> **A spell is a cantrip -- costing no mana and requiring no Skill Check -- when:**
> `Spellcraft [Tradition] rank + Foundation rank > mana cost + Foundation difficulty modifier`

The threshold is strictly greater than, not equal to. This preserves a gap between the cantrip ceiling and the caster's maximum investment, ensuring deliberate mana spending remains meaningful at high rank.

### Foundation Difficulty Modifier

The difficulty of the Foundation itself raises the effective cost for purposes of both the cantrip threshold and the safe mana threshold. The same modifiers apply to both, uniformly.

| Difficulty | Modifier |
|-----------|----------|
| Average   | +0       |
| Hard      | +2       |
| Very Hard | +5       |
| Incredibly Hard | +10 |

**Effect on cantrip threshold:** A caster must have combined ranks 2/5/10 higher than the mana cost to free-cast a Hard/Very Hard/Incredibly Hard Foundation.

**Effect on safe mana threshold:** The Foundation's difficulty modifier is subtracted from the caster's safe mana when casting that Foundation. This means harder Foundations are genuinely more dangerous to overcast regardless of the caster's Constitution or mana pool size.

> **Example:** A caster with combined rank 8 (Spellcraft 4 + Foundation 4) and safe mana 20.
> - An Average Foundation costing 7 mana: `8 > 7 + 0` → cantrip. Safe mana 20.
> - A Hard Foundation costing 7 mana: `8 > 7 + 2 = 9` → false, NOT a cantrip. Safe mana 20 - 2 = 18.
> - A Very Hard Foundation costing 7 mana: `8 > 7 + 5 = 12` → false. Safe mana 20 - 5 = 15.

The difficulty modifier creates a unified pressure that affects both free-cast access and overcast risk. A Very Hard Foundation is genuinely more dangerous at every skill level, not only when a caster is near their limit.

### Design Note (Deferred)

Foundation difficulty reducing the cantrip threshold independently of mana cost (treating difficulty as a separate gate rather than an additive modifier) was considered and deferred. The additive approach above is simpler and mechanically consistent. The separate-gate approach may be revisited if playtesting shows the additive model flattens distinction between difficulty tiers at high rank.

## Focus Pool

The Focus Pool is a separate mana sub-pool available to casters who designate specific Foundations as Focus Foundations through their Core Feat or tradition.

### Pool Size

> `Focus Pool = (sum of all Focus Foundation ranks) × 10`

Each rank invested in a Focus Foundation contributes 10 mana to the Focus Pool. Multiple Focus Foundations stack: a caster with Healing rank 3 and Harm rank 2 as Focus Foundations has a Focus Pool of 50 mana.

The Focus Pool is a single pool shared by all Focus Foundations. It is not split per Foundation.

### Spending Mana

When casting a Focus Foundation spell, the caster may draw mana from:
- The Focus Pool only
- The main tradition pool only
- Any combination of both pools

Safe mana thresholds are tracked per pool independently. Mana drawn from the Focus Pool counts against that pool's safe mana; mana drawn from the main pool counts against the main pool's safe mana. Overcast damage is calculated and applied separately per pool.

> **Example:** A caster has Focus Pool safe mana 18 and main pool safe mana 30. Casting a Focus Foundation spell costing 25 mana, they draw 18 from the Focus Pool and 7 from the main pool. No overcast damage. If they instead draw 22 from the Focus Pool, they suffer 4 unavoidable damage from that pool.

### Safe Mana Threshold for Any Mana Pool

Every mana pool -- main pool, Focus Pool, or any tradition-specific sub-pool -- uses the safe mana formula of the tradition that governs it. Trained and Untrained traditions keep their respective formulas. There is no unified formula.

| Tradition Type | Safe Mana Formula |
|---------------|------------------|
| Trained (Arcane, Divine, Bardic) | `(Tier + 6) + (Tier + 1) per Spellcraft rank` |
| Untrained (Bloodline, Pact) | `(Tier + 2) + Tier per Spellcraft rank` |

**Focus Pool safe mana:** Uses the formula of the caster's governing tradition. A Divine cleric's Focus Pool uses the Trained formula. A Bloodline sorcerer's Focus Pool uses the Untrained formula.

**Tradition-specific sub-pools (e.g., Arcane Siphon):** The Arcane tradition's Siphon ability (extinguish fire, gain Flame Mana pool equal to reduced damage) creates a sub-pool that uses the Arcane (Trained) safe mana formula. Any sub-pool generated by a tradition-specific ability follows the same rule: the governing tradition determines the safe mana calculation.

This preserves the design intent that untrained power is harder to control. An Untrained caster's Focus Pool of the same size as a Trained caster's will have a lower safe mana ceiling -- the sub-pool inherits the same risk profile as the tradition it comes from.

Foundation difficulty modifiers (-2, -5, -10) are applied after the base formula calculation, reducing the effective safe mana for that casting regardless of which pool is drawn from.

## Refocus

Refocus is an active recovery mechanic for the Focus Pool. It is separate from normal rest-based mana recovery.

### Recovery Schedule

| Method | Time | Focus Pool Recovery | Other Activity |
|--------|------|--------------------|-|
| Rest (passive) | 1 hour | 10% of Focus Pool | Permitted (light activity) |
| Rest (passive) | 8 hours | 100% of Focus Pool | Normal sleep/rest |
| Refocus (active) | 10 minutes | 10% of Focus Pool | Permitted (light activity) |
| Meditative Refocus | 10 minutes | Additional 10% (total 20%) | Not permitted; dedicated meditation only |

The Focus Pool recovers on the same hourly schedule as the main tradition pool (10% per hour, 100% per 8 hours). This recovery happens automatically during rest and does not require any action.

### Refocus Action

Spending 10 minutes performing Refocus activities (performing deeds connected to the tradition -- meditation, prayer, practice, performance, etc.) immediately restores an additional 10% of the Focus Pool. There is no daily limit on Refocus; it may be performed as many times as time allows.

Refocus may not be performed during combat or other time-pressured situations. The 10-minute period must be uninterrupted.

### Meditative Refocus

If the caster spends the Refocus period in dedicated Meditation (making a Meditation skill check, no other activities), a successful check restores an additional 10% of the Focus Pool on top of the standard Refocus recovery (total 20% per 10-minute period). No other activities may be taken during Meditative Refocus.

A failed Meditation check provides only the standard 10% Refocus recovery. A Critical Failure provides no recovery for that period.

### Interaction with Main Pool

Refocus restores only the Focus Pool. It does not restore the main tradition pool. The 1-hour rest recovery restores both pools simultaneously.

## Divine Focus

The Divine Focus sub-system governs how Divine tradition casters access the Focus Pool.

### Access Determination

Domain selection (through Background or Core Feat investment) determines which Foundations fall within Divine Focus scope. A Foundation is in scope if it is tagged with a Divine Domain matching one of the caster's domains.

There is no automatic Heal/Harm restriction on the Divine Focus. The Focus Pool is domain-defined, not role-defined. A Sun Domain cleric's Focus Pool includes Illumination and Radiant Foundations alongside Healing; a Death Domain cleric's includes Injury and Necrotic Foundations alongside Harm. The cleric's identity is defined by domain, not by a binary support/damage switch.

> **Mechanical effect:** Foundations tagged with a matching Divine Domain are cast at Focus Pool rates (drawing from Focus Pool, refocusable). Foundations outside the domain set are cast from the main pool normally.

### Heal and Harm Access

Healing and Injury Foundations remain available to all Divine casters from the main pool. The Divine Focus does not restrict or remove this access -- it only defines which Foundations receive the Focus Pool benefit. A cleric with a Fire Domain can still cast Healing from the main pool; they simply do not get Focus Pool access to it.

## Magus -- Studious Spells

Identical structure to Divine Focus. The hybrid path chosen at character creation determines which Foundations fall within Focus scope. The hybrid path is permanent (or very costly to change through story events) where Divine Domain access is theoretically more flexible.

Hybrid paths define a small, curated set of Foundations as Studious. This list is mechanically identical to a domain list in structure; the flavor and source differ.

> FLAG: Magus hybrid path Foundation lists are not yet written. Each hybrid path (Spell Warrior, Laughing Shadow, Inexorable Iron, etc.) requires its own curated Foundation list before Magus characters can be built.

## Bardic Composition

Bardic Composition uses the Focus Pool framework with one structural difference: the casting check uses a Perform skill (specific to the performance type) rather than Spellcraft.

The Bardic Performance Core Feat grants access to the Bardic tradition and its Focus Pool. The Composition Focus Pool is governed by the same rules as other Focus Pools: pool size from Focus Foundation ranks × 10, Refocus available, safe mana tracked independently.

> **FLAG:** The relationship between the Bardic main mana pool and the Composition Focus Pool requires clarification. Leading question: is the Bardic Performance Core Feat a standalone tradition (main pool only, Composition as sub-pool), or does the Focus Pool serve as the primary pool for Bardic casters, with the main pool used only for non-Composition access? The original Bard v1 design treated Bardic Performance as its own tradition; this should be confirmed before writing the Bard tradition entry.

## Summary of Confirmed Standing Rules (Ch08 Additions)

1. **Cantrip threshold:** `Spellcraft rank + Foundation rank > mana cost + difficulty modifier` (strictly greater than).
2. **Foundation difficulty modifier** applies identically to both the cantrip threshold and the safe mana threshold: Hard +2, Very Hard +5, Incredibly Hard +10.
3. **Focus Pool size:** `(sum of Focus Foundation ranks) × 10`.
4. **Mana mixing:** When casting Focus Foundation spells, mana may be drawn from either or both pools. Safe mana is tracked per pool independently.
5. **Safe mana per pool:** Every mana pool uses the safe mana formula of its governing tradition. Trained and Untrained formulas remain separate. This applies to Focus Pools, Siphon pools, and any other tradition-specific sub-pool.
6. **Refocus:** 10-minute action, no daily limit. Restores 10% of Focus Pool. Meditative Refocus (check required, dedicated time) restores 20%.
7. **Focus Pool passive regen:** Same schedule as main pool (10%/hour, 100%/8 hours).
8. **Divine Focus:** Domain determines in-scope Foundations. No automatic Heal/Harm restriction.
9. **Bardic Composition:** Focus Pool framework, Perform check governs instead of Spellcraft.

## Refocus Activities

### Three Refocus Modes

**Refocus (Sole Action)**
Spend 10 minutes in dedicated refocus activity. No skill check. No downside. Flat 10% Focus Pool recovery regardless of circumstances.

Valid activities: any exploration-mode activity that does not require a skill check. Traveling at normal pace, light prayer or ritual, quietly performing, tending equipment.

**Refocus (Background Action)**
Refocus occurs alongside another exploration activity. No additional time cost. No skill check. Flat 10% Focus Pool recovery as a bonus to the primary activity.

The player must declare intent to Refocus before beginning the primary action. The GM approves or denies based on whether the circumstances plausibly allow the caster to maintain focus on their magical connection while performing the primary activity. Approval is not automatic even when the activity matches the Foundation type -- a chaotic environment, a demanding primary task, or other situational factors may make simultaneous Refocus implausible.

If approved, both the primary activity and the Refocus complete at the end of the 10-minute period. If denied, the player may choose to switch to Refocus as a Sole Action instead, foregoing the primary activity.

**Dedicated Meditate**
Spend 10 minutes in uninterrupted meditation focused entirely on restoring magical connection. No other activities permitted. Requires a Meditation skill check.

| Result | Recovery | Time | Additional Effect |
|--------|----------|------|------------------|
| Critical Success | 20% | 10 min | -- |
| Success | 10% | 10 min | -- |
| Failure | 10% | 20 min | Fail forward: recovery granted after additional 10 minutes |
| Critical Failure | 10% | 20 min | Fail forward; cannot use Dedicated Meditate for Refocus again until after a full 1-hour rest |

On Failure or Critical Failure, the caster still receives the 10% recovery -- it is not lost -- but the meditation session runs long. On Critical Failure the additional lockout applies only to the Dedicated Meditate variant; Refocus as a Sole Action or Background Action remains available normally.

### Exception List -- Foundation-Matched Background Refocus

| Foundation Type | Valid Background Activity |
|----------------|--------------------------|
| Healing | Any Heal checks (treating wounds, stabilizing, diagnosing) |
| Animal, Plant, other nature | Survival checks (tracking, foraging, identifying terrain, determining direction) |
| Charm | Social checks (Diplomacy, Gather Information, sustained social interaction) |
| Travel | Forced march; Survival checks to navigate or determine direction |
| *(additional entries added per Foundation during entry writing)* | |
